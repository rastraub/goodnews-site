A food-recovery cooler now sits at the New Ulm Public Library, KEYC reported. The point of the cooler is to move surplus food to neighbors instead of into the trash. The library is a public building people already use, so the fridge is somewhere a donation and a pickup can both happen without a special trip across town.

KEYC's report is the placement itself: a cooler, at the library, for food that still has a use.
