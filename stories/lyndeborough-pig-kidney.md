Molly Falco of Lyndeborough, New Hampshire, has become the longest-living recipient of a pig kidney, WMUR reported. One year after the transplant at Massachusetts General Hospital, she holds the record for living with a non-human organ and is off dialysis.

The year is how long the kidney has functioned in her. Off dialysis means she is not depending on that treatment now. The record is for time living with a non-human organ, not a claim that the approach is routine care.
