An experimental malaria vaccine blocked infection in 87 percent of volunteers in an early human trial led by UW Medicine, the university's newsroom reported. The NIH-sponsored phase 1 study used a human challenge. Of 15 people given PfSPZ-LARC2, 13 stayed infection-free when exposed 12 to 16 weeks later to a malaria strain different from the one in the vaccine. None of the 7 people on placebo stayed infection-free. The results were presented at the American Society of Tropical Medicine and Hygiene meeting.

This is an early-stage trial, not a finished vaccine campaign. Larger trials are still needed. The 13 of 15 and 0 of 7 figures are the challenge results in this small group.
