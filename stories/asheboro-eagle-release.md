A female bald eagle that was found barely responsive in a landfill is flying again after nearly two months of care, WCTI reported. The North Carolina Zoo's wildlife rehabilitation center nursed her back to strength and released her at the zoo. The release comes in the center's 25th year. She arrived too weak to respond and left flying after that stretch of care.
