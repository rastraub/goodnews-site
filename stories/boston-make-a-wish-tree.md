Livy, a girl being treated for leukemia, thought she was at Faneuil Hall to light Boston's Christmas tree, Boston 25 News reported. Make-A-Wish then told her and her family they are going to Disney World.

The tree lighting was the cover for the surprise. The wish is the trip. Her family is going with her. The report is about that announcement, not about her treatment.
