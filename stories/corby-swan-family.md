A family of swans that wandered into Corby went back to a lake by car. The BBC reported on October 27, 2026, that two adults and four cygnets were first seen near a bowling green on Occupation Road on Saturday. The next day, volunteers from The Waterfowl Sanctuary in Godmanchester found them sunning in a front garden on Hazelwood Road, as if they were waiting for a taxi.

Graham Porter and a colleague settled the birds into big blue bags and drove them. A boating lake about two miles away was ruled out because people there do not usually have a swan family. Pen Green lakes, about a mile away, fit. Someone at the water had been wondering where the swans had gone and helped carry the six from the car. Porter said the cygnets slept while he drove.

The birds were lost in town, not injured. The ride put them back on water that looked like home.
