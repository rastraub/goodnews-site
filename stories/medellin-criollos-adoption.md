Medellín closed its 2026 calendar of big adoption days with 41 animals placed in homes. El Tiempo reported on November 23 that the city event, Todos Somos Criollos, ran on November 22 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. in the plaza of the Museo de Arte Moderno, in Ciudad del Río. Staff brought 80 animals from the Centro de Bienestar Animal La Perla, 40 dogs and 40 cats. By the end, 21 cats and 20 dogs had families.

Among them was Frank, a seven-year-old dog whose walk drew notice. Mariana and Sebastián, a couple who already kept pets, took him for his later years. Angie Obando adopted a dog named Colcha. Elizabeth Coral, the city's subsecretary for animal protection and welfare, said this was the fourth and last scheduled adoptathon of the year. The first, in the same plaza, had been aimed at older animals. The city said La Perla placed more than 1,600 animals, including dogs, cats, and other species, during 2026, and that about 4,200 dogs and cats have been adopted since the current administration began.

The plaza also invited people to sign an anti-fireworks pledge. The city said year-end explosions leave pets and wild animals frightened, and that abandonment reports rise around Christmas and New Year. Adoptions at La Perla, in Belén Altavista, stayed open after the event. The city asked families to be sure they can feed an animal, pay for veterinary care, and give it time. The news is one afternoon and 41 matches. Thirty-nine of the animals brought that day were still waiting.
