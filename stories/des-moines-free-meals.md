Small businesses in Des Moines handed out 100 free Thanksgiving meals. The owner of Big Boyz Treasures hosted the giveaway, the third year he has done it. He has said he knows what struggling is like. A hundred meals is a modest number next to a citywide drive. It is still a hundred tables that did not have to buy the dinner, from a shop that decided the giveaway was worth repeating.
