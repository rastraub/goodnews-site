Meals on Wheels volunteers brought Thanksgiving dinners and a visit to more than 650 Oahu residents. Lanakila Pacific delivered hot meals to homebound seniors and people with disabilities, many of whom spend the holiday alone. The route is a list of doors that will not open onto a family table. The food is hot because it was cooked for that day. The stop is short, and it is still the company some of those kitchens get.
