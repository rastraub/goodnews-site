Tech Goes Home has graduated 25,000 people from its free digital-skills classes, the Chattanooga Times Free Press reported. The program, part of the Chattanooga-based Enterprise Center, gives a device to each person who finishes a 15-hour course. Director Melissa Callejas said it has partnered with more than 200 organizations since it began in Chattanooga in 2015. It expanded across Tennessee in 2021 and into Georgia and Alabama this year.

The menu includes workforce skills and college and career readiness. The most requested class is for older adults learning telehealth, a digital calendar for appointments, and video calls with family. Other sessions, including ones with ArtsBuild, show younger artists how to build a site and present their work. The milestone is a count of people who finished the hours and left with a device, not a claim that a class replaces medical care.
