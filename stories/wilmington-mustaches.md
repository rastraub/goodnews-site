Mustaches for Kids kicked off in Wilmington with more than 50 volunteers set to shave Nov. 5 at Wilmington Brewing Company, then grow mustaches through the month, WECT reported. The campaign has raised more than $1 million since 2003.

The money supports the Carousel Center, NourishNC, the Boys and Girls Clubs, and the Children's Museum. A specialty beer, Mustache Million, is part of this year's kickoff. The million-dollar figure is the running total since 2003, not the amount raised on shave day.
