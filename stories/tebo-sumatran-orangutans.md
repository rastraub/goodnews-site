Five male Sumatran orangutans that had been rescued and raised back to health were released into Bukit Tigapuluh National Park, in Tebo district. ANTARA reported the release on November 19, 2026. Himawan Sasongko, head of Jambi's natural resources conservation agency, said the five had passed assessments and were expected to settle into the forest. They had been at the Open Orangutan Sanctuary at Danau Alo, in the same landscape, in West Tanjung Jabung.

The agency's list names all five and where they were found. Digo, 7, came from Dumai in Riau. Rapala, 6, Kike, 7, and Serangin, 4, were rescued in Langkat, North Sumatra. Jating, 9, was rescued in North Padang Lawas, also in North Sumatra. Sasongko said the team wanted the move to line up with the start of the rains, both so the animals would have a better chance and so forest fires would be less of a threat. The report is this one release of five males. It is not a new survey of Sumatra's orangutans.
