Cincinnati Bengals players and Ben-Gal cheerleaders spent Tuesday at a house in Liberty Township, the Journal-News reported. With Operation Ramp It Up, and a crew from a mobility company, they installed a ramp for Navy veteran Ken Wackenthaler. Tackle Ted Karras was among the players on the job, which the paper described as a workout that did not involve a football.

The staff photos show the group fitting the ramp at the home on Oct. 27. The point of the day was a door the household can use.
