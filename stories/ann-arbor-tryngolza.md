Genetic testing gave a Michigan man a name for a rare triglyceride disorder behind decades of pancreatitis attacks, Michigan Medicine reported. He then entered a clinical trial of Tryngolza, a drug the Food and Drug Administration approved in June 2026. In his case, the attacks stopped.

That is one patient's course after testing and a trial, reported by the university medical center in Ann Arbor. It does not show that the drug ends pancreatitis for every person who has it, or that it treats every disorder involving high triglycerides. The attacks stopping is what happened for him. It is not a measure of how long that change will hold, and it is not a result from a large comparison group in this account.
