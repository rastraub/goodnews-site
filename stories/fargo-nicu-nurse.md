Twenty-one years after nurse Jackee Haak cared for her as a preemie in the NICU at Sanford in Fargo, Jaida Cox is a nursing intern working under Haak on the birth center floor, InForum reported.

The 21 years are the gap between the hospital stay and the job. Cox was the baby. Haak was the nurse, and now she supervises the intern on the floor where babies are born.
