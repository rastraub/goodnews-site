Yene, a gorilla at Rostock Zoo, gave birth to a son on November 23. NDR reported the news on November 25, 2026. Zoo director Antje Angeli said keepers had expected the baby in December, so the earlier arrival was a surprise. The zoo said the infant weighs 2 kilograms and is strong and lively.

Keepers do not expect Yene and the baby in the visitor area for a few days. Yene is 25. Eight years ago she had Rostock's first gorilla baby, Kesha, who is now six. Staff said she looked after Kesha well, and they are optimistic she will do the same for this second infant.

The news is this birth and the plan to keep mother and baby off public view while they settle. NDR did not give the infant a name, and it did not say when visitors will be able to see him.
