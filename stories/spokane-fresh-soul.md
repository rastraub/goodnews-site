Fresh Soul handed out 1,000 Thanksgiving meals and $150,000 in cash in East Central Spokane. Owner Mr. Brown and volunteers gave people a meal and an envelope of money. The event started with 100 meals. The food is one gift. The cash is another, for bills a turkey does not pay. Growing from 100 plates to a thousand, with envelopes added, is a neighborhood giveaway that outgrew its first year by a wide margin.
