The Hindu reported on November 18, 2026, that Bhutan was one of five winners of the 2026 Earthshot Prize, announced in Mumbai on November 17. Each winner receives £1 million. Karma Tenzin, director of the Department of Forests and Park Services in Bhutan's Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources, told the paper that conversations around the prize had opened chances to partner on forest fires, forest conservation, and funding. He also mentioned a possible summit in Bhutan to bring in investment.

The award is money and a set of introductions. The Hindu did not publish a hectare count, a planting total, or a date when new work would start. Tenzin described talks, not a finished project. The other winners named in the same story were a French campaign on persistent chemicals, an ocean trust in Niue, an Indian electric-vehicle company, and a Danish offshore-wind company. This note is only about the forest department's share: the prize, the partnerships he said he is now able to seek, and the summit that is still an idea.
