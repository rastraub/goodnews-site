ArtHouse Jersey has hung Luke Jerram's Moon in the St. Helier Methodist Centre through Sunday, November 29, the BBC reported on November 17, 2026. The sphere is 6 metres wide, about 20 feet, and its surface uses detailed NASA photographs of the lunar landscape. Light and sound accompany it. The same artwork has been shown in other cities. Chief executive Tom Dingle said the point of a piece in a public room is that people can share it at the same time and pause to think about the world beyond the island.

In 2023 more than 31,000 people visited a related work, the Floating Earth, on Queen's Valley Reservoir. Visitors this month included nine-year-old Eva, who called the Moon cool, and Primrose Keenan, who had seen the Earth on the lake. Toby Balderson said the size made the surface easier to read than the small view from the ground. Dingle said free public art can change the pace of a day. The news is this installation and these weeks it is open. It is not a new NASA mission.
