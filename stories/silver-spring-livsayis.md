The FDA approved Livsayis, a shot given four times a year, for adults with anti-AChR-positive generalized myasthenia gravis, WebMD reported. It is the first siRNA drug for that form of the disease. In a 24-week trial of 123 adults, 77 percent of people on Livsayis improved meaningfully on daily-living scores, compared with 44 percent on placebo.

The approval comes with a warning about meningococcal infection, and vaccination is required. The 123 adults are the trial group. Seventy-seven percent and 44 percent are the shares who improved on those scores. This is an approval based on that trial, not a claim that every patient will see the same result.
