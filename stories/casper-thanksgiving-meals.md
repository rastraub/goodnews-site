Three Casper nonprofits teamed up to give 500 families Thanksgiving meals. Joshua's Storehouse, the Salvation Army, and the Food Bank of Wyoming set up the giveaway as an assembly line on a cold morning. Five hundred meals is a lot of hands. Splitting the morning among three groups, and splitting each box among people standing in a line, is how that number got packed.
