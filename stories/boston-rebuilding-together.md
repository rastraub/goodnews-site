WCVB gave the first Karen Holmes Ward public-service award to Rebuilding Together Boston. For 35 years the nonprofit has made free repairs for seniors, veterans, families with disabilities, and households under 80 percent of the area median income. It also works on community spaces, not only private kitchens and porches.

Executive director Janice Walker accepted the award for a volunteer network that shows up with tools. The group has a workforce partnership with Franklin Cummings Tech, so students in the trades put hours into the same houses. The station's award is new. The repair list is not.
