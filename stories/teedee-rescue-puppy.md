Teedee, a puppy rescued from a hoarding case in Idaho, had a heart defect repaired at Washington State University and was adopted by his foster vet. WSU Insider reported the outcome on October 5, 2026. He now has a forever home in Pullman.

The surgery, the rescue, and the veterinarian who said yes are in the WSU Insider story. This page only carries the facts that report established, plus a link back to it.
