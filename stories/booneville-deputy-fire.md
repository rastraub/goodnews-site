On the morning of Nov. 4, Moore County Sheriff's Deputy Maygan Silavong was near the communications center when she heard Dispatcher Jordan Hall taking a call about a house fire, The Lynchburg Times reported. The caller was a disabled woman in her 80s who said she could not get out. Silavong left with the address before the call had been sent to anyone else.

The house is on Stone Lane, off Highway 129 in the Booneville community, less than a mile behind the Jack Daniel's warehouses and easy to miss. Silavong said a road sign had been stolen. GPS took her to the lane. A woman in her 80s was outside, leaning on a walker, and asked her to put the fire out.

Silavong got a fire extinguisher from the back of her patrol car. Smoke in a back room was so thick that the light was already on and she still could not see. Flames ran up a wall from the floor to the ceiling. She sent the woman back outside, then went in and emptied the extinguisher. She went back in more than once to be sure it was out.

Volunteer firefighters arrived short-handed on a weekday morning. John LaCook of the Moore County Volunteer Fire Department said Silavong had put the fire out, not just knocked it down. Crews pulled sheetrock and used a thermal camera to check that it had not moved into the walls or the attic. It had started with an electrical appliance on a desk, and it never left that room. The house needs cleaning and some sheetrock work. The homeowner had insurance, a neighbor called the insurer from the scene, and she stayed with neighbors that night. Public Safety Director Jason Deal also worked with the Red Cross so she would have help.

Paramedics checked Silavong after she came out covered in soot. Among them were her sister, critical care medic Rheannon Gagnon, and her brother-in-law, paramedic Kris Gagnon. The paper, citing a post from retired fire chief Mark Neal, said Barbara Patton gave $2,000 so the county's patrol cars would carry extinguishers. LaCook said Patton has family in the fire service and has long backed the department.
