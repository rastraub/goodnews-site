Volunteers at the Black Mountain Banding Station, behind Kearsarge High School in New London, have banded nearly three dozen Northern saw-whet owls this fall, the Concord Monitor reported. N.H. Audubon and the Forest Society run the work.

Each band joins the continent-wide Project OwlNet network, so a later encounter can show where a bird has been. Nearly three dozen is this fall's count at that station, not a population estimate for the species.
