Los Angeles County firefighters rescued a woman from fast-moving water in a flood-control channel near the City of Industry on Thursday, ABC7 reported. Department video shows a firefighter paramedic lowered from a helicopter into the channel, then the woman brought out of the water.

The county fire department said that hoist was one of several swift-water rescues that succeeded the same day. The report does not give the woman's name, and it does not say how she ended up in the channel.
