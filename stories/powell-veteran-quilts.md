In its fifth year, the Crossroads Stitchers in Powell made handmade quilts for 17 veterans, the Powell Tribune reported. That was nearly three times as many quilts as last year. The group also held a brunch for the veterans and their families. Seventeen is who received quilts this year. The fifth year is how long the stitchers have been doing it.
