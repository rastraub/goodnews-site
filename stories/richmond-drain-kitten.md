Richmond firefighters pulled a young female cat out of a storm drain, WTVR reported. Richmond Animal Care and Control nicknamed her RFD Bearwy and posted photos of the work. The shelter said the teams kept at it and refused to give up on her. Getting her out took hammering, cutting, pulling, and digging. Firefighter B. Curry lifted her clear.

The shelter thanked the fire department for the equipment and for treating one small animal as worth the call. RFD Bearwy will be available for adoption next week. The report does not say how she entered the drain or how long she was there. It does say she is out, and that the next step is a home.
