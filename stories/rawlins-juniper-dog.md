Wyoming Public Media's Thursday roundup passed along a Facebook account of a dog at the Split Rock Rest Area. The post, which the station did not attribute to a named writer, said the stray called Diya would not be trapped. A man laced up his boots and walked, and she followed him and his dog about 90 miles over four days to his house. He renamed her Juniper for the plants that had sheltered her at the stop.

The same roundup noted a second dog story: Laramie Live reports that Zara, a year-old German shepherd, is the Laramie Police Department's first K9 since 1999, trained for tracking and drug detection. The Juniper account rests on that Facebook post as WPM relayed it.
