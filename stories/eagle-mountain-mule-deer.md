About 20 volunteers planted sagebrush and native grasses near Eagle Mountain on a route used by about 2,000 mule deer, Utah Public Radio reported. The work is the start of a planned 160-acre restoration of that migration corridor.

About 20 is who showed up to plant. About 2,000 is the herd that uses the route. One hundred sixty acres is the restoration they are starting, not a claim that the whole corridor is finished.
