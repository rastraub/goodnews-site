Frank LaFerrara, 73, received the Carnegie Medal on Nov. 19 for swimming about 200 feet into a rip current near Ocean City and towing a panicked girl to safety, the Carnegie Hero Fund Commission reported. He said the honor was the nicest thing that had ever happened to him.

The swim was about 200 feet, into the current, and back with the girl. Nov. 19 is the day of the medal presentation. He lives in the Somers Point area. The medal is the Carnegie Hero Fund's award for that rescue.
