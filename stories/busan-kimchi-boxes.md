The Asia Business Daily reported on November 18, 2026, that Good Moonhwa Hospital joined Busan's Jabongine Kimjanghan Day, a kimchi-making event at the Dong-gu office. The hospital gave 2 million won and sent 10 employees, including volunteer group leader Kim Myeongcheol, to prepare ingredients, mix seasoning, and pack boxes. The Dong-gu Volunteer Center organized the day. About 110 volunteers from local businesses and groups took part, with a budget of 22 million won. The hospital has joined every year since the first event in 2022.

The 800 boxes made that day are scheduled for 800 households: older adults living alone, people with disabilities, and multicultural families. Kim said the point was to keep showing up with other local groups. The news is those 800 boxes and the people who packed them. It is not a claim that the deliveries had all been completed when the story was published.
