LoveWay held its fifth Barn Boo-Nanza on Friday, October 23, at its barn outside Middlebury, ABC57 reported. The free event is built for families of children with disabilities. LoveWay provides equine-assisted services. Stalls became candy booths decorated by 14 community partners, spaced so the barn would not overwhelm guests. An activity area added ring toss, a target toss, cornhole, and a rubber chicken toss. A miniature horse greeted people at the door.

Jessica Estep, the organization's director of development, said one family told organizers their child would otherwise never have trick-or-treated. Organizers expected 200 to 300 people. Kathryn Lane of Williams Warriors, taking part for the first time, said her group brings non-candy choices so children with feeding tubes, allergies, or other conditions can still leave with something.
