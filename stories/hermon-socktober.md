Hermon Middle School's sixth annual Socktober brought in 1,425 pairs of socks, FOX Bangor reported. The goal had been 1,000 pairs. Fifth grade collected the most.

The socks are headed to people who need them. The 1,425 count is this year's haul, past the goal the school set, and sixth annual means the drive has been on the calendar for six years.
