Seven young California condors are flying in Yurok Country, Popular Science reported. The Yurok Tribe and Redwood parks released the birds in Northern California. Among them is the 200th condor hatched at the San Diego Zoo Safari Park to go to the wild.

Seven is this release. Two hundred is a running count of Safari Park birds that have left for the wild. Neither number means the species is restored. It means this cohort is out, on land where the Yurok people are leading the return.
