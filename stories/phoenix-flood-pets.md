Ten Arizona Humane Society workers drove 29 dogs and cats from the Humane Society of the White Mountains in Lakeside to Phoenix, Arizona's Family reported. The move gives the flood-hit shelter space to rebuild.

Adoption fees on the transferred pets are cut 50 percent. Twenty-nine is the number that made the drive, and the fee cut applies to those animals, not to every pet in the Phoenix shelter.
