Philadelphia renamed the 3900 block of Lancaster Avenue Chosen 300 Way. The nonprofit has been serving meals since 1995. It marked the new name the way it marks most weeks: by holding its food giveaway on that block, turkeys included. The sign is new. The line of meals is the thing the block was already known for.
