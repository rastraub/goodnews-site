The University of Arizona Players Foundation donated 150 turkeys toward Gospel Rescue Mission's Thanksgiving Blessings in Tucson, a giveaway aimed at about 3,000 meals. The event also hands out clothing and hygiene supplies. The donation is one gift inside a larger table. One hundred fifty birds do not, by themselves, equal 3,000 plates. They are the piece the players added so the mission's dinner, and the coats and soap beside it, could go further.
