Visitors at Rushikonda can now sit with a book between swims. The Hindu reported on November 27, 2026, that Andhra Pradesh Tourism and the Visakhapatnam Public Library opened a corner called Reading Books at Sea Shore. The library donated about 2,000 books. Some of them are on racks at the information centre and police outpost on the beach. A reader takes a title, sits in one of the reclined wooden chairs, and returns the book before leaving. There is no fee. The shelves mix children's books, fiction, nonfiction, and books on spirituality.

District tourism officer J. Madhavi told the paper the idea came from families, including visitors from West Bengal, Chhattisgarh, and Odisha, who want a quiet hour as much as a ride on the water. Library secretary D.S. Varma said a register will track what is on the racks and what people pick up, and that the collection will be replenished every month. Signs in English and Telugu explain the rules. The corner is open daily from 10:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., and books stay on the beach. The news is a free reading spot that is already open. It is not a new public-library building.
