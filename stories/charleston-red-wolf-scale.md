Students and Navy scientists in Charleston built a scale that weighs endangered red wolves without tranquilizers, The Post and Courier reported. The animal can stand on it and be weighed while awake.

The point is a less invasive check for wolves already in care. A weight on that scale is a health measurement. It is not a count of wolves recovered in the wild, and it does not replace the rest of the work of keeping the species going.
