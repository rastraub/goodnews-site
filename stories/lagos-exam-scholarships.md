The Guardian Nigeria reported on November 14, 2026, that Abraham Seed Private School International invited at least 2,000 students from across Lagos to be considered for its 2026/27 scholarship round. Director Dr. Abraham Oladidupo Olaluwoye said the students were tested in mathematics, English, and a general paper, and that 200 will be selected. He said the school has paid WAEC and NECO exam fees as a community project for 10 years. Last year the offer stayed close to the school's own area. This year it reached more of the city. He also said money still limits how far the school can go.

Separately, the school has given full scholarships to about 40 students a year for a decade, from nursery through senior secondary, which Olaluwoye put at about 400 students in all. The current full scholars arrived last year, and 40 of them will keep that support. Parents, including a local chief imam, told the paper their children are using it. The 2,000 figure is the number invited and tested. The 200 exam awards are still a selection, not a list of winners. The 40 full places are the scholarships already under way.
