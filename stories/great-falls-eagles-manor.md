Eagles Manor in Great Falls served more than candy on Halloween, KRTV reported. Cassie Campbell, owner of Spices N' Spurs, put out a free meal, and more than 250 plates were served. Donations from the night support the meal program she runs for neighbors.

The figure is plates at this celebration, plus gifts aimed at the meals that come after it. It is one evening in Great Falls, not a tally of every hungry neighbor in the city.
