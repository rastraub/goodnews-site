A portable shrine rode a fishing boat around Okirai Bay on October 11, the first time in 16 years that Niiyama Shrine in Ofunato has held its autumn festival. NHK reported on October 13, 2026, that the shrine stages the event every four years, and that it had not happened since the March 2011 earthquake and tsunami. Neighboring districts helped revive it.

The mikoshi left the shrine at about 8 a.m. under a clear sky, went aboard, toured the water, and came back to shore. Residents then offered a folk performance. A boy in second grade who danced told NHK he managed it with skills from two months of practice. A resident whose house was destroyed in the tsunami played a drum and said people had wanted the festival back, and that he wants to help pass it on.

The head of the organizing committee told NHK the day was a way to mark a point in the town's recovery and to move toward what comes next. The report is about the festival being held. It is not a claim that recovery is finished.
