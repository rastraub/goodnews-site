About 90 fishermen in Gia Lai Province have released 49 sea turtles and one Cuvier's beaked whale this year, Tuoi Tre News reported on November 23, 2026. The figures were given at a year-end review of a volunteer program run by the International Union for Conservation of Nature and the Gia Lai Fisheries Association. The turtles included hawksbills, green turtles, and other endangered species. Fishermen also reported seeing dolphins, sperm whales, killer whales, and ocean sunfish.

IUCN coordinator Bui Thi Thu Hien said research from the Vietnam Research Institute for Marine Fisheries, done several years ago, estimated that nearly 1,000 sea turtles a year are taken as bycatch in lines, nets, and abandoned gear. The volunteer program trains fishermen to spot animals in trouble and to report them on a phone app. Nguyen Thi Ai Trinh of the fisheries association said the work has turned fishers into people who can act, not only people who hear a conservation message.

The 49 turtles and the whale are the animals these participants reported releasing. They are not a national total, and they do not show that accidental capture has ended.
