Kent County District Courthouse in Warwick finalized 11 adoptions on National Adoption Day, WPRI reported. Each one was met by applause from a packed courtroom.

Eleven is the number of children whose adoptions became official that day. The applause followed each case. The courthouse is where the celebrations happened.
