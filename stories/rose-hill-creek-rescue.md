Rose Hill firefighters were called to a crash in Duplin County early Thanksgiving morning and found a car in a creek with the driver still inside, WITN reported. The Magnolia Fire Department arrived soon after Rose Hill's crews.

The two departments worked together to pull the driver out of the vehicle. EMS then took over care. The report does not name the driver, the road, or say whether the person was hurt. What it does say is that firefighters reached someone still inside a car in the water and got that person out.
