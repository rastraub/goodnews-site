Seward's third annual Barktoberfest was Oct. 11 at Junto Winery. The Seward Independent published the account on Oct. 14. More than 20 dogs came in costume for a pup cup shuffle and a photo booth. The afternoon benefits Blue River Pet Rescue.

The rescue opened in 2012 as a foster-based program and finished a building on the south side of Seward in 2019. Director Cody Brown said 2026 is a record year, with nearly 200 adoptions so far. Foster Kris Beckler-Kocian is caring for Bunker, a dog who came from Lexington.
