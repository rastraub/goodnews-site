Joplin firefighters crawled beneath a house to reach two dogs trapped in a well about 6 feet deep, KOAM reported. They brought both dogs out. The dogs are recovering at the Joplin Humane Society.

The rescue is two animals and one tight space under one home. Both are out and in care. The report does not name the dogs or say when they will be ready to leave the shelter.
