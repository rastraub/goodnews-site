Lewisville firefighters were called Tuesday, October 27, for a cat wedged deep inside a Tesla, the Cross Timbers Gazette reported. The department said this was not a case of opening the hood and waiting for her to jump out. She was stuck. Crews took the undercarriage apart piece by piece.

About 40 minutes later they could reach the spot where the cat, Sakura, was hiding, and they pulled her out. Her family had been searching for about three hours. They were about to drive off and keep looking when they heard a faint meow under their own car. Firefighters got her back into their arms. The department said the crew stayed patient around the car's machinery, and that the calls they answer come in every size.
