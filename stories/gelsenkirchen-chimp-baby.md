A chimpanzee infant was born at ZOOM Erlebniswelt in Gelsenkirchen. Der Westen reported the birth on November 12, 2026, from a zoo post the day before. Mother Jahaga delivered in the early morning. The birth had no complications, and the zoo said mother and baby are well. The first observations suggest the baby is a girl. As chimpanzee infants do, the newborn is holding on to Jahaga's belly.

Jahaga is 33 and already has sons Kojo and Lisoko in the same group. The zoo described her as an experienced mother who has looked after the new baby with care from the start. She was born on January 6, 1993, in Rijswijk, in the Netherlands, later lived at Leipzig Zoo, and moved to Gelsenkirchen in August 2016 with a chimpanzee named Alexandra under the European breeding programme for the species. The group lives in the ELE Dschungelabenteuer area of the Africa section. The zoo said mother and baby may withdraw in the first weeks, so a visit will not always include a look at them. This is one birth in a managed group. It is not a count of chimpanzees in the forest.
