Operation Give Birds, from Iowa Giving Crew, is expanding into northern Iowa with Thanksgiving meals for 250 families, KCRG reported. Volunteer Shannon Wooge brought the effort north. The crew has already set up pantries at four schools. The 250 is the number of families the meals are meant to reach. The four pantries are the ones already in place.
