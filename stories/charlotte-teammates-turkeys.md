The Carolina Panthers hosted more than 1,200 children for Teammates and Turkeys. Players served a hot meal at Topgolf Charlotte with Second Harvest, and families left with a turkey and sides. The meal at the venue was one sitting. The bird in the car was the next one, so the day did not end when the plates at Topgolf were cleared. The players were on the serving side of the table.
