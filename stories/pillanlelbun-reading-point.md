A permanent reading point opened in Pillanlelbún on November 24, in the commune of Lautaro in La Araucanía. Chile's ministry of cultures, arts, and heritage published the account that day. The project, funded by the ministry's book and reading fund, answers a request from the community, which for years had to travel to Lautaro for library services. The new collection adds children's and young-adult literature, graphic novels, comics, manga, and general titles for different ages.

The ceremony was at the local community center, with the municipality, library staff, schools, and neighbors. Regional culture secretary Samir Manukian said the point widens access to books for children, older people, and families. Education secretary Aaron Ríos Morales said more than 300 new copies are available and tied the shelf to reading work in schools. Mayor Ricardo Jaramillo Galindo called it a wish the village had held. Pedro Martínez Montero, who runs the community center, said neighbors had waited several years. The news is this opening, as the ministry described it. It is not an independent count of how many people used the room on the first day.
