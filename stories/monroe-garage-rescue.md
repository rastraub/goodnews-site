Snohomish Regional Fire & Rescue was dispatched around 9:31 p.m. on Nov. 10 to a house fire on 147th Street SE in Monroe, KING 5 reported. Smoke alarms were sounding when firefighters arrived. The first command officer on scene found one person already outside, with smoke coming from the garage.

Crews went into the house and found heavy smoke throughout. They closed the door between the garage and the living space to slow the smoke and the fire. Moments later they heard someone calling for help. The crew from Engine 31 located an elderly woman and brought her outside.

The fire stayed in the garage and did not move into the living areas. Paramedics evaluated both homeowners and released them at the scene. KING 5 reported no injuries. The Snohomish County Fire Marshal's Office is investigating how the fire started.
