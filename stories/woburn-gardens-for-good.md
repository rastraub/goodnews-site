Volunteers with Gardens for Good tend 15 backyard and community gardens in Woburn, Winchester, Arlington, and Medford, and they give all the produce to local food pantries, CBS Boston reported. The gardens yield nearly 2,000 pounds of food a year. That weight is the yearly harvest from those 15 plots. None of it is held back for sale. It goes to the pantries.
