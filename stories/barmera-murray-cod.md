About 600 mature Murray cod, some up to a metre long, were released into Lake Bonney at Barmera in South Australia's Riverland in early October, the week before ABC's October 18, 2026, report. RecFish SA chief executive Asher Dezsery described the release. Cod had died out after the lake was cut off from the River Murray and after fish kills. Berri Barmera mayor Ella Winnall was among the people who helped put the fish in the water.

Charles Sturt University's Paul Humphries said the cod can work as habitat engineers in the food web. He did not say they are certain to reduce carp. Catch-and-release fishing is set to resume on January 1, 2027, and not during the breeding season. The news is the release of these adult fish. It is not a count of how many will spawn, or a promise that the lake's other fish will decline.
