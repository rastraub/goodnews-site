Just after 5 p.m. Sunday, emergency crews were sent to the area of Palmer Motorsports Park on West Ware Road for a report of lost hikers, Western Mass News reported. They set up a command post and began searching. A Massachusetts State Police Air Wing helicopter joined the search, along with a drone from the Hampden County Sheriff's Office.

Around 6:30 p.m., about an hour after the call, crews found the hikers and got them out safely. The report does not give their names or say how they became lost.
