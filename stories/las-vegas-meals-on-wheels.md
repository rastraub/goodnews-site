The A's gave $10,000 to Meals on Wheels in Las Vegas and helped carry the meals, FOX5 reported. The gift backs Catholic Charities of Southern Nevada, which delivers free meals to homebound seniors. Dave Stewart and broadcaster Chris Caray delivered meals themselves.

The $10,000 is the team's donation. The meals are free. Stewart and Caray went on the route, so the help was both the check and the delivery.
