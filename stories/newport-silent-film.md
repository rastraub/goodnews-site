A silent film made more than a century ago around Lake Sunapee has turned up in Japan, and a Newport archivist is bringing it back for a public screening, WMUR reported on October 20, 2026. "Van-ar-chy" was made by Billy B. Van, a Newport resident who helped shape early movies. John Tariot had the title on a list of lost Van films. A film student in Japan found the print while researching a project. Tariot told the station he believes someone carried it there, where it sat unknown.

Dean Stetson of the Newport Historical Society said Van was a regular sight at town meetings in the opera house, enough that people came to hear him talk, and that he was also a farmer who sold soap and shampoo. Stetson said this particular film includes scenes people in town can still recognize. Tariot is preparing a community screening next month. He said the hunt for other missing Van films does not stop with this one.
