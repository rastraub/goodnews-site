Caldera High School's Green Team held a Pumpkin Smash in Bend, and residents dropped off Halloween pumpkins that went to a commercial composting facility instead of the landfill, Central Oregon Daily News reported. The jack-o'-lanterns were collected for composting off site, not broken down in a school garden. Organizers hope to hold the drop-off again next year.
