PAWS Wildlife Rehabilitation Center in Snohomish released eight rehabilitated black bears after months of care, KIRO 7 reported. It was a record year for the center. The public donated more than 10,000 pounds of food that helped feed the bears.

Eight is the number that went back to the wild. The months of care are what came before the release. More than 10,000 pounds is the food neighbors sent.
