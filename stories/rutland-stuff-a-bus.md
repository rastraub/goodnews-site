Stuff A Bus is back in Rutland for a run that has lasted more than 30 years, the Rutland Herald reported. From Nov. 5 through Nov. 7, collections at Market 32 and other sites fill Marble Valley Transit buses.

The food goes to the Salvation Army, Rutland Community Cupboard, and BROC. Schools are competing to bring in hundreds of pounds. The campaign is the annual collection, timed for a season when pantry shelves get thin.
