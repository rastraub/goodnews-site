Search-and-rescue crews flew two hoist missions near Las Vegas on Wednesday, Nov. 11, FOX5 Las Vegas reported. The first call was Frenchman Mountain, on the east side of the city. Two hikers had left the trail and were stranded on a ledge. Officials said neither hiker was hurt.

A rescue specialist was lowered to them and secured both in rescue seats. The helicopter hoisted them up and handed them to people waiting on the ground.

The crew was later heading toward Red Rock Canyon for hoist training when a new call came in. A hiker and a 6-year-old were lost near the Turtlehead Peak Trail. They had gone off the trail, were getting cold, and said they could not keep going. The helicopter, AIR1, turned around. A rescue specialist was lowered to the pair and worked to keep the child warm. Both were flown to the Bureau of Land Management helipad and turned over to ground crews. FOX5 did not name the hikers.
