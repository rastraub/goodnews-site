HMSA employees raised more than $116,000 for the Aloha, Hawaiʻi Island, Kauaʻi, and Maui United Ways, Maui Now reported. The total is statewide, from a workplace campaign rather than a single island event.

A Holiday Fair returns Nov. 12, and its proceeds go to Aloha United Way. The $116,000 is money already given. The fair is a later event, not part of that total.
