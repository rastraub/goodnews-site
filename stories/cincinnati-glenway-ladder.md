Cincinnati firefighters were called around 2:20 a.m. Thursday to a house on Glenway Avenue in West Price Hill, near Olivia Lane and not far from Guerley Road, for the smell of smoke, WLWT reported. Assistant Fire Chief Matt Flagler said four people and a child were inside. All of them got out on their own except one man. He was at a third-story window.

One group of firefighters went inside to put the fire out. Another team raised a ground ladder. Firefighter Josh Ross, who has been with the department for one year, climbed it and pulled the man out. The man was taken to UC Medical Center. WLWT reported that his injuries were serious and that he was alert and talking. No one else was hurt.

Flagler said he was proud of the rescue. He said he believes the fire was an accident. The cause is still under investigation, and no smoke detectors were found in the house. The Red Cross is helping the people who had to leave.
