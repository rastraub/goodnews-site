The Kansas City Chiefs said the 35th annual flag sale, this year called Red Thursday, raised $1 million for Ronald McDonald House Charities of Kansas City, KMBC reported on October 20, 2026. That gift brings 14 years of giving to the house to $8.5 million. A Hunt Family Foundation 50/50 raffle at the opening game also contributed. The report does not separate that raffle from the $1 million flag total.

Fans bought flags on September 10 at Hy-Vee and McDonald's locations for a $5 donation, and online for $10. This year's flag carried a line associated with coach Andy Reid: "How 'bout those Chiefs?" Running back Kenneth Walker III and wide receiver Jalen Royals helped reveal the total and spent time with families at the house, including a Halloween craft. House chief executive Tami Greenberg said the players came for the families. The houses give relatives a place to stay near a child who is in a Kansas City hospital. The million dollars is this year's flag sale. It is not a figure for every bill those families face.
