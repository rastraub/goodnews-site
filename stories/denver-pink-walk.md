Thousands of people joined Susan G. Komen's More Than Pink Walk at City Park in Denver. The walk raised more than $550,000 for breast cancer research, for help getting screening, and for support that patients use while they are in care.

The total is what the event brought in. It is not a claim that any one study or screening was finished that day.
