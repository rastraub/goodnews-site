Oral surgeon and veteran Dr. Robert Ioppolo has chosen Marine veteran John Gonzales for free dental implant work through his Smiles for Soldiers program in Lubbock, KCBD reported. Gonzales has not smiled confidently since 1993. The implants are planned for the coming months, so the care is still ahead. What has happened is the selection. The work itself has not been done yet.
