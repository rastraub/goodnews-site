A KARE 11 reporter knocking on doors in Crystal found Esther and Abe Holte hosting Thanksgiving for the first time. A year earlier their daughter had arrived five weeks early, and Esther's parents and Abe's sister had carried the family through that hard stretch. This year the Holtes cooked for them. They also made room for two guests who had not been on the list. The meal was the thank-you they could put on a table: the people who had shown up for a difficult birth were the ones invited to sit down, and the door stayed open for two more.
