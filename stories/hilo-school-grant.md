Hoʻoulu Lāhui, a school displaced by the 2018 lava flow on Hawaiʻi Island, received $500,000 from the Office of Hawaiian Affairs toward a permanent home, Maui Now reported. The grant supports the school's education work while that campus is still ahead of it.

Half a million dollars is the award. It is aimed at a lasting building. The report does not say the campus is finished or that classes have already moved in.
