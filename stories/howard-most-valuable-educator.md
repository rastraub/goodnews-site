Christy Schneeberger, a second-grade teacher at Meadowbrook Elementary in Howard, is Wisconsin's Most Valuable Educator, WBAY reported. She was chosen from more than 150 nominations and surprised at an assembly. The prize includes Packers tickets and a Lambeau Field tour for her class.

More than 150 is the nomination pool. The assembly is where she found out. The tickets and the tour are for the class, not a prize she keeps to herself.
