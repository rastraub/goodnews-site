The Banquet in Sioux Falls took in 71 turkeys on Wednesday for Thanksgiving meals, Dakota News Now reported. Members of the National Wild Turkey Federation's East Dakota Chapter brought them. Executive director Tamera Jerke-Liesinger said the feeding ministry would be struggling to find turkeys without the delivery. The same men have made the trip for more than 20 years. They commit the time and the money, and she said they enjoy handing the birds over as much as the kitchen enjoys receiving them.

The gift landed during National Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week. The station's account stops at the 71 turkeys and the 20-year habit. It does not say how many plates those birds will fill.
