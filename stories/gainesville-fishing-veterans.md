The University of Florida's Fishing for Success program hosted nearly 300 people for a veterans fishing day, WUFT reported. Veterans and families fished catch-and-release in the UF/IFAS ponds and shared a barbecue. The lead volunteer is Bob Centofanti, a Navy veteran called the Rod Doctor, who found the program 10 years ago and fishes there almost daily. Nearly 300 is the size of this gathering. His near-daily visits are his own routine.
