Albuquerque Fire Rescue helped New Mexico Game and Fish move a mother bear and her two cubs off Tanoan Golf Course, KOB 4 reported. Just after 12:30 p.m. on Monday, firefighters were called after wildlife officers had sedated and trapped the mother. The two cubs were still in a tree. Firefighters decided the safer plan was for Game and Fish to set traps for the cubs, and that is what the officers did, so the family could be relocated together rather than split up.

The call was a wildlife move, not a capture for a zoo. Officers and firefighters got the mother into a cage and then worked out how to bring the cubs down with her. The bears were taken off the course as a family.
