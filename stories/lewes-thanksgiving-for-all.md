The Food Bank of Delaware, Perdue, and the Harry K Foundation are filling 1,000 Thanksgiving boxes, the Cape Gazette reported. Thanksgiving For All is sending roaster chickens and sides to families in Sussex and Kent counties through local schools. The effort is based in Lewes.

One thousand is the box count. The chicken and the sides are what goes in each one. Sussex and Kent are the counties. Schools are the delivery path.
