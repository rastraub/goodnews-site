Staff at Cafe Continental in Stalybridge split a £1,000 tip after Stevie Wonder ate there in the middle of the night. The BBC reported on November 10, 2026, that the visit followed his sold-out performance of Songs in the Key of Life at Manchester's Co-op Live. Owner Ryan Robinson said band members had called the cafe about 18 months earlier, while they were playing behind another singer, and stayed in touch. On Sunday they asked to come back after the show.

The band arrived around 11 p.m. About 12:30 a.m., four minibuses pulled up and Wonder was in one of them. The staff had been warned they would work late and serve something other than the Sunday roast. Robinson said Wonder, 76, talked with people and was easy company, and that his daughter and backing singer Aisha was among the party. He said the tip, from Wonder and the band, was a first for the room. The cafe opened two years ago. The news is this one late meal and the £1,000. It does not say how the money was divided.
