Inland Northwest Honor Flight brought veterans from the Spokane area home from Washington, D.C., KHQ reported. The trip cost the veterans nothing. They visited memorials for people who served and for people who were killed, spent the day there, then rested and returned to Spokane the next day.

The station said photographs of the return showed the group back in Spokane. It did not say how many veterans flew. The report is narrower than a full itinerary: a no-cost day among the memorials, and a flight home that had already landed.
