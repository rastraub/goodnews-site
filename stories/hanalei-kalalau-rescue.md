Kauaʻi Fire Department rescuers went to the Kalalau Trail on Friday, Nov. 13, after a report that a hiker had chest pain, trouble breathing, and could not walk out, Kauai Now reported. Rescue 3, aboard Air 1, launched shortly before 4:45 p.m.

The crew found two women, both visiting the island, near the Hanakāpīʻai Beach landing zone. One was 39 and also dehydrated. The other was 35, and the department reported an ankle injury. Air 1 carried the first hiker to Princeville Airport, where American Medical Response took over with help from Hanalei Fire Station. The helicopter went back for the second hiker and made the same handoff. The scene was clear shortly before 6:37 p.m. The department did not release their names.
