Black Hills quilters are making a bunk quilt for every sailor on the USS South Dakota, KOTA reported. Navy veteran Robert Turner is leading the Rapid City guild, which has finished 146 handmade quilts.

The goal is one quilt per bunk. One hundred forty-six is the number finished so far. The submarine is the USS South Dakota, and the sewing is happening in Rapid City.
