Fifteen-year-old Ethan Weary and other New Orleans teens built Brick by Brick, a clothing brand that sells out its drops, WWL reported. Members push each other in school and away from bad choices, and the group keeps a good-grades rule.

Weary is 15. The drops are the clothing releases that have sold out. The grades rule is part of belonging. The push is from the members themselves, not only from adults outside the brand.
