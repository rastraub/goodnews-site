Harley Pakuris, 6, of Woonsocket, asked to help children and mothers in shelters for Christmas, The Valley Breeze reported. With her mother and grandmother, she is placing donation boxes at local businesses to collect clothes, food, and toys. The boxes are going out. The story is the wish and the collection she has started, not a final tally of what the boxes will hold.
