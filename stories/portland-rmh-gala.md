Ronald McDonald House Maine's 26th annual gala in Portland raised a record $240,000, WMTW reported. That is 47 percent more than last year. The money supports free lodging and meals for families of children in treatment.

The 26th gala is this year's event. The $240,000 is the record total. Forty-seven percent is how far it rose above last year. The lodging and meals are what the house gives those families at no charge.
