Riders with Samaritan Cycling USA stopped in DeRidder on a trip of nearly 2,000 miles and packed 576 hygiene kits in under an hour, KPLC reported. They worked with Catholic Charities of Southwest Louisiana. The kits are for people experiencing homelessness.

The hour and the 576 kits are this stop. The miles are the longer ride. The story is the packing they finished in town, not a count of every kit the charities will need this winter.
