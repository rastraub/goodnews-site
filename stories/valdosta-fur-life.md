Students from Lowndes High School's Friends 'Fur' Life club visited the Humane Society of Valdosta and Lowndes County with treats, food, and toys, WCTV reported. They also walked dogs that are waiting for adoption.

The visit put supplies on the shelf and gave dogs time out of their kennels. It was a club outing, not an adoption event with a tally of new homes.
