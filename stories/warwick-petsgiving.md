The Rhode Island SPCA's first Petsgiving drive-thru in Warwick fed pets for more than 200 families, NBC 10 reported. A $10,000 Rhode Island Foundation grant stocked the pet food so owners who are struggling can keep their animals at home.

More than 200 is the family count. The $10,000 is the grant. The drive-thru is new this year. The aim is keeping pets in their homes.
