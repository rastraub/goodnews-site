Colchester Zoo has a second chimpanzee baby this year. BBC News reported on November 6, 2026, that the infant was born on Tuesday, November 3. The zoo said it was especially glad because another chimpanzee had been born in January, and that January birth was the first at Colchester in 17 years. The father is Napoleon. The baby's name and sex are not confirmed. A zoo spokesman said the mother, the father, and the baby are doing well and are in their first days together.

Visitors are asked to stay quiet around the enclosure so the family can settle. Chimpanzees are listed as endangered by the International Union for Conservation of Nature. The BBC report does not describe a wild release or a name for the mother. The news is this birth, the second of the year, and a family the keepers say is well.
