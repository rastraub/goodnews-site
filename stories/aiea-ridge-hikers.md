The Honolulu Fire Department received a 911 call at 2:01 p.m. Thursday about two teenage boys who were lost and could not find their way out of the Aiea Ridge Trail, the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reported. Five units, with 13 people in all, responded.

The first unit arrived at 2:27 p.m. to prepare for air operations, and a second unit secured a landing zone nearby. At 2:58 p.m. the department's Air 1 helicopter lowered rescue personnel near the hikers. By 3:20 p.m. the helicopter had lifted the teens to that landing zone.

The department's account does not give their names and does not say they were hurt. It says they were brought off the trail.
