A school program in Tenkasi district is widening from golden jackals to the Madras hedgehog. The Hindu reported on October 22, 2026, that District Forest Officer R. Rajmohan is expanding Golden Jackal Ambassadors, which began in local schools and colleges last November.

Rajmohan said the jackal work had increased the number of animals people reported, because students and neighbors sent alerts when they saw hunting. The same idea is now aimed at the Madras hedgehog, known locally as mulleli. A survey of 867 people in 38 villages collected sightings, and 53 percent of those reports came during the monsoon. The next step he described is a "forest guard brother or sister" in every school and college.

He also said the hedgehogs are still losing habitat, getting hit on roads, and being taken from the wild. The announcement is an expansion of an awareness program. It is not a census of hedgehogs, and it does not say those threats have stopped.
