Volunteers roped into a waterfall gorge near Lintrathen and brought a stranded dog back up. BBC News reported that 11 members of Tayside Mountain Rescue Team and four from Police Scotland Mountain Rescue were called to Loups of Kenny on Tuesday afternoon. Rain was heavy and the light was going. They built a rope system, reached the dog, and hauled it out.

Tayside Mountain Rescue said the dog was a little cold and very soggy, and otherwise none the worse for the trip. The report does not name the dog or say how it got to the bottom. The news is a wet dog back on level ground. It is not a wider account of pets lost in the glens.
