Spencer, who runs SB Mowing in Wichita, was named to GoFundMe's first Kind 50 list, KWCH reported. He mows overgrown yards for free and raises money for neighbors' home repairs. The Kind 50 is GoFundMe's inaugural list, so this is the first time the company has published it. The recognition is for that work: yards cut at no charge, and money raised toward repairs in neighbors' homes.
