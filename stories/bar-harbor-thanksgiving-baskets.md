Sixth graders and other volunteers packed 187 Thanksgiving baskets on Mount Desert Island. Students at Tremont and Mount Desert Elementary collected and sorted more than 1,000 food items. Eight churches and five schools took part in the program. The baskets are the finished piles. The thousand items are what had to be gathered and sorted before anyone could pack a single one.
