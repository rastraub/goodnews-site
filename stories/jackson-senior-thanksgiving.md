Salvation Army volunteers delivered Thanksgiving meals to 1,500 seniors around Jackson. Each box came with a knock. The organizers put it plainly: that knock is a face a senior is going to see. Fifteen hundred dinners is a route. The face is why the route is not a drop on the step with the engine still running. The food feeds the day. The person on the porch is the company.
