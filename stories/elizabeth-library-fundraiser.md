The Parker Chronicle reported on October 6, 2026, that Pour On the Divide, a brew fest for the Pines & Plains Libraries Foundation, raised just under $12,000. The money is going toward the buildout of the Elizabeth Library. It is a small-town tally: neighbors bought a pint, and the foundation put the proceeds on a building that is still taking shape. The gift is for that library project, not a general town fund.
