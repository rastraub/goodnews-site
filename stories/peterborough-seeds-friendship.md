Friends of Woodston Library are spending October on Seeds of Friendship, workshops where people make seed packets and add a hopeful note for a neighbor. BBC News reported the project on October 13, 2026.

The sessions are at Woodston Library on Orchard Street in Peterborough. The packets hold flower seeds meant to be sown locally. Jenny Storey is among the people behind the idea. It is open to all ages and is aimed at loneliness. The report describes the workshops. It does not give a count of packets handed out.
