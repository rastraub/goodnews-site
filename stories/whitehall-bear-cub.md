Game wardens from Montana Fish, Wildlife and Parks, working with a bear specialist, caught a cub near Whitehall that had a plastic jar stuck on its head. They cut the jar off and released the cub. It was not hurt.

The cub went back to the woods without the jar. That was the whole job.
