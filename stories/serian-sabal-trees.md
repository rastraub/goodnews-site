About 100 people planted 700 native saplings at Sabal Forest Reserve on October 10, 2026. Sarawak Daily reported that the planters included students from Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak, staff of the Sarawak Forest Department, and people from the community. The species were selangan, resak, and meranti.

The reserve is in the Serian area of Sarawak. The report describes the planting day and the trees that went into the ground. It does not give survival rates, and it does not say who will maintain the saplings. The news is the 700 trees planted. It is not a claim that the reserve has gained 700 established trees.
