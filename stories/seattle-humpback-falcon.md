Cascadia Research Collective freed a humpback whale named Falcon from fishing gear in Puget Sound, KIRO 7 reported. On the third try, just before sunset north of Seattle, the crew hooked a grapple on Falcon's tail and pulled off all the tangled gear. The whale is safe and healthy.

The third try is the attempt that caught. The gear that came off was all of it. North of Seattle is where the rescue happened, in the Sound.
