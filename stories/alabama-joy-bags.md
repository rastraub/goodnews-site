Krystalyn Boman, 14, packs joy bags for younger children being treated at St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and brings them on her own follow-up visits, ABC 33/40 reported. The bags hold stuffed animals, Play-Doh, and other toys. She started them after meeting younger patients while she and her mother stayed at St. Jude for three months. Donations from people who follow the family now help fill the bags. The station's report calls her an Alabama teen and does not name a hometown, so this story does not add one. She is being treated for a brain tumor. After two years and more than 7,000 pills, her family says the cancer has shrunk significantly. That is progress her doctors described. It is not a cure, and her mother said the journey is not over.
