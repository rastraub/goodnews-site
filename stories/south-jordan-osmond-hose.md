Donny Osmond was leaving his granddaughter's birthday party, still in his Sunday suit, when a Traeger smoker caught fire in the Hongs' backyard in South Jordan. He grabbed a garden hose and put the flames out before they spread to the house. The Hongs credit him with saving it. He did not go home to change. The hose was already there, and the fire was still in the yard.
