Butte Central students, staff, and volunteers put on the LaVerne Combo Thanksgiving Dinner on Tuesday, NBC Montana reported. The free meal has been a tradition at the school since 1972, named for LaVerne Combo, a longtime school employee. This year they carved 36 turkeys and delivered about 500 meals across Butte. More than 100 additional meals were set to be served in the school gym starting at 5:30 p.m. Anyone was welcome to stop by.

Junior Creedyn Crawshaw, who helped cook and deliver, said people look grateful when the food shows up at the door. Chief cook Peggy Boyle, who has helped for 34 years, said students come back satisfied from the gym service and the deliveries. She plans to help for at least another year, with other people ready to take on the lead after that. Preparation takes weeks. The count this year is about 500 delivered meals, plus the gym seating, from 36 turkeys.
