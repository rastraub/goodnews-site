DNREC moved 123 Delmarva fox squirrels from Maryland to three Sussex County sites between 2020 and 2024, WBOC reported. Biologists have now documented reproducing females at all three sites, and squirrels born in Delaware.

The releases include the Redden State Forest area near Georgetown. The 123 animals are the ones that were moved. The new finding is that those populations are producing young, including a generation born in the state.
