About 200 volunteers staffed Ouachita Green's America Recycles Day drive-through at the Monroe Civic Center, KNOE reported. The event took electronics, appliances, tires, paper, and plastics. The aim was to keep those materials out of the landfill and off roadsides. About 200 is how many people volunteered. The report describes what was accepted. It does not give a total weight.
