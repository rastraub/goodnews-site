9&10 News reported on October 7, 2026, that Cherryland Humane Society and Hoop’s Pet Food Pantry will start a recurring pantry on October 28. It has not happened yet. The plan is to open from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. on every fourth Wednesday at the humane society, with dog food, cat food, and supplies. There is no registration, and items go first come, first served. Executive director Heidi Yates said the aim is to help people keep the pets they already have at home.
