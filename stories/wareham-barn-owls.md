Ringers fitted bands to 15 barn owl chicks and one adult at three farmland sites in Purbeck. The BBC reported on October 13, 2026, that the visit, with Birds of Poole Harbour, Carey Ringing Station, and the National Trust, was a first for the area and is meant to become annual. Mark Singleton and Kate Fox described the work. More nest boxes are planned for next year. Longer grass, hedgerows, and scrub are the habitat that supports voles, the owls' prey.

Barn owls are protected under Schedule 1, and the ringing was done under a British Trust for Ornithology licence. The count is the birds handled at those three sites. It is not a population estimate for Dorset, and it does not show how the chicks fare after they leave the box.
