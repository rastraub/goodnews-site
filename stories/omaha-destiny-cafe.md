Destiny Cafe students made chicken salad croissants for the Joslyn Castle Holiday Boutique, KETV reported. The Omaha nonprofit is in its third year. Founder Joan Allen said it teaches culinary skills to people with developmental disabilities so they can get better jobs in the community. Leaders, volunteers, and students prepared the chicken salad at Countryside Community Church in west Omaha. The boutique was set to include more than 40 businesses and vendors. The sandwiches were made for that sale. The station did not report how many croissants they finished.
