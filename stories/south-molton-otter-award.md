Lily Herniman started watching an otter sanctuary from the school bus and now spends three days a week there. The BBC reported on October 27, 2026, that the 18-year-old was named Countryfile Young Countryside Champion at the BBC Food and Farming Awards in Belfast.

She joined the UK Wild Otter Trust, near South Molton in north Devon, as a volunteer at 15. Founder Dave Webb and his wife Kath nominated her. They said it was clear early on that she would stay. The trust credited her with work on the wild ground at its new rehabilitation centre, including ponds, tree planting, and other habitat.

Herniman told the BBC the win had not sunk in. She described herself as a link between farming and conservation, and said the two are often treated as separate when both shape the same countryside. She called the chance to work with otters a privilege. The award recognizes that volunteering. It does not say how many animals the centre has returned to the wild.
