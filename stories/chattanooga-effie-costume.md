Chris Nesin built another costume around his daughter Effie's wheelchair for Red Bank Elementary's Halloween parade, WTVC reported. It is the eighth year he has done it. Effie was the star of the parade in Chattanooga.

The count is eight years of one family's costumes. The station's story is this parade and this build, not a pattern every parent is expected to match.
