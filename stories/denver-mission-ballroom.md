Mission Ballroom in Denver's RiNo neighborhood served its third free Thanksgiving meal, and the crowd has grown from about 50 guests to about 250. AEG Presents, which runs the venue, has pointed the day toward families and toward connection, strangers sitting down as neighbors. A ballroom earns its name from shows. On this holiday the same floor is a table. The jump from 50 to 250 is the measure of how many people now expect a seat there.
