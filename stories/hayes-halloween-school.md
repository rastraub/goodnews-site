Families in the Cheyenne community gathered at a one-room schoolhouse near Hayes for the annual Halloween party, the Capital Journal reported. The rural party is the area's way of marking the season together.

The schoolhouse is the room they used. The paper's account is this year's gathering, not a claim about every ranch family in western South Dakota.
