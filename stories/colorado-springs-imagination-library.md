A new local nonprofit is taking the reins of Dolly Parton's Imagination Library in El Paso and Teller counties, KKTV 11 News reported. The Pikes Peak Literacy Foundation will run the affiliate so the free monthly books can keep going out to young children.

About 9,000 children are enrolled now. The foundation's aim is to grow that list toward about 29,000 across the two counties. The story is the handoff and the larger goal, not a promise that every eligible child is signed up yet.
