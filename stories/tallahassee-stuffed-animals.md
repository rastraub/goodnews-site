Kinsley Locke has spent about ten years collecting stuffed animals and giving them to fire departments across Leon County. WCTV reported on October 7, 2026, that the North Florida Christian School sophomore has now passed 2,000 donations, a project she calls Kinsley's Cuddles for Kids.

She is widening the giving to children in foster care, including the Taunton Family Children's Home in Wewahitchka, and hopes to donate another 1,000 stuffed animals this year. The milestone is a long string of small comforts, counted one toy at a time.
