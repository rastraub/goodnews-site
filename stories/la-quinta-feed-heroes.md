La Quinta High culinary students hosted Feed the Heroes, a luncheon the school has held for more than 10 years, KESQ reported. Students cooked turkey, bread, and pies, served veterans and first responders, and then heard their stories.

The menu and the listening are both part of the event. More than 10 years is how long the luncheon has been on the calendar, and this year's meal is the latest sitting of that tradition.
