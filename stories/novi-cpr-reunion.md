Myles Stephens, 23, teaches CPR. When patient transporter George collapsed outside Henry Ford Providence Novi, Stephens and coworker Angela Marshall started compressions. George is recovering. On Nov. 23 the hospital honored the people who did the compressions, and George met them. The reunion is the part that comes after the sidewalk. He got to see the coworkers who were there in the minutes when he could not speak for himself.
