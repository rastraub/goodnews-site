A female grey seal named Monsoon is back in the sea after a flying ring was cut out of her neck, the BBC reported. Friends of Horsey Seals had been trying for weeks to catch her. They managed it on October 7 on a beach at Waxham, on the Norfolk coast. She went to RSPCA East Winch, where the ring was removed. About a month later she was released from a Norfolk beach.

Evangelos Achiellos, the centre manager, said her recovery was fairly quick given the injury, and he thanked the people who rescued and cared for her. He also said the case is a reminder of the harm flying rings can do to seals. The news is this one animal, caught in October and returned to the water after rehabilitation. It does not say how many other seals on that coast still carry debris.
