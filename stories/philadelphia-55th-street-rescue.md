Philadelphia firefighters rescued a person from a rowhome on the 200 block of South 55th Street early Tuesday, NBC10 Philadelphia reported. The Philadelphia Fire Department said the flames broke out about 2 a.m. The person was taken to a hospital. Officials did not say whether that person was hurt, and they did not say whether anyone else had been inside.

Firefighters worked from the roof and from second-floor windows while smoke rose off the building. They had the fire under control by about 2:30 a.m. The department did not give a cause. NBC10 reported that the investigation was still open.
