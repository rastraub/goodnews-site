The Texas Sea Life Center opened a wildlife hospital after two years of construction, KRIS 6 News reported on October 21, 2026. The center marked the opening Tuesday evening. Director and veterinarian Dr. Tim Tristan said the new work includes a hospital, a food-preparation building, and two more pools, each 12 feet across, for sea turtles. He said everything from one fence line to the other is new.

The center treats sea turtles, shorebirds, and raptors. It is one of three places in the Coastal Bend that take in injured wildlife, along with the Texas State Aquarium and The ARK in Port Aransas. Tristan said the three complement one another, and that the extra space lets his team accept more turtles. The center has operated for 12 years, run entirely by volunteers, and paid for by donations, tours, and admissions. People who find an injured animal can call the center. The opening adds room. It does not say how many more animals will come through the door.
