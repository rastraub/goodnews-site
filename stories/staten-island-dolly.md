Dolly was left at Staten Island's Ladder 85 firehouse thin and recently postpartum, the ASPCA reported. She bonded with the crew and stayed on as the firehouse dog.

The ASPCA's mobile clinic spayed her. The report is about that one dog and the firehouse that kept her, not a count of other animals the company has taken in.
