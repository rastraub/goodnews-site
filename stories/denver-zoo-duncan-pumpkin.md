Duncan, the Denver Zoo's Asian elephant, spent the weekend treating a giant pumpkin as both a toy and a snack, CBS Colorado reported. The Deevy family donated the pumpkin. Duncan stomped it flat, smashed it open, and took a bite. The zoo posted video of the mess.

The same season includes Boo at the Zoo, an after-dark trick-or-treat with nine candy stations, costumed characters, a fall market, and the animals. It runs Fridays through Sundays from 4:30 p.m. to 9 p.m., except Halloween night. The pumpkin was Duncan's piece of the fall calendar.
