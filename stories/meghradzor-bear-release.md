Two Transcaucasian brown bears listed in Armenia's Red Book are back in the wild with satellite collars, Armenpress reported on November 23, 2026. The Environmental Protection and Mining Inspection Body said it had transferred the cubs in 2025 to the Foundation for the Preservation of Wildlife and Cultural Assets rescue center in Urtsadzor.

One cub, about eight months old, had been caught in a wire snare near Yeghegis in Vayots Dzor Province. The other, about seven or eight months old, was found emaciated near Zangakatun in Ararat Province. At the center, specialists kept human contact low so the bears would stay wary of people and able to find food. Both were fitted with collars before release. Trackers will follow their movements, feeding places, and behavior for about a year.

The release was in the Meghradzor forestry of the Hrazdan branch of Armforest, in the Tsaghkunyats Mountains. The news is these two animals leaving care. It is not a count of how many brown bears live in those mountains, and the collars have not yet shown whether either bear is hunting on its own.
