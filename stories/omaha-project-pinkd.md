Project Pink'd delivered 300 Thanksgiving dinners to breast cancer survivors, KETV reported. More than 100 volunteers brought Hy-Vee meals and handwritten cards to people across Nebraska and western Iowa. The effort is based in Omaha.

Three hundred is the number of dinners. More than 100 is the volunteer count. The cards went with the meals. The deliveries reached both Nebraska and western Iowa.
