Animal-welfare groups in western Colorado have formed WestCO CART, the Grand Junction Daily Sentinel reported. Recent wildfires pushed the effort. The team will train community volunteers to rescue pets and livestock safely, staff emergency shelters, and reunite animals with their owners.

The team responds when law enforcement activates it. The news is that the roster and the training now exist, not that a particular fire has already been worked by the new crew.
