Second graders at Albany R-III wrote more than 230 thank-you letters to veterans in 16 states, KQ2 reported. Teacher Samantha Garland said the class writes to veterans every year and that she usually has a list of about 75 community members, family, and friends. The students set a goal of 100 letters for Veterans Day. With help from the community, they more than doubled that goal. The report does not name the 16 states. The more than 230 letters are what the class finished.
