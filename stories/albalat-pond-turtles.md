Twelve European pond turtles were released on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at Ullal de la Senillera, a freshwater spring in Albufera Natural Park. Europa Press reported the release the same day. The newspaper lists the species as vulnerable in the Valencian Community. The animals were bred at Oceanogràfic and at the El Palmar aquaculture centre under an agreement with the regional environment department.

The release is one of "40 años, 40 acciones por l'Albufera," a series marking 40 years since the park was declared in 1986. Regional environment minister Vicente Martínez Mus called it another step in reintroduction work the regional government has backed since 2007. Park director Ignacio Lacomba spoke about the collaboration behind the day. Twenty-four students from IES Sucro in Albalat de la Ribera attended. Volunteers with Acció Ecologista-Agró's Projecte Emys will keep monitoring the site. These are freshwater turtles, a different release from the loggerheads that have gone back to the sea at Benidorm.
