Frisco Creek Wildlife Rehabilitation Center in Monte Vista has cared for 47 orphaned black bear cubs this year, KRDO reported. The center says it has averaged about 20 a year for the past 15 years. The first cub, a 6-pound bear from Evergreen, arrived in mid-April, and 46 more followed. Colorado Parks and Wildlife counted 21 from the southeast, 18 from the northeast, seven from the southwest, and one from the northwest. One yearling that arrived July 20 with burned paws from the Aspen Acres Fire weighed 31 pounds then. The burns have healed, he now weighs more than 120 pounds, and a late-November release is planned when he is ready to den. That release is still ahead, and the report does not say how many of the 47 will go back out.
