Montgomery County firefighters reached a house in the 300 block of Brewster Court in Silver Spring around 9:26 p.m. Wednesday, WJLA reported from the county fire and rescue service. The call said people were still inside. When crews got there, the fire was in a kitchen on the first floor.

A woman was near the doorway. Firefighters helped her out of the house. A man was trapped in a bedroom upstairs. They brought him out through a window, down a ladder.

Both adults were checked for possible smoke inhalation and taken to hospitals. The report, filed late Wednesday night, is the rescue itself. It does not name either person, and it does not say what started the kitchen fire.
