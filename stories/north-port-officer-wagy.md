On Saturday afternoon, Nov. 7, North Port police were called to a home because an infant was not breathing, The Daily Sun reported. The department said the baby boy was choking. Officer Dain Wagy reached him and used the infant form of the Heimlich maneuver, then patted the baby's back while holding him.

Body-camera audio caught Wagy checking on the child. The baby cried, and Wagy said he thought the boy was all right. The department later wrote that the job is about protecting life, and that a baby's cry can be the reward on a call like this. Public information officer Brittany Kammerer said Wagy did not want to speak with reporters about it. Chief Todd Garrison said the officer relied on his training, acted quickly, and helped save the child's life. The paper reported that the baby survived.
