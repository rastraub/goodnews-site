UM-Flint held its sixth Inclusive Halloween, ABC12 reported. The event is built for children with disabilities who need a night without the rush and the judgment of a typical sidewalk. More than 1,400 people attended, and about 460 volunteers staffed it.

Those two numbers are this year's turnout. They describe one campus party in Flint, not every child in the county who wanted a calmer Halloween.
