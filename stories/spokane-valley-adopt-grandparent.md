A nursing home in Spokane Valley is inviting neighbors to adopt a grandparent for Christmas. Residents wrote down wishes as simple as Reese's cups or a ball cap. Community members can choose one through Dec. 18. The program is not asking for a grand gesture. It is asking someone nearby to pick a name, read a short list, and show up with the thing on it.
