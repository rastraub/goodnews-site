The Food and Drug Administration approved bezuclastinib in combination with sunitinib for adults with advanced gastrointestinal stromal tumor, or GIST, who had already been treated with imatinib. In the 413-patient PEAK trial, the combination's median time without the cancer getting worse was 16.5 months, compared with 9.2 months for sunitinib alone. The share of patients whose tumors responded was 46 percent, compared with 26 percent. Those are the results the approval cites. The announcement does not say the combination is a cure, and it does not report whether patients lived longer overall.
