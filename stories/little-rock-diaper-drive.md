The seventh annual Spirit of Arkansas Diaper Drive collected nearly 80,000 diapers for the Central Arkansas Diaper Bank, KATV reported on October 21. The six-week drive ended with a sorting day at Pulaski Heights United Methodist Church in Little Rock. Volunteers from the station, Landers Auto Group, and Edwards Food Giant packed diapers for the bank's monthly distribution.

Pastor Katye Chambers said the day was a reminder that the community can support one another when times are tight, and she was struck by how many people gave up half a workday to sort sizes. The bank, which the church opened in 2019, gives diapers free to families in central Arkansas. Distribution is the first Wednesday of each month at the church on Woodlawn Drive.
