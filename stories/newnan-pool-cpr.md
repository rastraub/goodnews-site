Dr. Andrea Sansonnet, a physician from Venezuela who is not yet practicing in the United States, performed CPR on a toddler pulled lifeless from a Newnan pool, FOX 5 Atlanta reported. Surveillance video showed 1-year-old Maddox go from playing in shallow water to floating within about 90 seconds. His mother, Janay Shaw, pulled him out. Sansonnet said he had no pulse, was not breathing, and his lips were blue when she started CPR. Maddox is now 2 and thriving. Newnan police honored Sansonnet at a ceremony on a Thursday when she and Maddox shared a birthday. The rescue itself was on a hot day in July. Thriving is how he was described at the ceremony, not a full medical record.
