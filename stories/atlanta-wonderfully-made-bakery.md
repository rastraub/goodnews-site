Wonderfully Made, a bakery at Peachtree Church in Atlanta's Buckhead neighborhood, is a weekday community for adults with intellectual disabilities after high school ends, CBS News Atlanta reported. Participants bake about 20,000 cookies a year. Troy Millikan, known as T-Dog, is one of them.

The program is built for the years after school services stop and a lot of structured days disappear. Baking gives people a room to be in and a result they can see in a box of cookies. The count, about 20,000 a year, is the scale of that ordinary work.
