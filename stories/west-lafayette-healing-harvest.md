All 19 practical nursing students at the University of Saint Francis gathered donations from local businesses for months and then handed out Thanksgiving food through Healing Harvest, WLFI reported. Lafayette police helped with the delivery to families in Lafayette who are having a hard year.

Nineteen is the whole class. The months of collecting came before the handout. The food is the holiday meal those families received.
