Four friends staff the information desk at the North Dakota Heritage Center in Bismarck, and the desk has become a friendship of its own, KFYR reported. Once a week Chuck Peterson, Nadeane Silbernagel, Valerie Otto, and Erlys Fardal welcome visitors, point them the right way, and ask them to sign the guest book. Between people they talk over snacks.

Peterson and Silbernagel met in the early 1960s. Silbernagel knew Otto before either of them was at the center, and she began volunteering there in 1995. She met Fardal on the job and calls her a bonus friend. The four have worked the shift together for 10 years. Fewer visitors use the west entrance, which leaves a little more room for conversation. Peterson said there are few better chances to talk with people from all over the country. The report is about that welcome, not a new exhibit.
