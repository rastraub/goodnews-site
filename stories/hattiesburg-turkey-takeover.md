Elijah-King, 11, led the Great Turkey Takeover Challenge for the nonprofit Diamond Excursions Ladies' Edition and bought 50 turkeys, WDAM reported. The birds are for Friendsgiving food boxes for families in Mississippi's Pine Belt, and the Friendsgiving gathering is at Vernon Dahmer Park in Hattiesburg. The drive collected 144 turkeys in all. His 50 won the challenge. The 144 is the total the drive gathered.
