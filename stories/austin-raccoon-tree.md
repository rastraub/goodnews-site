Austin firefighters used the Jaws of Life to free a raccoon stuck in a tree hollow, UPI reported. The tool, usually used on wrecked vehicles, opened the wood enough for the animal to get out.

The call is one raccoon and one tree. The report is that the animal was freed, not a new use of the tool on every wildlife call.
