Santa Rosa police officers and a Sonoma County sheriff's helicopter crew rescued an 80-year-old man on Thursday evening near Annadel State Park, KSRO reported. The call came in about 5:53 p.m., to a house on the 300 block of Los Alamos Road. A neighbor said the man had not been seen or heard from in about two days. The house was unlocked, and his car and phone were still there. He has a heart condition that requires medicine every day, and he often walks to the park, so officers and detectives searched on foot and asked for Henry 1, the sheriff's helicopter.

The helicopter crew had barely started looking when it picked up a heat signature in the woods off Channel Drive. Officers reached that spot and found the man alive, disoriented, in thick brush on private property. He had fallen more than once. He was taken to a hospital to be checked. Police said the helicopter's quick pass over the area saved time in the search.
