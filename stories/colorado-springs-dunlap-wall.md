The Sam Dunlap Wall of Inspiration, a 120-foot tile mosaic under the Highway 24 MLK Bypass in southeast Colorado Springs, was finished in October after five years of work, CPR News reported. It holds more than 100 sepia portraits and quotes from neighbors, children, and leaders. Students from John Adams Elementary made tiles and carried out the interviews. The wall is done. The five years are the time it took to gather those faces and words and set them in tile.
