Volunteers built and delivered 50 beds for children around Lake Charles and Moss Bluff. Sleep in Heavenly Peace of Sulphur and Mt. Olive Baptist Church sent eight teams into the homes to set the beds up and make them. Building the frame is one job. Carrying it in, putting it together, and making the bed is the part the eight teams did on delivery day.
