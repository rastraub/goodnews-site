Michael Barker is Butler County's 2026 Veteran of the Year, WCPO reported. The Air Force B-52 tail gunner, who lives in the West Chester area, was nominated in secret by his wife. Through his VFW post and Honor Flight, he connects veterans who served after Sept. 11, 2001, with veterans from older generations. The title is the county's award. The mission he has taken up is those introductions, so newer veterans and older ones can find each other.
