DJ Chark came home to Alexandria and, through his family foundation, gave 200 Central Louisiana families a Thanksgiving feast. Chark is a former ASH Trojan and an NFL receiver. Each basket is meant to feed up to 10 people. Two hundred baskets at that size is not a snack table. It is a holiday meal for a long list of households in the region he left for football and came back to feed.
