AbleLight in Colorado Springs walks people with developmental disabilities through a job search and then stays for the work itself, KKTV reported. Coaches help with resumes, applications, interview practice, and support once someone is hired. Manager Sherree Perez said the first step is an assessment of skills, goals, and what support a person wants.

Georgie, who asked that her last name not be used, has been a spa concierge for more than two years, greeting guests and keeping the place in order. The station said she has grown confident enough to ask for new tasks. Perez said transportation and employer worries still block a lot of people. She said many accommodations cost little, and that a coach can answer the questions a new workplace is afraid to ask.
