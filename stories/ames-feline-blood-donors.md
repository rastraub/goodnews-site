Fawn, a rescue cat with a life-threatening infection in her abdomen, pulled through after blood transfusions and surgery at Iowa State University's Small Animal Hospital in Ames, KCRG reported. The hospital's feline blood donor program supplies that blood and is a response to a national shortage of cat blood. Fawn's case used transfusions from that program plus surgery. She made it through. The shortage is the wider reason the donor cats are there.
