Santa Fe's Bag 'n Hand pantry sent 447 households home with Thanksgiving dinner. Volunteers from many churches loaded the meals, turkey or ham, and those boxes reached about 1,600 people. A neighboring church donated 900 cans of green beans. The household count and the people count are two ways of saying who left with food. The cans are a reminder that one extra gift from next door still changes what fits in the box.
