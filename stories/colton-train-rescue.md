Colton police pulled an 18-year-old driver from a burning car after a train struck it Wednesday night near Steel Road and Hunts Lane, ABC7 reported. The gray Mercedes-Benz overturned and caught fire. Body-camera audio captured an officer calling out that someone was still inside.

Jillian Nunez, a community liaison for the department, said less than a minute passed between officers arriving and the driver being pulled out. The driver was taken to a hospital. Police did not name the officer or the driver, and they did not describe the driver's condition or how the car reached the tracks. Nunez also credited the Colton Fire Department, which responded with the officers.
