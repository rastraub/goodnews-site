Boston Bruins players made their annual Halloween visit to patients at Boston Children's Hospital dressed as characters from Toy Story. Boston 25 News reported on the stop, which the team makes each year so children in the hospital can have a Halloween visit without leaving the building.

The visit is a tradition, not a treatment. Players wore costumes. The patients are children being cared for at the hospital. The report is about the team showing up again this year, in costume, for that one day.
