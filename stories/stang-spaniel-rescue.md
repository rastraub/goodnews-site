Volunteer rescuers from Swaledale Mountain Rescue Team pulled a cocker spaniel named Freddie out of a hole near The Stang. BBC News included the rescue in a Yorkshire roundup dated November 1, 2026. Freddie had been walking with owner Tom Hollingworth on the moorland ridge at the border of North Yorkshire and County Durham when he fell about 30 feet, or 9 meters, during Storm Boelo.

Hollingworth said the dog was largely unharmed and "happier than ever, just back to normal," and that Freddie would still have been stuck without the team. The Stang story is one item in a weekly collection of local news. It is this dog and this call. It is not a tally of the team's other rescues.
