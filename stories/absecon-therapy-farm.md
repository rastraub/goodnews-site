Absecon's public schools have a therapy farm, the first in a New Jersey public school, New Jersey 101.5 reported. Students meet the animals on campus. One goat is named Vincent Van Goat.

The farm is a place for that contact, not a clinic and not a promise that every student will be assigned an animal. Vincent is one resident of a new school program.
