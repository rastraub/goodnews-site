Tricia Sheldon of Council Bluffs met her stem-cell donor for the first time, WOWT reported. She was treated for myelofibrosis with a transplant in 2024. The donor is Trace Passmore, a volunteer firefighter and medic from Illinois. He is back on the registry and would donate again.

The meeting is the first time the two had seen each other. The transplant was in 2024. Passmore's donation is the stem cells Sheldon received. He told the station he would do it again.
