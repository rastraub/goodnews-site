Joe Brehm, co-chief executive of Rural Action, found river otter tracks along Sunday Creek a few miles north of Glouster, WOUB reported. No one had reported otters on that stretch of the watershed in more than a century.

The creek was badly polluted by acidic water leaving abandoned mines. Rural Action has spent decades on the cleanup. The tracks, spotted on a fall hike, are one sign that the work is showing up in the mud. They do not by themselves prove a settled population, and the station treated them as the first report in a hundred years, not as a finished recovery.
