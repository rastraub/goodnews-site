Heather Hall held an unresponsive man's head above the water in a Hurricane hot tub while her 14-year-old son, Jaymon, called 911 and guided responders in, KSL reported. Jaymon has autism and apraxia of speech. The man recovered. Hurricane gave Hall and her son its Citizen Life Saver Award. The rescue was the two of them staying with the man and getting help to the right place. The award is the city's recognition after he recovered.
