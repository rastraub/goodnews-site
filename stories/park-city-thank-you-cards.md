Jenn Drummond of Park City is trying to get 10,000 thank-you notes written by the end of November, KSL reported. The mother of seven, a mountaineer with two Guinness records, is asking Utahns to join card-writing parties and send the notes to people whose work usually goes unremarked.

The first two gatherings, in Salt Lake City and Park City, produced nearly 700 cards in a day. The drive runs through November. The number that matters so far is the stack already addressed, not the record she is chasing.
