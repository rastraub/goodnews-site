Humane World for Animals said responders removed 951 chinchillas from R & R Chinchilla in Jenera at the request of the U.S. Department of Justice. The first group was 642 animals in October. A second group of 309 left on Nov. 3.

Those two counts are the animals taken off the property. The organization reported the removal. It did not publish a town-by-town list of where every chinchilla went next.
