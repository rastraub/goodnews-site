The Westfield Fire Department hosted Indiana's first firefighter camp for women over the weekend, WTHR reported on October 20, 2026. The free two-day camp is called H.E.R.S., for Honor, Equity, Respect, and Sisterhood. It was a Hamilton County effort. Firefighters came from Westfield, Carmel, Noblesville, Fishers, Cicero, and Hamilton County Fire Fighters Local 4416.

The Carmel Fire Department's account of the camp, which WTHR cited, said it was for women 18 and older and that there was no cost to take part. Participants practiced victim rescues, search techniques, and other hands-on skills, and they had time with firefighters for mentoring. The departments said the two days also made room for conversations that do not happen on a regular call. They plan to hold the camp again in 2027.
