Concord's Windmill Restaurant served more than 1,100 free Thanksgiving meals, a tradition Elias Smirnioudis started in the 1990s and his son Koz now runs. The kitchen prepped 100 turkeys and more than 300 pounds of mashed potatoes. Longtime volunteers were back in the same jobs. A restaurant that charges for dinner the rest of the year set the price at nothing for this one. The son's shift is the proof the dinner was not tied to one cook.
