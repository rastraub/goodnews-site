Omaha's Salvation Army Turkeyfest delivered 1,097 Thanksgiving dinners to seniors in its 35th year. The kitchen went through 1,900 pounds of turkey. About 30 volunteers worked the meal, including one person who has peeled potatoes for 30 years and another who has volunteered since 1992. Families have carried the routes for generations. The pounds are this year's cooking. The 30 years at the potato pot are how a holiday delivery becomes someone's ordinary Thursday.
