Mongabay reported on November 17, 2026, that the scimitar-horned oryx is back in Chad and that its global status has improved from extinct in the wild to endangered. The antelope had disappeared from Chad by the late 1980s and was declared extinct in the wild in 2000. A reintroduction led by the group Sahara Conservation has built a herd in Ouadi Rimé-Ouadi Achim Wildlife Reserve, the country's largest protected area. Mongabay said that herd grew from 25 animals in 2016 to 700, and that it is now the largest in the world.

The short report also said the animals still face long droughts, shifting conditions, and competition with livestock for grazing. The news is the larger herd and the change in status. It is not a claim that the threats have ended.
