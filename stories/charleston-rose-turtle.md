South Carolina Aquarium veterinarians teamed with specialists at MUSC to treat Rose, a green sea turtle, with a tissue-regrowth matrix used in human wound care, WRDW reported. The matrix is covering her exposed spinal cord. She is swimming and eating normally.

The aquarium is in Charleston. MUSC is the medical partner. The matrix is the material on the injury. Swimming and eating normally is the update on how Rose is doing, not a claim that the injury is finished healing.
