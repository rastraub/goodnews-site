Juniors and seniors at St. Thomas More Catholic High School left class on Wednesday for the school's annual Community Day of Service, Acadiana News First reported. They volunteered at more than 20 sites across Acadiana. Senior counselor Christy Romero said the day is how students practice the school's aim of serving other people, work they do not get from a classroom alone.

One group spent the day at Good Hope Park in Maison Freetown, clearing ground for a greenspace neighborhood children do not have yet. They worked beside True Friends Society. Erica Melancon Fox, executive director of Maison de Creole Freetown, said the nonprofit has been tied to that historic neighborhood since 1883 and was incorporated in 1898. Romero said the students were laughing while they worked and were still doing something useful for other children. The report is about that day of labor. It does not say the park is finished.
