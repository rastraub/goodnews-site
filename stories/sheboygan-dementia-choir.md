More than 30 people sing together in Sheboygan's Janet L. Herrick Dementia-Friendly Choir, Channel3000 reported, in a story from WTMJ and CNN Newsource. The singers include people living with dementia, the caregivers beside them, and neighbors.

The choir has a performance set for November 21 at Uptown Social. The report is about that shared singing, not a treatment claim and not a promise about how any one person's memory will change.
