Albert Lea police found a spider monkey named McNugget out in the cold on Saturday morning, KTTC reported. She was loose, friendly, and cooperative after some negotiation. Officers let her warm up in a squad car and then reunited her with her owner. The report is that short chain: found outside, persuaded, warmed, and returned. It does not explain how she got out.
