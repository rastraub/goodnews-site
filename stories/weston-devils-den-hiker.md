On Thursday, Weston police and firefighters were sent to Devil's Den Preserve at 3:42 p.m. after a report of a lost hiker, Patch reported. The Weston Emergency Communications Center worked out an approximate location, then lost contact with the caller. A command post brought together Weston police, the fire department, and a representative from The Nature Conservancy.

The last reported spot was on steep ground, so firefighters went in on foot. They found the hiker and walked them out of the preserve by 4:30 p.m., without incident. The trucks were back in service by 4:49 p.m. The volunteer fire department later reminded hikers that the days are getting shorter: check the sunset, leave with a charged phone, dress for the weather, stay on marked trails, and tell someone the route and when you expect to be back.
