An Oklahoma nonprofit called One Veteran, One Dog, Two Lives Saved pairs rescued dogs with veterans. News On 6 reported on October 8, 2026, that Marine combat veteran Ashley Dunn Gonzalez was matched with a Labrador named Tux. She told the station the dog has helped with daily routines, anxiety, and loneliness. The two spend a lot of time in the yard. Gonzalez described the match as grounding and purpose for both of them.

The station's story is about that pairing. It did not name the town where Gonzalez and Tux live.
