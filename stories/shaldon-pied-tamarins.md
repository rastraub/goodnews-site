BBC News reported on November 6, 2026, that pied tamarin twins born at Shaldon Wildlife Trust in Devon are thriving. Director Zak Showell said they were born on September 29 to mother Bobby and father Pedra. Keepers waited weeks before announcing the birth so the family could have quiet. The infants have started to ride on their parents' backs as they move through the habitat.

Showell said pied tamarins are unusually delicate and hard to breed, and that a zoo of Shaldon's size is one of only two European zoos to manage it this year. In the wild the species is limited to a shrinking pocket of rainforest around Manaus, Brazil. The news is this birth in Devon and the care that got the twins through their first weeks. It is not a change in the number of pied tamarins still living near Manaus.
