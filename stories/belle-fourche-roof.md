A Belle Fourche roof was torn off and reshingled in a day at no cost to the neighbor, the Belle Fourche Beacon reported. Allied Construction donated the labor, Mead Lumber donated the supplies, and B&S Dumpsters provided a dumpster.

Monument Health United Way volunteers did the tear-off and the new shingles. One day is how long the crew needed once those donations were lined up, and the bill to the household was nothing.
