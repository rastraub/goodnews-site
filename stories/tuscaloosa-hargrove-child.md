Tuscaloosa Fire and Rescue answered a call at 11:45 a.m. Saturday for an apartment fire on Hargrove Road, near Station 6, WBRC reported. Firefighters went into the building and found a 3-year-old in one of the apartments.

The fire was limited to one unit. Three or four other units had smoke damage, and crews extinguished the fire quickly. Officials said they did not have a later medical update. The last report they passed on was that the child was doing okay. The story does not name the child.
