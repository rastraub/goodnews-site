Fifteen senior cheerleaders at Sun Valley High School spent a day walking the building with gifts, CBS News Philadelphia reported. They stopped in classrooms to thank teachers, and they included the head custodian.

The visits were a last-year tribute in Aston, aimed at the adults who had taught them and kept the school going. The station's account is that route through the building, not a claim that every staff member was on the list.
