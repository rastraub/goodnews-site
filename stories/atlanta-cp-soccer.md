Occupational therapist Lindsay Kraun started CP Soccer Atlanta after a patient told her he wanted to play, 11Alive reported. The team gives children with cerebral palsy a side of their own.

Eight-year-old goalie Tayden Merrell said it means he is not alone. The CP World Cups are scheduled to begin this week in Fayetteville, Georgia. The team is a place to play. It is not a medical treatment, and it does not change a diagnosis.
