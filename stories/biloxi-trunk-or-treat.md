Residents of the Armed Forces Retirement Home in Biloxi spent Halloween with two crowds, WLOX reported. They hosted fifth graders from Anniston Elementary for trunk-or-treat, then held a costume contest among themselves.

The home sits on the Mississippi coast, and the day was a visit plus a contest. The station's account is those two events, not a claim about every classroom in the district.
