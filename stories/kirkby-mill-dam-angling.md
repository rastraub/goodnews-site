BBC News reported on November 19, 2026, that children who are not in a mainstream classroom are spending time fishing at Mill Dam Park in Kirkby. The sessions come from Primal Mentality CIC, started by Chris Jervis, who told the BBC that being outside had given him his freedom back and that he now wanted to offer that to children. He described the mornings as more than fishing: there is a practical lesson in the tackle, and a quieter one in sitting by the water.

Parents at the lake said the learning shows up when a child needs it. One father said his 10-year-old son, who has been educated at home for almost his whole life, is not handed the same sentence as 30 other children. Another said his son, who goes to a specialist school, researches ponds and fish on his own. A mother said her son, recently out of year seven, still does maths with the fishing and sleeps better because the lake is calm. Those are family accounts of particular children, not a measured study of every pupil in Knowsley. The news is that the sessions are happening, on a real lake, for children who were not getting that hour anywhere else.
