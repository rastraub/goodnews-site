ReEntry Solutions gave away free coats, hoodies, and blankets in Alexandria as temperatures started to drop across central Louisiana, KALB reported. Anyone who needed one could take one. The giveaway was timed for before the worst of winter.

Founder Candy Christophe said the people who come to the organization are facing weather that can be dangerous without a warm place to stay. The station's account is the pile of coats and blankets going out the door, ahead of the cold.
