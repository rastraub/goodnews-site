Women in Shelter KC's recovery program delivered Thanksgiving boxes to about 100 Kansas City families, KCTV5 reported. They walked the neighborhood to invite neighbors, then handed out the boxes alongside a bounce house and face painting.

About 100 is the number of families. The walk is how the invitations went out. The boxes are the meals. The bounce house and face painting were part of the same gathering.
