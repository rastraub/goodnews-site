The Portugal Resident reported on November 5, 2026, that stainless-steel equipment from a closed café in Portimão has gone to the city's soup kitchen. Associação Cultural Ideias do Levante agreed to transfer three worktops and a wall-mounted unit from Espaço Portas do Sol, a café project that shut on August 31. The pieces had been put up for sale. Roberto Estorninho, speaking for the association, said they were bought years ago with neighbors' generosity and now have another home of the same kind. Joy Borgan, of the Portimão Soup Kitchen, said the kitchen had needed industrial counters and sinks, and that the gift met that need.

The kitchen has operated since 2010 and is run by volunteers. It serves hot meals, soup, sandwiches, fruit, and yogurt to anyone who needs them, three days a week, by the old bridge, with more than 600 meals a week. It depends on donations and local fundraising. The Portugal News had already described the same transfer in October, so the November report is a second account of one gift, not a second kitchen. The news is the counters moving to the people who cook those meals. It is not a claim that the weekly food budget is solved.
