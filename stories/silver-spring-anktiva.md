On Oct. 14, 2026, the Food and Drug Administration approved Anktiva, given with BCG, for adults with BCG-unresponsive high-grade papillary-only bladder cancer. The agency described it as the first treatment approved for that group.

The decision drew on a trial in a closely related form of the disease, carcinoma in situ, in which 62 percent of patients had a complete response. That figure is from the carcinoma in situ trial. It is not a result measured in the papillary-only patients covered by this expansion. Common side effects occur. The approval is not a promise that every patient will respond, and it is not a statement that the cancer is cured.
