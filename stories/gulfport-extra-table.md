Extra Table will launch Give 'Em A Bird on Nov. 10 at The Markham Hotel in Gulfport, Darkhorse Press reported. The holiday campaign is seeking 9,000 Mississippi-grown chickens for pantry partners the week before Thanksgiving.

Fifteen dollars covers three whole chickens. The line the organizers use is Mississippians feeding Mississippians. The Nov. 10 event is the kickoff, and 9,000 is the goal, not a delivery that has already been counted.
