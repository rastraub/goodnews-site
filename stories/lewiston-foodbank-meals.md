About 200 people sat down in Lewiston for the Idaho Foodbank's A Seat at the Table, DailyFly News reported. Chefs led the night. The money is enough to support more than 250,000 meals for North Central Idaho.

That total is what the food bank says this fundraiser can underwrite, not a count of plates served in the room. The evening was one dinner pointed at a much larger number of meals.
