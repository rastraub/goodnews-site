Thomas Eigenbrodt, 78, of Fond du Lac, was hunting with family on public land near the end of Campbell Hollow Road in the Town of Millville when he did not return to his truck, WKOW reported from the Grant County Sheriff's Office. Dispatch took the call at 6:47 p.m. on Nov. 22.

Deputies, local fire departments, and Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources staff searched steep ground through the night. Handlers brought search dogs from Grant and Iowa counties. Crews also used ATVs and drones. Around 10:30 p.m., a medical helicopter from Dubuque flew the area and did not see him. The search kept going into the early hours of Nov. 23 without a find, then started again after daylight.

About 10:30 a.m., a DNR pilot in a small plane out of Siren spotted someone in blaze orange and steered a ground team to the spot. It was Eigenbrodt. He was awake and alert, cold, and dehydrated. Rescuers got him onto a UTV, then to a West Grant EMS squad, which took him to Crossing Rivers Hospital in Prairie du Chien.

The sheriff's office said the terrain made the search hard. Volunteers, neighboring sheriff's offices, emergency managers, and Wisconsin SOS search-dog teams were part of the overnight effort. He was found alive.
