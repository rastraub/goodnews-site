The Ponta d'Pom sea turtle campaign on São Vicente recorded 86 nests and 184 tracks this season, and it helped release more than a thousand hatchlings. Inforpress reported the tally on November 2, 2026, from biologist Zuleica Duarte, who leads the work for the campaign's nonprofit. She called the season encouraging, and she also described worries about the animals' safety and about how few resources the monitors have.

Praia Grande held 63 nests and 133 tracks. Praia do Lazareto held 23 nests and 51 tracks. Duarte said a track is not the same thing as a different turtle, because one animal can come ashore more than once. The figures are this year's nests, tracks, and hatchlings on those beaches. They are not a count of every turtle in Cabo Verde's waters.
