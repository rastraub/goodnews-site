Rita Burns spent a workshop in Haines Junction teaching people to preserve smoked trout and whitefish, a skill she learned at an elders' camp. CBC News reported the session, organized by Champagne and Aishihik First Nations, on October 23, 2026.

The fish came from research at lakes in the First Nations' traditional territory and would otherwise have sat in freezers. The jars are being shared in the community, as an earlier batch was. Jordan Cote-Germain volunteered. His grandmother used to can fish, and this was his first time through the process. Kwägąya, Azzy Allen, has canned for potlatches but had not done the work from start to finish. Allen wants a Southern Tutchone lesson plan for children, with pictures. Fish is served at potlatches. The method uses a brine of salt and vinegar and a pressure cooker. Burns said groceries are costly, and a jar from the cupboard can stand in for a fish that might cost $50.
