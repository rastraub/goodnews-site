Off-duty North Collier firefighter Brandon Catron reunited with the toddler he saved from a pool, WGCU reported. Catron was honored at Naples' Distinguished Public Service Awards. He had revived the boy.

The reunion and the award are the news. Catron was off duty when he helped. The boy is the child he brought back. The ceremony was in Naples.
