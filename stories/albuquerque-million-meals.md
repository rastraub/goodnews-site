Meals on Wheels New Mexico's five-year Million Meals campaign is on track to finish in December, a year ahead of the end-of-2027 target, the Albuquerque Journal reported. Volunteers such as Jackie Addis have kept the deliveries going.

The goal is a million meals a year. December is the month they now expect to reach it. The original target was the end of 2027. The pace comes from volunteers making the deliveries, not from moving the goalpost.
