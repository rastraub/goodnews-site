Ten middle and high schoolers from Falls Church's Culmore community painted a mural of their neighborhood's stories, George Mason University reported. They worked with muralist MasPaz and the university's Carter School. The piece is about belonging and hope, and it will travel around the neighborhood.

Ten is the number of students. MasPaz is the muralist. The Carter School is the university partner. Traveling is how the mural will be seen in more than one spot in Culmore.
