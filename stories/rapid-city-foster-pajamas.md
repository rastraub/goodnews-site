A Rapid City office is collecting pajamas for children in foster care across the Black Hills, NewsCenter1 reported on November 6, 2026. Huether Associates of Thrivent is holding its sixth annual drive through November 30. The group wants new and gently used pajamas from newborn through extra-large, dropped at 2210 Jackson Boulevard, or by appointment. Each set is matched with an equal cash gift.

Matt Huether said the idea is simple: every kid deserves a warm pair of pajamas, around the holidays and through the year. The pajamas go to Black Hills foster families with help from the Black Hills Foster Parents Association and Youth and Family Services. The office said it has given more than 7,500 pajamas to local foster children in past years. That total is the record of earlier drives. This year's collection is still open.
