A wallaby swimming off the Portland marina is back on land because a visiting boater rowed out to it. ABC News reported on October 30, 2026, that Matt Smith was tidying the back of a yacht that morning when seagulls started squawking near the end of the marina in south-west Victoria.

He thought the shape in the water was a dog. It was a wallaby, already tiring, and it was heading into deeper water more than 100 metres from shore. Smith said he did not think it would have lasted much longer. He rowed a dinghy out. The wallaby climbed in, then jumped straight back out because it was scared. He got it onto dry land, and wildlife carers met it there. ABC said the wallaby is now resting with those carers.

Smith had heard of wallabies swimming to get away from a dog or a dingo. He also mentioned a similar rescue about two weeks earlier near Port Lincoln. The Portland account is one animal brought ashore. It does not say what sent this wallaby into the sea.
