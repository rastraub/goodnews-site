Lekki Port and the Nigerian Ports Authority awarded scholarships worth 20 million naira to 100 students from host communities, The Guardian reported on November 19, 2026. Each of the 100 received 200,000 naira, which accounts for that 20 million total, under the Lekki Port Educational Support Programme. Two further students were recognized for academic performance with an additional 500,000 naira each, through an education scheme of the ports authority's managing director, Abubakar Dantsoho.

Lekki Port managing director Wang Qiang told the recipients their studies matter to the community that made the port possible, and he asked them to keep at the work. Port manager Emmanuel Anda said the authority already supported education and that this year's partnership with the deep seaport widened it to about 100 students from the host communities. He said he expected the effort to be larger next year. That is a hope he stated, not a sum already set aside.

One student named in the report is Taiwo Raheemat, a 200-level medical student at Lagos State University with a cumulative grade point average of 5.0. She said the award eases the cost of school and helps her stay on her studies. The article calls her one of the beneficiaries. It does not say she was one of the two who received the extra 500,000 naira. The verified gift is the hundred awards of 200,000 naira, plus those two additional prizes.
