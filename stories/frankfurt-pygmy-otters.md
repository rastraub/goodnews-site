17:30 Live reported on November 12, 2026, that three pygmy otters born in mid-August at Frankfurt Zoo are about three months old and leaving the den more often. Parents Dorie and Diabolo arrived about a year ago. Keeper Astrid Parys said the zoo had gone 14 years without young because the previous pair was old and, at the end, only females remained. The litter is two females and one male. In the Grzimek House they still treat a stone as a climb they need a parent for, and they enter the pool only with an adult. Parys said swimming is learned, not automatic.

Pygmy otters are the smallest of 14 otter species and live in South and Southeast Asia, eating fish, mice, and insects. The station said they are more social than the Eurasian otter, and that they are threatened because wetlands are drained and the animals are hunted and traded illegally. The news is these three pups practicing the pool. It is not a restoration of their home rivers.
