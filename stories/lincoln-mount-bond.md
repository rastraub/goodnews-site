New Hampshire Fish and Game used a Saturday night rescue to remind people that the days are getting shorter and colder, WMUR reported. Crews were called around 7:30 p.m. to a pair of hikers near the summit of Mount Bond in Lincoln, about ten and a half miles into the Pemigewasset Wilderness. Officials said the hikers were not prepared to spend the night in cold, snowy conditions.

Safety personnel reached the pair shortly before midnight. They got them to the trailhead around 2 a.m. Sunday. A trailhead is the place where the hiking path meets the road. WMUR did not name the hikers, and it did not describe any injuries. The reminder was about the season: less daylight, and weather that can turn a long walk into a night outside.
