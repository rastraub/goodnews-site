Bella Rose, a dog missing near Soldotna since August, came home after 70 days, Alaska's News Source reported. She walked into a custom trap at the landfill. Landfill staff, volunteers from KPAL, and the borough mayor had helped with the search that put that trap in place. The 70 days are the time she was gone. She was found when she entered the trap, and then she went home.
