Food 4 Thought received a $64,000 grant to buy and outfit a van for food delivery in Anne Arundel County, Eye On Annapolis reported. Impact100 Greater Chesapeake funded the award through Bringing Hope Home.

The nonprofit already serves more than 2,600 families a year through a pantry, a baby pantry, and wellness programs. The grant is money to get a dedicated outreach vehicle on the road, not a report that the van has finished a new route.
