The Harvest Center of Charlotte is preparing a campus of about $10 million on 10 acres, with housing, daycare, a gym, and a coffee shop. The ribbon-cutting is set for Nov. 17. The campus is meant to help families move out of homelessness.

It is not open yet. The November ceremony is the start, and the homes and the daycare are what the center is building toward.
