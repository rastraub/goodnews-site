Project Lemonade lets young people in foster care in Portland choose their own clothes. They can shop at no charge up to three times a year and walk out with enough for a full week. The nonprofit just opened a hub in the Albina District.

Its Sock-tober drive is collecting socks, with a goal of more than 18,000 pairs. The shopping trips are the regular work. The sock drive is the extra pile the hub is trying to fill this month.
