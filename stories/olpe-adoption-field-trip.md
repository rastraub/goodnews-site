A first grader in Olpe wanted his whole class at his adoption, and the school said yes. KWCH reported that the first graders of Southern Lyon County USD 252 took a field trip to the day the adoption became official. His mother said he had insisted they be there.

The class went as guests, not as a lesson with a worksheet. He got to see the same kids he sits beside at school while the court made his family official. The district is small enough that a field trip can be a room full of six-year-olds showing up for one of their own.
