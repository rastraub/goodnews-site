A squirrel that spent months being treated for squirrel pox is back in the trees, WNDU reported. Rescue Release Repeat, the only federally licensed wildlife rehabilitator in St. Joseph County, took him in thin and crusted and released him fluffy and determined to leave. Founder Rachelle Marshman has been answering those calls for six years.

He is one of more than 2,000 wild animals the group takes in each year. Raccoon, fox, or squirrel, the job is the same: care, then release. The pox case is one animal back in a tree, not a claim about every patient.
