Customers at Cafe 393 in Gallatin sat down to breakfasts that were already paid for, WSMV reported. The station's Surprise Squad covered the checks as a thanks to co-owner Tanya McPherson, who has spent nine years serving free Thanksgiving meals and stocking teachers' classrooms.

The paid breakfasts were one morning. The nine years are the work the surprise was pointing at. The report is that cafe and that visit, not a promise that every check in town was picked up.
