Germany is funding 1,200 scholarships for Kenyan youth in food and agriculture training. Tuko reported on November 11, 2026, that the German Embassy in Nairobi announced the Wings to Fly TVET programme on November 10. The scholarships are financed through KfW, the German development bank, with Kenya's Ministry of Education and the Equity Group Foundation.

Applicants must be Kenyan citizens aged 18 to 35 and already meet the entry rules for a Level 5 certificate or Level 6 diploma in a food or agriculture subject at one of 51 partner technical colleges. The programme is open in all 47 counties. Tuko said special consideration will go to women, people with disabilities, and people from vulnerable or marginalised backgrounds. People can use a paper form from the Equity Group Foundation and hand it in at an Equity Bank branch, or apply online through the link shared with the announcement. The deadline is November 18, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. The news is that applications are open. It is not a list of people who have already been awarded a scholarship.
