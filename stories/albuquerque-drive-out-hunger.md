Albuquerque high school students organized Drive Out Hunger for classmates experiencing homelessness, KOB 4 reported. The Saturday drive, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the Bernifacio Professional Development Complex at Louisiana and Comanche, benefits Albuquerque Public Schools' McKinney-Vento program. The district said it will take any non-perishable donations. Raising Cane's said it will donate 15 percent of net sales that Saturday, from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., at its Albuquerque restaurants when customers mention the fundraiser or use the code RCFUND96. Those are the plans on the books. The station did not report a total collected, because the drive had not happened yet.
