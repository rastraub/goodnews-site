North Little Rock officers Parsons and Breckenridge turned a call about suspicious activity into a kitten rescue, KARK reported. A volunteer with Community Cats of Central Arkansas heard a kitten near the four-way stop at Faulkner Lake Road, along Highway 391, and found it under rocks in a steep ditch. Volunteers had already spent nearly two hours trying to reach it when someone called police.

The officers arrived with their lights on. After they learned what the group was doing, one reached under the rocks and pulled the kitten out. Administrator Sarah Richardson said they also helped manage traffic. The kitten was severely dehydrated, weak, and had fleas. She has received fluids and medication and has improved. Richardson said she may have been stranded for several days, and volunteers think she could have fallen from a vehicle's engine compartment. One name being considered is Miss Demeanor, after the call that brought the officers. The rescue expects she could be ready for local adoption in about three weeks, once she has more veterinary care and has gained weight.
