Humane World for Animals said Minnesota Lynx guard Kayla McBride's regular season funded 319,750 pet meals through Points for Pets. Her line was 775 points and 126 three-pointers.

Those baskets were converted into meals for pets in underserved communities nationwide. The meal total is what this points program produced from her season, not a count of animals adopted.
