ʻUlu O Lele, a marketplace at the former Outlets of Maui site on Waineʻe and Papalaua streets, opened on Nov. 10 and drew thousands of people, Hawaiʻi Public Radio reported. Seventeen modular storefronts were built in Hilo and shipped to Maui, with food trucks alongside them. The Hawaiian Council led the $8 million project with Maui County and support from the Hawaiʻi Community Foundation. It is the first commercial project to reopen in Lahaina. A community committee picked 24 businesses from more than 100 applicants, and vendors will rotate. The marketplace is planned for about two years while longer commercial rebuilding continues. Boarded buildings and empty lots are still around it. Organizers describe the opening as one step, not a finished recovery.
