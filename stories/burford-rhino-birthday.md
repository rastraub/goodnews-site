White rhino calves Markus and Mo turned one at Cotswold Wildlife Park, near Burford, with cakes of soaked pellets, carrots, and salad leaves. BBC News reported the birthday on November 1, 2026. The pair were born at the park last year. Each weighed about 70 kilograms at birth and now weighs about 500 kilograms.

Their arrival took the park's rhinos to nine, the most in its 56-year history. The park said white rhinos were close to extinction in the early 1900s, with fewer than 50 thought to remain in Africa, and that they are now the most numerous of the five rhino species. It also said conservation is still vital. The report is the birthday, the weights, and that history in a sentence. It does not give a current wild population, and it does not say the calves will be moved to Africa.
