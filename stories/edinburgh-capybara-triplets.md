Three capybara pups were born overnight at Edinburgh Zoo and are staying close to their mother. STV News reported on October 28, 2026, that keepers found the trio that morning with parents Luna and Rodney.

The Royal Zoological Society of Scotland said the pups are doing well. A health check in the coming weeks will tell keepers their sexes, and names will be chosen then. Visitors who spot them are being asked to keep quiet while the family settles. Capybaras, the world's largest rodents, can breed more than once in a year.

The arrivals follow Bru, a male pup born in May who was the first capybara born in Scotland in 18 years and became well known online. Darren McGarry, head of animal care, said the triplets are exploring while staying near their mother, and that Bru is grumpy about sharing the attention. McGarry said he expects Bru to settle in with them. The birth is a zoo record for this family, not a claim about wild capybaras.
