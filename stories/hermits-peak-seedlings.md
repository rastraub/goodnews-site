Nearly 500 young pines have gone into the ground in the Hermit's Peak/Calf Canyon burn area since October, KOAT reported. Volunteers, the Rocky Mountain Youth Corps, and the Los Vigiles Land Grant did the planting. The scar is the largest in New Mexico. Another 500 seedlings are planned before winter. The nearly 500 already planted are this season's start, not the full number the groups hope to put in the ground.
