After flooding in southwest Colorado, San Juan County partners and more than 100 rotating volunteers looked after displaced animals in Farmington, the Tri-City Record reported. Pets stayed with families at McGee Park. Livestock went to Four Corners Stockyard.

The animals included dozens of dogs, cats, cattle, goats, and horses. The volunteer count is the rotating crew, and the two sites are where those animals were being housed, not a final list of reunions.
