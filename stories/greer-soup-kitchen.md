FOX Carolina's Surprise Squad, with United Community, gave Greer Soup Kitchen $2,000 for holiday meals, the station reported. Executive director Martha Bennett said the need has never been greater. The kitchen serves hot meals 365 days a year. More than 100 people come through daily, and many have no home.

Bennett said donations are down while the kitchen keeps feeding people every day. The $2,000 is a holiday boost on top of that daily work, not a replacement for it.
