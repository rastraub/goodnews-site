Snooze, the brunch restaurant at 7th and Colorado in Denver, is sending coffee grounds and other kitchen scraps into a container from Ecotone Renewables, The Colorado Sun reported. A couple of times a week an employee opens those bags and fills a biodigester. Liquid fertilizer comes out, is bottled, and is sold as Soil Sauce, including at the restaurant. Scraps from neighboring businesses on the block go in too. Chief growth officer Adam Schlegel said hauling the food waste separately would have cost about $500 to $1,000 a month, and the restaurant uses a hauler discount to pay Ecotone. Snooze is negotiating a smaller unit for its RiNo shop. That second site is not running yet.
