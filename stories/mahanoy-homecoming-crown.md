At a pep rally in Mahanoy City, Keny Cedanio Ramirez had just been named homecoming king when he saw his classmate Axton Kuhl start to cry. He handed Axton the crown and the sash. Axton became Mahanoy Area's homecoming king. 6abc reported the moment on October 20, 2026, after video of it spread.

Keny told the station he had not expected to win. He was happy for a few seconds, then more interested in cheering his friend up, which he said is what any friend would do. His younger brother has autism, and Keny said he hopes someone would treat his brother the same way. He credited his mother with teaching him the priorities behind the choice. He also said the dance was better than he had imagined, even without the crown and the title.
