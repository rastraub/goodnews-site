Community Harvest Food Bank in Fort Wayne said Friday that Kroger gave it $75,000. 21Alive reported on October 9, 2026, that the money is reserved for Farm to Food Bank, the program that lets the bank buy fresh produce from farmers across Northeast Indiana. That food goes to Community Cupboard, the Farm Wagon mobile pantries, Hope for Heroes, and other programs the bank runs.

The station's account is short. It does not say how many pounds the gift will purchase or which farms will sell first. What it does establish is that the check is for local produce, not for general overhead, and that the bank already uses farm food in the pantries neighbors walk into.
