Light of Life served 1,200 Thanksgiving meals at its 74th Hope and Turkey outreach in Pittsburgh. Volunteers included Don Waite, the "Green Bean Man," who has shown up for 30 years, and Jody Johnson, who was once helped by the mission, has been clean for 23 years, and now works with women in need. The line is long enough to need a green-bean specialist and a 30-year streak. Johnson's place in the room is the other half of the day: someone the mission fed and steadied is now one of the people doing the steadying.
