A hiker who got lost at Red River Gorge was found after he called for help, WKYT reported. Menifee County Dispatch traced the call, and he followed directions while heading back toward the trailhead.

As daylight faded, Wolfe County Search and Rescue told him to stop walking and not to use his phone as a flashlight, so the battery would last. The team kept moving toward him and made contact.

Searchers said calling as soon as he knew he was lost can matter a great deal once the light goes. They listed reminders for the gorge: know the route and the intersections, carry a charged phone but save the battery if you are lost, bring a flashlight or a headlamp instead of relying only on the phone, consider an offline map or a GPS unit, and if you are lost, stop, stay calm, and call for help.

The report does not name the hiker or say which day he was on the trail.
