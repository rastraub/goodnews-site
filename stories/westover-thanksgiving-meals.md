Pantry Plus More used part of a $250,000 gift from the Hayhurst family to put Thanksgiving meals on 350 tables, WDTV reported. The meals went to Monongalia County Schools families in Westover.

The $250,000 is the family's gift. Part of it, not necessarily all of it, paid for these dinners. Three hundred fifty is the number of tables. The schools are how those families were reached.
