A Texas father and his college-age daughter got onto Angels Landing because strangers had two permits to spare, KSL reported. Flight delays and a denied permit had blocked the hike they wanted in Zion National Park. They stopped to take a photo for a solo hiker. Her group turned out to have two extra Angels Landing permits and gave them away.

The permits are what the trail requires. The photo was the meeting. The two extra permits went to the father and daughter.
