Neighbors stepped in so Chef Angie could serve free Thanksgiving meals again at Workmen's Cafe on James Island. A church helped, a customer roasted turkeys, and other volunteers filled in. Nearly 100 meals went out at no charge. A cafe that sells lunch the rest of the year needed other people's ovens and other people's hours to make the holiday free. The outpouring is the story: the menu happened because the island brought the birds and the hands.
