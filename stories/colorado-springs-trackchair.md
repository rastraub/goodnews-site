Colorado Springs runs guided hikes on all-terrain TrackChairs for people with limited mobility. The Gazette reported that the city has added Stratton Open Space and Rock Ledge Ranch, so the program now covers six sites.

The other four are Ute Valley Park, Blodgett Open Space, Red Rock Open Space, and Bluestem Prairie. Riders reserve a chair through the city and go out with a guide. The chairs are built for dirt and rock, which opens trails that a standard wheelchair cannot manage.
