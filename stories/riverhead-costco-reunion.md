John Christina, a Vietnam-era veteran, found Erin Reiser of Sag Harbor, the woman who had quietly bought $50 Costco gift cards for two veterans, the Riverhead News-Review reported. They reunited at the same Costco table where the gift had happened. She had not put her name on the gesture. He tracked her down, and the meeting put the giver and one of the veterans back across that table.
