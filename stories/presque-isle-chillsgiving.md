Silver Smith, 16, threw Chillsgiving in Presque Isle, a free gratitude party for teens at the Congregational Church. The room had music and lattes, and the activity was writing letters to favorite local businesses. Thanksgiving usually gathers a family. This one gathered teenagers and asked them to thank the shops they already use. Smith did not charge admission. The letters are the part that leaves the church and lands on someone else's counter.
