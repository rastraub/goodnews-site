The Colorado Gives Foundation granted $1 million to make Jefferson County neighborhoods easier to get around, the Jeffco Transcript reported. Twenty-seven local nonprofits received grants. They include the Golden Optimists Bike Recycle Program, which gives free bikes to residents who need them, and Butterfly Pavilion, for pollinator habitat. The $1 million is the grant total. The 27 is how many groups are sharing it.
