Colorado Parks and Wildlife, Southern Plains Land Trust, and the Smithsonian released 38 black-footed ferrets on Nov. 18 at Heartland Ranch in Bent County, a 43,000-acre spread near Lamar. Researchers are tracking 22 of them with accelerometer collars and watching with drones, so the next release can be planned from what these animals actually do. The collars record movement. The drones keep eyes on the ranch without a person walking every acre. The goal is a more successful release next time, not a one-day count.
