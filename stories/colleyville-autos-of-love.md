Dane and Cathy Bailey, who own an auto shop in Colleyville, gave away eight repaired cars this year through Autos of Love, KHOU reported. People donate cars, and the Baileys fix them for neighbors who need one. Their goal had been one car a year. One of this year's eight went to a mom whose kids had been walking nearly two hours to school.

Eight is the number given away. The one-a-year goal is what they had set. The cars start as donations and leave as working transportation.
