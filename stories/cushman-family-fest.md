Students at Faithful Roots Academy in Cushman planned, priced, and cooked a Thanksgiving feast for their families. The school runs from preschool through 10th grade. They used math to figure out what the dishes would cost, then served the meal at the school's first Family Fest. The arithmetic was part of the cooking. The families sat down to food the students had budgeted before they turned the ovens on.
