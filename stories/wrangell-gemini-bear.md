A brown bear named Gemini received the Tongass National Forest's Helicopter Mom award, Popular Science reported. She is known from Anan Creek, in the forest of southeast Alaska.

The award is a name the forest put on one bear's mothering. It is not a medical finding and not a ranking of every sow on the creek. The people who watch Anan Creek are the ones who picked her out.
