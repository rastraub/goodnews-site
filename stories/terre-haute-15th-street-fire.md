Just after 10 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 12, Terre Haute firefighters were sent to a house in the 400 block of South 15th Street, WTHI reported. They reached the block in about three minutes and found flames and smoke at a home where people lived.

Firefighters helped one person out of the house. That person was looked over at the scene and chose not to go to the hospital. The report does not name the resident or the firefighters who made the rescue. Investigators have not said what started the fire.
