Before a single bushel went into a new bin, Agwrx Cooperative turned it into a concert hall. On October 7 the Webster High School choir stood inside the empty 1.3-million-bushel bin and sang, KXLG reported. The set included "Good Day Sunshine," "The Stars with the Voyager," and "The Star-Spangled Banner."

Chief executive Kent Borstad invited the students so the first sound in the bin would be music, not machinery. The steel walls made a room that will not exist again once the grain is in. After the concert, the bin went to work storing the harvest.
