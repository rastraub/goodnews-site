Tyler Isenmann's Best Chance Ranch, in the Birmingham area, takes horses that were neglected or bound for slaughter, lets them settle, and looks for homes. The work is rehabilitation first and a next owner after that.

The ranch is a pause between a hard start and a barn where a horse can stay. The homes are the point of the pause.
