The Capital Yacht Club held its 34th annual picnic lunch on Sunday for public safety workers, WTOP reported. The club, at the Wharf in Southwest D.C., was not calling them to an emergency. More than 15 members cooked hot dogs and hamburgers and helped park arriving police and fire vehicles. Wharf restaurants, including Kirwan's, Pink Tiger, Lupo Marino, Hank's Oyster Bar, Bartaco, and City Cruises, supplied sides, drinks, and desserts.

Commodore Guy Shields, who organizes the lunch, said the workers take care of the city every day and the picnic is a small thanks. Every first responder in the city was invited, including National Guard members patrolling the Wharf. Some picked up boxes for colleagues who could not leave the station. Longtime member Kathy Ellingsworth and D.C. Deputy Fire Chief Thomas Chenworth both described the lunch as a standing partnership, not a one-time gesture.
