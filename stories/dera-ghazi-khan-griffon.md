Wildlife officers in Dera Ghazi Khan picked up a Eurasian griffon vulture that was sick and could not fly. Dawn reported the rescue on November 26, 2026. Deputy Chief Wildlife Ranger Sajjad Hussain said the bird was found near the Education University on Wednesday and is now being treated at DG Khan Zoo. The plan is to release it once it can fly again. That release has not happened.

Umer Waqas, a former senior conservation officer at WWF-Pakistan, told Dawn that the Eurasian griffon is a large scavenger and that a quick response matters when a wild bird is in trouble. He also said South Asia's vultures have collapsed over recent decades, and that scientists in Pakistan were among the first to identify the cause. Dawn's report does not spell out that cause, and it does not say how the bird in Dera Ghazi Khan became sick. The news is one bird in treatment, not a recovery of the wider population.
