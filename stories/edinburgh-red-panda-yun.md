Edinburgh Zoo has a new red panda. Edinburgh News reported on October 30, 2026, that one-year-old Yun arrived from Banham Zoo in Norfolk and will live alongside Bruce, the zoo's 11-year-old male. The Royal Zoological Society of Scotland said she was born in June 2025. Her name is an East Asian word for "cloud," used for lightness, beauty, and good fortune.

Keepers hope the pair might eventually breed. The paper quoted that hope as the "patter of tiny paws." No cub has been announced, and the article does not say the animals have been paired successfully. Yun is still settling in, and visitors are being told they can look for her.

Edinburgh News noted that wild red pandas live in high-altitude temperate and subalpine forests of the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China, and that they are known for reddish fur, ringed tails, and climbing. The arrival is one animal moving between two zoos. It is not a change in the wild population.
