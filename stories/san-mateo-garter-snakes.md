The San Francisco Zoo released 62 young San Francisco garter snakes across San Mateo County, Popular Science reported. The snakes were born at the zoo and head-started, raised there until they were ready to go outside. It is the first time this endangered snake has been released into the wild. More than 130 more were born this year for releases planned in 2027. Those later releases are still ahead. The step that has happened is this first group of 62.
