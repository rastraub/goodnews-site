The turtle camp at Punta Sur on Cozumel had protected 625 nests by September 30, 2026, and 3,705 hatchlings had reached the sea. NTCD reported the camp's own figures that day. Of the nests, 547 belonged to green turtles and 78 to loggerheads. Of the hatchlings, 2,965 were green and 740 were loggerhead. Beach cleanups at the camp removed 1,513.5 kilograms of waste.

Rafael Chacón Díaz, the park foundation's director of conservation and environmental education, presented the season so far. Technical coordinator Ricardo Peralta Muñoz said crews would still open finished nests in the coming weeks to count eggshells, and that the season was not over. The numbers will go to Quintana Roo's sea turtle committee and to the federal wildlife office at SEMARNAT. Juanita Alonso Marrufo, director general of the Fundación de Parques y Museos de Cozumel, thanked staff and volunteers. These figures are only for the Punta Sur camp. They are not a total for every beach on the island.
