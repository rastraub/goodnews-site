Dogs Deserve Better Blue Ridge held a free clinic in Farmville that spayed or neutered animals and gave rabies shots to 19 cats and 18 dogs, the Farmville Herald reported. The group also paid for unexpected medical care.

Six dogs left the full Prince Edward County shelter for foster homes or other rescues. Thirty-seven is the number treated at the clinic, and six is the number that got out of that crowded shelter through the same effort.
