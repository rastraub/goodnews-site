Jonesboro Right Now reported on October 7, 2026, drawing on a city announcement, that Jonesboro Animal Services is running a Stock the Shelter drive for the whole month. The list is dog and puppy food, cat food, litter, laundry detergent, Fabuloso, Dawn, bleach, paper towels, and Lysol wipes. Donations can be dropped at the Municipal Building, 300 South Church Street, or at the shelter, 6119 East Highland Drive, or shipped to the shelter. Dogs adopted in October receive a free lifetime microchip. The outlet did not publish a running total of what has come in.
