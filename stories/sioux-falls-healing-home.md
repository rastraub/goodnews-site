St. Francis House launched its Healing Home campaign at the Greater Sioux Falls Chamber Community Appeals kickoff, Dakota News Now reported. The ask is $1.5 million toward a $10 million campus.

Plans call for 38 efficiency apartments plus on-site health care, recovery support, jobs help, counseling, a cultural space, and a Donation Depot. The $1.5 million is the campaign goal announced at the kickoff, not a building that is already finished, and the $10 million is the larger campus budget.
