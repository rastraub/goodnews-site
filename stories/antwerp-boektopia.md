Boektopia opened at Antwerp Expo on Tuesday, November 10. VRT NWS reported the first Antwerp edition that afternoon. The fair had been in Kortrijk the week before. Antwerp's older book fair ended after the 2019 edition, after the coronavirus crisis and money troubles. This week's fair runs through November 15, with signings, reading sessions, and chances to meet authors. Director Lore Vanaudenhove said Kortrijk drew more than 46,000 visitors and that Antwerp is not stating an attendance target yet. She said the aim is to get people reading and to bring them back next year.

Readers treated the opening as a return. Marte came from Heist-op-den-Berg and said the trip no longer means going all the way to Kortrijk. Larissa and her mother Nadia came from Sint-Truiden and had booked a hotel so they could arrive early. Nadia, a former teacher, said the old fair had been a family habit. Schools have a free program on November 10, 12, and 13. Nearly 3,000 fifth-grade children are invited to author meetings, a show, and a treasure hunt. Reader Sabrina said she has 408 books on her shelves and more than 1,000 on her e-reader, and that she already had five books in her bag while she was still in the first hall. The story is the fair's opening week. It is not a count of how many people will come by Sunday.
