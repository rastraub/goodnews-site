The ASPCA and IPAW are putting $1.4 million, over multiple years, into Dog Is My CoPilot's rescue flights, Pet Age reported. The Jackson-based group already flies more than 8,000 at-risk shelter animals a year to communities with more room to adopt.

The $1.4 million is new support meant to expand that flying. The 8,000 figure is the pace the organization already keeps, not a count of seats added by the grant on day one.
