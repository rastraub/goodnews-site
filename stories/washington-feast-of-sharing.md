Feast of Sharing served 1,500 meals at the Washington convention center. Hundreds of volunteers fed more than 1,000 guests. Free haircuts, flu shots, and tables of resources sat alongside the dinner, so a person could eat and also leave with a trim, a shot, or a lead on other help. The plate was the reason to come. The rest of the room was there so one visit could cover more than hunger.
