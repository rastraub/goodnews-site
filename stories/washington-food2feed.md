WHUR's 48th Food2Feed radiothon raised $40,000 for holiday meals, the station reported. Listeners and partners gave. Howard University students worked a Georgia Avenue bucket brigade that brought in about $10,000 in cash. The money is for Capital Area Food Bank and SHABACH! Ministries, and a truckload of food was collected too.

The 48th year is this edition of the radiothon. The $40,000 is the total. About $10,000 of that cash came from the buckets. The truckload is food, separate from the dollar total.
