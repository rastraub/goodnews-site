Crews and volunteers documented 697 Kemp's ridley sea turtle nests on Texas beaches in 2026, Padre Island National Seashore reported. That is a 55 percent jump from 449 nests in 2025. The Kemp's ridley is the world's most endangered sea turtle. In 1978, these beaches saw about one nest every three years. The 697 and the 449 are nest counts for those two years. They measure how many times turtles came ashore to lay eggs, not a census of every turtle at sea.
