Hundreds of people came to Mill Pond Plaza for the fifth annual trunk-or-treat put on by Casa Della Luce, a Westerly pizza shop, NBC 10 WJAR reported. Children in costume collected candy. The owners also asked families to bring canned goods and other food that keeps. Organizers said last year's event gathered more than 2,000 pounds for a local shelter.

The night put a parking-lot Halloween and a food drive in the same place. Neighbors donated candy, and other Westerly businesses helped sponsor the event. The report did not give this year's pound total. What it did say is that the line of families has grown, and that the cans are part of the tradition.
