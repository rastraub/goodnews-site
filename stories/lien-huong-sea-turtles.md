Ninety-two sea turtle hatchlings left a nest at Ba Hon Beach, in Hon Cau Marine Reserve, and reached the sea on October 31. DTiNews reported the count on November 1, 2026. The reserve, in Lien Huong commune in Lam Dong province, said a female had laid 101 eggs there on September 12. About 91 percent of them hatched.

Staff checking the beach on the afternoon of October 30 saw the sand over the nest sinking. They watched through the night. The first hatchlings came out around 2 a.m. By 6 a.m., all 92 had made it into the water.

An earlier nest at Tau Beach had already done well. A female laid 65 eggs there on September 4. After 50 days, 61 hatchlings emerged, about 94 percent. Hon Cau Marine Reserve covers about 12,500 hectares, including the 140-hectare island, and turtles usually come ashore to nest from May through October. The news is those two nests, not a count of every turtle in the reserve.
