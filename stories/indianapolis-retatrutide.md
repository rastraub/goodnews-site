Medical Xpress reported results from The Lancet on TRIUMPH-2, an 80-week Phase 3 trial of 1,152 adults who had obesity and type 2 diabetes. At the highest dose, Eli Lilly's experimental weekly injection, retatrutide, was associated with an average weight loss of 18.8 percent, compared with 5.1 percent for people who received a placebo. Hemoglobin A1c, a longer-term measure of blood sugar, was about 1.5 points lower.

The drug is still experimental. It is not approved by the Food and Drug Administration, and this trial is not a decision that it will be. Gastrointestinal side effects were common. The study does not show that retatrutide cures diabetes, and it does not replace care a person already has. A clinician is the right person to talk with about treatments that are already approved.
