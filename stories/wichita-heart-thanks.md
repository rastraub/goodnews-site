Cyndi Wiles thanked the people who restarted her heart, The Wichita Eagle reported. On August 10 she was hanging wallpaper at home when she sat down and then became unconscious. Her husband, Gary, called 911. She stopped breathing, and her heart stopped. Firefighters from Wichita Fire Station 11 arrived in four minutes and began chest compressions. Sedgwick County EMS paramedics arrived about five minutes later and delivered one shock. That shock restarted her heart.

On Tuesday she met the 911 call taker, the Station 11 crew, and the paramedics, including lead paramedic Josie Gallagher. They received lifesaver coins and certificates. Deputy Chief Angela Fuller of Sedgwick County EMS said an out-of-hospital cardiac arrest is rarely survived. Wiles said her physicians have found no heart damage and no other life-threatening effects. After she recovered, she finished the wallpaper project. In the weeks after the call she had spent several days on a ventilator.
