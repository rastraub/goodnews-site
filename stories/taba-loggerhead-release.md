Youm7 reported on November 4, 2026, that South Sinai's protected areas rescued three loggerhead sea turtles and released them on the beaches of Taba, on the Gulf of Aqaba. Environment minister Manal Awad said the turtles were found exhausted. Teams gave them veterinary care, followed their health, and rehabilitated them until they could return to the sea. Sherif Abdel Rahim, head of the Egyptian Environmental Affairs Agency, said the release went ahead only after staff were sure the animals could manage in the water again.

Abdel Rahim said loggerheads may not be caught, sold, displayed, or traded under Egypt's environment law and its protected-areas law. The species is also listed on Appendix I of CITES, the treaty on trade in endangered animals. The ministry asked people to report strandings, injuries, or threats. The article describes this rescue and the Taba release. It does not say where the turtles were found, how long they were in care, or whether any of them carried a tag. The news is these three animals back in the Gulf of Aqaba.
