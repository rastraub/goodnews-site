Students at Riverhead High packed 50 Thanksgiving baskets and donated to a local pantry. The cooking club, Key Club, and student government raised the money with a football tournament. A local restaurateur added turkeys and gift cards. The students did the packing. The tournament paid for part of what went in the boxes, and the extra turkeys and cards came from someone who already feeds the town for a living.
