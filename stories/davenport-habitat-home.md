Habitat for Humanity Quad Cities dedicated its 145th home in Davenport on Saturday, and Katelyn Kleinsmith told her sons, "Boys, we're home," KWQC reported. She said ownership meant stability and security for her boys, and a sign of the work she had put in to give them a safe place.

Partner families in the program complete at least 250 volunteer hours and homeownership classes before they buy the house. Volunteers sent this family off with quilts, gift baskets, supplies, and a handcrafted key signed by people who had worked with the affiliate. The affiliate builds with the City of Davenport's HOME program and the Quad Cities Housing Council. The news is this dedication, the 145th for the local group.
