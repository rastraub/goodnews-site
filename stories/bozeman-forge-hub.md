Montana State University opened Forge in Strand Union room 102 during National Nontraditional Student Week, KULR8 reported. The hub is meant as a single stop, a place with no wrong door, for advising, scholarships, and workshops aimed at first-generation and nontraditional students.

Student Aidan Walker credits Forge scholarships with keeping him enrolled. The opening is a new front door for that help, not a claim that every student who walks in will get the same award.
