Sunday night, just before 10:20 p.m., Trumbull County dispatch took a call for a house fire on Draper Street SE in Warren, WKBN reported. Howland Fire Department crews found a duplex. No one was home on the right side. On the left, a couple was still inside. The husband is paralyzed, and his wife had not been able to get him out.

Dispatch said the crews got him outside. He was checked in an ambulance at the scene, and his wife also got out. WKBN reported that no one was injured. The street was closed in both directions and later reopened. The front right side of the home was damaged. The cause of the fire is under investigation. The station did not name the couple or the firefighters who brought the man out.
