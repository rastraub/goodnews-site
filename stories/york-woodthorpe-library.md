A pupil at Woodthorpe Primary School in York opened a library built from his own drawing. YorkMix reported the ribbon-cutting on November 23, 2026. Archie led the ceremony. His Eco Library, with solar panels, a living wall, grass-effect flooring, cloud-shaped lights, and softly shaped shelves, won Portakabin's Inspiring Libraries competition. Judges chose it from 1,901 entries by pupils and teachers across the United Kingdom and praised the way it tried to bring the outdoors in. After meetings with Archie, the company built a equipped library for the school from that design.

Archie said he was surprised he won and glad to watch the idea get built. Staff from the school, from Portakabin, and from Explore Libraries attended. Author Catherine Jacob then read her picture book Betsy Buglove Saves the Bees. Portakabin regional manager Wendy Jones said the contest was meant to make reading feel exciting. Vice principal Helen Elsworth called the room a calm place for curiosity. Winners of the contest were named in February 2026, during the National Year of Reading. Portakabin has said about 1,700 UK primary schools still have no library. The news is this one school and this one room. It does not say those other schools now have one.
