Trumpeter Dawn West, in West Virginia University's Mountaineer Marching Band since 1983, was honored at halftime for a Guinness World Record, WDTV reported. The record is the longest career as a female marching band member: 43 years and 280 days. Bandmates know her as Grandma.

1983 is when she joined. Forty-three years and 280 days is the span Guinness recognized. Halftime is when Morgantown stopped the game to mark it.
