Interim Chief Jeffery Carroll and the Metropolitan Police Department brought flowers and a motorcade to Bessie Johnson for her 102nd birthday, 7News reported. Grandma Bessie has lived in the District for 87 years. The D.C. Council honored her for her warmth and her sweet potato pies.

One hundred two is the birthday. Eighty-seven years is her time in the city. The flowers and the line of police vehicles were the department's visit.
