Berkeley Springs High science students still got to drop their pumpkins, The Morgan Messenger reported. Roof work had closed the usual spot, so the Great Cacapon Volunteer Fire Company raised a ladder truck and the class used that height to test designs meant to keep a pumpkin intact. The trials are practice for a challenge at West Virginia University.

The truck was a workaround for one blocked roof. The designs are what the students are taking to the university contest. A drop that survives in Berkeley Springs is a test, not a trophy from WVU.
