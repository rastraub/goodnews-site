Anam Cara Sanctuary in East Haddam is asking neighbors for leftover Halloween pumpkins and other produce, WFSB reported. The food is a snack for farm animals the sanctuary took in after neglect or abuse.

Donors should call ahead before they arrive. The sanctuary is also glad to have money and volunteers. The ask is for surplus pumpkins people already have, not a promise that every gourd is the right meal for every animal.
