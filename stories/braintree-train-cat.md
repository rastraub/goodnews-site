Zeus, a four-year-old cat, boarded the 20:08 from Braintree on November 10 and got off 37 miles later at London Liverpool Street. BBC News reported the trip on November 23, 2026. Greater Anglia staff took the half-British shorthair into the station office, then escorted him on a train back to Braintree. Owner Lily Burroughs said her first thought was that he had done it again.

He already had a reputation on the railway. From an earlier home in Stansted Mountfitchet he had ridden toward London Stansted Airport, and in Braintree he was known for turning up at the Co-op. He wears two trackers. Burroughs said shutting him in only leads him to hurt himself trying to get out, and that he is a social cat who depends on other people noticing him. She called the London ride his farthest yet and thanked the staff. A Greater Anglia spokesperson said he liked the attention on the way home. The news is this one journey and the people who turned him around. It is not advice to let a cat ride the train.
