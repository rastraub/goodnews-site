The Food and Drug Administration approved Bacfenssi, a doxycycline paste, as the first treatment applied at the surgical site to reduce infections after colorectal surgery. In a 798-patient phase 3 randomized double-blind trial, surgical-site infections fell from 10 percent to 4 percent when the paste was applied to the wound before it was closed. The approval is for that use, during that operation, not for infections in general. Four percent is not zero. The trial measured infections at the surgical site. It does not claim the paste replaces the other steps hospitals already take to keep a wound clean.
