A cashier named Tim at a Trader Joe's in Bloomington noticed a nurse who was anxious about going back to work after leave for her mental health. He gave her flowers and chocolate, and a hug.

It was one shift at a checkout. He saw that she was having a hard morning and answered with something she could carry out the door.
