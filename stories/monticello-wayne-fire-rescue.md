Wayne County sheriff's deputies Zach Bybee and Nick Bertram got a bedridden woman out of a house fire on November 7 before fire units arrived, WKYT reported, citing the Monticello Fire Department. She was taken to the hospital. Neighbors and family helped firefighters extinguish the fire, and the department said the damage was minor. The report does not name the woman, describe her injuries, or give the street where the house stands. What it does record is that the deputies reached her first and that the people around her helped keep the fire small.
