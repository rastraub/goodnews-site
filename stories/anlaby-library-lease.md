Hull City Council approved a 35-year community asset transfer of Anlaby Park Community Library to the volunteers who kept it open. BBC News reported the decision on October 14, 2026.

The branch was due to close in March 2013 because of budget cuts. The closure was delayed, and the friends group took the lease in 2014. Somewhere between 60 and 80 people volunteer there. Chair Steve Ashton said the longer lease gives the group security to plan a refurbishment and to bring on the next generation of helpers.

The council said in June that it would fund work on the outside, with the interior paid for by volunteer fundraising. Work started in September and is due to finish next year. During the building work, a reduced service runs from a temporary building in the park.
