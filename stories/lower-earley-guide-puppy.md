BBC News reported that Guide Dogs is asking for more volunteer homes in Berkshire. The charity has 17 guide dog puppies being raised there and wants another 25 homes. Volunteers look after a puppy for the first year to 14 months, before more advanced training. Sue and Mervyn Wiseman of Lower Earley are raising Heath, a two-month-old Labrador and golden retriever cross, until that next step. Sue told BBC Radio Berkshire that the attachment is real, and that parting with the dog is sad.

About 1,700 puppies are being raised by volunteers across the United Kingdom. The charity's Berkshire base is in Burghfield Common. Stephanie Ranson of Guide Dogs said the work is to help people who have lost their sight keep their freedom, and that giving a puppy back is hard and rewarding at once. The news is the puppies already in Berkshire homes and the ask for 25 more. It is not a tally of new volunteers signed up since the report.
