Migori County commissioned a public library at Msomi Centre in Migori town on October 19. Education News reported the opening that day. County Secretary Oscar Olima commissioned it for Governor Ochilo Ayacko. Suna Migori Rotary donated more than 40,000 books, which the club valued at 52 million shillings. Club president Charles Koyoo said the project began about three years ago after members noticed the town had no public library.

The county paid for building work, paint, furniture, staff, and locally published books. Rotary partners added books, shelves, laptops, signs, internet equipment, and barcode tools. The Kenya National Library Service added reading materials, staff training, and devices for digital learning. Olima said the county hopes to open more libraries in its sub-counties. That is a plan, not a second library already open. The news is this building, this partnership, and this gift of books. It does not say how many people have used the shelves since the ceremony.
