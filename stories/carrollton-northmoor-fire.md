A fire broke out around 1 a.m. Tuesday at a house on Northmoor Drive in Carrollton, where Deena Butler lived with her sister, NBC 5 Dallas-Fort Worth reported. Neighbors heard the fire crackling and saw flames at the back of the house. One neighbor ran outside without stopping for shoes and called 911. Others tried the front door and were turned back by thick smoke.

Butler's sister got out as the fire spread. Butler was still inside. She had gone to bed hours earlier in a back bedroom near the flames and did not know the house was burning until something woke her. She opened her door and found firefighters there, in masks, with lights. They had been breaking windows one by one and searching for her.

Butler got out and met her sister across the street. Neighbors brought shoes, pants, and other clothes so the two women could stay warm. Later that day the sisters went back to see what they could salvage. Butler said investigators believe the fire started on the patio and have not determined what ignited it. She said she came close to not getting out, and she credited the neighbors and the crews who came.
