Salida's free Thanksgiving dinner is set up to feed about 900 people at the Community Center. Donations and grants cover the cost, so a guest does not pay for a plate. About 90 volunteers are cooking 300 pounds of turkey. The meal also goes out by delivery to Salida, Cotopaxi, and Villa Grove, and about 200 of those orders were already in. The hall is one table. The deliveries are how the same dinner reaches people who are not walking through the door.
