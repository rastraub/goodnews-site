On Friday night, Nov. 6, Winlock firefighters were called to a house fire at about 10:53 p.m., the Bellingham Herald reported from a Lewis County Fire District 15 news release. Three of the four people inside got out and gathered in a side yard. They told the crews that a disabled elderly man was still inside and could not get out on his own.

Firefighters saw heavy flames and smoke coming through the doorway where he was. They knocked the flames down enough for two of them to go in. They found him in the living room and helped him outside, where paramedics treated him.

The district said he and another man who lived there were taken to Providence Centralia Hospital. It described possible smoke inhalation and did not say how either man was doing later. About 20 firefighters from Winlock and neighboring Lewis County departments put the fire out. None of the firefighters were injured. The district did not name the two who went inside.
