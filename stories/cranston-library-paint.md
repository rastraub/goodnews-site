Cranston Online reported on October 7, 2026, that volunteers spent Saturday painting the Oaklawn branch of the Cranston Public Library, a one-story building on Wilbur Avenue in the city’s Oak Lawn Village. Library director Ed Garcia said the branch had needed the work for a long time and that funding was scarce. The Cranston Rotary, Cranston Cares, Scouting for America Troop 66, and the library’s teen volunteers helped round people up. Garcia said the crew made more progress than he expected and was on track to finish the following weekend. The main part of the building began in 1838 as a schoolhouse, moved to its current site in 1896, and reopened then as the Oak Lawn Free Library. Locally owned Durfee’s Hardware discounted the paint.
