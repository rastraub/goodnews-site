Students from every Albuquerque Public Schools high school brought non-perishable food they had collected to the annual Drive Out Hunger event, KOAT reported. The food is for the district's McKinney-Vento warehouse, which serves families in need. This is the same annual drive an earlier report had described as upcoming. KOAT's account is of students delivering what they gathered. It does not add a pound or dollar total.
