Jefferson County Family Courts finalized 37 adoptions on National Adoption Day, WLKY reported. Louisville marked the day as its best. One couple adopted the three boys they had fostered for a year.

Thirty-seven is the day's total. The three boys are one of those cases, after a year in that foster home. The court is where the adoptions became final.
