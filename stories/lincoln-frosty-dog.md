Lincoln firefighters pulled a dog named Frosty out of an apartment fire and got her breathing again with a donated pet oxygen mask, 10/11 NOW reported. Crews were called Tuesday around 8:40 a.m. to a building near Y Street and North 23rd. Neighbors had seen smoke at the eaves. The fire stayed in the kitchen of one unit and caused about $100,000 in damage. The cause is undetermined. The owner was not home.

Frosty was breathing shallowly and had a weak pulse. Spokeswoman MJ Lierman said firefighters gave her oxygen through a pet mask that had been donated to the department. She regained enough strength to walk into the veterinary clinic on her own. People in the other apartments were evacuated. The damaged unit is uninhabitable, so Frosty and the three people who live there still need another place to stay. By Wednesday morning she was back with her family and had only a slight cough. The save is the dog. The family is not back in the apartment.
