A pygmy hippo was born on November 4 at Khao Kheow Open Zoo in Chonburi, and the calf makes Moo Deng an aunt. Thai PBS reported the birth on November 5, 2026, after Natural Resources and Environment Minister Suchart Chomklin announced it. The mother is Moo Manow, 14. The father is Moo Tun, 7, an older brother of Moo Deng.

Zoo director Narongwit Chodchoi told the minister that veterinarians checked mother and calf and found both in good health. The calf was already moving and exploring, with Moo Manow staying beside it. The zoo has left them together. Moo Manow is guarding the calf and keeping staff at a distance, which the report described as ordinary maternal behavior. A public introduction is planned. Thai PBS did not give the calf's name or sex.

Moo Tun and Moo Manow had a marriage ceremony on January 24, 2025. Princess Ubolratana joined the procession and signed as a witness. The news is this calf. It is not a new count of pygmy hippos in the wild.
