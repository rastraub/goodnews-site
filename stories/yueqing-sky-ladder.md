The South China Morning Post reported on November 16, 2026, that five siblings in Yannan Village, Yueqing, in Zhejiang province, built a mountain path of 430 steps and 11 turns so their 86-year-old mother could get home. The paper, citing Chao News, said Chinese social media nicknamed the trail a "filial piety sky ladder." The mother's name was not published. She has three sons and two daughters.

Huang, one of the daughters, told the story this way: their mother held their hands down the mountain when they were children, and now it is the children's turn to bring her home. The report says the path has been widely shared because it is a practical fix for one steep walk, not a public works project. It does not say the old route is gone, or that every older neighbor on the mountain has a new path. The checked facts are the 430 steps, the 11 turns, the village, and a family that built them by hand.
