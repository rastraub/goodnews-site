The nonprofit Saving A Place For You opened a transitional home on South Saulsbury Road in Dover. Veterans can stay for up to a year while they connect with services. The nonprofit is waiving application fees. It will host a Friendsgiving open house on Nov. 8.

The stay has an end date on purpose. A year is time to line up the next place, not a permanent assignment of the rooms.
