Seoul Grand Park put its first naturally bred red panda twins on view on October 14. Haps Magazine reported on October 12, 2026, that Maru and Marong, born June 19, had started exploring beyond their indoor room under the care of their mother, Lian.

Their father, Ravi, arrived from Calgary Zoo in Canada through a species-survival plan. Lian came from Tama Zoological Park in Japan. The parents reached the park in 2023 through those exchanges. A public naming campaign chose the cubs' names: Marong, the female, with a hope that she would be warm and generous, and Maru, the male, with a sense of being precious. Both weigh more than a kilogram, have had their vaccinations, and have pine chips and climbing structures in the habitat.

The park said the twins are less active in the middle of the day, so a visit is more likely to catch them early or after 4 p.m. Viewing will widen only as keepers watch how the cubs handle it. A first public introduction is not a release, and the magazine does not claim one.
