Four capybara pups were born overnight on October 15 to Pepita at Shepreth Wildlife Park in Cambridgeshire. BBC News reported the births on October 23, 2026.

Litters are typically four to eight, so this one sits at the small end of the usual range. The park said that once the pups' sexes are known, the public will help name them. Youngsters in the Shepreth capybara family are traditionally given names drawn from Latin American foods. Those names are not chosen yet.

This is a separate birth from the eight pups reported the same month at Curraghs Wildlife Park on the Isle of Man. The parks, the dates, and the numbers are different.
