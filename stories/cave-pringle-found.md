Steven Pringle, 72, was found on November 25 after a night in the open near Timaru. RNZ reported that he had left for an hour-long bike ride about 11 a.m. on Tuesday and was reported missing that afternoon. His e-bike turned up on Monavale Road, in the Albury area near the town of Cave.

Inspector Matt Scoles said Pringle was in remarkably good health given the night outdoors, and that medical staff were checking him. Scoles thanked Land Search and Rescue volunteers, neighbors, and Pringle's family, many of whom searched from first light. Staff from Temuka, Geraldine, St Andrews, and Timaru Search and Rescue worked with police officers. The news is this find. RNZ did not describe an injury, and it did not say how far he had walked after leaving the bike.
