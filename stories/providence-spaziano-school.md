Providence opened the new upper campus of the Spaziano PK-8 School. The $34.5 million building is for 312 middle schoolers and includes STEAM learning centers, a modern media center, and classrooms set aside for special education. The opening completes a campus that cost $78.5 million in all.

The ribbon cutting was for rooms the students will use, not a plan still on paper.
