East Baton Rouge Parish started free on-demand rides Monday for older residents who need to reach a geriatric clinic, WBRZ reported. Lotus Care Transit comes from the East Baton Rouge Council on Aging, CareSouth, and the Capital Area Transit System. Riders have to be 60 or older, live in the parish, and be homebound or living with a disability. The trip starts at home and ends at the CareSouth Geriatric Clinic at Lotus Village.

People sign up through CATS On Demand. The launch is the first day of that door-to-clinic ride. It is for residents who qualify, not a claim that every senior in the parish already has a seat.
