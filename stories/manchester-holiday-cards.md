Holiday Cards for our Military Challenge will deliver its 2 millionth handwritten holiday card this Christmas, WMUR reported. The New Hampshire effort began as a family tradition. Card number 2,000,000 is going to 25-year-old Navy sailor Alex Gannon.

The cards are handwritten. Two million is the number this one completes. Gannon is the sailor who will open that card. The family tradition is how the challenge started.
