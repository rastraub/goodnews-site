Students at the UNT Dallas College of Law took over when associate professor Mike Maslanka collapsed from ventricular tachycardia during a lecture, NBC 5 Dallas-Fort Worth reported. They split into teams. One team, including a Navy SEAL veteran, gave CPR. Another called 911. Maslanka is back teaching. That return to class is as far as the account goes. It does not describe a longer medical recovery.
