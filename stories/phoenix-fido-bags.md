The Fetch Foundation, based in Phoenix, gives fire departments free Fido Bags. Each kit includes reusable pet oxygen masks, burn-care supplies, and bags for carrying animals. Departments around the country can request them.

Phoenix crews used the kits this week after they pulled three dogs and three cats from a house fire. The bags are for treating pets at the scene. They are not a substitute for a veterinarian once the animals are out.
