Newark's Military Park Carousel reopened on October 23, and rides are free through the end of 2026, Patch reported. The Newark City Parks Foundation is covering the cost. The carousel runs with an attendant Thursdays and Fridays from 3 to 8 p.m. and Saturdays and Sundays from noon to 8 p.m. at 51 Park Place.

The ride was designed for Military Park, built by Carousels and Carvings of Marion, Ohio, with engineering donated by Thornton Tomasetti, and given to the city by Newark native Ray Chambers. Its 16 horses each pay tribute to a horse tied to Newark's history, and the carousel appears in the park's logo. City officials said it was meant to let children meet that history through play, not only as an amusement. Foundation executive director Marcy DePina said the horses are ready and the music is coming back.
