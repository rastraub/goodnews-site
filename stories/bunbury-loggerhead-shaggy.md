A juvenile loggerhead is recovering in Bunbury after a volunteer drove nearly 700 kilometres from Esperance. ABC News included the rescue in its South West and Great Southern briefing on November 13, 2026. A local boater spotted the turtle floating in seaweed. It is nicknamed Shaggy for the thick green algae on its shell. It is the second tropical loggerhead to wash up at Esperance in recent times, and it is expected to stay in care until April next year.

Dolphin Discovery Centre representative Emily Ehlers asked people in Esperance to watch for stranded turtles among seaweed and to contact the centre or the Esperance Wildlife Hospital if they find one. The briefing gives the nickname, the drive, and the expected stay. It does not describe the turtle's injuries or say when it might be released beyond that April window.
