Aidan Apperley, an English teacher at CAST STEM High School, was surprised with the KENS 5 EXCEL Award during a visit to the Southwest Independent School District campus at Palo Alto College, the station reported. The award came with a $2,000 check from Credit Human. The surprise brought him to tears, especially when he learned about the check.

Besides teaching English, Apperley is a team lead, head coach of the Academic Decathlon team, and an ACE Mentoring champion. Students told the station the recognition was deserved. He said he had not always pictured himself as a teacher, and that one of his own English teachers saw that he might become one. His goal, he said, is to understand what students need as individuals. He also told them it is all right to make mistakes and to stumble. He plans to put the $2,000 toward home renovations already underway. The award is this recognition and the check. It is not a ranking of every teacher in the district.
