A large tree fell onto an occupied house in the 10600 block of Lombardy Road on Thanksgiving morning, off Lockridge Drive near University Boulevard and Northwood Park in Silver Spring, WJLA reported. Montgomery County Fire and Rescue found a single-family home with major structural damage.

Two adult residents were still inside. Firefighters helped them out through a window and down a ladder. Department spokesperson Pete Piringer said neither person was injured. A building inspector was notified, and the two adults were displaced because of the damage.

The morning the department described is a rescue with no injuries: both people got out of the house.
