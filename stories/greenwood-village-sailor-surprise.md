Navy Lt. Cmdr. Kevin Vassighi surprised his son at lunch on Friday at Campus Middle School in Greenwood Village, CBS Colorado reported. Luke Vassighi is a sixth grader. His father had been deployed for 11 months and had been back in Colorado for two days. When Luke saw him, he did not think it was real. Vassighi picked him up in a bear hug.

They planned to get a burger and spend the rest of the day together. Vassighi credited Luke's mother with caring for Luke and his brother during the deployment. He said it had been nearly 11 months since he had seen them, and that he was glad to be home.
