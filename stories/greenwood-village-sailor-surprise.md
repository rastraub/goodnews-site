Navy Lt. Cmdr. Kevin Vassighi had been away for nearly 11 months when he walked into lunch at Campus Middle School in Greenwood Village, CBS Colorado reported. His son Luke, a sixth grader, stared and said it was not him. Then Luke closed the distance with a bear hug.

Vassighi had been home for about two days before the Friday surprise, and the visit included Luke's brother as well. He credited Luke's mother with keeping the family going while he was gone. The moment in the cafeteria was the part Luke will tell.
