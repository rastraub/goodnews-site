Taiwan's Public Television Service reported on November 13, 2026, that Shoushan Zoo in Kaohsiung put a Bengal tiger cub on public view starting November 12. The zoo calls him Xiao Hu. He was born at the end of 2025 to Er Niu and A Sheng, and the station said it is the first Bengal tiger birth in the more than 20 years since the zoo moved to Shoushan. The zoo keeps three adult tigers. The report put the wild population under 4,000.

His mother did not produce enough milk at first, so veterinarians from Leofoo Village and the keepers hand-reared him. Director Zhuang Xuan-zhi said staff were rostered around the clock until weaning, then shifted the diet from meat paste to chunks. After nearly a year of that care the zoo judged him healthy enough to meet visitors. Because he is curious and active, the first days are half days so he is not exhausted. A visitor told the station the cub scratches at things the way a house cat does. The debut is this one cub. It is not a change in the wild count.
