Pamlico 911 took a call at 8:48 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 13, for a man with chest pain, the Pamlico County Sheriff's Office told WCTI. By 8:53 a.m. he had collapsed. At 8:57 a.m. people there started CPR, and dispatchers told the responding crews that compressions were already underway.

When Pamlico Rescue arrived, the crew delivered a shock with a defibrillator and revived him. The sheriff's office said he is expected to recover. Officials credited the early call, the bystanders who started CPR, the dispatchers who relayed what was happening, and the rescue crew. The report does not name the man or the town where the call came from.
