West Memphis cut the ribbon on the Carolyn and Ness Sechrest Animal Shelter and Adoption Center, Action News 5 reported. The project cost $2.3 million, and Southland Casino Hotel contributed $1.7 million.

The shelter stands on the site of the former Mid-South Greyhound Adoption Center. The dollar figures are the building budget and that gift. The ribbon-cutting is the opening, not a count of animals already adopted there.
