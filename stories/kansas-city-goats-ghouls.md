Port KC threw a first Goats and Ghouls party so visitors could meet the goats clearing brush along the Riverfront Heritage Trail, Axios Kansas City reported. The Goats Gone Green herd is eating about two acres of invasive plants. They do it without chemical sprays. Wildflowers for pollinators are planned next.

The party is a chance to see the animals doing the mowing. The work itself is slower: goats on the trail, eating what people would otherwise spray, and a next step of flowers for bees and other pollinators once the brush is down.
