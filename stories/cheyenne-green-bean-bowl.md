Cheyenne high schools turned a rivalry into Thanksgiving groceries, Wyoming News Now reported. The Great Green Bean Bowl, a canned-food contest, collected enough to feed 550 families.

That figure is the meals the drive can cover. It is one competition in the capital city, not a measure of every household that will need help in November.
