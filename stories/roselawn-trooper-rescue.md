Sunday afternoon, off-duty Indiana State Police troopers Israel and Jordin Rosillo pulled a pregnant woman from a rollover on Interstate 65 near the Roselawn exit, The Times of Northwest Indiana reported. Police said one vehicle rolled, and the driver was pinned inside while it smoked and sat on the verge of catching fire. The Rosillos were traveling the interstate when they saw the crash. They climbed into the vehicle and got her out before it caught fire or the smoke overcame her. She was taken to Franciscan Health Hospital in Crown Point. The Times said police had not released how the crash happened, or how she was doing after she reached the hospital.
