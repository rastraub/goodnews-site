Volunteers with Quilts of Valor presented handmade quilts at Yakima's Veterans Stand Down, the Yakima Herald-Republic reported. The gathering at the SunDome was the 27th annual stand down in the valley.

The quilts were given at that event by the local group. The report is this year's presentations, not a count of every veteran who has waited for one.
