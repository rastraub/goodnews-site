A 911 call reached Shelby Township police and fire dispatch about 3:56 a.m. Monday, from a condo near 22 Mile Road and M-53 in Macomb County, CBS Detroit reported. Officer Tyler Nikkel, who has been with the department for three years, was among the first people there. He stepped into the open garage. A woman said her husband and their son were still inside, and she pointed the way through heavy smoke. Officers brought her outside. Someone on the radio said two people were in the house.

Nikkel started in, and the smoke drove him back out. He went in a second time, on his hands and knees. On the body camera he called, "Where are you?" and told them to come straight to him. He followed coughing and a child's cry, found the disoriented father holding the 4-year-old, and walked both of them out through the garage. "It felt good that they were still conscious, breathing, and being able to get out was a lot of relief," he said. Police have not said how the man and boy were afterward, or what started the fire. Nikkel was taken to a hospital to be checked and treated for smoke inhalation. Chief Robert Shelide said he could not be more proud of what the officer did that morning.
