A 60-year-old hunter who went missing Nov. 8 near Prospect was found safe on Nov. 11, KDRV reported. Fellow hunters located him at 10:26 a.m. on the 900 Road near County Line Road, outside town. He had been out for more than 72 hours in cold weather and was in good condition.

Before that, a wide search was already underway. Jackson County Sheriff's Office Search and Rescue used people on the ground, K9 teams, airplanes, and a helicopter. Deputies and search teams widened the area and asked the California Oregon Regional Search and Rescue task force for help. More than 50 search-and-rescue personnel and about 80 community volunteers took part. Teams also came from the Josephine, Klamath, Del Norte, and Siskiyou county sheriff's offices.

He was taken to a nearby search checkpoint and reunited with family and friends. KDRV did not publish his name. Searchers reminded people who head into the wilderness to tell someone the plan, to carry extra food, water, and warm clothes, and to stop and call 911 if they realize they are lost.
