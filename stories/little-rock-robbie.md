Linda Satter noticed a cat on the Mississippi Avenue overpass in Little Rock, FOX16 reported. Firefighters tried to reach her. Friend Gary Iverson then reached into a drain opening and pulled the cat out.

Satter adopted her and named her Robbie. The rescue was a handoff from a failed first try to the grab that worked, and the adoption is the home that followed.
