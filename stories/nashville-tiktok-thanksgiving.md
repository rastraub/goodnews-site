Chibi Emejue is in Nashville, far from his family, so he asked TikTok to adopt him for Thanksgiving. The video passed 120,000 views and drew more than 600 comments, many of them invitations to a table. He set out to visit about a dozen homes, including the Duarte family in Franklin. Adopt, in the post, was a seat and a plate, not a courtroom. The dozen stops are how one request turned into a route of living rooms that decided a stranger could sit down.
