Green Bay House's sixth annual tailgate raffle raised more than $70,000, WBAY reported. The money benefits Sierra Delta, which helps veterans train their own dogs.

The event was a tailgate. It was the sixth year. More than $70,000 is the amount raised. Sierra Delta is the group that receives the funds, and Green Bay House hosted the raffle. The dogs in the program are dogs the veterans train themselves.
