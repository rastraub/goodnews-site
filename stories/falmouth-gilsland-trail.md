Maine Audubon opened a wheelchair-friendly trail at Gilsland Farm in Falmouth. It runs more than 2,000 feet, through forest, and ends at an overlook on the estuary.

The path is new, and it is built so someone using a wheelchair can get from the woods to that view without being turned around by the trail itself.
