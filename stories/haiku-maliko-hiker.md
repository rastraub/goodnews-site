Firefighters on Maui brought a lost hiker out of rough ground near Maliko Gulch on Monday evening, Maui Now reported. The call, from third-party reports and text messages to 911, came in about 6:21 p.m. on Nov. 16. It described a visitor who was lost and had a leg injury on a trail off the Hāna Highway near mile 10.

Engine 2 found the 55-year-old and started medical care. Firefighters from Rescue 10 rigged a rope system and moved him over uneven ground that was slick from rain. They carried him to the road and turned him over to emergency medical services for the trip to Maui Memorial Medical Center. He was in stable condition. The crews were done around 9 p.m. The report did not give his name.
