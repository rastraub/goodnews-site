Singapore's National Library Board opened a children's library on the third floor of Plaza Singapura, a downtown mall, with close to 10,000 books. The Straits Times reported on October 28, 2026, that the self-service room started on October 27 and runs every day from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. through September 19, 2027.

The collection mixes physical and digital children's books, including Peanuts titles, and the room is dressed as that comic world: Charlie Brown's classroom, a school-bus nook, and a Snoopy garden. A booth asks a few questions and prints a bookmark with a suggestion. Chief executive Melissa Tam said the point is to make reading easy to rediscover at any age, as part of the board's ReadSG effort. Entry is an NLB eCard, a library-compatible identity card, or a free QR day pass. Borrowing goes through the library app.

The count is the books in this one pop-up, and the dates it is open. It is not a claim about reading habits across the city.
