Two African penguin chicks hatched at ZooTampa under the Association of Zoos and Aquariums Species Survival Plan. One pair belongs to first-time parents Kai and Tuxie. The chicks bring the colony to 18. A black-naped fruit dove chick hatched at the zoo as well. African penguins are critically endangered, which is why the pairings are planned instead of left to chance. Two chicks do not restore the species. They are two more birds in a colony that now numbers 18, plus a dove chick in the same season.
