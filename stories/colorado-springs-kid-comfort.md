Operation Kid Comfort volunteers in Colorado Springs have sewn nearly 3,000 quilts for children of deployed service members, KRDO reported. The quilts and pillows are free. Each one is custom, printed with photos of the deployed parent, and shipped to military kids anywhere.

Nearly 3,000 is the count so far. The photo on the fabric is the part that stands in for the parent who is away. The shipping is how a child receives it no matter where the family is.
