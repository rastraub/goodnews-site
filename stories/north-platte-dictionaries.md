North Platte Elks completed a 23rd year with the Dictionary Project, delivering 467 dictionaries to third-graders at 16 area schools, KNOP reported. Each student receives a personal copy.

The books are reference tools, with presidents, states, and guides to Braille and sign language alongside the definitions. The count is this year's delivery, and the 23 years are how long the lodge has kept the project going.
