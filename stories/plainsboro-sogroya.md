The FDA approved Sogroya as the first once-weekly growth hormone for children with growth failure linked to Turner syndrome, Healio reported. The approval covers children 2.5 and older. It is based on the phase 3 REAL8 trial, in which weekly dosing matched daily injections. That schedule spares families 313 injection days a year.

Once a week is the new dosing. Daily injections are what the weekly shot matched in the trial. 313 days is the difference in injection days over a year. This is an approval based on that comparison, not a claim about every child's growth.
