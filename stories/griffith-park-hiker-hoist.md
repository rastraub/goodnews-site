Los Angeles firefighters were called at 2:06 p.m. Saturday to the 4700 block of Crystal Springs Drive for what the department described as a hiker incident involving an adult man, ABC7 reported. Park rangers and firefighters found him near the Griffith Observatory.

They treated him where he was and prepared him to fly. A bystander's video showed a helmeted firefighter lowered on a cable from the helicopter, then the hiker lifted into the aircraft. He was flown to a hospital.

The Fire Department's statement did not say whether the man was injured or had a medical problem. ABC7 reported that part as still unclear. City News Service contributed to the station's account.
