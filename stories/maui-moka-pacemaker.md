A Maui family credits neighbors with getting 3-year-old Moka to Washington State University for a pacemaker, Hawaii News Now reported. His heart had been failing. He is back on the beach, and his heart rate is stable.

The pacemaker is the device that steadied this dog's heartbeat. It is not a promise that every dog with heart disease will run again. The news is Moka's trip, the community that made it possible, and the stable rate his family is seeing now.
