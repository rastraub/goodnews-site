Ohavi Zedek Synagogue's fifth annual Northern Nosh Jewish food festival brought Jewish and non-Jewish Vermonters together in Burlington, WCAX reported. Guests shared matzo ball soup and Yiddish music. The festival drew its biggest crowd yet.

It was the fifth year of the event. The synagogue is Ohavi Zedek. The gathering was not limited to Jewish Vermonters. Soup and music were the things the station described people sharing.
