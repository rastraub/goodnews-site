Alumni Joe and Lana Batts gave $1 million to fund graduate students in the University of Montana's ecology and evolution program, which the university says is ranked in the nation's top 15. The fund provides $100,000 a year for a decade, for tuition and stipends. The gift is not a lab with their name on the door. It is ten years of support for the students doing the work, paid out at $100,000 a year.
