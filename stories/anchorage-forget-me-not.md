About 60 people with cognitive impairment and their supporters sang the Forget-me-not Chorus fall concert at the Anchorage Senior Activity Center, Alaska's News Source reported.

About 60 is the group that took the stage and the seats around it. The singers are people living with memory loss. The supporters sing with them. The Senior Activity Center is where the fall concert was held.
