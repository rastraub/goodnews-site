Hundreds of volunteers packed 7,000 Sharing Thanksgiving boxes for God's Pantry Food Bank, WKYT reported on November 7, 2026. The Basket Brigade ran in Lexington and in London. Alex Maddox of the food bank said each box holds the ingredients for a holiday meal and should feed about six to eight people. The boxes go out to families in the coming weeks. Recipients for this round have already been chosen. Maddox said people who still need food and are not on the list can contact the food bank.

Maddox also cited Feeding America figures for the 50 counties the pantry serves: food insecurity there rose a little over 12 percent in the latest data, and more than 314,000 neighbors in central and eastern Kentucky are unsure where their next meal will come from. The news in this story is the 7,000 boxes packed for this holiday program, not a claim that those county numbers have fallen.
