A Little Help is looking for more hands in the Denver area, 9NEWS reported. The nonprofit matches volunteers with older neighbors who need yard work, and this round brought in more than 300 requests, a record for the group. Volunteers have already been raking.

The next call is Service Saturday, November 7. The record is the size of the ask, not a claim that every yard is finished. The group is recruiting so more of those requests can be met.
