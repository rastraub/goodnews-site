A young bald eagle found struggling on the Marginal Way in Ogunquit on Nov. 12 is improving at the Center for Wildlife, WGME reported. The bird is being treated for lead poisoning. The Ogunquit Fire Department said the eagle is getting better.

The rescue was Nov. 12. The update is that the eagle is improving in care, not that it has been released. The treatment is for lead poisoning. The center is the Center for Wildlife.
