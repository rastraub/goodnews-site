At the Tulsa State Fair, Neal McCoy spotted a young fan named Harrison singing every word of "Wink" and pulled him on stage to finish it with him. The song is the one Harrison uses when he walks up to bat.

It was one verse in the middle of a fair concert, and the singer made room for the kid who already knew it.
