Libertyville firefighters pulled a driver from an SUV that crashed into a retention pond on Tuesday morning, CBS Chicago reported. Around 11 a.m., crews responded to the 1200 block of South Milwaukee Avenue and found the vehicle partly underwater.

The Libertyville Fire Department said rescue teams got the driver out safely. The SUV was later towed out of the water. Medical personnel evaluated the driver at the scene. The report does not name the driver, and it does not describe any injuries.
