The Hindu reported on November 14, 2026, that children filled Mysuru Zoo for Children's Day. Children 12 and under could enter free until noon on Friday, and a large number came with teachers and parents. The zoo offers that free morning every year on November 14. The paper said the children spent the visit walking the grounds and looking for favourite animals.

The report does not give a head count, a list of species, or any new arrival. The news is the crowd that used a free morning the zoo already sets aside for children.
