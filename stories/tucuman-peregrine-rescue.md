A peregrine falcon was picked up in Parque 9 de Julio after a call to the 911 emergency line. La Gaceta reported the rescue on November 11, 2026. Officers from the rural and environmental unit for the capital went to the park, identified the bird as a peregrine falcon, and took it into care. They called the Dirección de Flora, Fauna Silvestre y Suelos. That office told them to keep the bird at the unit until wildlife staff could collect it and move ahead with a return to the wild.

The work was done under Tucumán's wildlife law, Ley Provincial N° 6.292, and was supervised by Comisario Mayor Silvio Luna, director general of rural and environmental matters, and subdirector Comisario Mayor Adrián Hisa. The paper does not say whether the falcon was injured, how it came to be in the park, or when it might be released. The reported step is the handoff from a caller to officers and then to the wildlife office.
