Off-duty Lynnfield deputy fire chief Kevin Mutti stopped at an overturned tanker on Route 1 in Danvers, the Lynnfield Weekly News reported. He kicked out the windshield and pulled the driver free. A police officer and a civilian helped.

The rescue was one stop on the way, not a department call he was already assigned to. The report is about getting that driver out of the cab.
