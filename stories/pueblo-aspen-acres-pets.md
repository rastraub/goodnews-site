The Humane Society of the Pikes Peak Region in Pueblo housed more than 420 animals displaced by the Aspen Acres Fire, including at overflow sites when the main shelter could not hold them all, KRDO reported. Nearly every one of those pets was home within 18 days. The shelter marked the work at its Whisker Brunch, a gathering to celebrate the volunteers. The count is more than 420 animals taken in, and the outcome is that nearly all of them were reunited with their people inside that stretch of days.
