The Hindu reported on November 15, 2026, that the Tamil Nadu Forest Department has started its olive ridley season along the Chennai coast before the turtles are expected to nest in December. A Chennai Wildlife Division official said beach and boat patrols began in November under the department's usual procedure. Beach walks of two to three hours are planned three or four times a week from Marina Beach to Neelankarai and Uthandi. The Pulicat range is doing the same on the Pulicat coast, with extra attention on stretches where turtles have nested before.

Crews will mark sensitive beach, clean it, and remove obstacles. The department also plans to train volunteers to recognize nests and to report trouble quickly. The Marine Elite Force, with the Fisheries Department, has started boat patrols to watch trawlers and other boats, because turtles are caught in nets. The news is that the patrols are underway. It is not a nest count. The season has not started.
