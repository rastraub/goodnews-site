A storm dropped a silver maple through 71-year-old Gilbert Kirk's roof in Wyandotte County and left him and his dog, Beetle, trapped under the collapse for hours. Good News Network, citing KCTV5, reported on October 7, 2026, that Kirk told local news he was not asking for a miracle or an insurance payout. He wanted a tarp.

Two days later a stranger said he would be back in half an hour with a tarp, lumber, and a crew. Kirk Mata, Steven Vega, Dominic Foreman, and Brigette Henson covered the hole. Phil Lopez had already helped pull the tree out of the house. Kirk said fans are drying the inside. The neighbors tarped the roof. They did not rebuild it.
