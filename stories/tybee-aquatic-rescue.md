Tybee Island Fire Rescue is the first agency in Georgia to earn Aquatic Rescue Response Team certification from the U.S. Lifesaving Association, WTOC reported, citing the city. The association approved the application at its biannual board meeting. The certification is for emergency agencies that meet the association's standards for training, equipment, and readiness for open-water rescues.

On the island, the team is firefighters trained to respond to a range of emergencies in open water. The city said that work complements the seasonal Ocean Rescue lifeguards, who watch designated beaches, warn people about hazards, and respond when swimmers need help. Fire Rescue answers water-rescue calls and more complex aquatic emergencies both during that season and outside it. Fire Chief Justin McMillian and Capt. Tadd Rubin worked with partner departments and other certified agencies to standardize the training before the department applied. Mayor Brian West called it an accomplishment for the department and the community. A recognition ceremony is set for 10 a.m. on Thursday, December 3, in the City Hall courtroom.
