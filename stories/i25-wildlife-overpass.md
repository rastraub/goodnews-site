In the 10 months since the Greenland wildlife crossing opened on I-25 near Castle Rock, drivers have hit two animals on a stretch that used to average about one collision a day. That is a 91 percent drop. Pronghorn crossed I-25 on the overpass for the first time in decades.

Six more crossings are being designed. The count is collisions, not a claim that every animal in the corridor now uses the bridge.
