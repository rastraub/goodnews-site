South East Radio reported on November 4, 2026, that Read While You Wait has put children's books into five health waiting rooms in County Wexford. The launch was at the paediatric outpatients department of Wexford General Hospital. HSE Dublin and South East developed the project with Wexford Library Service. The other shelves are at Wexford Primary Care Centre, Gorey Health Centre, New Ross Health Centre, and the Children's Disability Network Centre in Barntown.

Families are invited to read a book together while they wait and to leave it for the next family. Senior health promotion officer Lisa Nangle said reading with children supports language, learning, and wellbeing, and that the shelves turn waiting into time together. Physiotherapist manager Orla Fahy said the books should give children at the hospital something engaging to do. Senior executive librarian Yvonne Smith said the library service selected and supplied the books, and that the project can point families toward the public library as well.

The Wexford rollout follows a pilot in County Waterford and is supported by the Waterford and Wexford Education and Training Board and Adult Literacy for Life. The report names the five sites and the partners. It does not say how many books are on each shelf, or how many families used them on the first day.
