Des Moines University and UnityPoint Health launched a rural health collaborative, KCRG reported. It starts in Webster County, around Fort Dodge. The work targets earlier cancer screening, radon risk reduction, and an online hub of local resources.

The two partners are the university and the health system. Webster County is where it begins. The three pieces named so far are screening, radon, and the resource hub. This is a launch of that effort, not a report of screening results.
