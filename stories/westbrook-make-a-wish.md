Nearly 80 people deadlifted at Iron Legion's ninth annual Operation Change in Westbrook to benefit Make-A-Wish Maine, WMTW reported. The aim was about $100,000, enough to grant ten wishes. Before the first lift, the fund was still a few thousand dollars short of that mark. The event started nearly a decade ago with about 20 lifters and just over $2,000. The shortfall is where things stood at the start of this year's lifting. The report does not say the $100,000 goal had already been met.
