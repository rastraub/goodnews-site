A farmer-led program from the Indiana Corn Marketing Council trained 196 firefighters in 2026 and sent grain-bin rescue tubes to nine rural departments, WISH-TV reported. The equipment and training have already been part of saving three people.

The 196 firefighters are this year's class. Nine is the number of departments that received tubes. Three is the number of lives the program's gear has already helped save, not a forecast of future rescues.
