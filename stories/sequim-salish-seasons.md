The Jamestown S'Klallam Tribe's Jamestown Salish Seasons is the northern Olympic Peninsula's first inpatient mental-health center, The Seattle Times reported. It has 16 voluntary beds in Sequim. Patients no longer have to travel to Puget Sound or beyond for that care.

The beds are voluntary. Sixteen is the number of beds. The tribe runs the center. It is the first inpatient mental-health center on the northern peninsula, which is why patients had been leaving the area for this kind of stay.
