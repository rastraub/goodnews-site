A North American porcupine was born in the Desert habitat at Royal Burgers' Zoo in Arnhem on the night of October 2. Omroep Gelderland reported on October 15, 2026, that the young animal arrived between that Friday night and the early hours of October 3.

Mother and offspring stayed off exhibit for the first weeks. Keepers followed them on camera and weighed the porcupette every day to check that it was getting enough milk. The regional broadcaster said the youngster was growing well. On Wednesday, October 14, the pair moved into an enclosure visitors can see.

The report does not give a name or a birth weight. What it records is a quiet stretch of care before the first public look, rather than a debut on the morning of the birth.
