Two school libraries in Esposende opened on the day set aside for them. O Diário de Portugal reported on October 27, 2026, that the municipality inaugurated renovated rooms at EB de Esposende and at EB do Barral in Palmeira de Faro on October 26, International School Library Day.

The town paid for the spaces to be rebuilt and brought up to date. The paper, drawing on the municipality's account, said school libraries are where students practice curiosity, learn to handle information, and build a habit of reading when screens are everywhere. It thanked the school grouping António Correia de Oliveira, the librarian teachers, other staff, parents, and the parish councils that helped.

No book count or budget figure was included. The reported result is narrower and still concrete: two schools that had unfinished library rooms now have them open for children.
