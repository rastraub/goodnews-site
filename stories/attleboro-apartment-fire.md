Attleboro firefighters searching a Peck Street apartment found a resident who could not get up and carried them out to an ambulance, Boston 25 News reported. The apartment was full of heavy smoke, and carbon monoxide readings were high.

The rescue was that one carry from the unit to the ambulance. The report does not add a later medical update. It is about the crews finding the resident and getting them outside.
