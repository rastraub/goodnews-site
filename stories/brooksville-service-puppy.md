Jose Sanchez, a paramedic and Army veteran in Brooksville, was surprised with a service puppy named Waffles. His wife nominated him after decades of service. Waffles is a donated Belgian Malinois, and the surprise included a $3,500 check. Sanchez did not apply for the dog himself. The nomination came from the person who has watched the years of work up close, and the puppy is the help that arrived without him asking for it in public.
