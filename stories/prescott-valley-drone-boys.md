A drone from the Central Arizona Fire and Medical Authority found two boys with developmental disabilities who had wandered from their Prescott Valley home on Nov. 8, FOX 10 Phoenix reported. One was in a ravine. The other was waist-deep in a creek. Both were unharmed, and both were back with their family in under 30 minutes. The search ended with that reunion. The report does not go on to a longer account of the day.
