The Alaska Turkey Bombers air-dropped 42 Thanksgiving turkeys to remote families, from Alexander Creek out toward the Denali foothills. Pilot Heidi Hastings flew. Esther Keim made the drops. Donations covered the flight, and none of the turkeys were lost. A road does not reach every kitchen in that country. The plane is the grocery run, and the count that matters is simple: 42 birds left the aircraft, and 42 reached the ground.
