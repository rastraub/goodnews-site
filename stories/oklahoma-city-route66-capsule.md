Oklahoma City buried a time capsule at the Kicks 66 sculpture to mark Route 66's centennial, KOCO reported. More than 1,000 postcards went inside, from Oklahoma, from more than 30 states, and from 12 countries. The capsule is set to be opened in 2126. The burial is done. The opening is a century away.
