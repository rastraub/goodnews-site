Students from St. Vrain Valley Schools' student-run Drone Performance Team traveled to Holyoke and staged a custom drone light show for a 9-year-old named Bubba, CBS Colorado reported. Bubba lives with SCN2A, a rare condition, and the students built the show around the lights he loves. The flight was arranged through Make-A-Wish. The team designed the performance and brought it to his town.
