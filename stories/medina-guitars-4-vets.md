Veterans in Medina County who finish ten free lessons through Guitars 4 Vets receive an acoustic guitar to keep, WOSU Public Media reported. About 40 veterans have taken part locally. They are planning a holiday performance at a nursing home. The guitar is the reward for finishing the ten lessons. The nursing-home show is still ahead, not a concert that has already been played.
