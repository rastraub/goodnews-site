A 10-year-old who had trained by biking the block with her cousins finished her first kids' triathlon in Manukau. RNZ reported on November 27, 2026, that Olivia O'Donnell, of Ngāpuhi, went to Mountfort Park on Tuesday morning with her mother, her aunt, and her cousins. She told RNZ Pacific there was no pressure to win, and that she saw friends at the start. Preparation had been riding around the block and running at home.

The Weet-Bix Kids TRYathlon is described as the country's largest triathlon series for children, aimed at ages 6 to 15 and set up as a first try rather than a cutthroat race. Dame Valerie Adams, the Tongan shot-put champion who grew up in South Auckland, is an ambassador this year after handing out finishers' medals for years. She told RNZ there were 18,000 children in the scale of the event, and she compared that crowd with competing at the Olympics. The 18,000 is her description to RNZ. The article did not cite a separate official tally.

Adams said the day was about resilience and about families, teachers, and schools standing with the children. The news is one race morning in Manukau and a champion on the finish chute. It is not a claim that every child in Auckland took part.
