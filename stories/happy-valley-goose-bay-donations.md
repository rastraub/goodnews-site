The animal shelter in Happy Valley-Goose Bay opened its holiday fundraiser with gifts large enough that the staff reread the emails. CBC News reported on November 25, 2026, that the SPCA has taken in more than $65,000 Canadian over the past month. One anonymous donation was $30,000. Another was $10,000.

Manager Sarah Bond said she kept staring at the $30,000 message to decide whether it was real. Employee Jonathan Brown said his first feeling was disbelief that someone would do it, and then thanks. Bond said the shelter has paid off a veterinary bill of about $15,000. The rest is meant to carry the shelter through the winter, including building work and more expensive pet food. More than 280 animals have come through this year. Brown said the fundraiser continues, and that smaller gifts still matter. The news is money already in hand for animals already in the building. It is not a new shelter.
