Denver Public Schools and its Community Hubs packed a truck with winter supplies for Durango School District 9-R families, CBS Colorado reported. The load included coats, bedding, diapers, and other essentials. The families are recovering from the historic floods in southwest Colorado.

The truck is the link between the two districts. Denver packed it. Durango's school families are the ones receiving it. The floods are why the supplies were gathered.
