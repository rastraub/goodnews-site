A pickup was in South Holston Lake on Monday night, and the driver was not in it when fire crews arrived, WCYB reported from Green Spring Volunteer Fire & Rescue. The call came in around 7:32 p.m. near Sportsman's Marina and Barefoot Lane. Finding the truck empty turned the response into a search of the water and the shore.

About 25 first responders took part. They used boats, a rescue swimmer, and a drone that could see heat. They found the driver and treated that person at the scene. The report did not give the driver's name, and it did not say how the pickup got into the lake.
