Eight children had their adoptions finalized on Michigan Adoption Day at the Third Circuit Court, CBS News Detroit reported. Judge Karen Braxton made them official on Friday. The cases included a guardianship adoption, infant adoptions, and a stepparent adoption. Braxton said each timeline is different and that the average is about two years from start to finish. Loretta Duncan-Brantley, who adopted her son Silas after being his guardian for well over a year, said, "It's so worth it, I would do it all over again." The eight adoptions are the ones celebrated that day.
