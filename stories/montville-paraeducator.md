Lisa Atkinson, a paraeducator at Leonard J. Tyl Middle School, was named Connecticut's 2027 Paraeducator of the Year, NBC Connecticut reported on October 20, 2026. Lt. Gov. Susan Bysiewicz and Education Commissioner Charlene Russell-Tucker announced the award on Tuesday.

Atkinson has worked for Montville Public Schools for 21 years. She started in 2005 at Mohegan Elementary, later worked at Oakdale Elementary, and moved to Tyl in 2015. There she works with speech-language pathologists and physical therapists so students can communicate and get around the building. She also takes part in Tyl Buddies, which pairs students with and without disabilities. Gov. Ned Lamont's statement praised two decades of helping Montville students feel included and able to succeed. The recognition is for that daily work: communication, mobility, and friendships that the buddy program is built to start.
