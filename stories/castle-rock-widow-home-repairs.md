Volunteers with Rebuilding Together Colorado and Lowe's of Castle Rock spent Veterans Day on Ann Giovanazzi's house in Douglas County, The Denver Gazette reported. Giovanazzi, nearly 88 and the widow of a Navy veteran, has lived there for 20 years. The crew painted the house and fence, installed comfort-height toilets and grab bars, and replaced the refrigerator. The repairs are meant to let her stay safely in that home.
