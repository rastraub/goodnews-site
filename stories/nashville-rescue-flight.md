Race for Life Rescue's 200th mission flew 112 dogs and cats from overcrowded Louisiana shelters to partner groups in the Northeast, Hoodline reported. The animals rode a converted Beechcraft, with the flight tied to the Nashville rescue.

The nonprofit has now flown more than 12,000 animals. The 112 count is this mission. Two hundred is the mission number, and 12,000-plus is the total across the flights so far.
