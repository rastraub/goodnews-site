Edinburgh Zoo has lion cubs again, for the first time since 2019. STV News reported on October 22, 2026, that two Asiatic lions were born on October 11 to Bindee, a first-time mother of about 10, and Jay, also called Jayendra, a 15-year-old male that keepers describe as calm. Alison MacLean of the Royal Zoological Society of Scotland said staff are watching the family by camera. The cubs will stay off exhibit for several months, and the habitat is closed.

The zoo's previous cubs, Mitaali, Keshari, and Kushanu, were born in September 2019. Asiatic lions are endangered. About 350 remain in the wild, all in Gujarat's Gir Forest. They are smaller than African lions, and males grow shorter manes. Two cubs in Scotland add to a zoo population. They do not add lions to Gir.
