Mt. Carmel Veterans Service Center, MedRide, and partners spent weeks arranging a donated car for Marine veteran Thurman "Hank" Jones Jr. in Colorado Springs, KRDO reported. Jones, who has been living on the streets, said the car will get him to doctor appointments and to apartment viewings with his dog, Dottie. He said the gift makes him feel worthy.

The car is the result of that multi-week effort. Appointments and apartment viewings are what he said he can reach now. Dottie is the dog who will ride with him.
