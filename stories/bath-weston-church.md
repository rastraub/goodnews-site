Neighbors have opened an emergency appeal to buy Weston Methodist Church in Bath, the BBC reported on November 20, 2026. Newbridge Community Hub, a not-for-profit run by local residents, hopes to raise £600,000. The church went up for sale in August. Chair Peter Spraggs said it is a community resource they want to keep after a sale. The building houses Peter Pan Pre-School and adult wellbeing activities. The hub has raised £24,000 and has had the building listed as an asset of community value through Bath and North East Somerset Council.

The Rev Dr Andrew Goodhead, superintendent of the North East Somerset and Bath Methodist Circuit, said a sale would support plans at Walcot Methodist Church in central Bath. He said the circuit, as a charity, has to seek the best value and will look at offers alongside what buyers plan to do with the building. Offers will be considered before a decision on December 9. The news is this appeal and the £24,000 already in. The building has not been bought.
