A male kangaroo was in the water about three kilometers offshore in Boston Bay, near Port Lincoln. ABC reported on October 21, 2026, that diver and farmer Jake Murray, skipper Ryan Smith, and crewmate Miguel Villarroel, a diver from the Galápagos, lowered a sea door and lifted the animal aboard. Murray said it was the first kangaroo Villarroel had seen. Murray cradled it. The kangaroo was exhausted and waterlogged.

Murray did not know how it had reached open water. He guessed it might have been caught by the tide. On the beach it rested, then hopped away. The report is that rescue and that departure. It does not identify the kangaroo's species beyond the crew's account, and it does not follow the animal after it left the shore.
