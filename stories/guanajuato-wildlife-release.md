Nine wild animals are back in a protected area in Guanajuato, and two owls are not, because they are still being treated. El Universal reported on November 26, 2026, that Profepa, led by Marina Boy Tamborrell, released the nine with Celaya's wildlife assistance centre and the state secretariat of water and environment. The agency's list was three casquito turtles, two caracaras, one false coral snake, one alicante snake, one opossum, and one barn owl.

The paper said two other owls remain under veterinary care, and that Profepa will follow their condition before deciding whether a later release is realistic. Farther down, the article added a general explanation of sea turtles and the threats they face. That passage does not match the nine animals named for this release, which are the casquito turtles, birds, snakes, and opossum above. The news is those nine back in a protected area, and two owls still in treatment. It does not name the protected area, and it does not say how long the nine had been in care.
