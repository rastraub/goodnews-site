Bruce Springsteen and Patti Scialfa funded an inspirational wall at Monmouth Medical Center in Long Branch, Jersey Shore Online reported. The Path to Wellness wall is at the hospital's Center for Behavioral Wellness. It is meant to welcome mental-health patients.

The couple funded the wall, and the hospital unveiled it with them. The wall is a welcome for patients at that center. This story does not describe anyone's treatment.
