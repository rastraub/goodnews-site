Decatur County tried a pop-up sensory zone at a trunk-or-treat in Bainbridge, WALB reported. It was the county's first. The space offered headphones, fidgets, a calm play area, and a picture communication board for children who needed a quieter way through the event.

The zone was built for that night. The station's account is what was on offer, not a count of how many children used each item.
