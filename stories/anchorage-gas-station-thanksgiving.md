Insook Baik knows what it is like to be hungry. For about 20 years she has answered that memory by feeding hundreds of people, free, every Thanksgiving at her Shell station in Anchorage's Mountain View neighborhood. She buys 42 hams and 42 turkeys. Church friends, longtime staff, and her son help her serve the homemade meals. The pumps are still a gas station. The tradition is the meal. On this day the food is the business that matters, and the guest list is whoever needs a plate.
