The Carter G. Woodson Home in Shaw has reopened after a full restoration, Washingtonian reported. Visits to the national historic site in Washington are free.

The reopening is the end of that repair work, not a new chapter of Woodson's writing. The house is open again for people who want to walk through the place where he lived.
