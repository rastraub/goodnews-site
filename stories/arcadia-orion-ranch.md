Special Olympics Kansas athletes spent Tuesday with rescued horses at Orion Ranch Sanctuary's second annual Boo Bash, KOAM reported. The Arcadia gathering mixed a dance party, lunch, and games with time in the barn and the pasture. Organizers said the horses have a calming effect on some of the athletes.

Olivia Ryan, southeast region director for Special Olympics Kansas, said the point was comfort: some athletes already love horses and have never been close to one. Athlete Lincoln Laverack said he grew up in the country and was glad to be around horses again. Founder Meghan Holland said the sanctuary cares for nearly 50 rescued horses and that the animals respond to people immediately. She described the time together as good for the horses and for the people who come to see them.
