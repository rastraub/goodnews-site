Jaylin Williams of the Oklahoma City Thunder gave 200 families Thanksgiving dinner, the fourth year he and his own family have hosted the meal. The guests were client families of Restore OKC. The dinner was at The Market at Eastpoint. Four years is long enough for a one-time gesture to become a date people expect. Two hundred families is the size of the room this year.
