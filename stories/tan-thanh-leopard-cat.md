A leopard cat that spent ten days in official hands is back in protected forest. DTiNews reported on November 26, 2026, that Tan Thanh commune police, working with the commune people's committee, the Ham Thuan Nam Forest Protection Department, and the management board of Ta Kou Nature Reserve, released the cat that day in sub-zone 300, forest the reserve manages. The household in Thuan Quy village had been losing chickens and set a trap. On November 16 they found an animal with pale fur and black spots in it, and they handed it to the commune police.

Forest officers identified it as a leopard cat, Prionailurus bengalensis, weighing about 4 kilograms. At the release, DTiNews said, it showed no odd behavior, moved quickly, and took to the surroundings. The paper's account is the handover and the release. It does not say whether the cat was treated by a vet in the ten days between the trap and the forest, or whether anyone will check on it again.
