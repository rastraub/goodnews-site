Arizona Animal Welfare League trained a first cohort of about 20 community health workers, known as promotoras, KJZZ reported. They finished a Spanish-language pet-care curriculum covering vaccines, behavior, and safety.

The workers can take those answers to Latino pet owners in Maryvale, near the shelter. About 20 is the size of this first class. The curriculum is what they are prepared to explain, not a tally of appointments already completed.
