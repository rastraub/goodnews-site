Brattleboro firefighters were called just after 3 p.m. Friday to 51 Linden Street, an occupied nine-unit apartment building, the Keene Sentinel reported from the department's account. One person jumped from a third-floor window before crews arrived. Firefighters searched the building, found another person on the first floor, and brought that person out.

Both people were taken by ambulance to Brattleboro Memorial Hospital. The department said they were hurt and did not release their names. Firefighters also carried several pets out and gave lifesaving care to one of them. Fire departments from the area and Cheshire EMS helped at the scene and covered the town's two stations while the crews were out.
