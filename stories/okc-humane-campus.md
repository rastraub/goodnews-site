Oklahoma Humane Society has started construction on a $40 million campus in southwest Oklahoma City, KOCO reported. The plan is 53,000 square feet, with an adoption center, a high-volume clinic, low-cost veterinary care, education programs, and the organization's first overnight housing for dogs.

Opening is planned for spring 2028. The groundbreaking is the start of construction. The building is not open, and the $40 million and 53,000 square feet describe the project being built.
