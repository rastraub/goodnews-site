Two loggerhead sea turtles, Triangle and Acute, went back into the Gulf at Dauphin Island. Fishermen had hooked them by accident. They spent months recovering at the Mississippi Aquarium, then neighbors cheered as they were released. The names stuck while they were in care. The water does not need the names. It needed them well enough to swim, which is what the months in the aquarium were for.
