Columbia Heights students are learning DJing and music production through Save The Music, WJLA reported. A Save The Music grant and Words Beats & Life give them hands-on training in beat making, DJing, and live sound.

The neighborhood is Columbia Heights in Washington. The grant is what brought the program in. The three skills are beats, DJing, and live sound, practiced on the equipment rather than only described.
