Te Kākahu, Kaikohe's new library and community hub, opened on November 9 after 15 years of planning. RNZ reported that the $14.8 million building, at the corner of Broadway and Raihara Street, was blessed at 5:30 a.m. and opened for business at 2 p.m. It holds a library, a council service centre, a creative space, meeting rooms, a gaming room, a podcast studio, and a cafe. The making room has a 3D printer, a laser cutter, a T-shirt and screen printer, a sewing machine, and overlockers.

School groups were among the first through the doors after the dawn blessing. The former library at Marino Place closed at the end of October, and the council is still deciding what to do with that building. RNZ said the hope is that the new hub will give young people a safe place to learn and make things, and bring more people onto the main street. The news is the opening itself. It does not say how many books moved across town.
