Pupils at Ysgol Cemaes on Anglesey spoke by video with NASA astronaut Frank Rubio, who is set to fly on Artemis III. BBC News reported the meeting on October 21, 2026.

Rubio spent 371 days on the International Space Station after a fault in the spacecraft that was meant to bring the crew home. The stay was supposed to be about six months. Pupils asked him about living in space. Earlier this year the school designed a badge that flew to the station and was seen floating there. The chance came through former pupil Dr Melanie Coathup. Acting head Guto Rhys described what the call meant for the school. The pupils hope to send another badge next year. That second flight is still a plan.
