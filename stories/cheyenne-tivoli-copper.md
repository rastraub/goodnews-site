Cheyenne's 1892 Tivoli is getting its copper crown back after hail damage in 2025, Cowboy State Daily reported. Coppersmith Tillman John Nelson will hand-hammer 36 copper profiles to restore the Victorian landmark's facade. The work is a job of about $660,000.

The hail year is 2025. Thirty-six is the number of copper pieces. About $660,000 is the cost. Hand-hammering is how Nelson will shape them for the front of the building.
