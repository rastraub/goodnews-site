Cheff Therapeutic Riding Center in Augusta cut the ribbon on the Suzanne U.D. Parish Therapy Arena, MLive reported. The center has served southwest Michigan since 1969. The new arena is for one-on-one sessions, so riders can work with mental health specialists and physical or occupational therapists away from a crowd. The opening is the new private space. It is a place for those quieter sessions, not a claim about a medical outcome.
