Spy Coast Farm and Stable Recovery are widening a program that puts women in addiction recovery to work with horses, WKYT reported. The course runs 90 days and teaches horsemanship aimed at jobs in the equine industry around Lexington.

The expansion is more room in a job-training program, not a claim about recovery for every participant. The station's account is the partnership and the length of the course: three months of skills pointed at work with horses.
