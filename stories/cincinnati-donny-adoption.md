Donny, the dog who had waited longest at Cincinnati Animal CARE, has been adopted after 426 days, WLWT reported. A Good Samaritan paid the adoption fee. A crate and a collar were donated, along with $200 for training. The shelter posted video of him heading out.

He had been one of hundreds of dogs at a shelter the station said was at critical capacity. The day that matters is the one he left with a family, a crate, a collar, and money set aside for training.
