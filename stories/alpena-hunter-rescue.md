Michigan Department of Natural Resources conservation officers led the search for an 83-year-old Alpena County hunter who had been missing since Monday, the department said. He was found around 2:30 p.m. Wednesday, conscious, sitting up, and alert, about three-quarters of a mile from his hunting property near Wessel Road and Kirchoff Road in Alpena Township. A ground team heard him calling.

Searchers started Monday night in thick woods, with canine units, aircraft, and thermal imaging. The search stopped at sunset Tuesday and started again Wednesday morning. After he was found, crews cut a path through the trees and carried him out on a stretcher. An ambulance took him to a hospital for evaluation. The department did not name him and said his family asked for privacy.

Brandon Kieft, chief of the DNR Law Enforcement Division, said he was grateful for the outcome and thanked the agencies and community volunteers who searched together. Groups that assisted included the Michigan State Police, the Alpena County Sheriff's Office, the Alpena city and township fire departments, and search-and-rescue teams.
