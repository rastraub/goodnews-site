Hard-of-hearing birder Nick Parker organized American Sign Language-interpreted bird walks at North Branch Nature Center in Montpelier, VTDigger reported. The walks use the center's Universal Access Trail. Organizers called it Vermont's first Birdability walk.

The walks are meant to open birding to deaf and disabled birders. Parker is hard of hearing. The interpretation is ASL. The path is the Universal Access Trail at the nature center, not a separate new trail.
