Denver Rescue Mission filled its tables again for a Thanksgiving banquet that has now run for more than 30 years. Hundreds of people came for a full turkey dinner. More than 40 volunteers served it. Leftovers went out through Friday, so the meal did not end when the chairs were pushed in. The Mission is inviting volunteers back for Christmas and for next year's banquet. The long run is the point: the same dinner, with a new crew welcome to stand on the serving side.
