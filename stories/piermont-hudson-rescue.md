Piermont Fire Department divers and Tarrytown firefighters carried a badly burned man through chest-deep water in a Stokes basket to a fireboat, the Rockland Report said. The rescue was on the Hudson River at Tarrytown. The crews later received a Unit Citation and a Life-Saving Award.

The man was badly burned. The water was chest-deep. The basket was a Stokes basket, and the boat was a fireboat. The honors were a Unit Citation and a Life-Saving Award for that rescue.
