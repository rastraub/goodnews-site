Len Place of Oshkosh received a lifetime achievement award from the Bluebird Restoration Association of Wisconsin, WBAY reported on October 20, 2026. For more than 30 years of his retirement he built nest boxes, walked bluebird trails, and checked on the birds using them. A friend and mentor got him started. He told the station it never felt like work.

The memory he kept was a bird that stayed on the nest. Before he closed the box he wondered if she would let him touch her. He petted her three times, and she did not leave. Place said the award is welcome and that what he actually cares about is keeping bluebirds in the state. At 90 he has stepped off the trails and left that walking to other people. He plans to watch birds in his own yard and to spend more time writing.
