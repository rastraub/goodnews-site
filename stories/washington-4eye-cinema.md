Southwest D.C. has its own indie movie house, Axios reported on November 6, 2026. Nonprofit 4EYE Film Center opened last month in the new Revada Arts Center at 340 Maple Drive SW, near Fourth and I streets. It screens in the building's 58-seat cinema. The Revada building also holds a 140-seat black-box theater and a cafe, and Axios noted that wine from the building is allowed into the movie.

Founder Emily Lenzner, who previously worked in Hollywood and at the Motion Picture Association, said the theater will not book Marvel movies. The slate is independent and local films, documentaries, international titles, and classics, with Q&As, themed series, and matinees for families with young children and for older viewers. Tickets run from $8 to $16. Axios described it as the neighborhood's only dedicated cinema aside from the IMAX at the Air and Space Museum. The story is the opening. It does not report a ticket total.
