Baptist Health System held its annual NICU Family Reunion at Brackenridge Park in San Antonio. Children who started in the neonatal unit came back doing well, and they got to see the nurses and doctors who had cared for them. The group included 6-year-old triplets and a boy who had spent eight months in the NICU.

The afternoon was a reunion of people who already know one another from those first hard weeks, now meeting in a park.
