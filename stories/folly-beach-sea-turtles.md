The South Carolina Aquarium put three rehabilitated sea turtles back in the ocean at Folly Beach, the Association of Zoos and Aquariums reported. Loggerhead Honey and Kemp's ridleys Hope and Buffalo were releases 497, 498, and 499. The aquarium's count is one release short of 500.

The three names are individual animals that finished care and went into the water, not a forecast for every turtle still in treatment. The next number the aquarium is watching is 500.
