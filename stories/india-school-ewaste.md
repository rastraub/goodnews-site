Schools across India turned an eco-club campaign into a large pile of collected electronics. The Hindu reported on October 29, 2026, that the E-Waste Awareness and Reduction Campaign gathered 4.27 lakh items, about 427,000 pieces, from 57,271 schools.

The drive sits under the education ministry's Eco Clubs for Mission LiFE. Bajaj Foundation organized it with the Department of School Education and Literacy, and UNICEF YuWaah supported the work. The Hindu said 33 lakh participants, about 3.3 million students and teachers, took part in awareness sessions and the collection.

The roundup does not name a single campus, a date range, or a recycler. It does not say the items have been broken down or kept out of a landfill for good. The figure that is on the page is the collection itself: hundreds of thousands of pieces brought in by schools, and millions of people in the rooms where the lesson was taught.
