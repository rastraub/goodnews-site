The WindSwell Foundation surprised 1,141 special-education teachers in Fort Wayne with $300 gift cards for their classrooms, WPTA 21Alive reported. Each card was the same amount. The list was the district's special-education teaching staff.

The total is 1,141 cards at $300 each. The station's account is the surprise and the purpose, classroom supplies, not a change in anyone's salary.
