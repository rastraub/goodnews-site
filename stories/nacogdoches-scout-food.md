Cub Scout Pack 107 in Nacogdoches collected nonperishable food for local veterans, KTRE reported. The pack partnered with Hunger Free Nac Sin Hambre, sending Halloween bags out for neighbors to fill and gathering them ahead of the Veterans Day parade.

The drive is aimed at veterans facing food insecurity. The parade is the deadline the pack collected toward, and the bags were the tool that turned a Halloween night into a food donation.
