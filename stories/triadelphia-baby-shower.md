Triadelphia held its first free community baby shower, and The Intelligencer called the turnout a success. Once Upon a Child at the Highlands organized the day in a donated event hall. Expecting parents could meet WIC, Head Start, CASA, and other groups, and they received free gifts. The shower was a first for the town. The help on offer was those introductions plus the gifts, in a room the organizers did not have to rent.
