More than 60 volunteers with the San Luis Frontera Rotary Club filled Colibri Supermarket early Saturday to pack food. KYMA reported on October 10, 2026, that the goal was 10,000 meals, donated to the Salvation Army to help with local hunger. The station said neighbors and volunteers turned out until the packing reached capacity.

The meals are a one-morning push, not a new grocery store. They go to the Salvation Army, which will get them to people who need them in the San Luis area.
