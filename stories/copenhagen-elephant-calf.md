DR reported on November 10, 2026, that keepers at Copenhagen Zoo came in to find that Surin had given birth late the previous night. The zoo said the calf, a male, weighs just under 100 kilograms and arrived about a month early. Zoological director Mads Bertelsen said the calf appears to be doing well, and that the whole herd gathered around the newborn. Keepers are reviewing the surveillance recording to see how the birth unfolded.

The station's account comes from the zoo's announcement that morning. It does not name a sire, a birth weight more precise than "just under 100 kilograms," or a wild population figure. The news is this early, heavy calf and the herd's response. Follow-up care is still ahead of the keepers.
