Aspiro's Turkey Trot at Murphy Park in Green Bay was an accessible walk, with hot cider along the way and 50 pies at the finish. Clients of the disability nonprofit ended it by pie-ing the staff. The course was built so the people Aspiro serves could be in it, not watching it. The pies were not a prize for the fastest walker. They were the last laugh, and the staff were the ones wearing them.
