Hundreds of runners came out for the 23rd New Mexico Great Pumpkin Chase, KRQE reported in an account carried by Yahoo. The race is in Albuquerque, and the money it raises goes to school running programs.

Twenty-three is the year-count of the event. Hundreds is the turnout this time. The proceeds are aimed at students who run at school, not at a prize for the winner alone.
