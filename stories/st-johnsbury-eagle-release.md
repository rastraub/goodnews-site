A young bald eagle found in a manure pile is flying again in St. Johnsbury, WPTZ reported. VINS rehabilitated her and released her where she was found. She was the center's 370th patient of the year, a record for VINS. The release put her back at the spot of the rescue after that care. The 370 counts patients taken in this year, and she is one of them.
