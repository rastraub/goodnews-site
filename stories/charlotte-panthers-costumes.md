Carolina Panthers rookies put on costumes and visited Atrium Health Levine Children's Hospital for Halloween, WBTV reported. Dressed as superheroes, princesses, and other characters, they moved through the hospital and saw more than 100 patients and families.

Officials said the visit is an annual tradition. The station's account is the costumes and the rounds, a few hours of a different kind of practice day inside the children's hospital.
