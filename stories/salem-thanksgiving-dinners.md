Churches and volunteers served about 1,200 free Thanksgiving dinners in Salem and Centralia, Southern Illinois Now reported. The cooks used 80 smoked turkeys. About two dozen volunteer cooks did the work, and several churches took part. Guests also got blankets for winter.

About 1,200 is the number fed. The dinners were free. The blankets went out with the meals. Salem and Centralia are the two towns in the count.
