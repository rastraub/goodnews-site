Forsyth police spent Friday morning loading turkeys into their trunks and taking them to Union Hill Apartments and Forsyth Gardens, 41NBC reported. A local business donated 150 birds. The station did not name the business.

Gabrielle Sands, who lives at Union Hill, said she was glad to see a turkey. She told the station this is the first Thanksgiving in a long time she will spend with her family and cook, and the first in seven months with her mother. She said the gift kept her from having to spend money she needed for other things.

Lieutenant David Asabell said the department wants to do more in the city than answer calls. He said a neighborhood grows when people put something into it, and that the officers went to the places where they want to see that happen.
