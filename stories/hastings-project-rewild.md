Children taking part in outdoor sessions in Hastings told the BBC the time outside helps them feel calmer. BBC News reported on November 13, 2026, from Project Rewild, which is based in the town and supported by BBC Children in Need. The sessions are free, and they run in whatever weather comes during half-term. George said it is about being outside, making friends, and enjoying nature, and that he likes getting out of the house and away from screens. Sophia said she used to stay indoors and now goes outside more often and climbs trees she would not have tried before. Daniel called it an escape. Charlie said being outside makes him feel relaxed, and Seb said they go home feeling at peace.

Founder and director Luke said the point is remembering that people are part of nature, and that children who spend so much time on screens do not build a relationship with it. He said the Children in Need funding is what keeps the sessions free, so cost is not what stops a child from coming. The organization said it wants to give children time and freedom to explore. The report is these children's accounts of the half-term sessions. It does not measure a change in health, and it does not say how many children the project reaches in a year.
