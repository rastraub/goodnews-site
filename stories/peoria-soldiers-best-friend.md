Soldier's Best Friend, based in Peoria, trains shelter dogs as service companions for Arizona veterans living with PTSD or traumatic brain injury, Arizona's Family reported. One veteran said he and his dog, Midnight, are "changing two lives at once." The pairing takes a dog out of a shelter and gives that veteran a trained companion. The quote is his description of that exchange, not a measured result for every participant.
