Bundle Up and Boo stocked Lewis County families for colder weather, WDTV 5 reported. The event, which began with West Virginia University nursing students, handed out more than 900 coats in Weston, meals for more than 500 people, and candy.

Those are the counts from this giveaway. They measure coats, meals, and a Halloween table in one county, not every closet that still needs a winter coat.
