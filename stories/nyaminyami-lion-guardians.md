Mongabay reported on November 17, 2026, that Wildlife Conservation Action, founded by conservation biologist Moreangels Mbizah, has spent six years in two wards of Nyaminyami rural district helping people live with lions. The group says incidents there have fallen by 98 percent. Across the districts of Mbire and Binga, it says incidents have fallen by 53 percent. Those figures are the organisation's record, as told to Mongabay.

The work uses guardians called Batabilili, people from the communities themselves. They help farmers keep livestock in raised kraals, watch where lions move, and hold meetings. Mbizah said neighbors trust them because they are family, not outsiders, and that the same presence has reduced retaliatory killings of lions that had taken stock. She also described a mentorship program for women university students, and said the Whitley Fund for Nature gave her a Whitley Award this year. The news is that reported drop and the guardian model. It is not an independent census of lions.
