Reading Central Library has counted 100,000 visits since it moved in the summer. The BBC reported on October 27, 2026, that the library opened at the town's Civic Centre on Bridge Street in June, after 41 years on Kings Road. The old building closed for the last time in May.

Reading Borough Council said 2,700 new members have joined since the move and that about 42,000 books have been borrowed. Jacopo Lanzoni, the lead councillor for leisure and culture, said it was fantastic that so many people had come in four months, and that the large, colourful children's area had been popular with families.

The figures are visits, memberships, and loans at this one library since June. They are not a measure of reading across the town.
