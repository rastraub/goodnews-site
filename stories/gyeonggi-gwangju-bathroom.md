Firefighters answering a noise complaint in Chuja-dong, in Gwangju, Gyeonggi Province, rescued a woman in her 80s who had been trapped in a bathroom for about three hours, Herald Business reported from the Gyeonggi Gwangju Fire Department.

A 112 call came in around noon on Tuesday, Nov. 10, about thumping from an upstairs unit in a four-story building. Firefighters and police checked the homes. No one answered at one unit on the third floor. Crews used a small ladder truck, reached a window, and went inside. They heard knocking from the bathroom. The resident, who lives alone, was stuck in there. Firefighters got the bathroom door open and brought her out.

Herald Business said she had bruising on the back of a hand. Fire Chief Bae Yeong-hwan said treating the noise call as a possible emergency is what led them to her. The report does not give her name.
