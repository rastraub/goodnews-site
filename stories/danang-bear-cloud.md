Multimedia students from FPT University Da Nang opened a free exhibition on November 22, 2026, about sun bears and Asiatic black bears cared for at the Vietnam Bear Rescue Center in Bach Ma National Park. Báo Văn Hóa reported that the show, called Van Gau, or Bear Cloud, ran from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Da Nang Museum on Tran Phu Street.

The students built four areas: displays on the two species and on rescue and rehabilitation, a code-breaking game about mistaken ideas of keeping bears, a short quiz, and a corner for talking about what young people can do. The material came from documents, a field visit the group made in early October, and information from the center and the park. Project leader Phan Thi Nhu Quynh said the hope was that Bach Ma's bears would feel less distant to students in the city. Nguyen Minh Duc of the park management board said rescue is only part of the work, and that public understanding matters too. Lecturer Hoang Tran Tu Phuong said the students went to the center before they designed the show.

What they took from the visit, the paper said, is that rescue does not end when a bear leaves a cage. Care and rehabilitation take a long time. The news is the one-day exhibition and that student project. It is not a count of bears moved or released.
