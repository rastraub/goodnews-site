St. Vincent de Paul's Thanksgiving drive in Boise is feeding 17,000 neighbors. It has now run for 50 years. Volunteers handed out more than 3,500 boxes across the Treasure Valley. One of them was on his 19th year of showing up. The boxes are this year's work. The 19 years, and the 50, are how a single holiday giveaway becomes something a valley can count on.
