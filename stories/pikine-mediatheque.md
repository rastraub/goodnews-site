Pikine, a large suburb of Dakar, has opened a municipal mediatheque that the city says it paid for itself. Agence de Presse Sénégalaise reported on October 22, 2026, that Mayor Abdoulaye Timbo inaugurated the building the day before.

The cost was 600 million CFA francs, drawn from the city's own funds. Timbo called that choice a sign that a local government can carry a major project when the books are kept in order. He said residents across the department had a hand in the design, and that the building should be a place for connection, digital research, and study in the heart of the suburb. He asked for careful management and invited people to use it.

Elected officials, neighborhood sponsors, students, cultural workers, and a delegation from Nanterre, France, attended. The wire story does not list a book count or the hours. It records an opening, a budget, and a public invitation.
