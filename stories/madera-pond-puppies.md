Madera County officers closed a search that had already run for weeks, KMPH FOX26 reported. They had been looking for a pregnant dog that stayed out of reach. They found her puppies in a ponding basin as rain approached, then used hot dogs to lure the mother in so the family could be reunited and moved out.

The account is one litter and one basin, after a long stretch of near misses. It does not say every stray in the county has been found.
