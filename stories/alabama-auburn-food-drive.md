The 33rd Beat Auburn Beat Hunger and Beat Bama Food Drive brought in more than 800,000 pounds of food, the Tuscaloosa News reported. Alabama students raised 385,638 pounds for the West Alabama Food Bank. Auburn students raised 449,626 pounds for the Food Bank of East Alabama. Auburn's total was the larger one.

The 33rd year is this edition of the rivalry drive. The two pound counts are what each campus collected. Together they pass 800,000 pounds.
