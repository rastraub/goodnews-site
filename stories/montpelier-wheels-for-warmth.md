Vermont's Wheels for Warmth used-tire sale raised a record $126,000 this fall, WCAX reported. The money is expected to help about 166 households fill heating gaps this winter. Since 2005 the program has raised more than $1 million.

The sale is used tires. The $126,000 is this fall's record. About 166 is the number of households the station said the money should help. More than $1 million is the total across the years since 2005.
