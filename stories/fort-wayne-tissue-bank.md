About 100 women donated breast tissue for cancer research at a collection event at IU Health Fort Wayne, WPTA reported. The Susan G. Komen Tissue Bank at Indiana University ran the event, with about 200 volunteers. Organizers encouraged women under 40 and women of color to take part so the research sample is more diverse. About 100 is the number of donors. About 200 is the number of volunteers. Donated tissue is material for study. The event is not a treatment, and it is not a cure.
