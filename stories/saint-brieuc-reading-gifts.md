From October 17 to November 20, three bookshops in Saint-Brieuc are taking part in Donnez à lire, a national drive for children's books. Ouest-France reported the collection on October 22, 2026.

A customer buys an extra children's book and leaves it in the shop. Secours populaire gives the books out at Christmas distributions. The drive is in its 11th edition and has been run for ten years by the Syndicat de la librairie française. In 2025 the Saint-Brieuc shops collected 70 books. This year's count is not in yet. Régis Dumas said the local Secours populaire accompanies about 3,000 children.

The shops are Bojangles at 6 rue Jules-Ferry, BD-West on rue Saint-Gilles, and La Bouquinerie on rue Saint-Gouéno. Rosalie Cave of Bojangles has partnered with the drive since it began.
