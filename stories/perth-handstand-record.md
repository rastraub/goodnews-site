Esther Fischer, 75, is the oldest person to hold a handstand, after Guinness World Records certified the mark this week. ABC News reported it on October 24, 2026.

The record required 15 seconds unaided. On August 31 she held the pose for 55 seconds. She was not a gymnast growing up. She started at a Perth gym around age 64 or 65, has been a member for about ten years, and spent about three years on the handstand. Friends suggested it over coffee after a 74-year-old man in the United States held the record.

Gym owner Jacob Moffitt, whose gym is not named in the report, began a longevity class for people 60 and older. About half a dozen people now come. Fischer is working toward a one-arm handstand. She has not achieved that yet.
