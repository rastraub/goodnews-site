More than 50 children got forever families on South Carolina Adoption Day. The ceremonies were in four places, including Sumter. Muriel Loney adopted three boys she had already cared for over five years. The day did not start the care. It made official a household that had already been theirs, and it did the same, in other courtrooms, for the rest of the children whose adoptions were finalized that day.
