Three middle-school students started CPR on a subway platform in Seongnam, and the man they helped is breathing again. The Korea Herald reported on November 27, 2026, that NeoTrans, the operator of the Shinbundang Line, said a man in his 70s collapsed about 10:28 p.m. Wednesday at Migeum Station. Kang Da-in and Oh Si-eun, both third-years at Suwon Dasan Middle School, and Ahn Si-hyeon, a second-year at Ihyeon Middle School, found he was not breathing and began compressions. A station employee took over. When the man still was not breathing, the employee used an automated external defibrillator and delivered one shock. He started breathing and regained consciousness.

Emergency responders arrived about ten minutes after the collapse and took him to Seoul National University Bundang Hospital. NeoTrans said his condition has improved significantly, and that the company plans to give the students certificates and gifts. The account comes from the operator and from station video the company shared with the paper. The news is a completed rescue on the platform. It does not say what caused the collapse, or how long he will need care.
