Troy's 79th Turkey Trot drew its biggest field since 2013. More than 7,700 people registered, from 35 states, and about 10,000 spectators lined the course. The race raised more than $30,000 for the Regional Food Bank, Joseph's House & Shelter, and the Salvation Army. The head count is the record people can see. The checks to a food bank, a shelter, and the Salvation Army are what the bibs were for.
