An elephant named Mridula has given birth to a male calf at Manas National Park. The Press Trust of India reported the news on November 3, 2026, from a social media post by Assam's forest minister, Jayanta Malla Baruah. Head mahout Dandeshwar Boro described the calf as encouraging for the people who care for the park's elephants.

The same note also mentioned a rhino calf in the park. That birth, in the family of a rhino rescued years ago from floods, was already reported on its own. This account is the elephant. PTI did not name Mridula's calf, did not say which range the elephants use, and did not give a birth date beyond the minister's "a few days ago" wording, which the wire attached to the rhino. The park covers about 850 square kilometres along the India-Bhutan border, in the Baksa and Chirang districts, and it is a UNESCO World Heritage site. The news is this calf. It is not a new total for Manas's elephants.
