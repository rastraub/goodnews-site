Chester County Council presented a plaque to deputy fire director J.P. McNeil III for a rescue on Aug. 1, WSOC-TV reported. Off duty in York County, he came upon a vehicle on fire and pulled several people out, including children.

The plaque is the county's recognition of that stop. Aug. 1 is when the crash happened. The report is about the people he got out of the car, and it does not add a medical update on how they fared afterward.
