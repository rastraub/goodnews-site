The Pittsburgh Firefighters Foundation's annual giveaway outfitted kindergarten through fifth-grade students with about 2,000 winter coats, WPXI reported. Temperatures were dropping into the 30s.

The coats went to K-5 students. About 2,000 is the number handed out. The foundation is the Pittsburgh Firefighters Foundation, and the giveaway is yearly. The station tied the handout to the colder weather.
