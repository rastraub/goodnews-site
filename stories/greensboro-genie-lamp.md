Isabella Myers, a second grader at David J. Jones Elementary in Greensboro, rubbed a genie lamp in the school office and got her wish, WXII 12 reported. Her mother, Quiana Quiros, who is serving in the military, had given her the lamp and told her a wish would come true if she rubbed it. Isabella explained that to the people in the office. She also said how much she missed her mom.

Quiros then walked in. Guilford County Schools lists Isabella at David J. Jones. The station did not describe a long deployment or a branch of service. The story is the wish, the lamp, and a mother stepping into the room.
