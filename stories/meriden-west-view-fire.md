The Meriden Fire Department was called to 90 West View Drive at about 10:59 a.m. Monday, FOX61 reported, citing Deputy Fire Chief Rocco Fierravanti. Smoke was coming from the house, and crews were told a person was trapped inside. Firefighters from Engine Company 5 went in with a hose line, found the occupant on their first search, and brought him out.

Neighbors told FOX61 the man who lives there is Paul, an older resident who lives alone and comes to their holidays. One neighbor said she stayed beside him until the ambulance came. Joseph Anderson, a neighbor who is a firefighter in Hamden, said people on the street would see what they could do for him.

Emergency medical crews treated Paul and took him to a hospital. Three other people were checked at the scene and turned down a ride. Officials said the fire was under control and no firefighters were hurt. The fire marshal's office is looking into the cause. FOX61 said no update had been released on how Paul was doing.
