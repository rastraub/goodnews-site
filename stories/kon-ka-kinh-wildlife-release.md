Thirty-one wild animals went back into the forest at Kon Ka Kinh after a stretch in care. VietnamPlus reported on October 2, 2026, that the release happened on October 1 in Gia Lai province. The group covered 10 species listed in Vietnam's strict protection categories, including mountain tortoises, northern pig-tailed macaques, Asian water monitors, reticulated pythons, a Java pangolin, and a pygmy slow loris.

The park received them in August and September through voluntary handovers and from agencies in Gia Lai. Many arrived in poor health, injured, or without the habits they would need in the wild. Director Tran Van Thu said that after care, all 31 were judged able to survive on their own. The release is a return for this group. It does not count animals still in rescue centers, and it is separate from other releases elsewhere in Vietnam this year.
