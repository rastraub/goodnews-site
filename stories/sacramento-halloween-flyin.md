Sacramento Executive Airport's first Halloween fly-in pulled a crowd the hosts did not plan for, the Sacramento Bee reported. Experimental Aircraft Association Chapter 1714 put on the free event, sponsored by Flight Level 510. A couple of hundred people were expected. More than 2,000 had arrived by the halfway point. Candy ran low in less than an hour, the airport lot filled, and some visitors parked across Freeport Boulevard.

Chapter president Anastasia Long said the line was still out the door and she had not heard a complaint. Caitlin Mullan, who owns the Flight Level 510 rental business at the airport with her husband, said the trunk-or-treat was a way to let families walk up to decorated planes and take candy. Pilots Caleb Ragan, Viliam Nowitki, and Travis Boyd, all 19, flew in from Livermore and handed candy out from under a wing. Boyd said they wanted to volunteer and share the fun of flying.
