On Wednesday, October 21, 2026, volunteers from Picardie Faune Sauvage released a young common kestrel in the heart of the château at Coucy-le-Château. L'Aisne Nouvelle reported the release that evening.

The bird was five months old. It had been recovered as a nestling near Beauvais airport. The paper noted that children do not see a release like this every day. The published account does not name the people who opened the carrier, and it does not say where the kestrel went next. The confirmed news is the release itself: a young falcon, cared for since it was a nestling, returned to the air inside the fortress.
