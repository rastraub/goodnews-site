Riders are already on the new asphalt pump track at Cole Park in Alamosa, ahead of the Nov. 5 ribbon-cutting, the Alamosa Citizen reported. Kids and adults are both using it.

Great Outdoors Colorado funded the track, and American Ramp Company built it. The path sits in a city park, so neighbors can ride without leaving town for a trail. The official opening celebration is still on the calendar.
