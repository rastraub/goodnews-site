We Don't Waste opened its Park Hill warehouse for Potatopalooza and handed out 80,000 pounds of free potatoes, Denver7 reported. That is nearly double the group's July giveaway. We Don't Waste also sends rescued food to more than 190 nonprofits.

The 80,000 pounds are this event. The nonprofit network is the longer route those potatoes, and other rescued food, already travel. The story is one giveaway aimed at waste and grocery bills, not a claim that every pantry in Denver was stocked.
