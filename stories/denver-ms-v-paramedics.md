Vernita Lewis, whom Denver Health crews call Ms. V, cooked Thanksgiving for about 150 paramedics who were working the holiday. She has done it for nearly 20 years. Her mother used to cook beside her and died in 2024. Lewis kept the meal going. The crews call her family. The shift does not stop because the calendar says Thanksgiving, so the dinner comes to the people who are on the clock. The tradition is hers now, carried forward from a kitchen that used to have two cooks.
