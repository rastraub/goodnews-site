LaPorte County 4-H members collected 23,246 pounds of food and took the statewide title in Fight the Hunger, Stock the Trailer, Hoosier Ag Today reported. Across 40 Indiana county fairs, 4-H youth gathered more than 181,000 pounds for 52 local food banks. Educator Kelsy Guadarrama said the LaPorte youth set the goal of raising the most, after finishing second in their region last year, and they made their own timeline. Adult volunteers helped pick up food from the barns. The 23,246 pounds are LaPorte's winning total. The larger figure is what all 40 fairs collected together.
