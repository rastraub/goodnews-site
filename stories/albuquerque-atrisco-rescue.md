Albuquerque Fire Rescue crews dug a woman out of a hole beside the Rio Grande on the night of Nov. 18, KOAT reported. The call came in about 8:05 p.m. for a woman stranded in the river, in the area of Atrisco Drive NW. When crews arrived, she was not in the water. She had fallen into a hole about 10 feet from the bank, and she was trapped up to her chest.

An extrication team used hand shovels to free her while other crews gave emergency medical care. She was out at about 8:48 p.m. and in stable condition. Albuquerque Ambulance assessed her after the rescue. The department asked people to be careful on the riverbanks, which can give way. A rescue there is not always a water rescue.
