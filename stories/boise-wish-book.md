Boise State's Albertsons Library is collecting new and gently used children's books for Operation Wish Book from Nov. 1 through Dec. 1, with a goal of 650 books, the university reported. The library has been a partner since 1994 and has gathered more than 4,000 books over that span.

The 650 is this year's target. The 4,000-plus is the historical total, not the contents of the current box.
