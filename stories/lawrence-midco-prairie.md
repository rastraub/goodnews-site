Midco Prairie in downtown Lawrence opened on Oct. 22 on ground that had been a parking lot. It holds more than 125 native plant species, a boardwalk made of stumps, and workshops on foraging.

The lot no longer stores cars. It stores a planted prairie, with a path through it and classes about what is growing there.
