Broncos defensive lineman Zach Allen donated Thanksgiving meals and helped hand them out with Food Bank of the Rockies, CBS Colorado reported. It is the second year he has done this. The meals included turkeys, ham, and sides, and he took part in the distribution as well as the donation. Teammate Marvin Mims Jr. made a separate visit to Aurora West Preparatory Academy, where he encouraged students to look after their mental health. The food is Allen's gift through the food bank. The school stop is Mims's own visit.
