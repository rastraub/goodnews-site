Broncos defensive lineman Zach Allen bought 120 Thanksgiving turkeys himself and helped hand them to 120 families through Food Bank of the Rockies, CBS Colorado reported. It is the second year of the effort. Teammate Marvin Mims Jr. made a separate visit to an Aurora school to talk about mental health. The turkeys are Allen's gift, paid for by him and distributed with the food bank. The school visit is Mims's own stop, not part of the turkey count.
