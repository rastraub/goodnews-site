A small library opened inside the Bapatla Collectorate so government employees have books at work. The Hindu reported on November 12, 2026, that District Collector V. Vinod Kumar inaugurated it on Wednesday in the New Video Conference Hall. Four bookshelves are in place. He donated five books and asked employees to give at least one good book each.

He said staff should keep learning while they do their jobs, and he suggested titles on the Indian Constitution, service rules, administration, personality development, and general knowledge. He told in-charge district revenue officer Lavanna to name someone to look after the shelves and the books. The news is those four shelves and the first donations. It is not a public library with a counted collection, and the paper did not say how many books were on the shelves after the opening.
