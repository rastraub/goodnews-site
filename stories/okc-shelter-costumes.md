City Rescue Mission in Oklahoma City was 80 costumes short of a Halloween for every child in its care, KOCO 5 reported. Neighbors closed that gap, along with a foundation tied to Thunder center Isaiah Hartenstein. The costumes covered all 120 children.

Eighty was the hole. One hundred twenty was the full count. The story is that the shortfall was filled for this Halloween, not a standing closet for next year.
