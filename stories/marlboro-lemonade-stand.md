Joey, a 15-year-old in Marlboro who has autism, ran his annual lemonade stand along the parade route and gave this year's proceeds to Autism Alliance, WHDH reported. The stand raised more than $5,000. Marlboro police added $1,000. The gift is this year's take from the stand, with the police department's donation on top.
