About 25 volunteers in the Longmont Quilt Guild's Bee Sew Brave group have sewn and donated hundreds of red, white, and blue quilts for veterans since 2009, KGNU reported. Some of the quilts are already waiting on the beds in tiny homes for veterans who are leaving homelessness. The hundreds are the running total since the group began. The beds ready with quilts are one of the places those gifts go.
