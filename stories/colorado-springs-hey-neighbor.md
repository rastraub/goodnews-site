Hey Neighbor passed its own mark in Colorado Springs, The Gazette reported. The drive was called 150 Tables Across COS, lined up with Colorado's 150th year. Residents signed up to host 155 neighborhood tables, five past the goal of 150.

Nearly all of the hosts said they would do it again. The count is tables people agreed to hold, a neighbor-to-neighbor project, not a claim that every street in the city took part.
