Students at the Alaska Native Cultural Charter School turned a haunted house into a walk through traditional stories, Alaska Public Media reported. More than 40 students and volunteers in Anchorage built it around seven Alaska Native legends.

The house is a school project with a count attached: seven stories, and more than 40 people who made the rooms. It is one October at one school, told by the students who chose those legends.
