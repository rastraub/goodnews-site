Oklahoma State University's College of Veterinary Medicine opened its doors and let families perform surgery on teddy bears. Children practiced the kind of careful handling a vet student learns, on a stuffed patient that could not be hurt.

One student in the program now first tried it at the open house when they were 8. The bears are a way in, and for at least one person they were the start of the real training.
