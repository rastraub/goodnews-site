Kendall County 4-H members packed care packages and carried them to area grain elevators during this year's harvest, Shaw Local reported. Each bag held a water bottle, baked goods, snacks, and a thank-you note for people moving the crop.

Clair Anderson, of the Lisbon Town and Country 4-H Club, said the best part is the handoff, when she can tell farmers she sees the work. The Kendall-Grundy Farm Bureau said harvest is underway after a late start caused by late planting and weather. Manager Jenna Siegel said a short thank-you reminds agricultural workers that the community notices them in a season when the hours run long.
