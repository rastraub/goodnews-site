A two-day Petco Love adoption event at Reliant Arena drew thousands of people, and several hundred dogs and cats from South Texas shelters went home with families, the Houston Chronicle reported. Adoption fees were waived on Sunday. The Chronicle described the result as several hundred adoptions, and this account stays with that figure rather than a more exact count.
