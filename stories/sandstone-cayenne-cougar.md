Cayenne, a cougar kitten missing for eight days in Washington, was rescued and driven 27 hours to The Wildcat Sanctuary in Sandstone, Minnesota, FOX 9 reported. She rejoined her three orphaned siblings there. The eight days are the time she was apart from them. The drive is how she crossed from Washington to the sanctuary, where the other three kittens were waiting.
