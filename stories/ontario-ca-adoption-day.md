More than 70 children joined permanent families at San Bernardino County's 26th annual Adoption Finalization Event, ABC7 Los Angeles reported. Children and Family Services held it at the Ontario Convention Center. The children ranged from 8 months to 16 years old. The event is where those adoptions became official. The count is the children whose adoptions were finalized there, across that age range.
