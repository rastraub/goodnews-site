Devine Carama walked 50 miles to raise money for children's coats, logging about 120,000 steps toward a $20,000 goal. He founded Believing in Forever, and this is the 13th year of the drive. Donations were nearly at the goal. The miles are the ask. The coats are the result he has been chasing every year since the drive began, one long walk at a time.
