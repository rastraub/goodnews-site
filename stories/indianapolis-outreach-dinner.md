The nonprofit Outreach served its annual Thanksgiving dinner for young people without homes at Cornerstone Lutheran Church in Indianapolis, WTHR reported. Guests also received coats and hats for the winter. Outreach is planning a youth Christmas party in December. The night combined a holiday meal with winter clothing, and the December party is still ahead.
