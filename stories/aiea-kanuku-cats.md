Honolulu firefighters carried four cats out of a house fire in Aiea on Thursday morning, KITV reported. The department got the 911 call at 11:40 a.m. for a two-story, multi-family home on Kanuku Street. Crews arrived within minutes and saw smoke coming from the right side of the upper floor.

The people who had been inside were already out. Four cats were not. Firefighters brought the cats out, had the fire under control at 11:56 a.m., and had it extinguished at 12:21 p.m. They checked walls and other hidden spaces so nothing was still smoldering. No one was hurt, and no one was displaced. Investigators are still working on the cause and on a damage estimate.
