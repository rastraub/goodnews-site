United Way of Kaw Valley has opened Christmas Bureau intake week, so Shawnee County families can sign up for holiday meals and gifts, WIBW reported. Last year the bureau reached more than 1,200 households, about 3,900 people.

Those figures are last year's results. This week's news is that signup is underway. Organizers are also asking for donations of basics such as blankets and socks.
