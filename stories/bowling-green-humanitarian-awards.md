The Black Swamp Humanitarian Awards in Bowling Green honored everyday lifesavers, BG Independent News reported. Among them was delivery driver Uyless Williams III, who went into a burning apartment building in Perrysburg to warn residents.

The awards were in Bowling Green. Williams is a delivery driver, not a firefighter on that call. The building was in Perrysburg. He went in to warn the people who lived there. The honor recognizes that he stepped into the building while it was burning.
