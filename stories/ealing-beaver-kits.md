Two beaver kits born this year at Paradise Fields now need names, and London children get to offer them. BBC News reported on October 13, 2026, that anyone in London ages 4 to 18 can send a suggestion until December 7. The Ealing Beaver Project and the mayor's office will choose the winners. Entries go through Citizen Zoo.

A family of Eurasian beavers was released at the Greenford site in 2023, the first return to urban London in more than 400 years. Elliot Newton of Citizen Zoo and Sean McCormack described the kits for the BBC. The project is run by Citizen Zoo with Ealing Wildlife Group, Friends of Horsenden Hill, Ealing Council, and funding from the mayor's office. Backers say the beavers have created wetland, improved water quality, and made the site more resilient to flood and drought. Paradise Fields is free and open 24 hours. A naming contest does not measure how many more kits will be born. The wetland is the part neighbors can already walk.
