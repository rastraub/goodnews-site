Samantha Lee, a 25-year-old Air Force veteran from Post Falls, left Daytona Beach in March to walk to the Oregon coast. She is nearly there. Along the way she has raised more than $17,000 for Back on My Feet, and strangers have handed her rides, food, and water.

The walk is not finished. The money already raised is for the organization, not a claim that the last miles are done.
