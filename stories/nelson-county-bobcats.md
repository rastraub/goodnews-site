The Wildlife Center of Virginia released two rescued bobcats in Nelson County after nearly three months of rehabilitation, WSET reported. The young pair left with strong hunting skills and healthy winter coats.

Nearly three months is the time in care. The hunting skills and the winter coats are what the center said they had at release. Nelson County is where they went back to the wild.
