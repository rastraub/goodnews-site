Jian Jian and Kang Kang, the first giant pandas born in Macao, left for the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding on November 8. TVB News reported that Jian Jian had lived in the city for ten years. They were driven in air-conditioned vehicles from Seac Pai Van Park to the airport. Secretary for Administration and Justice Wong Sio Chak, Ai Yiping of the liaison office, and about 20 elementary students saw them off.

Carers had kept the twins in quarantine for a month and checked appetite and demeanor every day. A manager at the Municipal Affairs Bureau said the flight meal was bamboo, high-fibre biscuits, and apples, and that the priority now is helping them adjust to Chengdu's climate and food. On arrival they face at least another month of quarantine. The news is this departure and those checks. It does not describe how the twins will be paired for breeding later.
