Firefighters in Douglas freed a 25-year-old mare who had sunk belly-deep in mud, NBC 10 WJAR reported. They laid plywood to make a path across the soft ground and pulled her out. She was unhurt.

The rescue is one horse and one patch of mud. The station's account is that she walked away from it without injury, not a claim about every animal that gets stuck.
