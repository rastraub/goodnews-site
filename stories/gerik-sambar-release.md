Four sambar deer were released into Royal Belum State Park near Gerik, Bernama reported on November 23, 2026. The Perak state secretary's office said the animals went in two days earlier, as part of work to rebuild the prey that Malayan tigers depend on in the Belum-Temengor forest. The release brought together the state parks corporation, the natural resources ministry, the wildlife department, and groups including WWF-Malaysia, with Orang Asli representatives also present.

Since 2021 the same program has released 30 sambar deer, two seladang, and 17 wild boars into the park. Delegates also visited a habitat plot planted to give sambar and muntjac more natural food. The office said Royal Belum's Conservation Assured Tiger Standards accreditation was extended in 2026, and that the park had gone 39 months without a recorded snare. Those are management marks. The news of the week is the four deer. It is not a new census of tigers.
