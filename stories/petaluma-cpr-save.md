Robin Giles has taught CPR for 30 years. The day Russ collapsed was her first real emergency. She started compressions the moment he went down. More than 35 minutes later he woke up. He is back at work, and the couple knows of no neurological damage. They are using what happened to urge other people to learn CPR. The skill she had been demonstrating for decades was the one she used, without a pause, on the person she lives with.
