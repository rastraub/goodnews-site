More than 100 volunteers slept outside for Covenant House Michigan's 13th Sleep Out and raised $500,000, WXYZ reported. The money supports the about 500 young people a year the program helps with shelter and job training in Detroit.

The 13th Sleep Out is this year's night outside. More than 100 is who slept out. $500,000 is what they raised. About 500 a year is who the shelter and training are for.
