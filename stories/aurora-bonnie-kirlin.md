Retirement did not slow Bonnie Kirlin down, Denver7 reported. For more than eight years she has volunteered at Food Bank of the Rockies, and the hours now add up to more than 500. She started through a partnership between the food bank and United Airlines, where she worked until she retired in May 2025.

She still comes several times a month, sometimes with former coworkers, sometimes with her husband, and sometimes to help cook. She told the station she tries to get there at least three or four times a month, counting the United shift, a shift with her husband, and kitchen duty. On a recent day she packed Totes of Hope, bags of food children take home for the weekend when school meals stop. Chief executive Erin Pulling said volunteers are part of the work itself, not an extra, as more families ask for help before the holidays. Denver7 named Kirlin an Everyday Hero.
