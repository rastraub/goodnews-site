A free community meal that Thomas Rucker Jr. started in Denison with one turkey has turned 50 and served more than 900 people, KXII reported. Three generations of the Rucker family now carry it on.

One turkey is the beginning. More than 900 is this year's table. Fifty years is the span. Three generations is who kept it going after the first meal.
