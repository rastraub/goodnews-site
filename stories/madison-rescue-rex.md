Rescue Rex, Metro Nashville's dancing dinosaur, visited Gateway Elementary in Madison on National First Responders Day, WSMV reported. The free program uses songs and games to teach children how to recognize an emergency, call 911, and tell a dispatcher where they are.

Nashville has brought the lessons to Davidson County students for more than 30 years. The station noted that a child may be the only person who can place the call if a parent or grandparent has a medical emergency. The visit was practice, out loud, before anyone needs it.
