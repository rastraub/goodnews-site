Seven green peafowl, two males and five females, were released on Handeuleum Island in Ujung Kulon National Park on November 5. Good News From Indonesia reported the release on November 8, 2026. Jagat Satwa Nusantara moved the birds from its conservation center to the park on November 3. They spent three days getting used to the island's plants, natural food, and water before they were set free. After the release, the team saw some peck the tips of dry leaves, hunt ants, and call.

Dedi Juherdi, who leads management for the park's first zone, welcomed the release and tied it to Indonesia's National Flora and Fauna Love Day. The green peafowl is listed as endangered. The team said it will keep watching whether each bird finds food, stays in good condition, and behaves like a wild bird. The news is these seven birds and the first signs that they are using the island. It is not a count of green peafowl in the park, and it does not yet show that they will breed there.
