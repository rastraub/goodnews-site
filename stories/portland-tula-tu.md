Tula-Tu, a young elephant at the Oregon Zoo, is expected to make a full recovery from elephant endotheliotropic herpesvirus, KPTV reported. Dr. Carlos Sanchez, the zoo's director of animal health, said Monday that she is out of the woods. Her white-cell counts are normal, her viral load is still falling, and she is eating well, running around, and looking like herself. She was diagnosed with the virus two weeks ago. The virus can be deadly in elephants. She no longer needs treatment.

Sanchez said the recovery came from careful care and planning, and that the zoo will keep watching for any sign of illness. Tula-Tu is still testing positive. He said it could take weeks before she tests fully negative. Staff will keep tracking her activity and her white-cell counts while that happens. The update is that treatment has stopped and she is acting like herself again. It is not a claim that the virus is already gone.
