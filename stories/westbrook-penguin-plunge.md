About 22 people went into Long Island Sound every hour for 24 hours at the Penguin Super Plunge in Westbrook, WFSB reported. Every plunger beat a $2,400 minimum. Together they raised more than $125,000 for Special Olympics Connecticut.

The water was Long Island Sound. A plunge happened each hour across the 24 hours. About 22 people took part, and each one cleared the minimum. The money is for Special Olympics Connecticut.
