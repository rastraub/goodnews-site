Macey Davis, now 4, was born in 2022 with hypoplastic left heart syndrome, a defect that left the left side of her heart too small to pump blood to her body, CBS News Atlanta reported. Hours after birth she was flown to Children's Healthcare of Atlanta. A first surgery did not go well enough, and she received a donor heart 16 days later. Most children wait six to 12 months for a heart. Four years on, her family says she runs, jumps, swims, and plays soccer with her little sister, Josie. That is how she is doing now. A transplant is not a cure, and the family has returned for later surgeries.
