A turkey hit by a car in Barrington is walking again. Congress of the Birds built him a wheelchair out of PVC pipe. Within a week and a half he was moving on his own legs. He is headed to a farm where he will not be dinner. The chair was a bridge, not a permanent address. The farm is the part that lasts: a bird who was in the road now has a place where the holiday table is not the ending.
