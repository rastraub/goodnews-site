Sleep in Heavenly Peace, local home builders, the chamber of commerce, and Lowe's built 50 free twin beds in Fort Smith, Talk Business & Politics reported. That was enough to clear a waiting list of roughly 45 children who had been sleeping on the floor. The beds are twin beds, and they are free. The build covered the list the groups had in front of them.
