A neighbor called at 8:23 a.m. Sunday about a fire at 3123 Galbraith Street in northwest Knoxville, WBIR reported from the Knoxville Fire Department. Crews arrived to a one-story house fully involved. They started water from the outside, then went inside, and had the fire out within about 30 minutes.

The resident was not home. Firefighters found four dogs and got them out. The department said all four are safe. They were taken to Young-Williams Animal Center until someone reaches the owner.

Two firefighters were hurt, one with a burn and one with a leg injury. Neither was taken to a hospital. The house cannot be lived in because of smoke, fire, and water damage. Investigators stayed on the scene to determine the cause.
