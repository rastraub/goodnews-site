Mobile Fire-Rescue academy recruits and volunteers walked adoptable dogs at the City Animal Shelter on Community Day, FOX10 reported. The walks got shelter dogs out of their kennels for a stretch.

The day was a visit and a walk, not an adoption event with a tally of new homes. The recruits are still in the academy, and the dogs are still waiting for adopters unless a family chose one that day. The station did not publish an adoption count.
