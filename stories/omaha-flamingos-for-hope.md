Cheryl Stevens, a breast cancer survivor who started Flamingos for Hope, received the Meaning of Care Award on Monday, KETV reported. The Methodist Hospital Foundation gave her the award during one of the group's final visits before she retires. The volunteer program at Methodist Estabrook Cancer Center is set to wrap up at the end of 2026.

Stevens started the flamingo-themed visits to bring laughter and encouragement to patients. She said the point is to give people permission to have fun during chemotherapy. Since the group began about two decades ago, the volunteers have reached more than 40,000 patients, caregivers, and staff. The award marks those years of visits. It is not a claim that the program will continue after this year.
