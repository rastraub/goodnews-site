The Palmetto Council of the Blind and Meta distributed Ray-Ban Meta glasses to legally blind veterans in North Charleston. The glasses can read print aloud, including mail and menus.

They are a tool for text a person cannot see. They are not a treatment that restores vision, and the giveaway is not a claim that every blind veteran now has a pair.
