About 150 kindergarteners from the Canyons School District gathered at the Utah Capitol on Wednesday for the state's annual Apple Crunch, KSL.com reported on October 21, 2026. The visit, held for National Farm to School Month, is a chance to meet local farmers and to follow an apple from a Utah orchard to a school lunch. Dani Perez, a farm-to-school specialist with the Utah State Board of Education, described it as learning the people and the nutrition behind a meal. Chelsea Prime, a kindergarten teacher at Silver Mesa Elementary, said the class talks about choosing food that is closer to the farm.

Utah's Apple Crunch began in 2017. Through October, schools and child-care centers around the state also do farm visits, taste tests, gardening, and classroom lessons. The Capitol morning is one gathering of about 150 children. It is not a count of every student who will taste a local apple this month.
