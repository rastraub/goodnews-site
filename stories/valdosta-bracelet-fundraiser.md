Graham Haskin, 10, made bracelets in Valdosta and sold them for two weeks. He raised $400. He used the money to buy food, water, and supplies for LAMP, which serves people who are experiencing homelessness in the area.

The bracelets were his project. The supplies are what left his hands and went to the organization.
