St. Vincent de Paul served hot Thanksgiving meals at four sites in Baton Rouge. Staff were in at 5 a.m. Volunteers followed at 8 a.m. The plates were turkey, sweet potatoes, and stuffing for neighbors who needed them. Four addresses means the dinner could meet people where they already were, instead of asking the whole city to find one door. The early hour is the unglamorous half: the ovens were on before the serving line existed.
