Phoenix firefighters got six people out of four vehicles stuck in a wash near 40th Street and Camelback Road on Friday, Oct. 30, 12 News reported. The station's helicopter, Sky 12, was over the scene and showed at least two vehicles sitting in fast-moving water.

The Phoenix Fire Department said crews found the four vehicles in the wash with six people trapped, and that firefighters used a shallow-water entry to bring them out. The department said there were no reported injuries. Every driver and passenger was checked at the scene, and all six declined a ride to the hospital.

The station did not name the people in the vehicles. The report also did not say how the vehicles entered the wash.
