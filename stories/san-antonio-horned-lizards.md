San Antonio Zoo and Zoo Miami released 55 Texas horned lizards on a Texas ranch, KSAT 12 reported. The animals are part of a reintroduction project that raises the lizards and returns them to land where the species has thinned out.

This release is one group of 55, from two zoos, on one ranch. It adds animals to the project. It does not mean the species is restored across Texas.
