Republika reported on November 14, 2026, that 12 students received full scholarships at a ceremony on November 12 in Balikpapan, marking five years of a Mahakam forest programme and the third class of Beasiswa Sobat Bumi Kalimantan. Parents received certificates with them. Of 364 applicants, 80 passed an administrative screen, 60 sat an academic test, and 26 were interviewed on September 23 and 24. Twelve were chosen, about 1 in 30. Nine are from East Kalimantan, two from North Kalimantan, and one from South Kalimantan. Seven are at Mulawarman University. The award covers tuition, living costs, and environmental training. With 30 scholars from the first two classes, the programme now has 42 students at 16 universities.

The same effort, run by the Pertamina Foundation with Pertamina Hulu Indonesia, has planted mangroves since 2022 at Muara Kembang and Muara Jawa. Through 2025 it had put in 2.52 million seedlings on 157.1 hectares, about 2.5 times the original target. Monitoring says about 1.74 million trees from the first four phases are still alive. A biodiversity survey counted 479 proboscis monkeys in 76 groups in the delta. Phase 5 this year aims to plant 576,176 more seedlings. That figure is a target. The 12 scholarships and the trees already in the ground are what the ceremony could point to.
