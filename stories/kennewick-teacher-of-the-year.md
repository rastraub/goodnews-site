Kori Cannon, a kindergarten teacher for multilingual learners at Westgate Elementary in Kennewick, is Washington's 2027 Teacher of the Year. NBC Right Now reported on Oct. 12, citing the state superintendent's office, that she was named at a ceremony in Olympia on Oct. 10 and will move on to the national competition.

Cannon has spent 16 years in the classroom and has taught children from 22 countries. She began as a substitute in the Kennewick School District in 2010 and became a full-time kindergarten teacher two years later. The state office said her work with families helped bring Westgate national recognition through a Johns Hopkins partnership network. The award is a title and a next contest. It is not a report card for the school.
