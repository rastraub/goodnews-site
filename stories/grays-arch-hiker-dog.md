Wolfe County Search and Rescue answered a mutual-aid call from Powell County Search and Rescue at about 4:30 p.m. Monday for a hiker and her dog lost near Grays Arch, WKYT reported. Wolfe County rescuers hiked the Grays Arch Trail and went on past the arch to Rough Trail. Powell County rescuers had already found the woman.

She had left the trail and was about 100 yards uphill in steep ground. Her first set of coordinates was off. A later update put the teams on her. She and her Labrador were uninjured and cold, and darkness was coming on.

The slope was too steep for a simple walk down, so the crews set a low-angle rope system. They lowered the dog first, then helped the hiker down with rescuers spaced along the route. Once both were on the trail, the group walked about 1.25 miles to the trailhead and came out of the woods around 9 p.m. WKYT, citing the rescue team, said the hiker, the dog, and the rescuers were not injured.
