Dave Lord and his shelter dog Hershey put years into Matchett Park, KJCT 8 News reported. They cleared glass, tires, and other trash from about 50 acres. Grand Junction has named a new one-mile trail Hershey's Loop for the dog who worked that ground with him.

The name sits on a path people can walk, not only on a plaque. The reporting is about that long cleanup and the city's decision to put Hershey's name on the loop. It does not say the whole park is finished, or that one pair of hands did every acre alone.
