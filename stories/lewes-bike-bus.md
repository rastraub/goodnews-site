Lewes Elementary tried a bike bus, WBOC reported. Students, teachers, and parents rode to school together, and more than 40 children were in the group on the first day. The town hopes the pilot in Lewes will become a weekly ride.

The first count is one morning. A weekly habit is what organizers want next, not a schedule that is already locked in.
