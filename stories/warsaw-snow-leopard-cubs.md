Three snow leopard cubs born at the Warsaw Zoo in July had their first outdoor hours on October 23. Xinhua reported the debut on October 24, 2026, and photographed one of the cubs at the zoo that day.

October 23 is the International Day of the Snow Leopard, marked each year to draw attention to the endangered cat and the high mountains it needs. The wire report does not name the cubs, give weights, or describe how the outdoor hour went beyond the debut itself.

A first walk outside a den is a small public step for a zoo litter. It is not a census of snow leopards in the wild, and the dispatch does not claim that it is.
