Two green turtles that were hurt as hatchlings on Wang'an went back into the sea on October 27. Taiwan's Central News Agency reported that the Penghu National Scenic Area Administration released them on the beach in front of the Green Turtle Tourism and Conservation Center, with the county agriculture and fisheries bureau and a fisheries research centre. Teachers and students from the island stood with the team.

Staff found both animals during nesting patrols on the Wang'an sand, one in September 2023 and one in December 2023, already injured. After a long period of care they were moved in April 2026 to the research centre's shelter on the island. A health check this year found they could manage in the ocean, and the release was timed for high tide.

The count is these two turtles. The agency did not publish a nest total for the season.
