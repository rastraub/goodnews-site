Kaitlyn and Jeffrey Bohnet's North Sky Raptor Sanctuary in Interlochen is trying to move from home-based rescues to a public raptor center, 9&10 News reported. The group had logged 700 rescue calls by September and still operates out of three private residences, often turning birds away. On Thursday the Grand Traverse County Parks and Recreation Commission voted unanimously to send a lease for a center at Camp Greilick to the county Board of Commissioners. The plan calls for a 5,000-square-foot education center and a hospital for birds of prey. Jeffrey Bohnet said a $100,000 fundraiser has a matching donor, and that groundbreaking could come in eight or nine months if the plan is approved. The lease vote is the step that happened. The center is not open.
