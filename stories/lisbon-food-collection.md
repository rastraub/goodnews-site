Portugal's food banks are asking shoppers to fill a bag this weekend. The Portugal News reported on November 26, 2026, that Banco Alimentar Contra a Fome is placing more than 40,000 volunteers at about 2,100 supermarkets on November 28 and 29. The collection had not started when the article was published. Shoppers take a bag from a volunteer and fill it with food that keeps: milk, rice, pasta, olive oil, other cooking oil, flour, sugar, canned fish, beans, and similar staples. The bags go to the warehouses of 21 food banks, where other volunteers sort and weigh them, and then to roughly 2,400 social organisations.

People who cannot shop that weekend can buy a supermarket voucher at participating checkouts until December 8, or give online through the Alimenta Esta Ideia platform, including from outside the country. The Algarve food bank, with offices in Faro and Portimão, is part of the same drive and was still looking for volunteers to collect, sort, and work in the warehouse.

In 2025 the network distributed 25,184 tonnes of food, worth about 41.3 million euros, and reached about 345,000 people through 2,335 organisations, as parcels and as meals. Those are last year's figures. They are not a count of what this weekend will bring.
