Friday at about 5 p.m., the Palma local police switchboard got an emergency call from the Foners street area, Diario de Mallorca reported, citing the Policía Local de Palma. A woman who was home alone said her 15-month-old was not breathing and had started to convulse. A patrol was already nearby. The building has no elevator, so the officers ran up five flights.

The mother was holding the baby, who was not breathing and looked bluish. One of the officers took the child and gave back blows to clear the airway. The baby started breathing again and cried. An 061 ambulance arrived soon after. The medical crew took over and took the child to Son Llàtzer hospital for a closer look. The paper did not name the officers or the mother, and it did not say how the baby was doing after that evaluation.
