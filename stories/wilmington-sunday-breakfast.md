Sunday Breakfast Mission in Wilmington got ready to serve a free Thanksgiving banquet for more than 900 guests. Volunteers report at 6 a.m. The meal is family-style and continues until everyone is fed. Parents leave with vouchers for the mission's December Toy Shop. The banquet is one sitting that refuses a cutoff while people are still hungry. The voucher is the next month, a toy still ahead, written down before the chairs are stacked.
