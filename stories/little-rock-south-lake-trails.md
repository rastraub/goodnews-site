South Lake Trails opened Friday, a 13.5-mile route from Pinnacle Mountain State Park to Bufflehead Bay on Lake Maumelle, the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette reported. From the Arkansas River Trail, people can now hike or bike from downtown Little Rock to the lake's south shore.

The path also ties in Blue Mountain Natural Area and Rattlesnake Ridge Natural Area. Arkansas State Parks, the Arkansas Game and Fish Commission, Little Rock, Pulaski County, Metroplan, and Central Arkansas Water built it together. The opening is a trail story: a new way across the woods to the water, on foot or on a bike.
