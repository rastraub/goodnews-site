Two red panda cubs born at Kiryugaoka Zoo in Kiryu went on public view on November 19, the Yomiuri Shimbun reported that morning. They were born July 12 to Kazunoko, a 5-year-old male, and Fran, a 6-year-old female. The first cub is male and the second is female. The paper listed each at about 1.9 kilograms, the male at about 68 centimetres long and the female at about 66, using the zoo's measurements.

For the time being they can be seen in the red panda house on weekdays from 9 to 10 a.m., starting at opening time on the 19th. The paper noted they may not come out of the bedroom. Mayor Keiji Araki said the zoo would watch how the visits go and hoped many people would see the cubs and that the animals would stay healthy.

Nickname ideas are being collected from November 24 through November 30. Five finalists will be voted on from December 8 through 14, with an announcement planned for December 25. The news is a public debut and a naming contest for two zoo-born cubs. It does not change the number of red pandas in the wild.
