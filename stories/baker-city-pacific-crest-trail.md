Nancy Zelick, a 63-year-old retiree from Baker City, finished the Pacific Crest Trail, the Baker City Herald reported. She walked from the Mexican border to the Canadian border in about seven months and reached the northern monument on Oct. 12.

The trail runs the length of California, Oregon, and Washington. Zelick's finish is that monument at the north end, after months of walking. The Herald's account is of one person from Baker City who kept going until the border was behind her.
