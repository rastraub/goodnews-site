Petty Officer Tamica Ellis came home and surprised her three sons, 41NBC reported. She walked into Heard Elementary in Macon while the school sang a welcome-home song, and fourth-grader Jemari ran to her, just as he had predicted. She had already surprised his brothers at Morehouse and at Rutland High.

Three schools is how the day was split. Jemari is the youngest of the three surprises. The song was already underway when she walked in.
