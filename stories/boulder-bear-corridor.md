Colorado Bear Coalition volunteers and Boulder Parks and Open Space planted a native food corridor on open space west of Boulder, CBS Colorado reported. The mix includes serviceberry, rosehip, currant, wild plum, and bearberry.

The planting is meant to intercept hungry bears after a drought year, so they can feed in the hills before they reach neighborhoods. It is a new strip of plants, not a claim that bears have already stopped coming into town.
