Detroit firefighters were sent to the 12-story apartment building at 888 Pallister at about 8:12 a.m. Thursday after a fire alarm, CBS Detroit reported. Smoke was coming from a ninth-floor window. Crews started moving people out of the building.

A mother and her two children left by the usual routes. An adult man had to be taken from a ninth-floor window on a ladder truck. Battalion Chief Jeff Urbas said the first companies saw fire with a small response and called for more people, so crews could search with ground ladders and platform trucks. "We're gonna go find you. We're gonna go get you," he said.

Firefighters found the fire in one ninth-floor apartment and kept it there. Smoke damage ran from the ninth floor through the 12th. Three people went to hospitals with injuries the department described as not life-threatening. The man went to Detroit Receiving Hospital. The two children, with their mother, went to Children's Hospital of Michigan. Firefighters and EMS checked several other residents outside. Warming buses waited for people who had to leave the building while crews looked at the smoke and water damage. The cause was still under investigation.
