On Monday, November 2, two people tried to cross a river near the waterfalls at Gunlock Reservoir in a Toyota Tacoma, KSL reported, citing Washington County Sheriff Search and Rescue. Water began entering the cab. They called 911, then climbed onto the roof while crews were still on the way. The sheriff's search-and-rescue team and local fire departments carried out a swift-water rescue. The agency did not release the pair's names or ages.

The reservoir had climbed from 37 percent full last month to 109 percent by Tuesday, the Utah Division of Water Resources said, after several nearby mountain sites received more than 8 inches of precipitation between October 1 and November 1. Sheriff's officials said familiar crossings can turn dangerous when the water is high, and they asked people not to drive through swift-moving water.
