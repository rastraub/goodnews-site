The Star reported on November 14, 2026, that Johor Zoo has a female elephant calf, the first elephant birth in the zoo's nearly 100-year history. She was born on October 24 to Takau and Raja. Datuk Mohd Jafni Md Shukor, the state housing and local government committee chairman, said the pregnancy lasted 22 months. He said he hopes the calf grows up healthy, and that visitors can watch her grow.

Johor Zoo opened in 1928 and is described as the oldest zoo in Malaysia. It was founded by Sultan Sir Ibrahim and was first called Kebun Binatang. From January 1 through October 25 this year, the zoo counted 513,284 visitors. The zoo's centenary is in 2028. The news is this one birth in a zoo. It is not a count of wild elephants.
