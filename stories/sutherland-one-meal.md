One Meal Sutherland Shire has marked ten years of giving out food with no questions and no paperwork. The St George and Sutherland Shire Leader reported the anniversary on November 2, 2026. The volunteer charity provides fresh produce, pantry staples, warm meals, and other basics at weekly stops around the shire.

Coordinator David Moran said the group is now packing Christmas hampers and that demand rises when other services close over the holidays. A focus is the high-school breakfast clubs, running in six schools and reaching about 100 students a week. Members of the Woolooware golf ladies raised $18,000 for that work. Moran said each school's program costs about $4,000 a year. He described the group as small and trying to stay steady.

The same article cites Foodbank's 2026 hunger report: one in three Australian households, about 3.6 million homes, experienced food insecurity in the past year, 100,000 more than in 2025. Chairman Robert McLachlan said the people coming for help include working families, students, pensioners, and children. The local news is ten years of a no-paperwork table and the breakfast clubs. It is not a claim that the national numbers have turned.
