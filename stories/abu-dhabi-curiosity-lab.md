Khaleej Times reported on November 18, 2026, that the Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi will spend November 19 to 22 marking the anniversary of its opening on November 22, 2025, in the Saadiyat Cultural District. The lasting addition is Curiosity Lab, a permanent room for children aged three and older, opening on the anniversary Sunday. Its first edition, Into the Micro World, uses microscopes and tactile stations for insects, plants, and minerals, with large photographs by Emirati photographer Yousef Al Habshi. The lab is included in general admission. Visitors 18 and under get in free and receive a complimentary Young Explorer membership.

The same Sunday the museum will announce permanent names for its Tyrannosaurus rex and its blue whale skeleton. A jury of museum experts chose the names from public nominations. They have not been published yet. Director Peter C. Kjærgaard said the anniversary was a chance to add experiences such as the lab. The paper also noted earlier loans and exhibitions, including the fossil Lucy from Ethiopia. Those are last year's record. The news this week is the lab, the free admission for children, and the naming still to come.
