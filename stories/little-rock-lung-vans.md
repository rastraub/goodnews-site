The University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences Winthrop P. Rockefeller Cancer Institute has put the state's first mobile lung-cancer screening vans on the road, KUAF reported. Each van offers a low-dose CT scan and carries a tobacco-cessation counselor. The vans serve the rural Delta and other underserved areas. Two are running now, and six are planned. A screening scan looks for signs of disease. It is not treatment, and it is not a cure. The six is the number planned, not the number already visiting towns.
