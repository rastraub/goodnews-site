A Coast Guard helicopter crew hoisted a 30-year-old woman off the beach at Cape Arago State Park on Friday after she was injured in a fall, The Oregonian reported, citing a Coast Guard statement. The incident was shortly before 2:15 p.m. Charleston Fire District search-and-rescue teams reached her and gave first aid, then found they could not take her out by land. The Coast Guard said the rough terrain of the beach was what stopped that carry.

The Coast Guard Northwest District command center sent an MH-65 Dolphin from Air Station North Bend. The aircrew hoisted her from the beach around 3:15 p.m. and flew her to Bay Area Hospital. Officials have not shared her name, and they have not given an update on her condition.
