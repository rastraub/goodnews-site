Firefighters rescued two adults and four children from a house fire on Frederick Road, near Auburn and Ryan roads, early Thursday, ClickOnDetroit reported. Crews were sent at about 3:25 a.m. after a 911 call that people were trapped. They found the six in heavy smoke and fire and brought them out.

The station reported that all six were taken to a nearby hospital and that their conditions were not yet known. Shelby Township police officers took three family pets from the house to an emergency vet.

Fire Chief Josh Fouchia said the crews' quick response was critical to getting all six people out, and he thanked dispatchers, neighboring fire departments, and police officers. Officials said the fire appeared to have started in the basement. The cause was still being examined.
