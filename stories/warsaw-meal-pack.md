Volunteers in Warsaw packed more meals than they had aimed for, InkFreeNews reported. The Feed My Starving Children mobile pack drew 4,209 people, and 2,376 of them were students. The total they boxed up was 1,124,712 meals.

That figure is the count from this pack, past the goals the organizers had set. It is a tally of bags filled in Warsaw, not a measure of every child who needs a meal.
