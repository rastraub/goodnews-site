St. Louis firefighters carried a mother raccoon and four kits out of a building fire and gave them oxygen. The mother and three of the kits recovered enough to scamper off. One kit went to a veterinarian. The crews' job was the building. On the way they treated the animals they found inside the same way they treat anyone who has been in the smoke: out first, then air. Four of the five left on their own feet. The fifth left in a carrier, toward a vet.
