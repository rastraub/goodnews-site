Katie Guidry, an English teacher at Denham Springs High School, was surprised at a school assembly on Friday with a $25,000 Milken Educator Award, WBRZ reported. Milken and Louisiana State Superintendent Cade Brumley were there for the 40th anniversary tour of the awards. Guidry also teaches Educators Rising, a class meant to grow future teachers in Louisiana. The $25,000 is the award announced at that assembly. The report does not describe how she plans to use it.
