Artist Calley O'Neill and volunteers are assembling a stained-glass mosaic along a wall at Ellison Onizuka Kona International Airport, the Hawaii Tribune-Herald reported. The mural is planned at 321 feet and is meant to honor aloha aina, a care for the land. Local kupuna, elders in the community, have helped shape the design. The pieces are still being set, so the wall is a project underway rather than a finished mural.
