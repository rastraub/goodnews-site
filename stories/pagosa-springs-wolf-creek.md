A month after historic flooding, Pagosa Springs is welcoming visitors back, CBS Colorado reported. CDOT reopened Wolf Creek Pass this week. Wolf Creek Ski Area has every run open, 100 percent of its terrain, on a 36-inch base.

The month is the time since the flood. The reopened pass is how people get there. Every run open is the ski area's report, on snow 36 inches deep.
