Colorado Springs Public Works built a custom zipline that runs off a truck winch, The Gazette reported. The line carries trash bags and bulky debris out of camps that crews cannot easily reach on foot or by truck.

A cleanup that used to take a day or more now takes two to three hours. The city has used the system on 13 large camps. Those figures describe the tool and where it has already been put to work, not a claim that every camp in the city has been cleared.
