San Diego lifeguard Griffin Houldin, 29, swam through repeated waves at La Jolla Cove to reach a struggling swimmer, NBC News reported. Video from Saturday shows him taking waves on the head and still moving, a red rescue can trailing on its tow line. The rescue lasted more than three minutes. The swimmer missed the can on the first try. On the second he held on through wave after wave while the pair were pulled back several feet at a time. Houldin told him to hold fast.

Fellow lifeguard Cortez Ritumalta came in on a jet ski and pulled Houldin and the swimmer out of the impact zone. Both have been lifeguards for more than 10 years. Houldin said the surf had roughly doubled from late morning to early afternoon, and that he would have told the man not to swim, or at least to use fins. The swimmer later said he had misjudged the conditions. San Diego Lifeguard Services, part of the Fire-Rescue Department, completed 175 rescues in the past week, officials told the network. Forecasters had high surf along the Southern California coast.
