Fifteen student-led projects on environmental problems were shown at BhooMe Festival 2026 in Puducherry. The Hindu reported the showcase on November 11, 2026. Svarnim Puducherry and the Sri Aurobindo Society organized it under the theme "Where Young Minds Can Heal the Planet." The festival began on World Environment Day as a three-month action-research project. Students from primary through higher secondary were asked to name a local environmental challenge and work on a practical response.

Organizers said 22 projects were registered and 15 were completed. Eight came from government schools and seven from private schools. The topics included pollinator surveys, soil health, sustainability, waste, coastal regeneration, farming and food systems, and water. Four projects won awards, two in a junior category and two in a senior category. The paper did not name the winning projects or the schools. The festival also gave students practice in action research outside a regular lesson. The result is 15 finished projects on display. It is not a measurement of pollution, rainfall, or how many pollinators the city has.
