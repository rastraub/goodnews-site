Two Indian Ocean humpback dolphins that stranded on the Vadhvan coast were back in the sea the same morning. Mid-day reported on November 28, 2026, that the animals came ashore on Friday. Forest staff, Dahanu's rapid rescue unit, and local fishermen used floating stretchers to move them. Honorary wildlife warden Hardik Soni said the dolphins looked fit. Rescuers carried them into shoulder-deep water and released them. The species is listed in Schedule I of India's Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.

Soni told the paper that similar strandings have happened on this stretch of the Palghar coast, and that rapid tidal changes could be one reason the animals beach. The report is a same-day release of two dolphins that appeared well enough to go. It does not say whether anyone stayed to watch them swim off, or whether this pair had stranded here before.
