A 50-year-old hiker who fell on a steep, rocky hillside near Valley Springs was hoisted off the slope by helicopter on Saturday, myMotherLode.com reported. He was in the Gann Road and Bear Mountain area near New Hogan Lake, and he fractured an ankle in the fall.

Calaveras Consolidated Fire said the difficult terrain and the patient's injuries led crews to start a helicopter hoist. California Highway Patrol Helicopter 24 completed the hoist and lifted him off the hillside. He was transferred to American Legion Ambulance and taken to an area hospital by ground. CAL FIRE and PHI Air Medical 42 also responded, along with the ambulance. The report does not give his name or say how he was doing after he reached the hospital.
