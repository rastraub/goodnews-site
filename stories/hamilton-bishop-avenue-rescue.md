Hamilton police Officer Terry Kiep was close by when a crash call came in for Route 4 and Bishop Avenue on Nov. 5, FOX19 reported. One of the cars was on fire, and a woman was still in the driver's seat. Video from the Fairfield Police Department showed flames coming through the windshield. Kiep said he had never seen fire come through glass.

He said the flames kept him from pulling her out, and he did not want to hurt her, so he went back for the fire extinguisher in his car. After he used it, he and officers from Fairfield got her out of the car and away from the fire. Hamilton and Fairfield first responders then helped her at the scene.

Kiep said the two departments had not worked a call like that together before. The cause of the crash was still under investigation. FOX19 did not name the woman.
