Bedford firefighter Josh Brown, a veteran driver, spent Thanksgiving with a man in crisis above a freeway. He talked with him and hugged him more than once. Then he brought him safely down in the aerial bucket. Drivers who saw the video, which has drawn about 250,000 likes, praised the calm of it. The coverage's line was that you don't know what a hug can do. If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988.
