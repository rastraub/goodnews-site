Kidsave used a trail in Richmond to introduce older foster youth to people who might become family, WTVR CBS 6 reported. Hike With Your Heart was built around that meeting. One couple is adopting a girl they met through it.

The hike is one event, and the adoption is one match that came out of it. The report does not say every young person on the trail has a family lined up.
