Penn Medicine's Scheie Eye Institute in MOTION van brings free screening for glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and other eye conditions into underserved Philadelphia neighborhoods, WHYY reported. Ophthalmologists read the tests by telehealth. Donor funds can cover treatment for patients who are uninsured. The van is a screening service in those neighborhoods. The donor support is for treatment when someone without insurance needs it. A screening find is not, by itself, completed care.
