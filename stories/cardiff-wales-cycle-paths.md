The Welsh government has set aside £4.5 million for cycling projects before the Tour de France Grand Départ in 2027. Positive News reported the package on October 30, 2026. The opening stages are due to start in Edinburgh on July 2 and finish in Cardiff on July 4.

Fifty-four local initiatives share a £3.19 million pot for cycling hubs, trail upgrades, and e-bike charging. Another £1.3 million goes to the Croeso i Feicwyr, or Welcome to Cyclists, Business Ready fund, which helps businesses and community groups become easier to reach by bike. Named projects include a "king of the mountains" loop on famous climbs in and around Bannau Brycheiniog national park, and the Holyhead Gateway Coastal Cycling Route on Anglesey, meant to make the island's coast easier to ride.

Cabinet minister Adam Price called the Grand Départ a once-in-a-generation chance and said the aim is a tourism and riding legacy after the race leaves. The report is the funding and those project types. It does not say which of the 54 schemes are already under construction.
