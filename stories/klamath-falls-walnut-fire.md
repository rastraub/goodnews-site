Klamath County Fire District No. 1 answered an apartment fire at 415 Walnut Avenue in Klamath Falls at about 5:23 p.m. on Monday, Nov. 16, KDRV reported, citing the district. Firefighters removed one person from the apartment and got the fire under control. Paramedics took that person to Sky Lakes Medical Center. The district said the patient was in stable condition and was going for a further medical check.

Nineteen people from the district went, on three engines, a ladder truck, two ambulances, and three command vehicles. Kingsley Fire sent another engine. Officials said no other residents were hurt and no firefighters were injured. The cause is under investigation. The district did not name the person who was rescued.
