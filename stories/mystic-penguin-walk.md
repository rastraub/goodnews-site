More than 1,000 people ran and walked at Mystic Aquarium's Penguin Run Walk, the biggest crowd the event has had since before the pandemic. The day raises money for conservation of African penguins, which are critically endangered.

The turnout is the news from Mystic. It does not, by itself, change the status of the birds.
