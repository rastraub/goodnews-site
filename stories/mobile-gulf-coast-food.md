Feeding the Gulf Coast took in more than 30,000 pounds of food through the America Gives campaign, FOX10 reported. The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints donated the load. William Parkes, speaking for the church, said the hope is that someone can think about something other than where the next meal will come from.

The number is the weight delivered in Mobile, a warehouse total, not a count of meals already served.
