Carlow County Library has a new sensory area inside a rebuilt children's section. The Carlow Nationalist reported on October 11, 2026, that the room opened during National Library Day, after about three months of work. Librarian James Durkan told the paper that neighbors had been clear for several years that they wanted a sensory space in the building.

The children's library was painted, refurnished, and given a new layout, with the work designed to sit alongside the older character of the building. Durkan described it as bright, open, and meant to be a place with something for everyone. Music Generation Carlow's young group, Reel Óg, played at the launch.

The library service got funding from the Department of Rural and Community Development and the Gaeltacht, matched by Carlow County Council. The staff's hope, the paper said, is that the room brings more people through the door. The article is an account of the opening. It does not report how many children have used the sensory area since.
