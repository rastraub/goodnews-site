Saturday evening, Minneapolis firefighters raised a father and son off a rock ledge south of Minnehaha Falls, FOX 9 reported. The department said the call came at 5:20 p.m. near 48th Street and Minnehaha Avenue. The two had been hiking when they ended up on a slippery ledge about 75 to 100 feet below the trail, and they could not climb up or down. The Technical Rescue Team set a rope system, went down into the ravine, and secured each person in a rescue harness. Crews raised them one at a time onto stable ground. The department said no injuries were reported. It did not say how the pair first reached the ledge, or how long they had been there.
