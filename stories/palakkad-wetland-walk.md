A Wildlife Week walk through wetlands in Palakkad recorded about 30 bird species, including a lesser coucal. The Hindu reported on October 8, 2026, that the Biodiversity Management Committee of Akathethara grama panchayat and the Birds Club of Government Victoria College, Palakkad, led the outing. Fifty-three people walked 2.5 kilometers through the Chendamangalam wetlands and Kunnampara Biodiversity Park in the rain.

The lesser coucal, known locally as Pulluppan or Variyuppan, is less common in Kerala than the greater coucal. Coordinators were Lijo Panangadan and Yesudas. The list is what that group saw on one wet morning. It is not a full survey of the wetland.
