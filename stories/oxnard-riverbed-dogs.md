Ventura County Animal Services and Dog Days Search and Rescue brought 22 dogs out of an Oxnard riverbed, the Los Angeles Times reported. Eight are puppies. The dogs are being vaccinated, spayed or neutered, and microchipped, and they are available for adoption.

The pack is 22 animals, safely in care. Adoption is the next step the shelter is offering. The story is this rescue, not a cleared riverbed for good.
