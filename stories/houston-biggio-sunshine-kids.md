At Daikin Park, Hall of Famer Craig Biggio stayed beside Charlie Garmon and walked him around the bases. Garmon is recovering from a rare brain tumor and from seven surgeries. The day was for children with cancer, hosted by the Sunshine Kids and the Astros Foundation. Biggio did not wave from a distance. He walked the bases with Charlie.
