Dottie Moore, a retired special-education teacher, volunteers every week at Ronald McDonald House of Delaware, and sometimes twice in a week, CBS Philadelphia reported. She has been doing it for two and a half years. Gluten-free muffins are often what she bakes for families staying there while a child is in the hospital. She is one of 300 active volunteers at the house. The muffins and the weekly shift are the help she describes. They do not take the place of the child's medical care.
