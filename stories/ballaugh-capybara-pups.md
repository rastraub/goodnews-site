Eight capybara pups were born to two mothers at Curraghs Wildlife Park, on September 26 and October 1. BBC Newsround reported the births on October 20, 2026.

The wetland park at Ballaugh, on the Isle of Man, had not had a litter since a single pup four years ago. Park manager Kathleen Graham said the October weekend was the busiest the park has recorded. Capybaras are from South America. They are the world's largest rodent and can reach about 1.3 metres long. Graham connects the animals to a lesson on wetlands, which soak up rain. The news is these eight pups. It is not a census of the species.
