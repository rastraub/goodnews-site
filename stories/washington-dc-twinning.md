D.C. United's Twinning Project, now in its third year, is an eight-week program inside the D.C. Jail, WTOP reported. It teaches 20 low-risk residents teamwork and leadership through futsal and coaching classes. Some participants go on to coach their children's teams after they are released. The 20 is the size of this class. The eight weeks are the length of the course. Coaching a child's team afterward is what some graduates have done, not a result promised for every person in the class.
