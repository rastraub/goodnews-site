More than 300 people walked through Bergfeld Park on White Cane Day with the East Texas Lighthouse for the Blind, the Tyler Morning Telegraph reported. Walkers used white canes along the route. Booths at the park offered information on programs for people with vision loss.

The Lighthouse was founded in 1976, so the walk marked 50 years. Alicia Lansford, vice president and chief mission officer, spoke about the community support behind the work. The organization provides employment, rehabilitation, education, and training so people with vision loss can live independently. It serves 40 East Texas counties, and the services are free.
