Children's librarian Amanda Bowden, known at the Portneuf District Library as Miss Amanda, is the Idaho Library Association's Librarian of the Year, KIFI reported. The award, given last week, recognizes her work in Chubbuck and Pocatello. The library said she learns the names of her smallest storytime visitors and builds events, including an Eggcessible Easter Egg Hunt, so more people can take part.

Bowden leads storytimes and programs for more than 400 preschool-aged children. She said her boss nominated her and colleagues wrote letters, and she did not know she had won until the email came. She told the station the honor that matters is getting to help children like reading.
