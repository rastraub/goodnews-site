Kensington Gardens Reserve has a new playground, opened on October 28 after the old equipment reached the end of its life. Glam Adelaide reported the reopening on November 1, 2026. City of Burnside chief executive Julia Grant said the replacement was meant to match current practice on design, accessibility, and inclusion.

The new space takes its cues from local birds. It includes a custom bird-hide play structure, climbing equipment, monkey bars, a flying fox, a cliff rider, a trampoline, swings, and a hamster wheel. Hidden artworks and a treasure hunt are built in. Accessibility is part of the design: an accessible trampoline, inclusive equipment, non-verbal communication signs, and pram ramps between the playground and the public toilets.

The project budget was under one million Australian dollars. The address is The Parade and West Terrace. The report is the new playground, not a count of how many children will use it.
