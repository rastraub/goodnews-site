The Hindu reported on November 15, 2026, that the District Central Library in Tiruchi is marking the 69th National Library Week with its Readers' Forum. A press note said Monday's plan includes a handicrafts fair, a health camp for women, and a cookery contest for healthy recipes. People who want to enter can call the number in the release.

The week had already started. On Saturday the library held a book exhibition and a numismatic exhibit. On Sunday more than 50 children took part in drawing and fancy-dress contests. The news is those events and the children who showed up. It is not a membership drive with a published total.
