Seven Happiness Dream libraries for children in farming and fishing communities were marked with an opening ceremony on November 8 at the Goa Community Child Center in Gumi. The Seoul Economic Daily reported the company's account on November 10, 2026. The program began in 2024 to narrow a gap in reading. It set up 15 libraries that year and 10 last year. This year's seven are in Gumi, in Jinan in Jeonbuk, and in Miryang in South Gyeongsang.

Across all 32 rooms, the paper said, about 33,000 books have been given, and about 2,000 children are the ones who benefit. The news is those libraries and that pile of books. It does not measure how reading scores changed.
