Firefighters from the Bay District, Ridge, and Hollywood volunteer departments were sent at about 10:13 a.m. Tuesday to a house on Cardigan Place in Lexington Park, Southern Maryland News Net reported. Callers said a 25-year-old man and a dog were stuck in a hole about six feet deep, and that the man might be hurt and felt faint.

Crews found the man and a large dog in the hole. They set a ladder and brought the man out. EMS checked him. He said he was not injured, and he was not taken to a hospital. The dog was large enough that firefighters built a tripod rope system, the Arizona Vortex kit named in the report, and lowered a firefighter in. Ridge and Bay District firefighters had the dog out at about 10:45 a.m. The report says no one was injured, and the units were back in service within an hour. It does not name the man or the dog.
