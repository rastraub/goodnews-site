Louisville Metro Animal Services held a Tailgate Adoption Pawty and adopted out 30 animals, WHAS11 reported. Sponsored fees helped. The total was triple a typical Sunday.

Families met animals at the Louisville event. Thirty is the number that were adopted. The fees were sponsored, which is why the cost did not fall on those families. A typical Sunday at the shelter is about a third of that day's adoptions, the station said.
