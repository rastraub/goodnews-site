A young grey seal wedged between rocks at Brixham was pulled free and swam off, BBC News reported. The rescue was on Saturday. British Divers Marine Life Rescue joined the Seal Project and the coxswain of the Brixham RNLI lifeboat after a caller reached Sarah Greenslade, a marine mammal medic and founder of the Seal Project.

Greenslade said the seal was well and truly stuck, very sleepy, and clearly exhausted, with the tide coming in. A usual approach is from behind, with a towel. This time only the head end was reachable, so the team pulled her out backwards by the rear flippers. A check found her unharmed, and she was allowed to swim away. Another seal that had been watching left with her. Greenslade thought the animal was about a couple of months old. The news is this release. It is not a later sighting of the same seal.
