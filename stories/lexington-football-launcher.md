Sam Roberts, 16, of Lancaster, wanted to throw a football with his friends. Kentucky Today reported that he has 4H leukodystrophy, a rare disorder with a few hundred known patients worldwide and no treatment that reverses the genetic cause. When he said so at the University of Kentucky's Child Development Center of the Bluegrass, engineering students took the request.

Audra Proffitt and her team adapted a ball cannon so he could launch passes. His first throws flew, and the people watching cheered. He is set to use the device with his teammates this fall. The launcher does not change the disorder. It gives him a way onto the field.
