A man was pulled from the water after a boat fire about three miles off El Capitan on Tuesday afternoon, News Channel 3-12 reported, citing the Santa Barbara County Fire Department.

County fire crews went with the U.S. Coast Guard and the Santa Barbara City Harbor Patrol. A Harbor Patrol vessel pulled the man from the water. The fire department said he had no injuries. Crews took him back to Santa Barbara Harbor for a medical assessment.

The department said the cause of the fire is still under investigation. The report does not name the man or say how the fire started.
