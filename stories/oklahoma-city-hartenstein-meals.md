Oklahoma City Thunder center Isaiah Hartenstein, his family, and his foundation served Thanksgiving meals to about 350 people on Monday, KOCO 5 News reported. The meal was at the Boys & Girls Club. Hartenstein said he wants to make the gathering bigger each year and help as many people as possible.

Carly Johnson, the club's director of development, said children and families know him and get excited to see him. The meal was part of the Thunder's 19th annual Holiday Assist, the team's holiday effort for families who need a hand. The news is Monday's meal for about 350 people. It is one stop in that larger program.
