Two hundred and forty freshwater turtle hatchlings were released into the Ganga at Lahara Ghat in Kasganj on November 4. Dynamite News reported the release on November 5, 2026. The turtles were two species, the brown roofed turtle, Pangshura smithii, and the Indian tent turtle, Pangshura tentoria. WWF-India organized the afternoon with the District Ganga Committee and the forest department. The date marked the anniversary of the Ganga being declared India's national river. The paper did not state which anniversary year it was.

The eggs had been collected from the Ganga in Kasganj district. After hatching, the young were raised under a conservation protocol and then returned to the river. Dynamite News named chief development officer Virendra Kumar, divisional forest officer Sanjeev Kumar, higher-education nodal officer S. K. Rawat, and WWF-India senior project officer Himanshu Sharma among the people present, along with a range officer and local farmers. The news is these 244 hatchlings. It is not a population estimate for turtles in the Ganga.
