A hiker set out around 6 p.m. on Oct. 22 at Lizard's Mouth, the sandstone formation above Santa Barbara, and lost the way while trying to return, edhat reported, citing Santa Barbara County Search and Rescue. The team was dispatched at 6:29 p.m. after he called 911.

Rescuers got him on the phone, collected details, and sent a text asking for his location. They prepared a search map and put three field teams out. Team 1 reached him and walked him back to the road and his vehicle. The agency said the paths are easy to misread once the light fades, and it told hikers to check the sunset time, carry a headlamp, and keep an offline map. The report is that walk out. It does not describe an injury.
