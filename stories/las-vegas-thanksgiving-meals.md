Las Vegas Metropolitan Police officers and volunteers filled holiday meal bags at department headquarters on Tuesday, FOX5 Las Vegas reported. The bags contained frozen turkeys and traditional side dishes. The LVMPD Foundation said more than 600 meals would go to families already selected in all 12 area commands. Community-oriented policing teams had identified those families, and officers were to take the bags to their doors.

Naqvi Injury Law and Broken Coffee helped pay for the meals, the foundation said. Farhan Naqvi, founder of the law firm, said people should get to have the turkey dinner without stressing over the bill. Karen Marben, executive director of the LVMPD Foundation, said the deliveries are a chance for families to see officers as people who want to help.
