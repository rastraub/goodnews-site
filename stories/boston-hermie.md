Hermie, a therapy guinea pig, is part of how some Boston students get through a hard day, The Washington Post reported. The animal spends time with Harvard Medical School students and with other people who want a quieter minute.

A therapy visit is company, not a treatment plan. The Post's account is Hermie's rounds in Boston, one small animal and the people who sit with that animal on purpose.
