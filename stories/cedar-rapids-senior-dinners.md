Volunteer drivers carried Thanksgiving dinners to nearly 730 Eastern Iowa seniors. The meals came from AbbeHealth Aging Services through Meals on Wheels, and the drivers brought a holiday greeting with the food. One route included a grandfather and his grandchildren. Most of the people on the list live alone. The turkey is what fits in the container. The knock, and a grandchild on the step, is why the route exists on a day when a banquet downtown would not reach them.
