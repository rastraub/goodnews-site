A Busan firefighter on the 119 desk turned a call with no words into a rescue on Monday, Yonhap News Agency reported. About 5:11 a.m., the situation room at the Busan Fire and Disaster Headquarters took a call that carried only a faint groan. Fire Sergeant Joo Sang-hyun kept the line open. He judged it an emergency and started tracking the phone. The first fix, from Wi-Fi, could be off by 10 to 100 meters. A GPS fix narrowed that to about 5 to 50 meters. He sent an ambulance crew and asked police to respond with them.

The ambulance crew saw white smoke coming off a building and told the situation room. The room sent a fire engine as well. The ambulance crew used an extinguisher from their vehicle to knock the fire back before it spread. The fire was out by about 5:41 a.m. The caller was in an inner room of a restaurant on the first floor, consciousness lowered after breathing smoke, and was taken to a hospital. Yonhap reported that the caller has regained consciousness and can communicate. Fire officials are still looking into the cause. A headquarters spokesperson said the center intends to keep treating even a small sound from a caller as a reason to move.
