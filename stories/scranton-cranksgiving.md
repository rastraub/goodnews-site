The Anthracite Bicycle Coalition's annual Cranksgiving ride, starting at Groove Brewing, collected nearly $1,400 in food and $400 in cash for the NEPA Youth Shelter, FOX56 reported. The two figures together are about $1,800, which is the total the station used for the drive. The food and the cash are what the ride brought in for the shelter's food bank. The event is the collection. It is not a description of how long those groceries will last.
