The Regional Food Bank of Oklahoma marked its one billionth meal at a ceremony in Oklahoma City, KOKH reported. The food bank has served 53 counties since 1980, and the billion is the total packed across that history, not a single day's output. The Oklahoma City Thunder surprised the gathering with a $25,000 donation. The milestone is the billionth meal. The Thunder gift is a separate $25,000 given at the celebration.
