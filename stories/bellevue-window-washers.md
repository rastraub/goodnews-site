Friday evening, rope-rescue crews from Bellevue and Redmond brought two window washers down from the 39th floor of a downtown high-rise, FOX 13 Seattle reported. The Bellevue Fire Department said the call came in around 5 p.m. at 555 108th Avenue Northeast. The workers were about 532 feet above the ground. Bellevue police sent a drone to help, and Redmond firefighters assisted. The departments said the crews worked in high winds and darkness. The report does not say how the washers became stranded. Both of them reached the ground.
