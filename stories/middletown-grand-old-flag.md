Anthony Chatowsky, an 89-year-old Navy veteran, asked to sing to the flag during a veterans pinning ceremony at his senior living community in Middletown, NBC 10 WJAR reported. He sang "You're a Grand Old Flag," and the room joined him. A video of the impromptu performance passed 17,000 views. The song was not on a program he had been assigned. He asked, people sang with him, and the recording spread past that view count.
