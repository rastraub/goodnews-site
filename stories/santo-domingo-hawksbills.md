The National Aquarium released 105 hawksbill sea turtles on Monday at Los Pescadores beach, on the Malecón in Santo Domingo. N Telemicro reported the release on November 2, 2026. The station said the turtles went back to the water as part of the aquarium's work to protect endangered species.

The same note said the aquarium is marking an anniversary with a week of science, education, and family activities, and that entry would be free on two days. The turtle paragraph is short. It does not say whether the 105 were hatchlings, juveniles, or animals that had been rehabilitated, and it does not name the beach conditions or a count from earlier releases. The verified number is 105 hawksbills at that beach on that day.
