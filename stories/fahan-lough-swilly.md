Lough Swilly RNLI volunteers launched twice on Saturday, the charity reported on November 23, 2026. The first call, at 11:40 a.m., sent the inshore lifeboat Davdot to look for a possible person in the water between Father Hegarty's Rock and Stragill Beach. The search found helium birthday balloons that had broken free. With no one in danger, the crew recovered the balloons and stood down.

At 4:30 p.m. the all-weather lifeboat Derek Bullivant launched for a 35-foot sailing yacht with two people aboard. A snagged propeller left it unable to continue safely under its own power. Volunteers found the yacht in mid-channel off Lisfannon Beach, in fading light and an offshore wind. They checked the people aboard and towed the boat to the nearest safe harbor. It was secured alongside at Fahan Marina. The news is those two calls: balloons, then a tow. The RNLI did not report any injury.
