Local Girl Scouts introduced Patch Pals at Oregon Humane Society campuses in Portland and Salem, Your Oregon News reported. The blueberry-muffin-style cookie is the first Girl Scout cookie made for dogs, and shelter pups got an early taste.

Online sales are set to start Jan. 12, 2027. They are not open yet. The debut was a tasting for dogs at the two campuses, not a store launch.
