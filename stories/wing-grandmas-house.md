Larry and Judy Bergquist of Wing want their collections to show people how they grew up, KFYR reported. Judy calls the house where Larry was raised Grandma's House. It is packed with objects she can explain one by one. Among them is a pump organ from the 1960s. When they were dating she asked him for a loan. He refused, then surprised her with the organ.

Earlier this month Larry brought out the tractors and threshing machines and ran them. He told the station that threshing let him miss school as a kid, and that it is history. The couple has held a threshing bee on the farm for years and still travels to other shows around North Dakota.
