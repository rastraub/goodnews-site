University staff in Tacloban have started a reading project that is meant to come back every month, not just on launch day. Eastern Visayas State University said on November 27, 2026, that its non-teaching personnel launched Project TUKLAS on November 26 for children in Barangays 43-A and 43-B. The name stands for Transforming Understanding and Knowledge through Literacy Activities for Children. At the opening, staff read stories aloud. Vilmar Silvano A. Solis, chief administrative officer for finance, told the room the point was to extend education past the campus.

City social welfare staff welcomed the books as something daycare centers are judged on when they are assessed. The chairwomen of the two barangays thanked the university for adopting the neighborhoods. Doris Ann S. Espina, chief administrative officer for administrative services, said volunteers will visit both places every month for storytelling and reading, and that the project will also hold a monthly reading contest. The news is a launch that already included a story hour, plus a schedule for the visits. It does not say how many children came on the first day, or how many books changed hands.
