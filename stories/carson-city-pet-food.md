A Fill a Vehicle pet-food drive at Michael Hohl Chevrolet, with the Reno Corvettes Club, collected 2,551 pounds of food and supplies worth $9,691, Carson Now reported. Donations went to Feeding Pets of the Homeless through FISH.

The drive filled one truck and started another. The weight and the dollar figure are what came in at this collection, and FISH is the local partner that will move it to people who need help feeding their pets.
