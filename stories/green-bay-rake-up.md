Volunteers are clearing leaves for Green Bay residents who cannot do it themselves, FOX 11 News reported on November 7, 2026. The Volunteer Center of Brown County opened this year's Rake Up Green Bay that morning, pairing helpers with people limited by age, disability, or resources. Rakers have until Sunday, November 15, to get leaves to the curb for city pickup. That date is the city's last day for the collection.

Organizers asked people who can to come back more than once. The online application is closed, and the center said it will still talk by phone with anyone who wants to help. The report is the start of the raking window. It does not give a count of yards finished.
