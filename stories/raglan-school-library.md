Raglan Area School has a new library built for sitting down with a book, and the windows face the sea. The Raglan Chronicle reported on October 28, 2026, that the room keeps the name Te Rapunga, given by the late Wini Bidois, a former teacher, so students would go looking for knowledge.

Librarian Nic Taylor said the shelves are on wheels and can be shifted for different lessons. Older students have a quiet lunch spot, and younger children have their own corner. Taylor told the paper it is a calm place to work and that the ocean view is the best at the school. Six-year-olds Quinn Sandwell and Byron Parsons were reading there. One said it felt relaxing and that books can go home. The other mentioned Minecraft and Pokémon titles and the view.

The Chronicle did not publish a count of the collection. What it did describe is a room arranged so children will stay and read, under a name that asks them to keep searching.
