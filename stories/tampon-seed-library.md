The university library in Tampon now has a seed cabinet anyone can use. The University of Reunion said on its site, in a notice dated October 27, 2026, that the cabinet was inaugurated on October 13.

The rule is simple: take seeds, sow them, harvest, and leave a new lot for the next person. Librarians looked at other seed libraries on the island and asked researchers in the humanities and environment faculty, and at CIRAD, for advice. The cabinet itself was built from recycled materials. The university describes it as a free, public way to share Réunion's plant heritage and to give students and neighbors a concrete part in that sharing.

The notice does not list species or say how many packets moved on the first day. It records an open shelf and a borrow-and-return rule, not a yield.
