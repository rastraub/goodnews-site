Bedford County officials said on October 19, 2026, that an anonymous donor is covering adoption fees for every animal at the Bedford County Animal Shelter through the end of the year. WSLS reported that the shelter has stayed near capacity for most of 2026 and has recently taken in more cats and kittens.

Deputy County Administrator D.W. Lawhorne said the donor is an animal lover who knew the kennels were crowded and hoped that dropping the fee would help more animals leave for homes. The gift does not empty the shelter by itself. It removes the cost that can stop an adoption, from now through December, for whatever animals the shelter is trying to place.
