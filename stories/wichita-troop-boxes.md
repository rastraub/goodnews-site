Volunteer Kansas held its Presents in a Package event in Wichita to support deployed troops at Christmas, KWCH 12 reported. For five years, volunteers have packed boxes for Kansas service members who will be away from home. The packages hold toiletries, food, entertainment, Christmas items, and cards from schoolchildren. Executive director Nola Brown said about half of the men and women who are deployed never get anything from home. The organization said it had enough supplies to make about 400 boxes this year. That figure is the supply on hand for the packing, not a confirmation that 400 boxes have already been delivered.
