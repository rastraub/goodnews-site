DART and the Food Bank of Delaware have opened the 29th Stuff the Bus Thanksgiving drive, WMDT reported. A bus is parked at grocery stores around the state through Nov. 7 so shoppers can leave nonperishable food for family meals.

The goal is 15,000 pounds. That number is the target, not a final weight. Collections are statewide even though the campaign is often associated with Dover.
