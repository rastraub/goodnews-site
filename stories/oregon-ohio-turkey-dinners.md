Afghanistan veteran Tyler Palmer has run every day for eight months in Oregon, Ohio, carrying an American flag, WTOL 11 reported. The runs raised $1 a mile, and a neighbor matched that money. Palmer and his family then handed out 118 full turkey dinners in town. They promised the effort would be bigger next year. The 118 dinners are what went out this round. The eight months of daily runs are how the money was gathered.
