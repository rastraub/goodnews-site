Diario del Soconusco reported on November 15, 2026, that students from Colegio de Bachilleres Plantel 255 in Puerto Madero returned to the regional sea turtle museum in Ejido Emiliano Zapata, Mazatán, to continue a research project. The paper said the students joined a program of the National Autonomous University of Mexico and received a national prize for work in Puerto Madero and at the museum. It does not name the prize.

Biologist José Alfredo González Gerardo, who advises field sampling at the museum, said he was proud to host Proyecto MUSA Latido Marino and Movimiento STEM again, because the project lets high school students learn conservation on a real reserve. Biologist Erika Rodríguez Morales, of the community flora and fauna protection group, came as a methods adviser. González also thanked donors for supplies in the last stretch of night patrols for Mazatán's turtle program. The news is the students' return and the prize the paper reports. It is not a new hatchling count.
