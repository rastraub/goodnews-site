VTV reported on November 18, 2026, that three capybara pups at Saigon Zoo and Botanical Gardens in Ho Chi Minh City have reached nearly one month old. They are named Anh, Yeu, and Em. Together the names mean "I love you." Hundreds of people came to see them despite the rain.

The report does not give a birth date, the parents' names, or whether the three are male or female. The news is the one-month mark and the crowd that turned out for it.
