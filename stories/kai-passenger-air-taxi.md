A two-seat electric aircraft with no pilot aboard carried one passenger on a short, pre-programmed hop in Yamanashi Prefecture. Euronews reported on November 21, 2026, that the flight was on Friday, November 20, at an academy runway in Kai, with Mount Fuji in the distance. The aircraft is an EH216-S built by the Chinese firm EHang, lifted by 16 rotors. The passenger flight followed the runway for about 380 metres. A second flight, with nobody aboard, covered about 1,800 metres above the nearby Kamanashi River.

Euronews said the type is engineered to skip a conventional runway, to reach speeds up to 120 kilometres an hour, and to cover roughly 30 kilometres on a charge. Those are the aircraft's design figures, not the distance flown on Friday. The demonstration was meant to show the public a possible short hop and to test the idea. The same report said regulatory, safety, and infrastructure hurdles still stand in the way of a commercial service. Friday's news is that one passenger flew a planned route and landed. It is not a ticket anyone can buy.
