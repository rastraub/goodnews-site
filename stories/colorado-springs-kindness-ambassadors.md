Six Kindness Ambassadors in Colorado Springs were celebrated at a screening of The Power of Small, The Gazette reported. The group includes Greccio Housing volunteers Gordon and Thea Platt. It also includes the owners of Urban Egg, who surprise one teacher a month with $500.

The TDB Family Foundation gave the ambassadors grants for their nonprofits. The honors were for that local work. The screening was the gathering where the six were recognized.
