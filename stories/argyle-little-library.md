The Post-Star reported on October 6, 2026, that a Little Free Library is now outside the Cornell Cooperative Extension office at 101 Main Street in Argyle. Dog Gone Kids 4-H donated the box, its third in Washington County. Grandparents Ron and Linda Fuller built it, and Reid’s Hardware set the post in concrete. Anyone may take a book or leave one. The club collected books for children and adults over the summer and plans to keep the box stocked. The extension office has asked neighbors to bring cookbooks and magazines, and to skip old encyclopedias and outdated books.
