The Hindu reported on November 15, 2026, that the Telangana Tourism Development Corporation has started a weekly nature walk called Wings and Wilderness. The first walk was on Sunday at Ghar-e-Mubarak in Nanakramguda, Hyderabad, with 30 birdwatchers and other nature enthusiasts. Guides used pocket field guides and pointed out how to separate species by their calls, and they talked about how native and non-native plants give birds somewhere to live. The corporation said the walks will run every Sunday.

The paper's list from that morning included shikra, Indian peafowl, black drongo, green bee-eater, kingfisher, golden oriole, Asian koel, red-vented bulbul, red-wattled lapwing, Indian silverbill, and grey francolin. The news is this first walk and the plan to repeat it. It is not a full bird census of Hyderabad.
