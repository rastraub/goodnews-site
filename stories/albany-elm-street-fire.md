Albany firefighters pulled an elderly man out of a house full of smoke on Wednesday afternoon, KPTV reported. Crews were sent about 4:30 p.m. to a fire near Southwest 14th Avenue and Southwest Elm Street. They found a single-story house filled with smoke.

The man was checked for smoke inhalation. He did not need a ride to the hospital. The fire was burning in one bedroom, and the crews kept it from spreading past that room. Two people who live there are displaced. The fire department said they are working with their own insurance on a temporary place to stay. The cause is still under investigation.

Deputy Fire Marshal Sandy Roberts used the fire as a reminder to keep a working smoke alarm in every bedroom and on each floor. "During a house fire, we only have about three minutes before smoke can overtake us," she said. "A working smoke alarm gives us every chance to get out safely."
