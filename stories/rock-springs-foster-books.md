Kelley Brown of Sweetwater County is again trying to put a personalized backpack of new books in the hands of every foster child in Wyoming, Wyo4News reported. The state has about 400 children in foster care. Last year every child received a pack. This season's drive is the aim to do that again by Christmas. It is a goal in progress, built on last year's completed gift, and it is organized from Rock Springs for children across the state.
