Priest River Police Officer Scott Kennedy and Bonner County Sheriff's deputies Keith Trout and Justin Penn received the Idaho Life Saving Medal and a letter of commendation, the Hagadone News Network reported. The Priest River City Council presented the honors at its Nov. 2 meeting. The rescue itself was on Sept. 29, at the Beardmore Apartments, a building for elderly residents and people with special needs.

A fire was burning in at least one unit, and smoke was coming from the roof. The apartments share an attic. Many people had already gotten themselves out. Chief Drew McLain said one woman was still inside, standing with her walker and calling for help, with fire on the ceiling and smoke filling the room. The three broke a window on the west side, but she could not reach it. They went to the east side, broke that window, and lifted her out. McLain said they breathed in a lot of smoke and stayed with the work until she was outside. They also kept other residents from going back in. Sheriff Darryl Wheeler called the rescue courage beyond a normal shift. Mayor Jeff Connolly said the West Pend Oreille Fire District's work, along with the three officers, kept the fire from spreading and saved lives.
