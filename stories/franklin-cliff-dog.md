Karsten Delap of Pisgah Climbing School and three teammates were honored for rescuing a dog that spent three days stranded on Ramsey Cliff, WLOS reported. They climbed the 200-foot face by headlamp, put the dog in a harness, and lowered him to his guardian.

PETA later gave the school its Compassionate Business Award. The cliff is in the Franklin area of Macon County. The award recognizes that one climb, and the dog went back to the person already caring for him.
