Wilmington reopened a renovated Eastlake Park with the Sixers Youth Foundation, CBS Philadelphia reported. The project refurbished the basketball court and added a wall-ball system, a splash pad, a drinking fountain, a pavilion, and benches. The playground was replaced.

Melody Phillips, director of parks and recreation, said the city has more than 70 parks and that Eastlake belongs to the people around the 30th Street Civic Association, with things for adults and for children. A longtime resident told the station it was the first makeover she had seen there in nearly 50 years.
