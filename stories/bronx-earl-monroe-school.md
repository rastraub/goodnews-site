Earl Monroe, Bill Bradley, Tracy Morgan, and NBA Commissioner Adam Silver helped dedicate the permanent home of the Earl Monroe New Renaissance Basketball School in the Bronx. The charter school prepares students for careers across the sports industry, not only for playing. It had been in temporary space since 2021.

The dedication was for the building the students will use every day. The guests were there to open it, not to hold tryouts.
