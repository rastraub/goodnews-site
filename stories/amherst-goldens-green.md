About 375 people and 235 golden retrievers turned out in Amherst for the eighth Goldens On The Green, the Nashua Telegraph reported. The event benefits Operation Delta Dog, based in Hollis.

The group trains dogs rescued from shelters to work as service dogs for veterans. The head count is who showed up on the green this year, and the mission is the ongoing training those donations support.
