Chesterfield Fire and EMS was called at 10:18 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 13, to a house fire in the 3300 block of Shady Creek Road, WRIC reported. Firefighters found the fire on the back porch, and it had already moved into the home.

They had the fire under control in about 20 minutes. While they worked, they brought several pets outside, including a cat. The department said nobody was injured. Two people who lived there could not stay in the house, and investigators are still looking at how the fire started. The report does not name the residents or the animals.
