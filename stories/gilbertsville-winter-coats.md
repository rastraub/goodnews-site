More than 300 students at Gilbertsville-Mount Upton received new school-branded winter coats, WBNG reported. Sidney Federal Credit Union is providing them. The same effort is giving coats to nearly 4,000 students across Chenango, Delaware, and Otsego counties. The more than 300 is the count at this school. The nearly 4,000 is the size of the gift across those three counties, which the credit union is still carrying out.
