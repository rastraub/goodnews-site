A Kaukauna High School student asked the Family and Consumer Sciences class to cook for people who use the town fridge, and the class took the job, Kaukauna Community News reported. Teacher Katie Lehrer turned a FLEX session into a small production line. The students made 12 containers of pasta salad.

The Kaukauna Community Fridge stands in the Electric City Chuters parking lot. It is open day and night, and no one has to register to take food. The class wants the batch to become a weekly habit, so the fridge is not a one-time project. The student who suggested it was not named. The pasta salad was the first answer.
