McHenry Township firefighters and McHenry County sheriff's deputies were sent around 1:09 a.m. Wednesday to the 800 block of Northeast Shore Drive in unincorporated McHenry, Lake & McHenry County Scanner reported. A neighbor who called said there was heavy smoke and flame and that the neighbor was trying to put the fire out.

Deputy Chief Paul Polep of the McHenry Township Fire Protection District said crews found heavy fire and smoke at the front of the one-story house and through it. A resident outside told them someone was still inside. Firefighters and deputies went in through the back and brought that person out.

The rescued resident was evaluated and released at the scene. Polep said no other civilian injuries were reported. A firefighter had a minor hand injury. Crews had the fire under control within about 30 minutes, just before 2 a.m. The street has no fire hydrants, so the call was raised to bring water tenders, and arriving units staged at Dowell Road and River Road because the road in was tight. The house cannot be lived in. The American Red Cross was notified. The cause is under investigation.
