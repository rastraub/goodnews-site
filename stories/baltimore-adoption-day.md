On National Adoption Day, Baltimore finalized adoptions for 26 children at the Mitchell Courthouse. Sixteen of those children were adopted by relatives. After the ceremony, the families went to a celebration brunch with face-painting and a visit from Bluey. The courthouse part is the legal finish. The brunch is the room where the new families get to be noisy about it.
