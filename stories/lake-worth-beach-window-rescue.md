Palm Beach County Fire Rescue brought a person out of a bedroom during an early-morning apartment fire in Lake Worth Beach, WPBF reported. Before the crews arrived, dispatchers passed along that a 911 caller thought someone was still inside.

Firefighters found heavy smoke. They used the time of day and the cars parked outside to decide a bedroom was the most likely place to look, then used the size and placement of the windows to choose which one. They cleared a window, went in, and shut the bedroom door so smoke and flames would not be drawn into the room. They found the person and brought them back out through that same window.

Outside, the crews turned to medical care and took the person to a trauma center. The department said the rescue depended on the information sent before anyone arrived and on the choices made at the fire. WPBF did not name the person.
