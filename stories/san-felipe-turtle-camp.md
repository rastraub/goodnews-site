Campamento Tortuguero Tata Meza has released 131 sea turtle hatchlings this year on beaches of the Upper Gulf of California. El Imparcial reported the figure on November 1, 2026, from the Alto Golfo de California y Delta del Río Colorado biosphere reserve. The camp looks for nesting turtles along about 180 kilometers of coast at San Felipe and nearby.

Workers collect the nests, move the eggs into a protected area they can watch, and release the hatchlings after they emerge. The project also checks four feeding areas: Ensenada Blanca, El Mostrador, Cerro El Machorro, and Punta Estrella. Ten nests were protected in 2026, and the camp expects more hatchlings if more nests are found. Support listed in the report comes from Mexico's protected-area commission, the federal environmental prosecutor, the navy, and the municipality of San Felipe. The 131 are hatchlings released so far this year. They are not a total for every beach on the gulf.
