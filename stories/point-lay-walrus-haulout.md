The Conservation Fund bought a conservation easement from Cully Corporation covering 47,000 acres at Point Lay. The land includes the barrier islands where thousands of Pacific walrus come ashore to rest. The easement bars development on that haulout. Local subsistence access stays. The deal is written to last, so the shore the walrus already use is not opened to building, and the people who have used those islands for food are not shut out of them.
