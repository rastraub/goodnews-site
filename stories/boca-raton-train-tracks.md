Howard Berman, 86, was walking home from a dentist appointment in Boca Raton when a wheel of his walker caught in the railroad tracks near Palmetto Park Road and North Dixie Highway, WPBF reported. He fell and could not get himself up.

Officer Shawn Lyman was close enough to see it from his patrol car. He got out, ran to Berman, and pulled him clear of the tracks. Lyman told the station he did not know when the next train would come and wanted Berman off the rails. Paramedics treated Berman for cuts and bruises. A second officer offered him a ride home.

Lyman has been with the Boca Raton police for 21 years and works as a canine officer. He said the stop was the job the department is there to do, and that he would want anyone who saw an older neighbor in the same place to get out and help. Berman, a retired New York attorney, said he was grateful and that the officers made sure he was all right. He still gets around with a walker and told WPBF he may think twice before taking it across the tracks. He also said he would like to buy Lyman lunch.

Berman spent years helping lead the Yonkers Police Athletic League, including the work to establish its foundation in 1982. WPBF reported that he is organizing a boxing fundraiser between that program and the Boca Raton Police Athletic League.
