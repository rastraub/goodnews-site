The Saint Louis Zoo has its first Przewalski's horse foal, FOX 2 St. Louis reported. Aylin was born in July at WildCare Park to Nika and Ziggy. Przewalski's horses were once extinct in the wild, and zoo breeding is what brought the species back. Aylin is this zoo's first foal of that horse. She is a new birth in a breeding effort that already pulled the species out of extinction in the wild, not a claim about how many now roam free.
