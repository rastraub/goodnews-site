The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources opened a braille trail at Havenwoods State Forest in Milwaukee. The path is about a third of a mile. Signs are in braille, audio guides are available, and hikers can borrow a white cane. Staff did most of the building, with a $15,000 grant.

The trail is there so people who are blind can walk the forest with information they can read by touch or hear, instead of a sign they would have to skip.
