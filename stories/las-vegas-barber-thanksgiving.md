Andre Scoggins, a Las Vegas barber, and his mother, veteran Andre'a Strong, cooked their own family Thanksgiving, candied yams included, and set it out for neighbors and for service members far from home. The tradition is eight years old. The menu is not a banquet contracted out. It is the dinner they would have eaten, with more chairs. Troops who cannot get to their own tables get a plate that still tastes like somebody's house.
