Matsapha Town Council has opened Silent Eco Park, a 15-hectare green space the paper called Eswatini's first park of its kind. Eswatini Positive News reported the opening on November 8, 2026. The park has walkways, jogging tracks, an outdoor gym with equipment meant to include people with disabilities, a plant nursery, and a wetland kept for birds and other wildlife. Birdwatching spots and a fish pond are part of the site.

Housing and Urban Development Minister Appolo Maphalala, speaking through Principal Secretary Simon Zwane, congratulated the council at the launch. More paths for walking and cycling are still planned. The paper said the park is open while that work continues. Sponsors named in the report are CONCO, YKK, AG Thomas, and MBF. The news is this opening: the paths, the gym, the nursery, and the wetland that are there now. It does not say how many people have used them.
