Rodriquez Thomas and Shavokia Bryant drove home from Atlanta to serve about 1,000 Thanksgiving plates in Albany. Their nonprofits have run the giveaway for eight years, with volunteers and local partners beside them. Atlanta is where they live now. Albany is the table they keep coming back to set. A thousand plates is a lot of driving and a lot of hands, and the eighth year means the drive home has become the plan, not a one-time trip.
