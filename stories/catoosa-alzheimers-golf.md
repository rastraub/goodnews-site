A golf tournament at the Hard Rock Hotel and Casino in Catoosa raised more than $50,000 for the Alzheimer's Association of Oklahoma, News On 6 reported. The Young Professionals Alzheimer's Society helped lead the effort.

The money is a gift to the association's work, reported on October 29. The station's figure is the amount raised at the tournament, not a description of a new treatment.
