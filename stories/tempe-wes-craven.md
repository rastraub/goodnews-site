An 11-pound Chihuahua named Wes Craven got home after strangers in Tempe found him on the far side of Interstate 10, ABC15 reported. Michael Terlisner and his wife were driving toward Tucson when the dog sitter called to say Wes had been missing for about three hours. They turned around and searched near 44th Street and McNeil in Ahwatukee. Terlisner said Wes had already been at Arizona Small Dog Rescue twice.

While that search was going on, Megan and Bailey came home in Tempe and found the dog sitting near their front door, around Warner and Priest roads. He had crossed I-10. They called animal control, which pointed them to Petco Love Lost. A photo uploaded there matched the report Terlisner had posted, and the finders reached him while he was still driving. Neighbors in Ahwatukee had also joined the search. The report is the reunion. It does not say how a dog that size crossed the freeway.
