Charlotte Miles won the 2026 ABC Young Performers Awards after playing Elgar's cello concerto with the Sydney Symphony Orchestra at the Sydney Opera House. ABC News reported the result on November 3, 2026. Benjamin Northey conducted. The 24-year-old cellist, originally from Victoria, has been in the Kronberg Academy's professional program in Germany and returned for the final. She came through two earlier rounds after the contest opened in February.

The first prize is $40,000 and a solo studio album on the ABC Classic label. The runner-up receives $15,000 and third place $5,000. The final was broadcast live on ABC Classic and will air on ABC TV on November 5. ABC said Miles has more than 100 music prizes already, has guested with the Berlin Philharmonic, and was picked this year to tour with cellist Gautier Capuçon. The adjudicators were Aura Go, Sophie Rowell, Adam Szabo, and Eliza Shephard, chaired by Marshall McGuire. The news is this final and these prizes. It is not a list of every score she has played.
