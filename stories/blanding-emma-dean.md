Emma Dean, a horse rescued years ago at Lake Powell by the National Park Service and Best Friends, has a home in Blanding, Best Friends reported. Rebecca Stoneman adopted her, and she shares a pasture with a horse named Lichee.

The Lake Powell rescue is the earlier chapter. The news now is the adoption and the pasture, not a new emergency at the lake.
