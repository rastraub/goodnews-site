A Panora quilting circle that has made more than 200 quilts for people in need won a statewide volunteer award, The Perry News reported. Guthrie County REC and the stitch-and-chatter team shared the recognition.

Two hundred is the quilts already given. The award names that work. It does not, by itself, sew the next one.
