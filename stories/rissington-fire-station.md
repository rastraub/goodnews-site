Volunteer firefighters in Rissington opened a station that can do more than hold a truck. RNZ reported on November 21, 2026, that a dawn service blessed the building on Saturday morning, with the community opening a few hours later. Hawke's Bay assistant district commander Hamish Smith, of the Rissington Volunteer Fire Brigade, said the old base was a basic shed with no bathroom and no meeting room. The new one has toilets, showers, plenty of stored water, a kitchenette, an office, a training room, and space to coordinate a response. Community groups can use it for meetings too.

Rissington, northwest of Napier, was cut off for weeks when the bridge over the Mangaone River was lost in Cyclone Gabrielle. Smith said some people could not leave their own driveways because the road had slipped away. The brigade has 10 firefighters and three supporting roles. It is one of three brigades along the 50-kilometre Puketitiri Road, with Patoka and Puketitiri, and all three will train and meet in the new building. Fire and Emergency New Zealand paid most of the cost. The Rissington brigade put in $67,000 of its own saved funds, and Smith said other organisations and the community backed the project as well.
