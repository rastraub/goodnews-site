Friends of Avondale Park volunteers cleared, compacted, and marked a stretch of the park's historic trails on Saturday, November 14, Bham Now reported. The Blue Trail and part of the Yellow Trail are now easier to follow. Avondale Park is one of Birmingham's oldest green spaces. The friends group is a volunteer nonprofit that looks after maintenance and restoration and hosts cleanups and workdays.

President Daniel Neummann called the trail revival one example of making the park more accessible and more fun to use. The next trail workday is planned for February 2027, when the group expects to add signage, install a small bridge, and repair century-old steps. The outlet said regular foot traffic helps keep the paths compacted and clear, and that volunteers cut vegetation and level the trails when more work is needed. Donations also support the projects. The news is the November 14 work on those two trail sections. The signs, the bridge, and the steps are still ahead.
