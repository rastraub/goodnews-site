More than 600 volunteers signed up to rake 100 lawns for Habitat for Humanity of Greater Charlottesville's 13th annual Rake-a-Thon, NBC29 reported. The group planned the day with the University of Virginia's Habitat club and local high school students. The event was expected to raise $40,000 for affordable housing. The 600 figure is how many people signed up. The $40,000 is what organizers expected to raise, not a checked final total.
