A person in the water near Ketchikan was pulled out by a U.S. Coast Guard boat crew in the early hours of Saturday, the Ketchikan Daily News reported.

Ketchikan police notified Sector Southeast Alaska around 2 a.m. The Coast Guard said the person was breathing and responsive, and EMTs gave medical attention. Watchstanders sent an urgent marine broadcast and dispatched a 45-foot Response Boat-Medium crew from Station Ketchikan. The crew pulled the person from the water at about 2:20 a.m.

Chief Boatswain's Mate Cameron Singleton, the officer in charge of the station, told the Daily News on Tuesday that the person was recovered about 100 yards from Berth 3. The report does not say how the person ended up in the water.
