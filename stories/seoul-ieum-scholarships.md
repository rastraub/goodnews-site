Two hundred and eighty-five students in three Seoul districts are getting help with school costs. Financial News reported on November 27, 2026, that Hope Friend, the group also known as Korea Food for the Hungry International, and GS Retail held a ceremony on November 24 for the Ieum scholarship campaign. High-school students receive scholarships. Elementary students receive kits of supplementary study materials. The students live in Gangseo-gu, Nowon-gu, and Yeongdeungpo-gu.

The program is in its 20th year. It began in 2007 with GS Home Shopping for young people from low-income households in Yeongdeungpo-gu. In 2019 the district office nominated recipients, and 20 high-school students at that year's ceremony received scholarships, books, and devices. GS Retail kept the program after it merged with GS Home Shopping in July 2021. The organization said the number of people supported through this year, added up, is 618.

Park Gyeong-rang, who leads ESG work at GS Retail, said the hope is that the children keep studying. Park Chan-wook, of the charity's sharing division, called the gift an example of a company working with the neighborhoods where the students live. The 285 is this round. The 618 is the running total the charity gave. Neither figure is a count of every student in Seoul who needs help.
