Kevin Horan stopped a moving car after he saw that the woman at the wheel appeared to be unconscious, CNY Central reported. He was driving on Route 12 in Boonville when the car came toward him and drifted into the oncoming lane. He pulled over, got out, and ran nearly 200 yards, waving to warn other drivers. He opened the door, took the wheel and the shifter, and put the car in park.

With the car stopped, he got the woman responsive before emergency crews arrived. He said a head-on collision had been a real possibility, and that no one else was hurt. The American Red Cross is honoring him as a Good Samaritan hero. Horan said he does not see himself that way. He had not spoken with the woman since the day of the stop.
