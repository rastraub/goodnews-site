Friends of North Augusta Animals raised $17,481 at the second annual Rock for Rescues, the Aiken Standard reported. The day mixed games, music, adoptable dogs, and community sponsors.

The money is set aside for veterinary bills, spay and neuter care, and adoptions. The $17,481 figure is the total from this fundraiser, not the shelter's whole yearly budget.
