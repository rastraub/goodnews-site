Volunteers with Sleep in Heavenly Peace's Chittenden County chapter spent Friday delivering beds and setting them up in local homes, WPTZ reported from Winooski. Since 2019 the chapter has provided more than 3,200 beds to children in the region. Crews build each frame, then assemble it in the house.

Mike Lemieux, the chapter president, said some of the children have been sleeping on the floor, on a couch, on beanbags, or piled in with parents and grandparents. One Friday delivery went to Hannah Cameron and her children. She told the station that a bed matters, and that the help is welcome when prices are high. The nonprofit's stated aim is that no child has to sleep without a bed of their own.
