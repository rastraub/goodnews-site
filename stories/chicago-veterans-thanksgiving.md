Veterans and their families picked up free Thanksgiving meals and winter clothes at the Lakeview East Jewel-Osco, ABC7 Chicago reported. The Salvation Army, the Chicago Bears, Jewel-Osco, and Miller Lite handed out turkeys, meal boxes, blankets, and winter clothing. The giveaway is that distribution at the store. The station did not publish a count of how many people received a box.
