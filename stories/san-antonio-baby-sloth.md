San Antonio Zoo's first baby sloth in 16 years was born Oct. 25, KENS 5 reported. The two-toed infant's parents are Animal Ambassadors Aluna and Sol.

The baby is bonding with its mother behind the scenes, so this is a birth announcement, not a date when the infant goes on public view. Sixteen years is the gap since the zoo's last sloth birth.
