Thanksfest, Vaughn Littrell's 12-year-old tradition, gave 364 Colorado Springs households everything for Thanksgiving dinner plus coats, gloves, and hats, KOAA News5 reported. The event is now hosted at CPCD. About 150 volunteers took part. The household count is up from 300.

Five families is where it started. Three hundred sixty-four is this year's reach. The dinner and the winter clothes went out together.
