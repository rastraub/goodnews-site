About 400 volunteers winterized 50 homes for older adults in Metro Detroit during Jewish Family Service's 30th Fall Fix Up, WXYZ reported. The crews included volunteers from Congregation Shaarey Zedek, and the work was centered on Southfield.

Four hundred is the turnout, and 50 is the number of homes. Thirty years is how long the agency has held the fix-up, not the age of the houses.
