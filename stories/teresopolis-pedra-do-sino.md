Two hikers who lost the Pedra do Sino trail in Serra dos Órgãos National Park were back at the park base by dawn, G1 reported on November 22, 2026. The Rio de Janeiro military fire department said the call came Saturday night. Firefighters from the 16th group started the search at 10 p.m. in the area called Cota 2000 and moved through the forest in the dark.

They returned with both hikers at 5 a.m. The department said the hikers were well. Rescue crews checked them and brought them safely to the park base. Their names were not released. On video of the arrival, the crew celebrated that they had made it back, that the mission was finished, and that the work had run from ten to five.

The department used the rescue to remind hikers to plan the route, watch the forecast, and carry a light, warm clothes, water, and spare batteries. It also pointed visitors toward accredited guides and the park's safety rules. In an emergency, the fire department number is 193.
