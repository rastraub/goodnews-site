Carpentry students at Croatan High School in Newport built shelves for Special Olympics Carteret County, WCTI reported. The shelving went into a donated shipping container that holds equipment for 15 sports. The program expects to save about $1,500 a year because that gear no longer needs a separate storage bill.

The students' work is ordinary carpentry with a specific address. The container was already there. What it lacked was a way to keep equipment off the floor and easy to find. The shelves are that system, built by the high school class and delivered to the local program.
