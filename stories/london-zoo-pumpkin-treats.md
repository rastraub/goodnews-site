Keepers at ZSL London Zoo spent October 29 hiding pumpkins in enclosures and stuffing them with food the animals already eat. BBC News reported the scatter the same day. Penguins had to find fish inside a pumpkin. Sulawesi crested macaques, including a baby, were among the animals meeting the seasonal setup. Daniel Simmonds, the zoo's animal operations manager, said Halloween gives keepers a reason to build a new version of a familiar job: encouraging natural skills. They lay scent trails that lead to the pumpkins, and sometimes they hide the meal inside so an animal has to work it out.

Simmonds called it part of an annual tradition. This year's version falls in the 200th anniversary of the Zoological Society of London. BBC's report is about that one day of enrichment. It does not claim a change in the animals' health, and it does not list every species that got a pumpkin.
