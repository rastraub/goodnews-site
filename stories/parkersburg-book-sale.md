The Parkersburg News and Sentinel reported on October 8, 2026, that the 76th Trinity Episcopal Church Used Book Sale was underway at 430 Juliana Street, with the entrance on 5th Street. Chair Lynn Fury said more people came than in past years. Proceeds support community charities, including the Salvation Army, CASA, and others. The remaining hours were that Thursday, October 8, from 9 to 6, Friday, October 9, from 9 to 6, and Saturday, October 10, from 9 to noon. Credit cards were accepted on October 8 and 9. Saturday returned to cash and checks.
