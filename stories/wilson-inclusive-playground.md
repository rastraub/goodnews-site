Rivercrest Elementary opened a playground its students helped plan, KAIT 8 reported. The project in Wilson cost $507,000. The equipment includes pieces a child can use from a wheelchair, and sensory pods for children who need a quieter kind of play.

The opening is one school yard, built with student ideas in the design. The dollar figure is the cost of this playground, not a price tag for every school that wants the same thing.
