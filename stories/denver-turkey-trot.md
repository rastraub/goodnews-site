Nearly 12,000 runners, walkers, and wheelchair users started Thanksgiving at Washington Park for the 53rd Mile High United Way Turkey Trot. The field beat last year's record of 11,600. Proceeds support emergency housing for families from the Aspen Acres wildfire, early learning for more than 4,000 children, and free tax preparation. The record is a head count, not a winning time. Wheelchair users were in the same start as the runners, and the money is what the morning was for.
