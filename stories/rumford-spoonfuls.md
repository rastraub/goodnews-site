Spoonfuls, a Massachusetts food-recovery nonprofit, has partnered with FeedRI and is sending recovered food into Rhode Island for the first time, the group reported. Since a summer pilot, the partnership has moved 57,000 pounds of still-good food, about 45,600 meals.

Those totals are what has crossed into the state since the pilot began. Recovered food is food that can still be eaten and is redirected instead of discarded.
