The Baltimore Ravens hosted a baby shower for 50 military parents-to-be, WMAR-2 reported. Service members and spouses who are expecting were the guests.

Fifty is the size of that gathering. The station's account is the team's event for those families, not a benefit calculated for every installation in the state.
