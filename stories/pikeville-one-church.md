One Church Thanksgiving prepared 12,000 meals this year. A Pikeville church of about 60 members started it 10 years ago, when the dinner was about 2,000 meals. Dozens of congregations now cook for neighbors in Pike, Floyd, and Letcher counties. The name still says one church. The pots say otherwise. What one small congregation began has become a three-county meal, six times the size of the first year.
