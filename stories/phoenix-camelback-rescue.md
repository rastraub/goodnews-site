On Thanksgiving morning, an off-duty Scottsdale firefighter helped a teen hiker on Camelback Mountain in Phoenix. He stabilized her near the summit and stayed until Phoenix Fire's Firebird helicopter lifted her down. She left alert and smiling, in stable condition. He was not on the clock when he reached her. The helicopter was the ride off the mountain. The stable condition is what the crews reported when she was on the ground.
