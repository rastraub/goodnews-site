Neighbors in Birmingham's Fountain Heights opened We All Eat Market, the neighborhood's first grocery store in 70 years, Bham Now reported. Fountain Heights Farms Cooperative grew from a front-yard garden to 16 farm plots, and the market is stocked with produce from that work. The 70 years are the gap without a grocery. The 16 plots are the farms the neighbors built, and the store is where that harvest is sold.
