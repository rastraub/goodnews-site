Firefighters in Seal Rock brought a 12-year-old cat back with a mask made for pets, the Lincoln Chronicle reported. The cat, Cloudy, needed oxygen while crews dealt with a fire. Cloudy is home and doing well.

The save is one animal and one call. The paper's account is that the cat recovered enough to go home, not a promise about every pet in a fire.
