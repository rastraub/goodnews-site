Students from Bear Creek, Golden, and Wheat Ridge high schools spent this week shadowing staff at Lutheran Hospital, CBS Colorado reported. The visits are a Jeffco Public Schools partnership that lets them see healthcare work before graduation. They moved through the emergency room, the pharmacy, and the lab, and they took a Stop the Bleed class.

Career-and-technical student Kenny Chiv said the useful part was watching how the jobs actually feel, including what people like about them. Ninety students have joined or are still in the program, and two have already found jobs through the partnership. The point, the station reported, is that students do not have to wait until after college to step into the field.
