For the third year, the Anchorage Firefighters Charitable Foundation served a full Thanksgiving meal at Baxter Elementary, Alaska's News Source reported. About 350 students ate. Nuvision Credit Union and Peppercini's Catering took part. Baxter is a Title 1 school.

The meal is the holiday lunch, served at the school. About 350 is the count of students. The third year means the foundation has done this lunch twice before.
