The sea turtle camp at Playa Holi in Puerto Vallarta has released about 31,000 hatchlings between July 15 and October 21, 2026. Vallarta Independiente reported the count on October 21.

The city's Dirección de Sostenibilidad Ambiental runs the camp with the group RANA A.C. Eggs are collected, held in an incubation corral, and the hatchlings are then released. In Puerto Vallarta the season starts in July and can run into November or December, depending on conditions. The paper asked people to skip camera flashes and other bright lights at night, when turtles come ashore and need the beach dark.

The environmental office is also working with PROFEPA and SEMARNAT. Those two federal agencies made a joint supervision visit to the camp. The report describes sea turtles and does not name the species. The 31,000 figure is this camp's releases for the season so far, not a count of every nest on the coast.
