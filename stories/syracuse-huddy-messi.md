A video of Hudson Droubay playing soccer traveled far enough to reach David Beckham, KSL.com reported. Huddy is 5, lives in Syracuse, Utah, and has Down syndrome. Beckham invited him to an Inter Miami game. There he scored goals with Beckham and walked onto the field with Lionel Messi.

The account is one boy's invitation and one match. It does not say he has joined the club, and it does not measure anyone else's chance of the same day.
