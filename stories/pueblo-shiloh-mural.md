Youth from the Roots and Rise enrichment program are painting a Pueblo-themed mural in the multipurpose room of Shiloh House's new day treatment program for elementary students, KOAA News5 reported. Artist Matte Refic is guiding the work. The mural is a welcome for those students. The painting is underway. The report describes the project in progress, not a finished wall.
