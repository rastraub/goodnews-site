Trinity County Search and Rescue brought two travelers out of deep snow on Thursday, in the team's first snow rescue of the 2026-27 winter, KRCR reported. The pair had left the French Gulch area of Shasta County on Wednesday night on a hike toward Trinity Lake, traveling on U.S. Forest Service roads that are not plowed in winter. They took a wrong turn and got stuck in 12 to 18 inches of snow, with no homes or services for miles.

They reached Trinity County dispatch about 4 a.m. on Nov. 19, on a weak cell signal. Deputies could not get to them through the snow, so the sheriff's office called in search and rescue. The team drove a tracked utility vehicle in. Snow on that vehicle was about 24 to 30 inches deep. The department has not released the travelers' names, and it has not said whether they were hurt.

The sheriff's office asked people who drive mountain roads in winter to check the weather, know what their vehicle can do, and carry food, water, extra clothes, blankets, fuel, and a way to charge a phone.
