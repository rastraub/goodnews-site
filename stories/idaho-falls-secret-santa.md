A Secret Santa surprised Samantha, a School District 91 lunch lady in Idaho Falls, EastIdahoNews.com reported. She was washing dishes when the surprise arrived. Her family is still recovering from a child's long MRSA bone infection. The gift came from East Idaho's $1 million Secret Santa.

Samantha works in the school kitchen. The Secret Santa effort is the $1 million giveaway. The visit found her at the sink, still showing up for the lunch line while her family recovers.
