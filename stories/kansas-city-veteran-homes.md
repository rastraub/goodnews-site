Nearly 100 volunteers painted 49 tiny homes for veterans in Kansas City, KSHB reported. The Veterans Community Project and the Veterans United Foundation, which share a goal of ending veteran homelessness, organized the day. The houses are transitional housing, and the new color is part of making them ready.

Michael Stradford, a veteran volunteer, told the station he joined the National Guard to help people in need and that the paint day continues that work. Erik Morse, the board president, was also on site as the volunteers worked through the rows. The story is the paint and the homes, not a count of people already moved in.
