A 4-year-old named Amber sold jujubes with Shawnee Mission High students and raised $369, KCTV5 reported. Her family and the farm each matched that amount. Half of the money goes to Melissa's Second Chances, the Shawnee animal shelter where she volunteers.

The $369 is the sale. The two matches copy that amount. Half is the share headed to the shelter. The fruit stand is how a 4-year-old and the high school students raised it.
