Houston Rocket, a 12-week-old golden retriever, started work as a therapy dog for the Fountain Police Department, KRDO reported. Sunrise Service Dogs, a Colorado Springs nonprofit that trains dogs for law enforcement agencies, veterans, and children, donated him. The department swore him in on Tuesday. On Wednesday he made his first visits to schools in Fountain-Fort Carson District 8, including Fountain Middle School.

School resource officer Jerri Reble said that at the high school that morning he lay down and let people pet him. She estimated he saw more than 100 students and well over four dozen adults, and that he paid attention to them. Principal Corrie Walker of Fountain Middle School called the visit phenomenal and said the students loved having a dog there. Reble said Houston's role is to support students with special needs and students who are stressed. One student who had lost his father cried when he saw the dog, which Reble described as a release of emotion. Houston still has a lot of training ahead.
