Last summer, 8-year-old Carter Hicks noticed a camper trailer that was smoking and starting to catch fire. Eagle Country 99.3 reported on October 8, 2026, that he told the adults around him, and firefighters were called. The Blooming Grove Volunteer Fire Department honored him the Saturday before the story, with a department patch and a certificate of recognition.

The department said his warning helped the fire get noticed before it became more serious, and that he was worried about someone else's property, not his own. The honor was for paying attention and speaking up.
