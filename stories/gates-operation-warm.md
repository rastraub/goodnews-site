Gates Professional Firefighters and Dispatchers Local 3792 donated 27 new winter coats to students at Hope Hall School, 13WHAM reported. It is the 10th year of the department's Operation Warm effort.

The coats are new, and they are meant to get students through the cold months. Twenty-seven is this year's gift, and the tenth year is how long Local 3792 has kept the drive going.
