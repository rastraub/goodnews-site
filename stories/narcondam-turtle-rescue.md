Police in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands cut two green sea turtles out of discarded fishing nets near Narcondam Island and returned them to the sea. The Times of India reported the rescue on November 25, 2026, citing the IANS news service and a video released by the Andaman and Nicobar Police. One turtle was an adult and the other a juvenile. The nets had wrapped the animals and limited how they could move. Officers cut the lines away. The video shows that work.

The paper described the nets as ghost nets, gear that is lost, abandoned, or thrown away and keeps drifting. The report does not give the calendar day of the rescue, only that the police video had been released. The news is these two animals. It is not a survey of how many turtles ghost nets catch around the islands.
