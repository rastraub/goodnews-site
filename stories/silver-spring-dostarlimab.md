On Oct. 19, 2026, the Food and Drug Administration granted accelerated approval to Jemperli (dostarlimab) for adults with previously untreated stage II or III rectal cancer that is mismatch-repair deficient (dMMR) or microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H). That subtype accounts for about 3 percent of rectal cancers. The approval does not apply to rectal cancer in general.

The evidence is the AZUR-1 trial. Of 154 patients treated with the immunotherapy, nearly three-fourths had not needed surgery, radiation, or chemotherapy after a median follow-up of more than two years. Accelerated approval still requires longer follow-up. The trial result is not a finding that every patient can skip those treatments, and it is not a statement that the cancer is cured.
