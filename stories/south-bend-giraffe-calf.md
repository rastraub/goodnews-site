Potawatomi Zoo in South Bend welcomed its first home-bred male giraffe, the South Bend Tribune reported. The Masai calf, born Nov. 16 to Zindzhi and Kellan, weighed 179 pounds and stood 6 feet 2 inches. He was up and nursing within hours.

Nov. 16 is the birth date. 179 pounds and 6 feet 2 inches are his size at birth. Home-bred means he was born at the zoo. Nursing within hours is how soon he was feeding.
