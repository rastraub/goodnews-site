Hope Blooms in Fargo is marking 10 years of taking flowers people would have thrown out and arranging them again. Volunteers make bouquets for memory-care residents, recovery centers, and cancer treatment centers.

RDO's Caters Taters fundraiser is backing the nonprofit as it expands into rural communities. The anniversary is about flowers that already have a second stop, and about taking that habit past Fargo.
