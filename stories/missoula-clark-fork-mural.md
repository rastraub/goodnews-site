Missoula's tallest mural is finished, NBC Montana reported. It covers more than 6,000 square feet on the west wall of the Clark Fork Riverside building, near the corner of Orange and Front streets. Ernest Doty and AJ Tiam put the last paint on over the weekend. From the first day to the last, the work took 22 days.

The two artists flew out on Sunday. Doty said he was grateful the city let visitors paint something people might enjoy for years, and that the welcome had been real. Tiam said they made a point of climbing down from the boom lifts to talk with people, and he asked the city to keep that habit going.
