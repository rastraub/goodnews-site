Chris and Vicki Sullivan, a Colorado couple who have each donated a kidney, finished a 17-mile Grand Canyon hike with other living donors, 9NEWS reported on October 29. They are using the trip to encourage people to learn what living donation involves.

The station's video is the account of a finished hike, not a medical recommendation. A living donor gives one kidney and keeps the other. The Sullivans' point, as 9NEWS framed it, is that people considering that step can ask questions before they decide.
