When a crash froze Interstate 70 near Georgetown, Lakewood Army veteran Kalen Ostrewic turned the wait into a roadside cleanup, 9NEWS reported. He grabbed trash bags and started on the shoulder. Other stranded drivers joined him. Together they filled about four bags.

The jam was the reason they were stopped. The bags were the work they did while they waited. About four is the count they filled.
