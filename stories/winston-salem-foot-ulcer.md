A first-in-human study from the Wake Forest Institute for Regenerative Medicine tested a placental cell therapy on stubborn diabetic foot ulcers, Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist reported. The study, published in Stem Cells Translational Medicine, included 10 patients. All 10 wounds fully closed within six months. Researchers reported no treatment-related serious adverse events.

This is an early safety study. Larger controlled trials are the next step. It is not a cure. The work is based in Winston-Salem, and ten patients is the whole group in this pilot.
