Branford Public Schools won a $100,000 New England Food Vision Prize from the Henry P. Kendall Foundation, Branford Patch reported. The grant will fund school chefs and farm-to-school coordinators.

The aim is more Connecticut-grown food in ordinary meals, at no extra charge to students. The $100,000 is the prize, and the staffing is how the district plans to spend it, not a menu that has already replaced every lunch.
