Retired teachers Cleo and Joan Boschee covered about half the cost of a new volunteer fire station in Wishek, KFYR-TV reported. The town built it without raising taxes and put the couple's name on it.

The gift is roughly half the project. The rest of the funding, and the decision not to raise taxes, are part of the same local plan. The station is for the volunteers who already answer calls in town.
