The Philippine Daily Inquirer reported on November 15, 2026, that Bacnotan has its first sea-turtle nest of the season. The conservation group Curma said the nest was found on the beach in Barangay Pandan on Saturday, November 14, and that it held 112 eggs. Resident Arsenio Adev Jr. saw fresh tracks early that morning and told barangay officials. Working with the municipal government, they moved the eggs to the hatchery at Alpas Beach Resort, where the eggs will be protected and watched.

Curma expects hatching around January 14, 2027, and thanked Adev and the Pandan and municipal officials for moving quickly. The article does not name the species. The news is 112 eggs in a hatchery because a neighbor reported tracks the same morning. It is not a count of hatchlings in the water.
