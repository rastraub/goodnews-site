Martin County deputies, Animal Services, and Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission officers freed a female bobcat that was caught in a barbed-wire fence in Stuart, WQCS reported. A wildlife hospital gave her a clean bill of health, and she was released near the spot where she was found. The rescue ended with that release, close to the fence, after the hospital check.
