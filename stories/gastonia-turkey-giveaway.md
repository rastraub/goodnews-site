Volunteers gave Thanksgiving turkeys to more than 600 Gastonia families, WSOC-TV reported. Gastonia Police, 101 Black Men of Highland, Bountiful Blessings Food Pantry, and Greek organizations ran the giveaway at Mt. Zion Restoration Church.

More than 600 is the family count. The church is the site. The police, the civic group, the pantry, and the Greek organizations are who staffed it together.
