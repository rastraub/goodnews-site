Milwaukee's holiday drive is aimed at the People's Food Pantry in Metcalfe Park, WISN reported. Residents started the pantry after the neighborhood lost its only grocery store. The Brewers Community Foundation gave $10,000. Nine drop-off sites stay open through Dec. 14.

City and county leaders pointed this year's drive at that pantry. The $10,000 is the Brewers gift. Dec. 14 is when the drop-off sites close. The pantry itself is neighbor-run.
