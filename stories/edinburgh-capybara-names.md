Edinburgh Zoo named its newest capybara triplets Tattie, Tunnock and Toddy after a public vote. BBC Newsround reported the names on November 10, 2026. The two brothers are Tunnock and Toddy, and the sister is Tattie. The names point to potatoes, a Scottish chocolate wafer, and a hot drink. The pups were born overnight to Luna and Rodney and were first spotted on October 28.

The zoo's previous capybara pup, Bru, was the first capybara born in Scotland in 18 years. These three are a later litter. Capybaras are the largest rodents, with teeth that keep growing, so they wear them down by grazing. They are semi-aquatic, with partly webbed toes and fur that dries quickly. They live in warmer parts of South America. The news is three named pups at the zoo. It is not a census of wild capybaras.
