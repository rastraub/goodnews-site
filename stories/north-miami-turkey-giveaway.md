North Miami police officers handed out 300 turkeys and other holiday items on Monday, WSVN reported. The giveaway was for residents who are struggling to afford a Thanksgiving meal.

The station said city officials expect to hand out 3,000 turkeys between that Monday and Thanksgiving Day. The report does not name the residents who received birds, and it does not list the other items.

Three hundred is the police department's count for Monday. Three thousand is the city's expected total through the holiday, not a count of what had already been given out.
