Peace River Wildlife Center released two rehabilitated ospreys together at Ponce de Leon Park in Punta Gorda. The birds had been rescued on opposite sides of Charlotte Harbor. One had been hurt by a catfish barb. Both had healed enough to fly, and the center sent them off in the same release. They did not arrive together. They left together.
