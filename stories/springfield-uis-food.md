University of Illinois Springfield students collected 6,220 pounds of food on a trick-or-treat for canned goods, the Illinois Times reported. The haul goes to the Central Illinois Foodbank.

The weight is this drive. It is not the food bank's whole year, and it is not a count of every door in Springfield.
