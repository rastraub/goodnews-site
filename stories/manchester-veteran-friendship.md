Jenelle, a pawn shop manager in Manchester, raised $460,000 so a 90-year-old veteran named Donald could pay for his wife's care, WMUR reported. A stranger who was moved by that story then planned Jenelle's wedding for free. The money was for the care. The wedding planning is the friendship that followed, reaching across state lines. The report gives those two first names. It does not spell out a diagnosis, and it does not say the gift covers every bill.
