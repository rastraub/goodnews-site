About 300 Vibe Credit Union employees used a Community Impact Day in Novi to work for two hours. They built sheds with Habitat for Humanity, assembled 51 bikes for the Samaritas Family Center, and packed 1,600 meal kits for Hospitality House.

One afternoon, split across those three jobs. The sheds, the bikes, and the meals were finished in that window.
