The POST reported on November 18, 2026, that the Manila City Library, with the Manila Department of Social Welfare, is bringing reading sessions and other learning activities to the Dapitan Sports Complex. The sessions are for children and older people the department describes as rescued from the streets.

The paper did not give a headcount, a schedule of dates, or a list of books. It said the city government framed the work as education that does not depend on age, and that the partnership is continuing rather than a one-day event. The news is those sessions at Dapitan. It is not a measured gain in reading scores.
