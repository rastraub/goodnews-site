Kindness Rocks served a Thanksgiving feast to the dogs, cats, and rats at Delta Animal Shelter in Escanaba, the second year the Upper Peninsula nonprofit has done it. The meals were made for the animals, not scraped from a human plate. A shelter day is usually bowls and routine. This one was a holiday menu on purpose, and doing it again means the animals were not a one-time guest list.
