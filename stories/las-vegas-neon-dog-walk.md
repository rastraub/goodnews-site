Nevada SPCA's sixth Neon Dog Walk at Silverado Ranch Park in Las Vegas raised more than $80,000. The money pays for shelter care, veterinary visits at no cost, and a pet food pantry for people who are trying to keep the animals they already have.

The walk is one day. The pantry and the clinic bills are what the total is there to cover afterward.
