The Hindu reported on November 17, 2026, that the wetland bird enclosures at Arignar Anna Zoological Park in Vandalur will reopen to visitors on Wednesday. Access had been closed as a precaution after avian influenza was found among birds. The zoo said two rounds of samples from the affected group, taken 15 days apart, tested negative at the ICAR National Institute of High Security Animal Diseases in Bhopal.

The zoo said no bird died during the restriction, and that the birds stayed healthy and active under veterinary watch. Samples from 26 animal keepers who care for the animals also tested negative by RT-PCR. Sanitation, disinfection, and regular health checks will continue. The news is the reopening after those negative tests. It is not a claim that avian influenza is gone from the region.
