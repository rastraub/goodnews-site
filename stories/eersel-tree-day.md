One hundred sixty-three primary pupils in Eersel planted trees on Boomfeestdag, Omroep Brabant reported on November 24, 2026. The national tree-planting day for children fell on Wednesday, November 18. Forty-six pupils from Lambertusschool worked at De Buikheide in Vessem. One hundred seventeen pupils from Basisschool Wereldwijs, 't Busseltje, Sint Jan, and De Groote Aard worked along the Postelseweg.

Alderman Eric Oosterbosch and Staatsbosbeheer, the national forestry service, were with the children. The station's story says the trees are meant to grow into a healthier place to live, and that the day is an annual chance for children to learn why trees matter. The article was assembled automatically by the station. It names the schools, the two sites, and the 163 pupils. It does not say how many trees were planted. That head count of children is the figure the report supports.
