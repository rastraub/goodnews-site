Eagles' Wings has opened Virginia Stables at Eagles Pointe, a donated barn in Tuscaloosa County, for adults with intellectual disabilities, WBRC reported. The nonprofit serves about 40 adults. Two horses, Louie and Dixie, are there now. Staff described the riding they are preparing as a calming kind of therapy, a way for a tense rider to settle after a turn or two around the ring.

The horses are not in full sessions yet. Trainers will work with Louie and Dixie for about three months before participants start to ride. Supporters donated the barn and money for the center around it. The stable is open. The rides it was built for come after that training.
