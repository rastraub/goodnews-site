Seattle-area metal detectorist Lucas Thrun found Paul Benson's St. Raphael Academy class ring, which had been lost in Puget Sound in 1979, People reported. Thrun flew to Rhode Island and returned the ring to Benson in person.

The ring is from St. Raphael Academy in Pawtucket. The loss was in 1979, in Puget Sound. The find was in the Seattle area. The return was a flight to Rhode Island so Benson could have the ring back by hand.
