The Wichita Wagonmasters smoked 323 turkeys for families who need a Thanksgiving meal. Twelve teams kept 23 smokers going. The tradition is about a decade old, and it started with 34 birds. The count is the point. What began as a few dozen turkeys is now a yard full of smokers and hundreds of meals that will not be eaten by the people who cooked them.
