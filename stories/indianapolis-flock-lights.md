Kurt and Kate Flock have switched on Flock Winter Lights for a sixth year at 1219 N. Alabama Street in Indianapolis. The display collects donations for Friends of Indy Animals, and the couple will match the first $10,000. People come for the lights. The jar, or the gift that follows, is for shelter pets. A match means a visitor's dollar does not stay a single dollar until that $10,000 is filled. Six years is long enough for a front yard to become a neighborhood appointment.
