Police officers and firefighters waited tables for more than 100 Fair Haven seniors at a neighborhood lunch that is now 15 years old. School choruses sang. Raffles, coats, and blankets were part of the room. The people who usually arrive when something is wrong spent the meal carrying plates. The coats and blankets were for the walk home, so the lunch was not the only warm thing guests left with.
