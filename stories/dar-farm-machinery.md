A three-year scholarship in Dar es Salaam will train 300 young people to operate and repair farm machinery. Daily News reported that VETA director general Anthony Kasore and Tanzania Breweries managing director Michelle Kilpin signed the Ujuzi Kwanza agreement on Wednesday at the company's Ilala offices. One hundred trainees are planned each year.

The courses will run at five vocational centres: Kihonda and Manyara regional centres, and centres in Mpanda, Arusha, and Dakawa. Kasore said the point is practical skill for a job or for self-employment in farming. Kilpin said the company already trains smallholders who grow sorghum, maize, and barley, and that the new partnership extends that support to more young people. Corporate relations director Neema Temba said the brewery depends on those crops for manufacturing.

The article is the signing and the plan: 300 places, five colleges, three years. It does not name the first students, and it does not report a class already completed.
