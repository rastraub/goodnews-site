More than a dozen seniors at the Optum Community Center in Mesa have new orange or yellow belts, AZFamily reported. The karate class is modified for balance, mobility, and strength.

Claire Charles, 80, told Good Morning Arizona she feels more confident, more flexible, and stronger, and that she likes the way the class connects mind and body. She was excited about the yellow belt and said she hugged her teacher and jumped around. Instructor Ilana Obrand called the work an honor and said Charles's balance has improved sharply. Obrand said the group trains hard and that students have gained coordination along with the belts.
