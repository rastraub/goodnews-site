Metuchen police were called around 8 a.m. Monday to a working house fire on Herold Place, MyCentralJersey reported. Officers forced their way in and pulled two residents and a dog out of the house.

Both residents went to a hospital for injuries from the fire and were in stable condition, police said. One borough officer who went inside was also taken to the hospital for smoke inhalation and was stable.

The Metuchen Fire Department put the fire out with help from firefighters from Edison, South Plainfield, and Woodbridge. Metuchen EMS and JFK EMS treated the residents and the responders at the scene. The paper did not name the residents or the dog, and it did not give a cause for the fire.
