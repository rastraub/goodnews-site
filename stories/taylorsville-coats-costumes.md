More than 500 families in Taylorsville picked up free winter coats and Halloween costumes from the Family Support Center. KSL reported that the clothes were for children from newborns through their teens, and that cocoa was part of the day. The center plans another costume giveaway on October 21.

The coats are for the cold months. The costumes are so children can dress up without a family having to buy one. The October 21 event is costumes again, not a second coat distribution. The center said the first giveaway reached more than 500 families.
