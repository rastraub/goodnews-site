At Parker Elementary in Longview, part of Pine Tree ISD, teacher Ashley Peterson saw third-grader Kassidy Childress choking. Assistant Principal Kyle Burns used the Heimlich maneuver and cleared her airway.

The student could breathe again because two adults in the building recognized what was happening and knew the maneuver. That was the whole save.
