Rosa Lopez saved nine photo albums that had been left outside her Waikiki condo. She spent weeks following the clues inside them until she reached the family in Chicago. The late owner's niece and sister-in-law then flew to Honolulu, met Lopez, and carried the albums home. The pictures had been one trash day away from disappearing. They are back with the people who know the faces.
