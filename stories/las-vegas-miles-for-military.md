Miles for Military marked its 2,000th free flight by bringing Marine Cpl. Dominic Morimando home to surprise his family in Las Vegas. He earned the ticket by volunteering at an elementary school in Virginia. The nonprofit calls the exchange service for service: hours given in a classroom become a seat on a plane. The 2,000th flight is a count of trips home that those families did not have to buy.
