A firefighter collapsed and went into cardiac arrest while crews fought a house fire on the 600 block of New York Avenue in Mansfield Township late Monday night, 6abc reported. The fire was reported around 11:15 p.m. Crews found heavy fire in the house and called second and third alarms.

Westhampton Township Emergency Services said Squad 272 started firefighter-down CPR without hesitation. The crew did several rounds of chest compressions and delivered several shocks with an AED before the firefighter was taken to the hospital. Officials said he was conscious and alert when he arrived and is recovering. No other injuries were reported.

Fire officials said the person who had been inside ran into a wooded area after the fire started and was later found nearby. The cause of the fire was still under investigation. The report does not name the firefighter.
