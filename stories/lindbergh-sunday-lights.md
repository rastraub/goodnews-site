Lindbergh High School held its annual Sunday Night Lights and put children who are being treated for cancer and other serious illnesses at the center of the night. The kids joined the football players, the band, the cheerleaders, and the dance team as the guests of the game.

Money from the night supports children's cancer research at Siteman Cancer Center. The evening was built so those children were the ones the crowd came to see.
