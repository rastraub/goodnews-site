Concord firefighters freed a barred owl whose head was stuck in a car grille, WBTV reported. The rescue was Sunday night in Cabarrus County. Photos showed how she was caught. The Concord Fire Department removed her, and she went to the Carolina Wildlife Rehabilitation Center.

She had no broken bones despite the impact. She did show what the center called obvious signs of head trauma, and she will need time. Staff said they are hopeful that a few weeks of care, rest, and monitoring will bring a full recovery and a return to her home territory. That is a hope, not a release date. The article does not say the owl is already flying.
