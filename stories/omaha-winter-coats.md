United Way of the Midlands and volunteers sorted 2,000 new winter coats and other cold-weather gear for Omaha Public Schools students, KETV reported. Distribution is set for this weekend.

The 2,000 figure is the gear prepared for this round. The weekend handoff is still the step that gets each coat to a student, and the sorting is the work volunteers already finished.
