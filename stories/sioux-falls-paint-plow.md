Sioux Falls held Paint the Plow for the 17th year. More than 20 school and youth groups decorated city snowplows. People can vote for a favorite, and the winner rides in the Parade of Lights on Nov. 27.

The plows still have a winter job. For a few weeks they are also rolling art the students painted.
