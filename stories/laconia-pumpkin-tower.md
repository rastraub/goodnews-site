Laconia spent the weekend with its Pumpkin Festival back on the calendar, WMUR reported. The city lit the pumpkin tower, a tall display built from hundreds of carved pumpkins.

The rest of the festival spread out around that glow: a laser show, a duck race, a cornhole tournament, and costume parades for dogs and for kids. It was a busy weekend because the tower was lit again.
