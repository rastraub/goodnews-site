Cindy Sitler, a St. Luke's nurse and a mother from Fogelsville, returned to competitive cycling after 19 years away and won a world title, WFMZ reported. She trained around full-time work, took two national titles, and then won gold in the team sprint at the UCI Masters Track World Championships.

The gold is that team event. The two national titles came on the way back, and the 19 years is the gap before she started racing again.
