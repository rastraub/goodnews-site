Amadeus Woody, a 7-year-old who uses a wheelchair, scored a touchdown in his older brother's last game of the season, The Oregonian reported on November 4, 2026. In a 5th- and 6th-grade matchup between Newberg and Sherwood, players fell in behind him and let him roll into the end zone. His mother, Tiffany Woody, said in a social-media post that the team let him keep the game ball, signed by the players.

Parent Laura Parks said the boys came together to make the play real, and she called it sportsmanship. The article does not name the brother and does not give the final score. The published account is the touchdown and the signed ball.
