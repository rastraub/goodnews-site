actu.fr reported on November 12, 2026, that a male gorilla was born on October 20 at the zoo in La Boissière-du-Doré. Director Sébastien Laurent said he had waited 24 years. For 21 years the zoo kept only males. Two females arrived in 2023, but their genetic lines were not a match for breeding. Mah Mah came in the spring of 2025, with another female, under the European breeding programme, to have a baby with Jaja, the only male still there. The other females are on contraception. The infant has no name yet. Laurent said the ape team will pick options and visitors will vote, which the zoo does for animals it treats as flagship species.

Jaja is 27 and weighs about 200 kilograms. Laurent said gorilla pregnancy runs eight and a half to nine months, and the birth fell inside the window keepers expected, from mid-September to early December. He said the group has been calmer than ever, Mah Mah is the oldest female at 24 and the most dominant, she is calm, and she has milk. She had a baby before at La Vallée des Singes. He expects her to nurse for months, with the infant starting to move toward the others between one and two years of age. The article is about this birth. It is not a census of gorillas in African forests.
