Trick-or-treating usually runs one direction. In Bluefield it ran the other way. Kids from the Boys and Girls Clubs of Greater Bluefield put on costumes and carried candy to downtown businesses, WVVA reported. They handed out Snickers and Skittles at the Railyard Restaurant, First Community Bank, and the Craft Memorial Library.

Chief executive Bill Garrett framed the walk as a thank-you to a town that shows up for the club. The national Boys & Girls Clubs of America is the wider organization. This visit was the Greater Bluefield club, on its own sidewalks, giving the treats instead of collecting them.
