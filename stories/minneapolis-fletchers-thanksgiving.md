Fletcher's Ice Cream & Cafe in Minneapolis is opening its doors for a free Thanksgiving dinner for about 300 people, KARE 11 reported. The seventh annual meal welcomes anyone, especially people who do not have family to celebrate with.

About 300 is the number of places. The dinner is free. The seventh year means the shop has kept the table going. The welcome is open, with a special note for people spending the holiday without family nearby.
