Fishing with Special Friends held its 15th free day on the water at Wrightsville Beach, WWAY reported. People with disabilities fished from Johnnie Mercer's Pier with volunteers beside them.

Fifteen is the year-count. The pier is the place. The report is this year's gathering, not a roster of every person who has come over a decade and a half.
