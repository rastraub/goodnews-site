A cut-a-thon in Lackawanna County collected ponytails for children who need wigs. Dickson City Great Clips and the Career Technology Center of Lackawanna County took the hair donations. Every dollar from the haircuts went to Wigs for Kids, which makes the wigs free for the children who receive them.

The hair and the money both left the shop headed for the same purpose.
