Detectorist Ed Trapper, the NJ Ring Finder, returned Tina Tomeo's grandmother's heirloom ring on his 500th find, News 12 New Jersey reported. He pulled the ring from drained Lake Barnegat and surprised Tomeo with it at work.

The 500th find is this recovery. The drained lake is where the ring was. The surprise at work is how she got it back. The ring is a family heirloom, not a new piece of jewelry.
