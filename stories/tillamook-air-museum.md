Vintage Aviation Museum, a Utah nonprofit, bought the storm-damaged World War II blimp hangar that houses the Tillamook Air Museum for $1, OPB reported. Hangar B is the world's largest clear-span wooden structure. The museum reopened on Oct. 29 with help from more than 80 volunteers. Roof restoration is the work still ahead. The sale is what kept the museum in that hangar. The roof is not finished.
