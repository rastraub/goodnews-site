The Great Fish Community Challenge raised a record $10.8 million for 89 Flathead Valley nonprofits, NBC Montana reported. That is 34 percent more than last year. More than 4,000 donors gave $9.1 million, and the Circle of Giving added a record $1.7 million match. The match covered 76 percent of the first $25,000 each group raised, up to $19,000 extra.

The Whitefish Community Foundation has run the drive since 2015, and the lifetime total is now above $53 million. The latest total was celebrated at a ceremony in Kalispell. The money stays with local groups rather than a single statewide fund.
