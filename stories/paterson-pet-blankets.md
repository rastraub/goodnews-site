St. Gerard Majella Youth Ministry in Paterson turned its Blessing of the Animals into a supply drive, The Beacon reported. The group delivered fifteen 40-gallon bags of towels, sheets, and blankets.

The bags went to local veterinary clinics, animal hospitals, and shelters, where linens become bedding. Fifteen bags is the delivery from this drive, not a standing weekly shipment.
