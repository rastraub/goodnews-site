A missed connection at O'Hare turned into a ride home for a kitten, NBC Chicago reported. Minnesota traveler Virgil Wiebe was waiting out a long delay when he stepped outside Terminal 1. A three-week-old orange kitten ran up to him. He looked for a mother and did not find one.

Staff at the airport said they would need a kennel. With boarding close, Wiebe's daughter arranged a delivery of a carrier and formula. United put the cat on the flight for a $150 pet fee. Wiebe named him Macanudo O'Hare, Harry for short, after O'Hare and the cigar he was smoking when the kitten appeared. He told the station the kitten is theirs now.
