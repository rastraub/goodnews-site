BJ Rottinghaus, a sophomore at Texas A&M, runs Y'alloween, a nonprofit he started in high school to give free Halloween costumes to children in shelters. The giveaway now covers Bryan-College Station, Houston, and Austin.

The costumes do not come with a bill. The project is how he kept a high school idea going after he got to college, in more than one city.
