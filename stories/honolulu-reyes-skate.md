Pro skateboarder Jaime Reyes coached 75 Honolulu middle schoolers at ʻAʻala Park, Hawaii News Now reported. Students from three middle schools got hands-on lessons and free gear from his nonprofit, REYES'ing Hope. The session is part of work with Trust for Public Land to make the park a welcoming space.

Seventy-five is the number of students. The three schools are where they came from. The gear was free, and the coaching was in the park rather than only a demonstration.
