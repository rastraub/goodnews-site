A male Mhorr gazelle named Songo was born on October 9 at Bioparc in Doué-en-Anjou. Ouest-France reported the birth on November 2, 2026, after the park announced it on November 1. The park said Songo is doing well and spending time in the sun. He is the second male calf in the herd this year, after Nioro, born in April.

The park said only a few hundred Mhorr gazelles may remain in the wild, after drought, scarce grazing, competition with livestock, and hunting pressure. Bioparc Conservation has supported a programme in Senegal since 2009. The work includes restoring damaged habitat, slowing an invasive cactus that crowds out grazing, and involving local people in the reserve. Last year the fund contributed 8,000 euros. The news is Songo's birth and that recorded support. It is not a new census of the species.
