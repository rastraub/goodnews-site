King Bard, 5, won a fishing tournament on Lake Sam Rayburn as the youngest angler there, KTRE reported. He told the station the part he likes is the excitement just before a fish bites. His father, Kory Bard, came home thinking more children should get that feeling, including kids who have never used a pole. He founded Reign Fishing.

Kory, a certified angler instructor, then partnered with the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department for a free youth day at Ratcliff Lake in Houston County. Thirty-five children signed up. The department supplied first-fish certificates, pins, and learning materials. One parent, Brendon Ward, brought his family and described the day as a chance to slow down outside.
