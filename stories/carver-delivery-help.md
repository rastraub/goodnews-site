Carver police credit an Uber Eats driver with getting help to a resident who was on the floor. The driver noticed a dark house, heard a faint cry, called 911, and stayed until officers arrived. The stop was supposed to be a drop-off. The dark windows and the voice were the reason it was not. Police say that pause is what brought them to the door.
