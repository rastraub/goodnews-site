The Pan-Mass Challenge beat its $79 million goal by $1 million and handed Dana-Farber Cancer Institute a record $80 million, CBS Boston reported. For the 20th year, 100 percent of the money riders raised goes to Dana-Farber.

The gift comes from the bike-a-thon's rider fundraising. The $1 million is the amount above this year's goal. The 20-year mark is the streak of sending all rider-raised dollars to the institute. The check was the Boston presentation of that total.
