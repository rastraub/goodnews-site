Addy's Playground of Possibility opened Oct. 13, 2026, at Connections Education Center, 1310 Old Congress Ave. in West Palm Beach. The sensory-friendly playground includes a communication board, sensory elements, an accessible safety surface, and community signs such as stop signs and a railroad crossing so students can practice real-world safety.

Volunteer and philanthropist Addy van Leeuwen funded the playground. School leaders said the space was designed for children with autism, with features that go beyond a typical play structure.
