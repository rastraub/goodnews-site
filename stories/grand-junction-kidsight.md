KidSight volunteers with the Lions Club spent the year inside more than 50 Grand Valley schools and preschools. They screened more than 3,200 children's eyes at no charge to the families. The checks flagged 363 children, 11.1 percent of those screened, as at risk of vision problems that could last, and those children were referred to an eye doctor. The visits also spare each school from buying its own screener, a machine that costs about $6,000.
