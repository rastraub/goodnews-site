The FDA approved Roche's oral pill giredestrant, given with everolimus, for ER-positive, HER2-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced breast cancer after hormone therapy, Reuters reported. In a late-stage trial of 207 patients, the combination cut the risk of progression or death by 62 percent. The median time without progression was 10 months, compared with 5.5 months without the combination.

The approval is for that specific advanced cancer after hormone therapy. The 207 patients are the trial group behind the 62 percent figure. This is an approval of a treatment studied in that trial, not a claim that every case follows the same course.
