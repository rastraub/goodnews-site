Fredericksburg officers Dylan Smith and Lawrence pulled a woman out of a burning home, WUSA9 reported. Bodycam video shows them finding her in heavy smoke and getting her outside.

Both officers were treated for smoke inhalation afterward. The story is that rescue and the treatment the two officers needed, not a wider count of fires in the city.
