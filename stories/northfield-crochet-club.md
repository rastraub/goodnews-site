A crochet club at Mercado Local in Northfield is a multigenerational group of Latina women who stitch together and then put the work where the town can see it. Their pieces now hang in trees downtown. The city is a partner in getting the art up.

The knots are how the group spends time with one another. The trees are how the rest of Northfield meets what they made.
