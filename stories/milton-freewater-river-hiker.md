Kathleen Mahony and Stephon Schulze, both of College Place, Washington, were hiking the Walla Walla River Trail near Harris Park outside Milton-Freewater when they fell down a steep bank into the river, the East Oregonian reported from the Umatilla County Sheriff's Office. It was the night of Nov. 8.

Mahony climbed out and called for help around 9 p.m. Milton-Freewater Rural Fire Department and EMS found her. She said Schulze was still in the river corridor about two miles up the trail and that he was hurt. The fire department asked the sheriff's office to send search and rescue.

Sgt. Adam Gregory, deputy Cody Gray, and fire personnel hiked those two miles and found Schulze at the bottom of an embankment about 40 feet down. The sheriff's office said he had a head injury, possible broken ribs, and signs of hypothermia. Crews used a rescue litter and ropes to raise him to the trail, then carried him toward the trailhead. They met more search-and-rescue members on the way and turned him over to medical crews. A helicopter flew him to a hospital. An ambulance took Mahony in for an evaluation.
