Veterans from every era spoke at the South Burlington library ahead of Veterans Day, WCAX reported. The gathering was a Vets Town Hall, a chance for people who served to tell their own stories. A Vietnam veteran said that after 35 years he finally feels welcome wearing his hat. The afternoon was built around those firsthand accounts.
