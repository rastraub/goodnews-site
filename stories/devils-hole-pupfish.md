The fall survey counted 137 Devils Hole pupfish, up from 69 a year earlier, SFGATE reported. That is more than double. The pupfish, which live in a single cavern in Death Valley National Park, are described as the world's rarest fish. Biologists think most of the fish in this count hatched in the wild cavern. Earthquakes had earlier knocked the population down. The 137 is the fall count. It shows a rebound from 69, and it is one survey of the fish that live in that water.
