Auckland's Diwali festival is back on Queen Street and in Aotea Square. RNZ reported on October 30, 2026, that the BNZ Auckland Diwali Festival, which began in 2002, returns this weekend with music, dance, art, food, and film. Event manager Taode, who grew up in Pune in western India, described a programme across three stages, plus theatre, exhibitions, yoga, Bollywood films, and activities for families.

Since October 24 the town hall has carried Diwali-themed art, and Te Waihorotiu Station has a projection that mixes sound, pictures, and story. The harbour bridge is lit fuchsia and orange through the Vector Lights display until November 1, with other landmarks joining in. RNZ said the weekend is expected to close Sunday evening with fireworks at about 8:50 p.m., then a projection show on the town hall.

The report is a guide to a free-feeling city celebration already under way, not a head count. It does not say how many people will come, or claim that every Auckland neighbourhood marks the festival the same way. What it does record is a long-running public programme built around performance, food, and family activities in the middle of the city.
