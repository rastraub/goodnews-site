Two capybara pups were born at Pairi Daiza in Belgium on the night of November 4 to November 5. Actu reported the park's announcement on November 12, 2026. The births were in the tropical greenhouse called Edenya. The park described the pups as already calm.

Capybaras are large semi-aquatic rodents from South America. They are known for a quiet temperament and for living alongside many other species. Some especially large adults weigh more than 70 kilograms. The report did not give the pups' sexes or names. The news is these two births. It is not a claim about wild capybaras.
