More than 20 dogs from Butterfly Paws walked the halls of Nationwide Children's Hospital in costume, ABC 6 reported. The group includes therapy dogs and service dogs. Patients, families, and staff were the audience in Columbus.

The parade was a Halloween visit, not a medical treatment. The station's count is the dogs that came, and the people in the hospital who got to see them.
