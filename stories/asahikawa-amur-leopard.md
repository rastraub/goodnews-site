KFB reported on November 12, 2026, that Asahiyama Zoo in Asahikawa began public viewing of an Amur leopard cub on November 11. The cub was born in August to Mirai and Den. The station, drawing on that morning's Good Morning broadcast, said the cub is about 70 centimetres long and weighs about 7 kilograms, and that it was filmed picking its way through snow. Name suggestions open on November 14.

Harada, of the zoo's carnivore house, said he wants visitors to watch how Mirai raises the cub and how the youngster's skills increase. Amur leopards live mainly in Russia, and the report said only about 100 remain in the wild. The debut is a zoo milestone. It does not change that wild figure.
