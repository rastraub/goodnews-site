After almost 30 years in her Southwest Atlanta house, an Army veteran needed a new roof, CBS News Atlanta reported. Owens Corning, Rooks Roofing and Exteriors, and Rebuilding Together Atlanta installed one. She said she was filled with joy and that the work arrived at the right time.

The roof went on in a day. Organizers said the preparation took about two months. Through its Roof Deployment Project, Owens Corning has provided roofs and materials for more than 850 veterans nationwide since 2016. The Atlanta job is one house in that longer list.
