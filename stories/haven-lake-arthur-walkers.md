Two bushwalkers from South Australia were brought out of Tasmania's Western Arthur Range on Sunday, ABC News reported. Tasmania Police said the women, 58 and 26, set off a satellite device about 8 p.m. Saturday and asked for help because of the conditions. They were on a track at Haven Lake. The weather kept the rescue helicopter from reaching them, so a search-and-rescue crew was dropped at Arthurs Plains at 5 a.m. and walked in from there.

The crew found the pair about 8 a.m. and was taking them back to Hobart. They were checked as a precaution and did not need medical treatment. Police said the women had packed for the overnight trip they had planned, and that their personal locator beacon is what brought help quickly. Senior Constable Cameron Rennie said walkers in remote country should carry enough food, water, a way to call, and shelter for a delay, and should pack gear so it stays dry.
