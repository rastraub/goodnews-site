A bird dog named Esther was rescued after falling 60 feet into a basalt fissure in southern Idaho, Outdoor Life reported. Magic Valley Paramedics' volunteer rope-rescue team lowered a rescuer into the crack, about 2 feet wide, and hoisted the pointer out. She was unharmed, tail wagging.

Sixty feet is the drop. About 2 feet is the width the rescuer had to work in. The rope team is who went down and brought her up. The wagging tail is how the story says she came out.
