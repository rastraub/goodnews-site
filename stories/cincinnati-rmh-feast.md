Ronald McDonald House chefs in Cincinnati cooked Thanksgiving for about 400 people, families staying there while a child is sick. Executive Chef Greg Wyatt's team spent three days on turkeys, hams, and 30 pies through the Taste of Hope program. The house is already where those families sleep when the hospital is the reason they are in town. The three days in the kitchen are so the holiday is a meal, not another errand. Four hundred is the size of the table. Thirty pies is the dessert count after the birds and the hams.
