Family and great-grandchildren marked Leland Jensen's 100th birthday in Fargo, InForum and WDAY reported. Jensen served in the Army Air Corps and has been an American Legion member for 80 years.

The gathering is a birthday, counted in public because both numbers are long: a century of life and eight decades with the Legion. The report is about that party and that service, not a history of the war.
