Mission Mules will return to Upshur County with Christmas gifts for children in households hit by July's flooding, WDTV reported. Band of Brothers, United Cajun Navy, and local partners are organizing the visit, called Mission Christmas Joy. Families can register now.

The gifts are for those children. The mules are part of how the group makes the delivery. July is when the flooding happened. Registering is open so households can sign up before the visit.
