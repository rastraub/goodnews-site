The 17th annual Day of Kindness raised about $238,000 to feed hundreds of Oklahoma City families, KFOR reported. Trial lawyers, OKC firefighters, the Salvation Army, and volunteers handed out turkeys and sides at Southern Hills Baptist Church.

About $238,000 is what the day raised. Hundreds of families is who received the food. The church is where the turkeys and sides were handed out. The 17th year is how long the event has run.
