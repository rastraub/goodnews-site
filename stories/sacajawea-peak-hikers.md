At 4:48 p.m. on Nov. 8, Gallatin County 911 got a call from a hiker south of Sacajawea Peak in the Bridger Mountains, the Livingston Enterprise reported from a Nov. 10 statement by the Gallatin County Sheriff's Office Search and Rescue. Three people were in the group. One appeared to have altitude sickness, and they were not ready for the weather.

Volunteers answered. Two teams staged at Fairy Lake and started hiking in. While they were on the way, the group hiked down from the spot where they had called. They met the rescuers on the trail. The teams walked with them the rest of the way out to Fairy Lake.

The sheriff's office asked people headed into the mountains this time of year to plan for cold and for dark, because a trip can run longer than expected and the sun is setting earlier. The report does not name the hikers.
