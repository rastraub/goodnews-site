Denver police are distributing Thanksgiving food to 1,000 families for the seventh year, CBS Colorado reported. Each bag holds a frozen turkey or Cornish game hen, plus green beans, mashed potatoes, stuffing, a gravy packet, and corn. Once a bag is filled, volunteers load it into a patrol vehicle to be delivered.

AT&T donated $20,000 for the meals. Walmart provided the food at a significant discount. The Denver Police Foundation coordinated the funding and the partnership. Chief Ron Thomas said those partners were making a traditional Thanksgiving meal possible for 1,000 families who might otherwise have to buy the food themselves or go without. The volunteers' stated goal was that no one would go hungry.
