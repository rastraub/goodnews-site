Nicole Rogich, a veteran in West Salem, has a service dog named Gilby after a community fundraiser that ran for two years. WKBT News 8000 reported that the La Crosse County American Legion and other local groups paid for Gilby's training. The dog is named for James Gilbertson, an Air Force veteran.

Training is why the wait was long. A service dog is matched to one person, and the community's role was to cover that cost. The news is the meeting, now that the training has been paid for. The report does not list the tasks Gilby will perform beyond the service-dog role those groups funded.
