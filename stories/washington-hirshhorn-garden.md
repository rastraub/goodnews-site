The Hirshhorn's renovated Sculpture Garden reopens October 31 after years of construction, WTOP reported. The 1.3-acre garden on the National Mall now holds 30,000 new trees and plants and 60 works of art, about 50 percent more than before.

Artist-architect Hiroshi Sugimoto designed stacked stone walls that form outdoor rooms for sitting with a piece. The underground passage that once ran under the street to the museum is open again, with an installation called Passage of Reflection along both sides. The reopen is a date, a plant count, and a longer list of sculpture, after a stretch when the garden was a construction site.
