A man driving on Gregg Way in Cheyenne around 6 a.m. Saturday met a pool of water from a broken water main, Cap City News reported, citing Cheyenne Fire Rescue. The water was deeper than he expected. His truck got stuck. He climbed on top of it and waited.

Battalion 1, Engine 4, and Ladder 1 were sent. The crew used Ladder 1's aerial ladder to reach him and bring him down. The department said no one was injured. Cheyenne Fire Rescue described the call as work that took skill and teamwork in conditions that were not an ordinary fire. The account stops at that safe ending. It does not name the driver.
