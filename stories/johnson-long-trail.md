The Green Mountain Club has the last easement it needed to take roughly 3 miles of the Long Trail in Lamoille County off a road and into the woods, Vermont Public reported. The talks with landowners took more than 30 years. Hikers in Johnson now walk about half a mile on West Settlement Road. The new route will follow a feature called Dry Ridge and skip that pavement.

The easement also means more than 98% of the Long Trail is legally protected. About 4 miles, on 13 properties, still lack that status. Separately, hikers on Belvidere Mountain in Eden are already using a rebuilt stretch north of Route 118, where the old line followed a steep, eroding logging road below a fire tower.
