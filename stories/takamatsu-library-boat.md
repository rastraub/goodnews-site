Kagawa Prefecture is opening a donation drive to help run a children's library boat. KSB reported that gifts for the Honnomori will be collected from November 13, 2026, through January 15, 2027. The goal is 1 million yen, in shares of 5,000 yen. Donors do not receive a gift. If they agree, their names can appear on the prefecture's website, and a gift of 300,000 yen or more can also be listed on the boat. The contributions qualify as furusato nozei, Japan's hometown-tax donation.

Architect Tadao Ando gave the boat to the prefecture. Since April 2025 it has gone out for weekday school events and weekend family events, including reading cruises, carrying books to remote islands and other stops. The news is the fundraising window and the boat that is already in service. The campaign itself had not opened on the day of the report.
