Red Cloud Renewable Energy Center on the Pine Ridge Reservation trains Native workers for solar jobs, KOTA and South Dakota News Watch reported. About 1,100 people have gone through the training. About 80 percent of graduates are working in solar. The center also won a national Power for All award.

The classes are hands-on job training, and the employment figure is the result the report highlights. Most graduates are in the trade. That is not a claim that every student was placed. The award is national recognition for the training and the work that followed it.
