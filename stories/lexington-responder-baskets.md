Lexington firefighters and paramedics joined a Thanksgiving food effort, WKYT reported on November 18. They partnered with Lexington Police, Baptist Health Lexington, Central Emergency Physicians, and local baker Miranda Florence.

The group assembled more than 100 meal baskets for people who needed a holiday dinner. They also delivered the baskets themselves, rather than leaving them for someone else to pick up.

WKYT did not list the addresses or describe what was inside each basket. The figure it reported is the baskets, more than 100, not a count of every person who will sit down to eat.
