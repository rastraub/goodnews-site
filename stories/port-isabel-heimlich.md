Desiree Vidaurri, a Point Isabel ISD student, used the Heimlich maneuver on a choking classmate, and the school board honored her for it, KRGV reported. Vidaurri said something in her friend's whipped cream, a cap, was swallowed and the friend started choking. In less than four seconds Vidaurri decided to act. She said she was hesitant and did it anyway because she did not want something worse to happen. She told the station she would not have known the maneuver except for her parents. Her mother, Jennifer Alaniz, had gone over it with her a couple of times, and Vidaurri had also seen it in movies.

On Monday night the school board gave her the new Tarpon Spotlight Award, for a student or staff member who goes beyond what is expected and sets an example in the district and the town. Superintendent Teri Capistran described the award that way. Alaniz said she was proud that her daughter recognized the moment and finished the maneuver successfully. The station said the choking happened last month. The award is the recognition. The report does not describe any later medical care.
