Friday morning, around 8 a.m., crews were called to a property on Taylor Lane near the Natchez Trace Parkway in the West End community, WKRN reported. Lawrence County Fire & Rescue said the first units found a man trapped in soybeans up to his neck. They checked the air in the bin and secured him with rope gear.

More crews assembled a GSI tube around him, a barrier meant to take the pressure of the grain off a person while rescuers work. They then drew soybeans away with grain augers. Outside, two ladder trucks were set up, one as a high point for the ropes and one as the way down. Lawrenceburg Utility Systems had a vacuum truck ready in case the grain shifted again. Once that was in place, the rescuers lifted him out without incident, helped him to the ground, and had medical personnel evaluate him. The department said he had no major injuries, and that the ending could easily have been different. The report does not name him.
