Durango's Community Thanksgiving is revived after seven years. Manna soup kitchen served more than 1,400 meals at the La Plata County Fairgrounds, with 700 to 800 pounds of turkey and 200 pies. The dinner had been paused through the COVID years. Neighbors who had been stranded by recent floods found new friends at the table. The food is the count. The seats next to strangers who needed a meal, and a conversation, are the reason Manna set the fairgrounds again.
