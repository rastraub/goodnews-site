Darwin's rheas are walking the Chacabuco Valley again because a women-led crew stayed with the eggs. Mongabay's October 10, 2026, film follows the Rheas Reproduction Center in Patagonia National Park, where the team works with Rewilding Chile. The report says 13 birds were released in April 2026. In the footage, keepers describe another opening as the third release of the year, after the birds spent about two months getting used to the valley.

Surveys once estimated only 11 to 15 rheas in the area. A ranger said the birds were rarely seen when she arrived in 2016. Rheas disperse seeds as they range. On the release day in the film they grazed and then moved kilometers from the pen. The work is a return of a missing bird, not a claim that the steppe is full again. The nearest town is Cochrane.
