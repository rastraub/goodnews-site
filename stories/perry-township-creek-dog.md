Perry Township firefighters from Station 12 lifted an elderly dog up a creek bank after she slid about 40 feet down the embankment, exploreJefferson reported. She weighs about 120 pounds, and her owner could not get her back up alone.

The call was that one rescue. The distance is how far she slid, and the weight is why the crew's help mattered.
