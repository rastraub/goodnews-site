Bay Area Service Teens are packing adoption kits through Cause for Paws, the Bay Area Service League reported. The kits go to Bay Area Pet Adoptions/SPCA in San Leon and to the Pasadena Animal Shelter.

Families who adopt can leave with supplies for the first days at home. The program is that steady packing and delivery, not a single-day adoption total.
