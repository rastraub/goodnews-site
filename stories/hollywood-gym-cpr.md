Michael Baugh collapsed during a CrossFit class at Training Pit in Hollywood, NBC 6 South Florida reported. Officer Cameron Graziadei was off duty and working out in the same class. He saw that Baugh had no pulse, was not breathing, and was unconscious. Graziadei started chest compressions and used his radio to call for more units and for fire rescue.

Officer Joseph Scheibl was finishing another assignment when he heard that call and went to the gym. CPR was already underway, and the gym's defibrillator was being placed. He stepped in and helped. The two officers kept compressions and the defibrillator going until more first responders arrived.

Baugh was taken to Memorial Regional Hospital, where his heart was stabilized. Randy Katz, the hospital's division chief of emergency services, said those first minutes of compressions and the defibrillator gave Baugh another chance.

On Tuesday, Nov. 24, days before Thanksgiving, Baugh was out of the hospital and met the officers for the first time since the class. He thanked them and said he wanted to take them to dinner. The station also noted that Hollywood residents can take a free CPR class on the last Tuesday of each month at Memorial Regional Hospital.
