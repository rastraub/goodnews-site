Harvest Volunteer Fire Department firefighters were sent around 10:40 p.m. Monday, November 2, to Old Railroad Bed Road and Yarbrough Road, Rocket City Now reported. The first crews on scene found two vehicles that had crashed. People were trapped inside one of them.

Firefighters removed two people from that vehicle. They gave medical care to three people in all until HEMSI, the ambulance service, arrived to take over. The department did not describe anyone's condition and did not give names. Harvest's units cleared the scene once that handoff was done and went back into service.
