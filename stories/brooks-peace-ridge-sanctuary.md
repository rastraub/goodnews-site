Peace Ridge Sanctuary in Brooks is marking 25 years of taking in farm animals, WABI reported. It cares for more than 650 animals on more than 1,000 acres, spread across five facilities. Since it opened in 2001, it has taken in more than 2,000. A gift match of up to $15,000 is part of the anniversary.

The work is daily care, not a single rescue photo. The animals stay. The anniversary is a count of years, land, and animals still being fed, and a request for help covering that cost.
