Pomegranate Market in downtown Sioux Falls served a free-will Thanksgiving lunch to benefit The Banquet, a free-meal program. Volunteers came, neighbors donated pies, and the lunch gathered gifts for that program. Guests could eat without a set price. The pies and the gifts were the part that traveled past the market's own tables, toward a program that feeds people on ordinary days, not only on the holiday.
