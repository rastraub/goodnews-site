Semper Fi Homestead in Las Vegas is teaching veterans how to grow food, put it in jars, and bake, and the classes are free, 8 News Now reported. Disabled Marine veteran and nurse Scott Rehm founded the homestead with Jeanalin Rehm. The Veteran Homestead Boot Camp runs eight weeks. Ten veterans are enrolled.

The lessons sit on a working homestead. The aim is practical skill: farming, canning, and baking, offered to fellow veterans at no charge. The current class is small, a first group of students rather than a seat for every veteran in the city.
