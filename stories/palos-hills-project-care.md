Students in every cohort of Moraine Valley's fire science academies stocked Project Care, the college's food pantry, the school reported. The service project filled the pantry with canned goods and household items.

The donation is what those academy classes brought in. Project Care is the campus pantry that will hand the supplies out, and the academies made the drive a standing part of the program rather than a one-time club event.
