A Lagotto Romagnolo named Hilkka had six puppies on a Finnish livestream, and the cameras will stay with them until they leave for new homes. Yle News reported on October 13, 2026, that the birth came early on Saturday, October 10: two females and four males. All six were gaining weight, and Hilkka was looking after them. The stream is planned for about eight weeks, with the puppies due to go to homes around mid-December.

The cameras are in the breeder's house. Yle is not publishing the town. The October 13 report said thousands of people followed the birth. It is the sixth season of the broadcast. Cameras for this litter went up on October 6. The breed was once used to retrieve birds and is now often trained to find truffles. The litter is healthy in the report Yle published. That report does not say how many people have already asked to adopt one.
