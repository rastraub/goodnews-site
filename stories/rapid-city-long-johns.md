For the seventh year, students at Rapid City Catholic Schools turned Long Johns for Long Johns into warm clothes for veterans without homes, KOTA Territory News reported. They raised money and donated thermals, gloves, hats, and socks to Cornerstone Rescue Mission. The seventh year is how long the schools have run the drive. The gift this time is those layers, delivered to the mission that serves the veterans.
