Jeff Kinney, author of the Diary of a Wimpy Kid books, visited Freeman Public Schools in Adams on October 27, KLKN reported. Students were off that day and had been told to come in for something else. Kinney's team and media specialist Becky Weyere had set up a Halloween assembly instead, for children who had just finished one of the books.

The visit mixed games and book talk with the surprise of the author himself. Kinney then announced a personal gift of $1,000 in children's books for the school library, including more Diary of a Wimpy Kid titles and other books chosen for a range of readers.
