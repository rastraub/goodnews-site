Students and communities in Dermer Dreams' second 100-Yard Food Drive Challenge collected more than 250,000 pounds of food, Good News Network reported. Yellow bags covered the football field at Howell High School.

The groceries are bound for about 30 food-bank partners. The weight is this year's challenge total, and the field was the place they stacked it so the scale of the drive was visible.
