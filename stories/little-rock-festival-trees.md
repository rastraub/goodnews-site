CARTI's 50th Festival of Trees raised a record $1.08 million for cancer care, Arkansas Business reported. The proceeds support CARTI's 18 locations in Arkansas. Over five decades the event has raised more than $13 million.

The 50th festival is this year's record. $1.08 million is the new total. Eighteen is the number of CARTI locations the money supports. More than $13 million is what five decades of the festival have raised.
