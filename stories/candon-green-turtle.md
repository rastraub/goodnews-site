A female green sea turtle weighing about 80 kilograms was released at Candon, in Ilocos Sur. The Philippine Daily Inquirer reported the release on November 3, 2026, citing a city statement. The City Agriculture Office, led by Eric Gacutan, carried it out with the Department of Environment and Natural Resources and the Philippine Coast Guard, under Candon's Pawikan Development Project. Mayor Eric Dario Singson led the city's account of the day. Veterinarian Ingrid Baglao represented the environment department. Staff from the Candon City Aquarium and the agriculture office also helped.

The Inquirer noted that green sea turtles are endangered in the Philippines and protected under the wildlife conservation law. The article is a short account of this one release. It does not say whether the turtle had been injured, how long anyone had cared for it, or how many other turtles the city project has returned to the water.
