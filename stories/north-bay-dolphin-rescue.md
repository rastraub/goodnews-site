A couple in North Bay Village called 911 Wednesday after a dolphin in Biscayne Bay stopped swimming normally, WSVN reported. Ahmed Raul Perez said his wife saw the animal from their apartment. He flew a drone and found the tail caught in a net, with the head bobbing and the tail pulled under.

North Bay Village and Miami-Dade marine patrol boats arrived within minutes. Perez kept the drone overhead so the crews could find the dolphin, and neighbors pointed from shore. Officers went into the water and cut the netting off the tail. The dolphin swam off. Village officials said it appeared uninjured.
