Deputy Matthew Arrington, a school resource officer, saw a Siegel High student choking on a piece of sandwich in Murfreesboro. He kept up back blows until the food came free and the student could breathe again. The department gave him a Letter of Commendation.

The student was all right because the deputy stayed with the blows until the airway cleared.
