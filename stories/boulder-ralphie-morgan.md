Emma Morgan is the first woman to run in the front position with Ralphie, the Boulder Daily Camera reported. She is a University of Colorado senior from New Castle. The live buffalo mascot has been guided onto the field for nearly 60 years, and the lead runner in that tradition had always been a man.

The change is one position on one mascot team. Morgan is in the front spot. The paper's account is that first, not a rewrite of every role around Ralphie.
