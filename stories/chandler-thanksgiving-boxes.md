Students from Arizona College Prep Middle School and Chandler High School spent World Kindness Day sorting Thanksgiving donations at the Chandler CARE Center, AZFamily reported. The center, at 777 East Galveston Street, is a school-based family resource for the Chandler Unified School District. Friday's job was to sort and prepare food already given for the annual distribution to district families. The station said the extra hands were a large help.

The drive is still open. The center is asking for two-pound bags of rice, two-pound bags of beans, gravy, cake mix, and frosting. The article does not give a count of boxes packed or of families on the list. What it does report is that students used the kindness-day holiday to get the food ready.
