Cascade Bicycle Club's Pedaling Relief Project has now rescued and delivered 2 million pounds of food for five Seattle-area food banks, the club reported. The haul covers 861 riders and more than 100,000 miles.

Some of those miles are home deliveries for people who cannot get to a pantry. The food is collected, then carried by bike to the banks that hand it out. Two million pounds is the running total for the project, not a single day's ride.
