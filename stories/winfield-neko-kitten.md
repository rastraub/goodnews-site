A lung doctor turned a tool of his trade on a wall, CBS News Chicago reported. Dr. Benjamin Seides, of Northwestern Medicine Central DuPage Hospital in Winfield, used a borescope to locate five-month-old Neko after the kitten slipped behind a basement wall. The camera is the same idea as the scope he uses to look inside airways: a way to see a space a person cannot enter.

He found the cat and got Neko out. The station's account is that one rescue, using a camera built for tight places, not a new treatment and not a promise about every animal in a wall.
