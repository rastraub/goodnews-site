A giant anteater pup born at Dortmund Zoo is riding on its mother, Bonita. Stern reported the birth on November 3, 2026, a few days after the pup arrived. The zoo has not named it. A city spokesperson said the sex of a giant anteater cannot be told until weeks after birth, so that is unknown too. Bonita, born at Singapore Zoo, is raising this pup the way she raised her first, a son named Noah, in 2025. Noah now lives on his own in a separate enclosure at Dortmund.

Father Antebus is not with them. Giant anteaters live alone, and only the mother cares for the young, so the zoo separated the pair before the birth. The Tamandua house, where Bonita and the pup are staying, is closed to visitors while they rest. The zoo has not said when people will be able to see them. This is the 66th giant anteater born at Dortmund. The news is this pup. It is not a count of giant anteaters in Central and South America, where the species lives.
