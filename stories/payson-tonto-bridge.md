A hiker was stranded on the far side of a creek at Tonto Natural Bridge State Park on Tuesday after rising water cut off the trail, the Payson Roundup reported. Tonto Rim Search and Rescue dispatched technical rope and swift-water teams at noon on Nov. 17. After looking at the creek, they decided they would have to cross the rushing water.

The team used a two-person kayak secured to a rope that rescuers controlled. They reached the hiker and brought the person back across the creek. The hiker returned to the trail and walked out. The rescue ended at 2 p.m. The paper said no further details about the hiker were released, and the report does not give a name.
