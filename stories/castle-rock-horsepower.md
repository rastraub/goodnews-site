Veterans and first responders are learning horsemanship, and leaning on one another, at Colorado Horsepower, a free program on 107 acres near Castle Rock, CBS Colorado reported. About 30 people take part. Many are combat veterans living with PTSD. Participants report better sleep and more confidence. Those are the reports from people in the program. The story describes that peer support and the work with horses. It does not present the program as a medical treatment with a measured cure rate.
