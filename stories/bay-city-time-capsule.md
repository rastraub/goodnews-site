Dozens of people gathered as Bay County opened its 1976 Bicentennial time capsule after 50 years, Spectrum News reported. A woman who had placed her baby brother's Bicentennial birth certificate inside as a fifth grader hopes to give it back to him for his birthday. Central Michigan University museum studies students will preserve the items. The opening is done. Returning that certificate is still ahead of her.
