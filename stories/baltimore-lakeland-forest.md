Audubon and Baltimore Recreation and Parks restored the forest at Lakeland Park, Audubon reported. The work put in nearly 500 trees and shrubs and more than 1,100 native plants, and it hauled out 2,000 pounds of trash. More than 500 residents helped plant. The forest had been neglected. Those plantings and that cleanup are what turned it back into a place for birds and for the people who live nearby.
