Oak Mountain High School junior Jai Varikuti started Alabama's first UNICEF club, and it now includes six high schools, WVTM 13 reported. The schools are Oak Mountain, Hoover, Spain Park, James Clemens, LAMP, and Bob Jones. Students have raised nearly $5,500 toward a $10,000 goal and rank first among more than 600 high schools in the UNICEF drive. Varikuti said last year's fundraiser amounted to a year of water for 460 children. This year's collections, including bake sales and car washes, continue until Nov. 21. The station also reported that a $5 gift can provide safe water for a year. That is the campaign's claim about a small donation, not a count of children already reached this year.
