NCW Peer Connection in Moses Lake is a small team of people with lived experience. They help with housing, school, jobs, and recovery. On an ordinary day they also have meals and a shower for someone who walks in.

The practical help and the longer plans sit in the same rooms. A person can eat and wash up there, and also start on a job, a class, or a place to live.
