Merci Ames, a science teacher and girls varsity basketball coach at Strasburg High School, is Colorado's 2027 Teacher of the Year, Chalkbeat Colorado reported. She told the outlet she did not like school much as a child, aside from P.E., and that basketball and parents who valued education helped her through. She was named in October.

A favorite lesson turns the classroom into a giant cell. Students take the roles of messenger RNA, transfer RNA, and ribosomes and move around the room as a sequence becomes a sentence that stands in for a protein. Ames is especially proud of a health-care pathway she helped bring to the school: human anatomy and physiology, plus patient-care, phlebotomy, and EKG technician courses. Students help teach hands-only CPR in the community, and the school holds an annual blood drive. She said the pathway also matters on the Eastern Plains, where health-care access can be limited. The school hopes to add EMT training and athletic-training certifications over the next couple of years.
