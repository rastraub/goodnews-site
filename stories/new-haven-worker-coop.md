Unhoused and formerly unhoused farmers are running the Whole Earth Worker Cooperative at Benincasa Community Farm near New Haven, Connecticut Public reported. The co-op pays wages, gives workers a voice in the operation, and offers a sense of home.

The farm is Benincasa. The co-op is the structure the workers built. Wages, a say in the work, and a place to belong are the three things the report describes.
