Operation Freedom Exchange handed out about 1,000 Thanksgiving meals to veterans and first responders in Sedalia, the Sedalia Democrat reported. Car after car was loaded with turkeys and the fixings. The money came from the group's Lake of the Ozarks Jeep event. Last year the handout was about 750 meals.

About 1,000 is this year's count. The Jeep event is what funded it. Veterans and first responders are who the meals were for.
