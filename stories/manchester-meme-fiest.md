Pauline Fiest had a crowded porch on Halloween, WMUR reported. Her grandson's post asked people to visit the Manchester grandmother, who is turning 70 and handed out candy dressed as Elmo. Dozens of trick-or-treaters came.

The night is one house and one invitation that worked. The station's count is dozens of visitors, not a citywide tally of who went without candy.
