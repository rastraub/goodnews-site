The Humane Society of Greater Dayton microchipped 73 animals at a free drive-through clinic during Animal Shelter Appreciation Week, Dayton 24/7 Now reported. A chip gives a finder a way to reach the owner.

The same week, the society pointed to help that is not an adoption, including volunteering and its boutique. Seventy-three is the number tagged at the clinic.
