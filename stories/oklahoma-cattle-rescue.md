Oklahoma City and Tulsa task force firefighters came home from a 15-day deployment in New Mexico floods, News9 reported. Among the jobs, they dug a cow out of neck-deep mud with boat paddles and used grain to coax a bull and 20 other cattle onto trailers.

That is 21 cattle moved to safety. Everyone on the Oklahoma crews came home safe. The animals were stranded by floodwater in New Mexico, and the grain was the lure that got the herd walking.
