More than 700 students in Chennai turned discarded clothes into a braid about 7,000 metres long. Chennai Online reported on October 22, 2026, that fashion design students at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram, attempted a world record with cloth that had been bound for a landfill, about 700 kilograms of it.

Students in the Faculty of Liberal Arts and Business Studies prepared and joined the braid. A representative of Goonj was at the attempt. The charity is due to receive the finished textile and make bags, mats, and similar goods. The paper calls it an attempt. It does not say an outside record keeper has certified the length. What it does report is a braid the students finished, a pile of waste cloth kept in use, and a recipient that already turns leftover fabric into things people need.
