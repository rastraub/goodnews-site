Seventeen wild animals were released on October 8, 2026, into Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park in Quang Tri. VietnamPlus reported the group the next day: seven Indochinese pig-tailed macaques, one crested serpent eagle, three pygmy slow lorises, two brown hornbills, two crested goshawks, one Hatinh langur, and one Sunda pangolin. The animals are listed in Vietnam's protected groups IB and IIB, under the rules for endangered species and for carrying out the CITES wildlife trade treaty. They had health checks, and each was taken to a release site judged suitable for that species.

The park is a UNESCO World Heritage site. This report is the return of these 17 animals. It does not estimate how many of each species live in the park, and it does not say how the released animals fared after they left sight.
