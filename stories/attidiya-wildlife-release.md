Forty-five wild animals that had been treated at the Attidiya Wildlife Rescue and Rehabilitation Centre were released on October 14. The Daily Mirror reported the release on October 22, 2026.

Vet Pramuditha Devasurendra described the group: six spotted deer, 13 grey langurs, one pangolin, one fishing cat, 11 snakes, eight star tortoises, and six toque macaques. Officials asked the public not to remove wild animals that are healthy or only lightly injured. A frightened but able animal is often better left where it is.

The count is these 45 animals going back out after care. It is not a measure of how the wider populations are doing.
