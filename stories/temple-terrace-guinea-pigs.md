Wednesday morning, Temple Terrace firefighters and Tampa Fire Rescue answered a house fire near 113th Avenue and 62nd Street, WTSP reported. Heavy smoke was coming from a one-story home. Crews made an interior attack, found the fire in the kitchen, and put it out before it spread through the rest of the house.

While they searched the home, firefighters found two pet guinea pigs and carried them outside. A neighbor took the animals so they would be looked after. No one was inside when firefighters arrived, and no injuries were reported. The department said the fire stayed contained, and investigators later determined it was accidental.
