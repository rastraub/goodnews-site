Prost! Pub in North Portland kept its free Thanksgiving dinner going, a tradition of more than a decade. About 15 volunteers helped serve everyone who came in the Mississippi neighborhood. A pub that charges for a plate the rest of the year set this one at nothing. The decade is the promise. The 15 people are how a small room feeds whoever walks in off that street.
