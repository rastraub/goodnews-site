Eastwood Library is set to reopen on Monday, November 2, after about six weeks shut for building problems. Nottingham Post reported the date on October 31, 2026. Inspire runs the library for Nottinghamshire County Council. It first closed in September after issues with the building were found. Temporary measures are going in so the slate cladding does not pose a risk before readers come back.

While Eastwood was closed, Kimberley Library kept longer hours, held the groups that usually meet in Eastwood, and handed out reserved books. Those sessions return to Eastwood from November 2, including rhyme time, chess, a weekly welcome hour, and a crime-fiction cafe. Kimberley then goes back to its usual hours.

The building is more than the book shelves. It also houses the local studies collection, Citizens Advice, and the food bank. The news is the reopening date and the return of those sessions, not a promise about the permanent building work still ahead.
