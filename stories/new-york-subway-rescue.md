NYPD Detectives Brett Devine and Carmelo Stracuzzi jumped onto the tracks at Chambers Street and pulled a 24-year-old woman back to the platform, the New York Post reported. She had fallen near the third rail. She was wearing angel wings. New video shows the rescue.

The detectives are with the NYPD. The station is Chambers Street in New York. The Post said she fell near the third rail and that the detectives got her back onto the platform. This account stops there.
