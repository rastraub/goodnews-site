The Ocean Plastics Recovery Project is giving marine debris a second use in Kodiak, Alaska Native News reported, drawing on Alaska Sea Grant. Crews removed 116,330 pounds from Kayak Island in 2024 and about 65,000 pounds in 2025.

At the Kodiak Innovation Center the plastic is sorted into about 100 categories and turned into reusable granules. Those weights are the island cleanups. The sorting is the step that makes the debris usable again instead of another pile on a beach.
