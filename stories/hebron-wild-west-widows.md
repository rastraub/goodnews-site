In Hebron, a growing circle of widowed friends calls itself the Wild West Widows. They gather to play Penny Drop and to laugh. The line that has stuck, reported by KFYR, is that it is good to live again, and to smile again. The group is built from loss, and the meeting is not a memorial. It is a game, a room, and the sound of people who decided the week still has a place to be loud and glad. The circle keeps getting larger.
