The Hindu reported on November 18, 2026, that the Niue Ocean Wide Trust was one of five winners of the Earthshot Prize, named in Mumbai on November 17. Each winner receives £1 million. Coral Pasisi, president of Tofia Niue, spoke at the winners' press conference about putting money into the natural systems the island depends on, not only into cutting emissions. Representatives of the ocean trust told the paper they hope to secure about $7 million more by 2028 so the conservation trust is fully capitalised and can keep protecting Niue's marine ecosystems for the long term.

That $7 million is a target, not cash already in the account. The £1 million is the prize. The Hindu did not publish a new fish count, a new no-fishing boundary, or a start date for extra patrols. The news is the award and the funding goal the trust stated in Mumbai. The work the money is meant to support is the protection the island has already organised. This report does not remeasure it.
