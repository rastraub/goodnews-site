Habitat for Humanity of Northeast Arkansas held its 16th Souper Sunday in Jonesboro, KAIT reported. The soup tasting raises money toward a new home, and this year's funds are aimed at a house in Paragould.

Sixteen is the year-count of the event. The house is the project the money is for. The report is the fundraiser, not a finished key in a door.
