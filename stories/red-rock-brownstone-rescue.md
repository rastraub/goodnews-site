Two climbers were stranded on Brownstone Wall in Juniper Canyon after a rope stuck while they were rappelling, FOX5 Las Vegas reported, citing Las Vegas Metropolitan Police Department Search and Rescue. The call came in around 6 p.m. on Nov. 17.

The department's helicopter, AIR1, flew three search-and-rescue officers and a lead climb volunteer to the base of the wall. The team climbed two pitches, freed the ropes, and helped the climbers rappel to the base. The climbers were experienced and uninjured. They had not been able to free the rope themselves, and they were stuck at the top of the route's second pitch.

AIR1 then flew the climbers and the rescue team to the Bureau of Land Management helipad, where ground crews met them.
