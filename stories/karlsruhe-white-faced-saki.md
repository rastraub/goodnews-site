ka-news reported on November 13, 2026, that a white-faced saki was born at Karlsruhe Zoo on November 9. The zoo posted the news on Thursday, November 12. Mother Oompa, described as experienced, is carrying the newborn close. The sex is not known, and the baby has no name yet. In the first weeks the infant stays against the mother, then gradually moves farther off and starts to explore. Visitors can see them in the large hall of the exotic house.

The paper's account is this birth and where to look for the pair. It does not give a wild population figure, and it does not say the baby is independent. An earlier infant at the zoo, born in February, died unexpectedly in June. That loss is a separate fact. The November baby is the one keepers are showing now.
