Ten European pond turtles were released into the pond below the church in Porqueres, with students from El Frigolet School watching and taking part. Diari de Catalunya reported the morning on November 16, 2026. The Consorci de l'Estany organized the day. The turtles were bred at the Centre de Reproducció de Tortugues de l'Albera, run by the Associació Amics de la Tortuga de l'Albera, and each animal carries a microchip for later checks. Experts were there to answer the children's questions.

The paper says the European pond turtle is endangered in Catalonia, pressed by lost habitat, broken-up wetlands, and invasive species. Since 2010 the consortium has released 207 of the turtles around Estany de Banyoles under LIFE Natura projects, and monitoring has found that animals adapted and reproduced. Those 207 are the running total for that lake area. Today's news is these ten, and the class that helped put them in the water.
