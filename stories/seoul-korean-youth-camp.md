More than 90 Korean-language learners and their teachers spent five days in South Korea. The Korea Herald reported on November 10, 2026, that the 92-person group, from 30 countries, had arrived on Friday for the International Korean Education Youth Camp. The students are middle and high school pupils who study Korean abroad. The Education Ministry hosted the camp, and the International Korean Education Foundation organized it. Students took Korean classes matched to their level, with AI used as a class tool, visited Gyeongbokgung, and sampled Korean food.

A literature contest on Sunday had them recite works and explain their readings. Education Minister's Awards went to Phung Khanh Linh of Vietnam, in fiction and essay; Siriwardena Chanthuki Thathyana of Sri Lanka, in intermediate poetry; and Prasista Zharafah Mulia of Indonesia, in beginner poetry. Siriwardena recited Hwang Ji-u's "While Waiting for You" and said the person waited for in the poem represented Korea. The students also rehearsed excerpts from "Sejong 1446," a musical about King Sejong's creation of Hangeul, and learned the camp theme song. A ceremony at the National Assembly on Tuesday evening was to include musical excerpts and a K-pop competition. The camp began in 2019 for exchanges with ASEAN countries and widened in 2022 to Korean learners worldwide. Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin said he hoped meeting peers in Korea would keep the students learning the language. The story is this one camp. It is not a measure of how many people study Korean.
