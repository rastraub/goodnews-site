The Pittsburgh Steelers and Giant Eagle will pay for free lunches for city students when Pittsburgh Public Schools are closed, TribLive reported. The meals are meant for closure days, when the school cafeteria is not open.

The announcement is the funding. It is not a tally of lunches already handed out, and it is not a change to the regular school-day menu.
