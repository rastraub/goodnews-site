Hundreds of people lined the streets of Washington on Sunday for the fourth National Veterans Parade, WTOP reported. Marching bands and visitors from across the country came out to thank veterans. Organizers said this year's crowd was bigger than the crowds in past years. The parade is the fourth of its kind, and the turnout is the larger one organizers described.
