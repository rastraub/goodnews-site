The North Fruita Desert trail network, known as 18 Road, finished a 30-mile expansion near Fruita, The Denver Gazette reported. Those new miles double the trail system. The project also added a paved access road, a large parking lot, and 68 designated campsites. A new route, the K2-Play Trail, is named for advocate Kristina Kittelson. The expansion is complete. The doubling describes the size of the trail system.
