The Mississippi fire marshal's office used a FEMA grant for 31,000 smoke alarms, and fire departments gave out more than 30,000 of them in one day, WLOX reported. The handout was Monday, statewide. Departments will also install the alarms for free.

The grant paid for 31,000 alarms. More than 30,000 is the number given out that Monday. The free install is the second part: the alarm does not stay in the box.
