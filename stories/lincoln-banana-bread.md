Students in the life-skills transition program at the Rhode Island School for the Deaf bake banana bread for the Lincoln Lions before every game. The players met the bakers on the field ahead of a win, and the greeting has become part of how the two groups know each other.

The bread shows up on game day. The meeting on the field is what turned a delivery into a friendship the team talks about.
