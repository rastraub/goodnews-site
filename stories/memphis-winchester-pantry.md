Fifth-grade teacher Denise Wilson started a food pantry at Winchester Elementary in Memphis with 30 students in January 2025, Chalkbeat Tennessee reported. It now sends about three-quarters of the school home each Friday with groceries, 252 students in all. Chalkbeat reported that chronic absences have dropped.

Thirty is where the pantry started. Two hundred fifty-two is the weekend count now. Friday is the day the groceries go home. About three-quarters is the share of the school those students represent.
