Lombard firefighters rescued 36 of 38 cats from a fire at a Cat Guardians shelter on East St. Charles Road, the Daily Herald reported. The cats that were brought out appeared to be in good condition.

Thirty-six is the number carried to safety. The paper's count of 38 is how many cats were at the home shelter. The report is about that rescue, not a later adoption tally.
