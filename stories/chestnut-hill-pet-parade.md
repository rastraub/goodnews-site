Dozens of dogs walked Germantown Avenue in costume for the fourth annual Halloween Pet Costume Parade in Chestnut Hill, Billy Penn reported. The procession is part of the neighborhood's Halloween on the Hill, put on by the Chestnut Hill Community Association. Mermaids, a Where's Waldo dog, and a dinosaur were among the outfits.

Neighbors have also been lining up to walk Cyrus, an adoptable rescue dog. The parade is the once-a-year version of a habit already on those sidewalks: people making time for the dogs, costumes included, and extra walks for a dog who is still waiting on a home.
