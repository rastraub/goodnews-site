University of Rhode Island fraternities and sororities opened their houses to local families, The Independent reported. The eighth Not So Spooky Halloween in South Kingstown offered games, haunted houses, and trick-or-treating.

The event is a campus tradition pointed at children from town. Eight is the year-count. The paper's account is that the houses were open for families, not a list of every room on campus.
