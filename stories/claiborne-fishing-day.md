Caris Healthcare and the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency set up a fishing day at Buchanan Place, a rehab center in Claiborne County, for Charles Clark, who is 92. They brought a stocked pond to him. He fished with his caretaker and his family and caught a couple.

The pond came to the center. Clark did not have to give up a day on the water to stay where he is being cared for.
