Around 4 p.m. on Thursday, October 29, Fresno firefighters helped a teenager and his cat out of a tree near E Street and Fresno Street, ABC30 reported. The Fresno Fire Department said the teen had climbed up to rescue the cat and, on the way down, realized he was stuck too.

Station 3 brought a ladder for Aiden and for Sebastian, the cat, who had been in the tree for several days. Aiden said his older sister texted that she had found Sebastian up there. He had been telling himself he would climb if nobody else could. He also said his mom and his sister had both warned him not to go up. His mom told the station she was happy that Aiden and Sebastian were back on the ground and safe.
