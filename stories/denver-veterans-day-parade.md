Hundreds of people gathered at City Park for Denver's 14th Veterans Day parade, The Denver Gazette reported. JROTC cadets from North, South, East, West, Abraham Lincoln, George Washington, and Thomas Jefferson high schools marched. A 5K and a 10K that morning raised money for the Colorado Veterans Project. The 14th is this year's count of the parade. The races were the fundraiser attached to the morning.
