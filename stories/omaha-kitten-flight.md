Muddy Paws volunteers spent a week catching a 10-week-old feral kitten named Serena, WOWT reported. She can use only about half of each paw. Rescue volunteer Jessi Corbin, a veterinary technician, said the kitten's legs point backward and she walks on her hocks. The condition is twisted leg syndrome. Corbin said the next step was a prompt look from specialists at the Kansas State College of Veterinary Medicine.

Volunteer pilots Bob and Vicki Dyer covered the time and the cost of the flight from Millard Airport, near Omaha, to Manhattan, Kansas, with Corbin and Serena aboard. The trip is about an hour and a half. Bob, an instrument-rated pilot, said it was his first cat and not his first Angel Flight. He has flown dogs and people. Vicki said they own the plane and want to put it to use. After landing, Corbin sent photos of Serena with the veterinary team. Once treatment is done, she will be ready for adoption.
