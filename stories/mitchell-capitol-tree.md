The U.S. Capitol Christmas Tree, called the People's Tree, stopped at the Corn Palace in Mitchell on its way to Washington, Mitchell Now reported. The tree comes from Idaho's Nez Perce-Clearwater National Forests. Residents signed its banner during the stop. The visit was one stop on a whistlestop tour. The tree is still bound for the Capitol.
