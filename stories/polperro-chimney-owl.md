A tawny owl that had gone down a chimney in Polperro flew off from the garden after a sweep lifted it out of the stove. The BBC reported on October 27, 2026, that Rob Stead, 36, was doing a routine clean at a house in the Cornish village last week when he saw the bird through the glass. The owner had been away for four weeks. Stead said he would do his best to free it.

He shone a torch in, wrapped the owl in a towel when it started flapping, and carried it outside. It did not leave at once. It flew to a corner of the garden and then took off. Stead, who works in south-east Cornwall and often finds nests in chimneys, said he is not an owl expert and used common sense. If the bird had looked worse for wear, he would have called a vet. This one seemed able to fly.

The story is one owl back outside. It does not say how the bird came down the flue.
