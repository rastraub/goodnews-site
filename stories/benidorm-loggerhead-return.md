Seven loggerhead sea turtles returned to the Mediterranean at 11:00 on Poniente Beach in Benidorm after a year and two months at Oceanogràfic Valencia. Cadena SER reported the release on October 22, 2026.

The turtles hatched in the early hours of August 7, 2025, on Levante Beach. Of 37 hatchlings, 30 were released hours later at Cala del Tío Ximo. Seven, picked at random, went to the aquarium. Mario Romero of Fundación Oceanogràfic said the point of that year of feeding and care is to send the turtles back larger and stronger.

About 150 primary pupils from Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, CEIP Ausiàs March, and CEIP El Murtal watched, along with staff from Serra Gelada natural park. Teacher Jaime Llorca brought 51 pupils ages 8 and 9. Councillor Mónica Gómez said Benidorm's beaches have been used for nesting in each of the last two years: a 2024 nest on Poniente, and the August 2025 hatch on Levante. Romero said more nests are being found in the Valencian Community, which may have to do with warmer temperatures shifting nesting north and with people reporting what they see.

Anyone who finds a nest is asked to call 112, not to touch the eggs or the animals, and to keep lights and noise down. The release is these seven turtles going back. It is not a census of the nesting beaches.
