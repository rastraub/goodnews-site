The Blue & You Foundation surprised Providence Park in Little Rock with $25,000. The village gives people leaving homelessness a permanent home, and it has medical, mental health, and dental care on the property. Residents talked about what having a house changed for them.

The park is planning 400 tiny homes. The gift is for the village that is already housing people, and for that larger plan.
