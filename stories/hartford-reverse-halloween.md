Staff at Connecticut Children's turned Halloween around, NBC Connecticut reported. About 70 employees dressed in costume and delivered treats to roughly 100 patient rooms in Hartford, a reverse trick-or-treat for children who were in the hospital.

The visits kept the holiday inside the building. The numbers are the hospital's count of staff in costume and rooms reached, not a description of any child's diagnosis.
