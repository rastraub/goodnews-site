The University of Otago has opened a seed library so people can grow food, not only borrow books. RNZ's Nights programme reported it on November 17, 2026. Students and members of the public can pick up seeds for vegetables and herbs at no charge. Sciences librarian Karri Hartley is behind the project, which the station said grew out of the cost of living. The segment's title calls it a library where you can grow what you borrow.

The broadcast did not publish a count of packets, a list of crops, or a rule about returning seed from the next harvest. Those details are not in the report, so they are not stated here. What the station did establish is the offer itself: free seed, for students and for anyone else who comes in, aimed at putting vegetables and herbs in reach.
