KRDO reported on November 6, 2026, that the Greater Pueblo Chamber of Commerce marked the end of Light Up Union, a project that put 30 custom light poles along three blocks of Historic Union Avenue. The poles carry more than 5,000 feet of overhead lighting and more than 2,000 bulbs.

The work took more than 10 months and cost about $200,000. The chamber said it was paid for with financial gifts, donated labor, and local contractors. A November 5 celebration included live music and local food and coffee vendors. The lights are a finished street project. The report does not claim a count of new businesses.
