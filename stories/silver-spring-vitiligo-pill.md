The Food and Drug Administration approved upadacitinib as the first daily pill for nonsegmental vitiligo. The decision rests on two phase 3 trials, Viti-Up-1 and Viti-Up-2, in 614 patients age 12 and older. At 48 weeks, about 1 in 4 people taking the medicine had regained at least 75 percent of the color on their face, compared with 6 to 7 percent of people on placebo. The results are also published in The Lancet. The approval is for that form of vitiligo, not every pigment condition. The medicine carries boxed safety warnings. The trials measured facial color over 48 weeks. They do not show that every patient regains color, and they are not a claim that the condition is cured.
