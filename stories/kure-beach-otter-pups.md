The North Carolina Aquarium at Fort Fisher has five new Asian small-clawed otter pups, WRAL reported. Four males and one female were born October 19 to Milli and Binx. The aquarium announced them on Thursday.

Otter keeper Kristen Upton said staff first picked out three separate chirps from the nest, then found five healthy pups. After the pups could be checked, each one was gaining weight. The count is five animals in the nest, not a promise about how soon visitors will see them.
