Trees Idaho volunteers delivered, and in some cases planted, more than 400 free trees for Boise residents this fall, Boise State Public Radio reported. Last year the group planted and distributed 200. The giveaway is part of the Elaine Clegg City of Trees Challenge, announced in 2020, which aims for 100,000 new trees before the end of the decade. This year's Boise trees were all claimed in less than 15 minutes. Executive director Emma Stammer said the city of Boise pays for that giveaway and requires the trees to be available to anyone. The trees are grown in western Oregon. Stammer said they are not native to Idaho, but they are chosen to thrive on city streets and to provide shade. The 100,000 figure is the challenge, not a count of trees already in the ground.
