Fortin Gage Flowers & Gifts brought Petal It Forward back to Main Street in Nashua. Each passerby received two free bouquets, one to keep and one to give to someone else. People who looked unsure at first ended up smiling.

This was the Nashua shop's giveaway. It is separate from other Petal It Forward events in other cities.
