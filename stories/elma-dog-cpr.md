East Grays Harbor Fire and Rescue was sent at about 9:20 a.m. Monday to a house fire in Elma, The Daily World reported. Crews found light smoke outside a one-story home. Inside, the fire had spread into a wall. They put it out. No residents and no firefighters were hurt.

During the fire, the two command chiefs on the call, listed as Chief 51 and Chief 52, carried two dogs out of the house. One dog was not breathing and needed CPR. Firefighters resuscitated that dog. Both dogs went back to their owners for veterinary care. The department thanked Grays Harbor 911, Elma police, Cascade Natural Gas, and Grays Harbor PUD for help on the call. The report does not name the dogs or the people who live in the house.
