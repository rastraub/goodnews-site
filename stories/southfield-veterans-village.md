Tunnel to Towers opened Southfield Veteran's Village on October 21, 2026, in a former Holiday Inn, FOX 2 Detroit reported. The building is housing for about 90 veterans who had been without a home. Frank Siller, of the foundation, said residents will have their own space, a community center downstairs, and services on the first floor. Those services include financial help, legal help, health care, and mental health care.

The foundation has villages in Tennessee, South Carolina, Texas, and Georgia, and said more are underway. Organizers cited a figure of about 40,000 veterans nationwide who are experiencing homelessness. Southfield adds rooms and on-site help for about 90 people. It does not, by itself, change that national count.
