After a trial run, Brookland Public Schools fully opened a drive-thru food pantry Tuesday, KAIT reported. It ran from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. at 115A S. School Street, across from the junior high. Superintendent George Kennedy said 22 families received food before the window closed. Any family with a child 18 or under in the district can use it.

Students and staff at the primary, elementary, and intermediate schools raised $7,000. The Food Bank of Northeast Arkansas, Hytrol, and Swept Away added support. Assistant superintendent Chris Moss said the pantry costs the district and families nothing to run, and that counselors used to send bags home when they could. The drive-thru lets parents pick up ingredients and cook. Moss said the need turned out to be larger than those occasional bags could cover.
