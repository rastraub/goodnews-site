More than 28 riders in the eighth annual Turkey Ride shopped for turkeys with Maine Sport Rockport and pedaled them to the Camden Area Christian Food Pantry, WABI reported. The delivery puts a turkey on the table for 58 families. More than 28 is the size of the riding group. Fifty-eight is the number of families. The eighth year means the ride has happened before.
