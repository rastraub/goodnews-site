The Boys and Girls Club on Grinnell in Peoria opened a Panda Cares Center of Hope this week, WMBD reported. A $45,000 Project Learn grant paid for the room, which is meant to get students ready for what comes next. It serves ages 6 to 18 with after-school tutoring and mentoring.

Members can use iPads, laptops loaded with Skyward and other school apps, a digital whiteboard, educational VR games, a 3D printer, and a reading corner. Program director Aaron Williams said the space was closed for six months while a classroom was rebuilt. The club won the project through a partnership grant, and Williams hopes a second room can follow at the club's Kansas site.
