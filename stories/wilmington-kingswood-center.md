Wilmington's Riverside neighborhood has a new Kingswood Community Center, Delaware Public Media reported. The building covers 81,000 square feet, about five times the old center, and it opens as Kingswood marks 80 years. Under one roof are a Montessori academy, Nemours pediatric care, a gym, and a community kitchen. The new space is that combination, not a single classroom or a single gym standing alone.
