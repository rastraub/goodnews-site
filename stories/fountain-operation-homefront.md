Operation Homefront's fifth Holiday Meals for Military event in Fountain gave 600 pre-registered Colorado Springs-area military families groceries and a $50 gift card, KOAA reported.

Six hundred is the number of families. The fifth year is this edition. The groceries and the gift card went out together to households that had signed up ahead of time.
