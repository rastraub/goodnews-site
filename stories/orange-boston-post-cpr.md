Officers Wnuk, Correia, and Esposito were sent Sunday night to the Puerto Vallarta parking lot on Boston Post Road in Orange, News 12 Connecticut reported. A car had hit a lamppost. A 51-year-old man was inside. He was not breathing and had no pulse.

The officers broke a window to reach him. They started CPR and rescue breathing and used a defibrillator. AMR paramedic Stephanie Miller and EMTs Tom Curwen and Mike Montanaro Jr. then joined them.

Police said the driver had a heart attack, and that the combined work of the officers and the ambulance crew saved his life. The station did not name him. The account is the medical save in the parking lot, reported on Nov. 25.
