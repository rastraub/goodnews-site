The Star reported on November 17, 2026, that a bull elephant nicknamed Yob Tonggang was walked out of Kampung Orang Asli Sungai Tonggang, near Ipoh, with two older females as guides. Perak wildlife director Dr. Fazil Abdul Patah said the 10-year-old, about two tonnes, had been caught on November 9. At 10 a.m. on Tuesday, Sanom, 22, and Abot, 30, from the National Elephant Conservation Centre in Kuala Gandah, Pahang, led him about 2 kilometres to a relocation point. The walk took about two hours. Mohammad Abu Huzaifah Abu Samah of the centre led a team of 40. Fazil said the bull is in good health and will be moved to Terengganu National Park.

Resident Zulkefli Ahmad, 65, told The Star the elephant had fed on jackfruit, banana, and coconut and had not damaged permanent structures. He said another wild elephant, believed to be Yob Tonggang's mate, was still in the area. The news is this guided walk and the plan to move him to the park. The second elephant had not been moved.
