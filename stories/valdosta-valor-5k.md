Valdosta Fire Department's 9/11 5K raised more than $13,000 for Valor Service Dogs, WCTV reported. The department directed the race donations to the nonprofit, which places trained dogs with veterans and first responders.

The total is what this race brought in. It is separate from other Valdosta animal stories, including student visits to the local humane society, and it is not the organization's full yearly budget.
