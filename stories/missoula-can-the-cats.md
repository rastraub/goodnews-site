Griz fans opened this year's Can the Cats food drive at Saturday's game in Missoula, KPAX reported. The friendly rivalry with Montana State collects food and money for Montana food banks heading into the holidays. The goal is 600,000 pounds or dollars. That figure is the target for the drive, not a total already raised. The kickoff at the game is the start of the collection.
