Colorado expects the Colorado State Forest Service nursery in Fort Collins to turn out 1 million to 2 million container seedlings a year, The NoCo Herald reported. The nursery, on Colorado State University's Foothills Campus, has been renovated with more than $15 million in state and federal money.

The work adds greenhouses and shade houses, better irrigation, and a new headhouse meant to mechanize seeding. The state said the extra plants are for restoration after wildfire, for watershed protection, and for habitat. The figure is a production target for the rebuilt nursery, not a count of trees already in the ground.
