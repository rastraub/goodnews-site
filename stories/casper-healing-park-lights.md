Donations brought back Casper's Healing Park light festival after the city cut it, Oil City News reported. Residents raised nearly $29,000. Local DJ E'Lanae Medford is running the free festival again on Nov. 20. Forty-five trees will be lit, and the lights stay on through Jan. 3.

The city cut is why neighbors had to raise the money. Nearly $29,000 is the amount. Nov. 20 is the free festival night. Jan. 3 is how long the lights stay on. Forty-five is the number of lit trees.
