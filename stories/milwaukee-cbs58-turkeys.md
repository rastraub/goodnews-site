CBS 58's 12-hour drive-thru food drive for Hunger Task Force collected a record 728 turkeys and more than 25,000 pounds of food, the station reported. The event was in Milwaukee.

Seven hundred twenty-eight is the turkey total, the largest the drive has posted. More than 25,000 pounds is the broader food weight. Twelve hours is how long donors could pull through.
