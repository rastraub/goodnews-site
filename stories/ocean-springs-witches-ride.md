The Witches Ride of Ocean Springs began in 2018 with about 30 people on bicycles. It has since raised more than $114,000 for local animal welfare groups, the Biloxi Sun Herald reported. The ninth ride returns downtown at 6 p.m. on October 24. Spectators watch for free while costumed participants hand out treats. Registration for riders 18 and older is $60 a person or $100 a golf cart.

The nonprofit, led by president Diana Odom, also raises money through a Halloween pub crawl, a raffle, and a pet parade. Riders sometimes spend months on costumes and on decorating bikes and carts. The Mississippi Main Street Association named it the 2026 outstanding holiday event. Odom said this year's funds are meant to ease costs so the groups can keep their attention on the animals.
