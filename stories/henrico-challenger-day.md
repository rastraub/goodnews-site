Henrico County Public Schools held Challenger Day for kindergarten through fifth grade students with physical and cognitive disabilities, the Henrico Citizen reported, with reporting from WTVR. The day teaches the basics of baseball in a way that invites those students in. Forrest Hunley, who specializes in adapted physical education, is among the teachers. Volunteers and partnering businesses help. Jimmy Bonnell, watching his son Grayson, called the scene pure joy. The account is about that day of play. It does not report a score or a season record.
