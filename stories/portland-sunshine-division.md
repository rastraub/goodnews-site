About 400 volunteer drivers delivered Thanksgiving meals to 2,000 households for Portland's Sunshine Division. The meals went to the doorsteps of seniors and neighbors who cannot get out. Teen volunteers loaded the cars at Portland International Raceway. The drivers are the last step. The teens filling trunks are the reason a car can leave the raceway with a route of meals already in it.
