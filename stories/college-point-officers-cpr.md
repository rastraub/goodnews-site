Mustafa Celebioglu, a 51-year-old city worker and father, went into cardiac arrest while driving near the NYPD Police Academy in College Point, Queens, about three weeks before a reunion on Wednesday, ABC7 New York reported. His silver SUV crashed near the Whitestone Expressway and was blocking traffic. He was unresponsive behind the wheel.

Officers who were leaving the academy and others passing through stopped. Detective Christian Campoverde said he saw a line of traffic and a commotion at the intersection as he pulled out of the academy lot. Sgt. Shaynell Parris-Mitchell said the officers fell into place one after another. They performed CPR for about 20 minutes, then decided not to wait for an ambulance. Campoverde said they took the seats out of his car and drove Celebioglu themselves. Nearly a dozen officers from different units took part.

Celebioglu met them on Wednesday. He said he does not remember the incident, and that he understood how serious it was only after he watched video. He said he is grateful to be alive and that he has no damage to his brain or his body. Parris-Mitchell said his wife thanked the officers for saving her husband.
