Skyridge High School's student council hosted its annual Veterans Day dinner on Nov. 11, the Lehi Free Press reported. Veterans and their guests walked a red carpet into a room decorated in red, white, and blue, then sat down to a catered meal the students served. Student council members sat with the veterans to hear their stories. The dinner is free. Students invite guests with flyers, visits to retirement communities, and knocks on doors. This year the program also included a fallen-soldier table for service members who were killed, missing, or imprisoned. Principal John Wallwork said the point of the night is those conversations, not a meal that people eat and leave.
