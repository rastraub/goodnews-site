Colby Weatherford, 10, of Friendswood, Texas, got his first Clemson home game after five months in the hospital for acute myeloid leukemia, The State reported. He was diagnosed in January, finished treatment around June 26, and was declared cancer-free over the summer. His father said doctors feel very optimistic about the long term. That is a medical outlook, not a guarantee, and it is not a statement that every child with leukemia has the same path. On Oct. 24, Clemson athletics brought Colby, his father Chris, and his brother Connor onto the sideline at Memorial Stadium. Colby gave defensive tackle Hevin Brown-Shuler, who is also being treated for cancer, one of his orange and white bracelets, and Brown-Shuler wore it on the field.
