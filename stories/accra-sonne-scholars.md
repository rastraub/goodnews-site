Graphic Online reported on November 13, 2026, that Drive to Inspire-Africa brought 20 new scholars into its programme at a ceremony in Accra on November 12, with Sonne Scholarships from Cerveau Initiative. The programme now counts 80 scholars. The new group is spread across 10 tertiary institutions, in courses that include registered nursing, conservation biology and entomology, medicine, and music education. Selection was competitive and weighed academic results and financial need. Fourteen scholars, new and continuing, also received laptops.

Founder Nana Adjoa Hackman said the point is not a larger headcount, and that some existing scholars are already taking leadership roles at school. Sonia Mendy, operations director for Sonne Scholarships, urged the students to use the opening. The organisations said fees, materials, and computers are the usual barriers, so the laptop is part of the award. The programme also runs mentoring, webinars, leadership sessions, and internships. Kate Wienu, an entrepreneurship and international business student at the University of Cape Coast, said the support gave her a reason to keep going. The news is this cohort of 20 and the 14 laptops. It is not a count of every student in Ghana who still cannot pay.
