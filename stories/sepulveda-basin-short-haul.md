A woman stranded in the bed of the Los Angeles River was lifted to dry ground on Thursday morning, MyNewsLA reported. Los Angeles Fire Department crews were sent about 8:20 a.m. to the 6300 block of North Balboa Boulevard, near the Lake Balboa Hiking Trail, in the Sepulveda Basin beside the Encino Golf Course. Heavy rain and flooding were already moving across the county.

Spokeswoman Lyndsey Lantz said an air crew completed a short-haul and set the woman down in a dry spot. An ambulance then took her to a hospital. Lantz said she was the only person trapped. The helicopter crew kept looking from above and did not see anyone else in the riverbed. The report did not say how she became stranded.
