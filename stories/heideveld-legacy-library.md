Heideveld Primary celebrated the opening of the Rosdien Desai Legacy Library on Thursday, October 22. Athlone News reported the event the next day. The library is named for the school's retired principal.

Principal Colleen Bouah said library periods will rotate with the computer lab. Classes of about 40 will be split into groups of about 20 so pupils can actually use the room. A partner company appointed a library assistant and trained that person for the work. Shelley de Wet of The Bookery, which helps schools build libraries, called the room a tribute to 40 years and more than a collection of books.

The school is in its sixth year. The opening is the start of those library periods. It is not a reading-score result.
