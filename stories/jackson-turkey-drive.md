Turkey Drive 16, the 15th annual WAPT and Mississippi Food Network drive, collected more than 1,100 turkeys and nearly $48,000, WAPT reported. The gifts are for families facing food insecurity.

More than 1,100 is the turkey count. Nearly $48,000 is the money. The 15th year is how long the Jackson drive has been running. The turkeys and the cash are both part of what came in.
