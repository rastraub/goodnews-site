Cherokee County emergency managers took a call around 6:30 p.m. on Nov. 24, WEIS reported. An adult man had left the Flag Pole Mountain trailhead, off County Road 8 north of Indian Mountain ATV Park, early that morning and had not returned.

Spring Garden firefighters and Polk County crews began searching where his family last knew him to be. Polk County 911 then told Cherokee County 911 that the man had called for help and that dispatchers had his location, a few hundred yards inside Cherokee County, on top of Flag Pole Mountain near the Pinhoti Trail. He said he was exhausted and could not make it out on his own.

Cherokee County's emergency management agency sent a drone, which gave the ground teams a view of where he was. Crews hiked several miles of steep terrain after dark. Polk County personnel reached him first. Spring Garden firefighters helped walk him down to a point an ATV could reach, and responders took him to a staging area where Piedmont Rescue was waiting. All of the crews were back in service shortly after 11 p.m.

The agency credited the work across the Alabama-Georgia line, and the location from the 911 call, with bringing him out. The report does not give his name.
