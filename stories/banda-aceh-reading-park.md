Banda Aceh has a pilot outdoor library in Bustanussalatin Park, The Korea Times reported on November 24, 2026, from an announcement by the Seoul Metropolitan Government. The branch, about 1,500 square metres, opened on Saturday. It follows an 1,800-square-metre library that opened on October 31 at Cuscatlan Park in San Salvador. Both sites are set to run weekend programs for eight weeks, through the end of the year.

Seoul's own outdoor libraries began in 2022, turning squares and a stream into places to read, and the city says they have drawn more than 10 million visits. For the two pilots, Seoul provided mobile shelves, beanbag seats, and books, and adjusted the hours and layout for local weather and families. About 100 people attended the San Salvador opening, and about 800 residents came over the first two days, including bookmark workshops and storytelling. In Banda Aceh, Mayor Illiza Sa'aduddin Djamal and about 400 residents attended a two-day pop-up that began on November 7, with Korean books and a storytelling performance in hanbok.

The paper said the article was prepared with generative AI and edited by its staff. The counts above are the ones the city gave the paper. They describe these opening weeks. They do not say the parks will keep the libraries after the pilot ends.
