Raices Sagradas Community Farm opened October 24 on 9,000 square feet in Albuquerque's South Valley, KUNM reported. The address is 1025 Citation Drive SW. The land had been an illegal dumping ground. Agri-Cultura Network, a collective of small farmers and ranchers, planned the site for food and for teaching.

Two large greenhouses sit on an off-grid solar system. The design includes pollinator habitat and a seed program that sorts, keeps, and shares seeds of drought-resistant native plants. The farm will host workshops for growers, elders, and local youth, and it will stay open as a classroom as well as a place that produces food.
