Law students and the Lions Club cooked 500 free Thanksgiving meals in South Royalton. The dinner has been a tradition since 1989. About 250 of the meals are delivered to homes, and the event has been growing about 20 to 25 percent a year. Half the work is the kitchen. The other half is the road, getting a plate to someone who is not coming to the hall. The growth is a bigger kitchen and a longer delivery list, every year since the late 1980s.
