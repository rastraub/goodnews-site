Seventy-one tourism students from the University of Technology and Applied Sciences in Ibra visited Al Saleel National Park in Al Kamil on November 8. The university posted the account on November 11, 2026. The Department of Economics and Business Administration organized the trip with Oman's Environment Authority, and two faculty members went along. Environment Authority staff gave a presentation on the park's ecology, its biodiversity, wildlife protection, and the work of keeping habitat intact.

The students saw Arabian oryx, which the university called one of Oman's most iconic endangered species. The session covered the animal's habitat, its characteristics, its conservation status, and the work done to protect it. The department thanked the Environment Authority for the talk and for arranging the visit. The page is one class trip and what the students were shown. It does not give a count of oryx in the park, and it does not say that the visit changed the animals' prospects.
