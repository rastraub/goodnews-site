Three residents of the retirement community Beò of Chattanooga took free open-cockpit rides on October 1 through Dream Flights, NewsChannel 9 reported. The nonprofit flies veterans and spouses for about 15 minutes, roughly 1,000 feet up, in a biplane restored to World War II specifications.

Joe DePrimo, a 79-year-old Air Force veteran, had a stroke a couple of years ago that left him paralyzed on his left side. He had not expected to get into an airplane. Pilots helped him aboard, and he buckled in. Two other residents flew the same day, including 79-year-old Gary Hedges, who spent 20 years in the Navy, the Army, and the Air Force, and 82-year-old Charles Smith.
