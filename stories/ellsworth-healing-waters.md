Project Healing Waters, sponsored by Down East Trout Unlimited, teaches veterans around Ellsworth to fly fish, tie flies, and build rods, and it does not charge them. One veteran had not left home in nine years. He now asks to come back. The lessons are knots, feathers, and a rod. The result WABI reported is a person who had stayed inside wanting another day with the group. The water is the classroom. The fee is nothing.
