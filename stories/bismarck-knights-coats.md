The Knights of Columbus gave new coats, tags still on, to more than 175 children in a few hours at Coats for Kids in Bismarck, KFYR reported. The community had donated more than 300 coats for the drive. The more than 175 counts the children who left with a coat during that short giveaway. The more than 300 counts the coats neighbors contributed. The coats handed out were new, not pulled from a used-clothing rack.
