Maeve McVeigh, 91, made a hole-in-one this month at Dungannon Golf Club and told the BBC she feels closer to 50 when she is playing. Niall McCracken's report for BBC News NI, published November 21, 2026, says she is the club's oldest playing member. The shot came in the ladies' winter league, on the sixth hole, a par three of 127 yards, about 116 metres. She said she hit the ball as she normally would, knew it had reached the green, and only found it when a friend asked whether she had checked the hole.

It is her second ace. The first was about 15 years ago, on the 18th at Fintona. She took up the game at 68. Her grandson Jack Kane, a PGA professional, said he started following her around the course when he was about five. Jane Henry, the lady captain, said a women's programme begun about a decade ago has brought in members, and that about 50 women play most Tuesdays, nine holes or eighteen. Club president Noel Gormley called her the life of the party off the course as well. McVeigh is pausing for winter and plans to be back in the spring. She told the BBC she hopes other retired women will pick up a club for the walk, the company, and the meal afterward.
