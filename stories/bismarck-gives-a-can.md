High school students across Bismarck and Mandan have started Bis-Man Gives a Can, a food drive for the Great Plains Food Bank, Prairie Public reported. Sophomore Teddy Andrist of Bismarck Century High School said he borrowed the idea from Fill the Dome, a Fargo drive that has been collecting food and money for neighbors for 20 years. Representatives from the area high schools are running this one together.

The goal is 2,000 food items from each school. St. Mary's student Olivia Lundeen said the drive is aimed at families who are not sure where the next meal is coming from. Food was being accepted at the Bismarck Event Center through Tuesday.
