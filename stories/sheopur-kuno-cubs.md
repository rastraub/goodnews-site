A young female cheetah brought from Botswana gave birth to four cubs in the wild at Kuno National Park on November 20, 2026. The Hindu reported the birth the same day, from an announcement by India's environment minister, Bhupender Yadav. The cheetah, known as KOMG 3, is about two and a half years old. Yadav said she is the first cheetah from Botswana to give birth in India, and that she had spent more than four months in the wild before the cubs arrived under natural conditions.

The paper said this is the second recorded birth in the wild since the reintroduction programme began in 2022. With the four cubs, the project's figure for cheetahs in India is 62, including 43 born in the country. Madhya Pradesh's chief minister, Mohan Yadav, credited project managers, veterinarians, and forest staff. Those totals are the programme's own count. They are not a new independent survey of every cheetah on the subcontinent.
