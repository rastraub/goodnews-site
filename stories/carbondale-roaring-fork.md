Roaring Fork Outdoor Volunteers logged a record 16,203 hours caring for public lands in the Roaring Fork Valley, The Aspen Times reported. In the 2026 season, 1,492 volunteers maintained 31 miles of trail and restored 24 acres.

The hours are the record. The miles and acres are what those hours covered. The group is based in Carbondale.
