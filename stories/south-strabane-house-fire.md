Five fire and EMS agencies were honored for pulling a South Strabane Township resident from a house fire, the Observer-Reporter reported. More than a dozen first responders received merit citations. They got the resident out of the Oct. 28 fire in under 10 minutes. The resident went home from the hospital on Nov. 4.

The citations are the honor. Under 10 minutes is how long the rescue took. Nov. 4 is the day the resident left the hospital. Five agencies and more than a dozen responders are who shared the citations.
