The Gulf Coast Veterans Parade moved through D'Iberville, WLOX reported. Veterans, service members, and families lined the route. The parade is annual, and it rotates among Biloxi, Gulfport, and D'Iberville, so the host city changes. This year the streets were in D'Iberville. The gathering was that lineup of people who served and the families who came out with them.
