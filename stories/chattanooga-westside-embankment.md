Chattanooga firefighters were dispatched around 1:16 a.m. Thursday to the 2500 block of Westside Drive for a crash in which a car may have gone down an embankment, Local 3 News reported. They found the vehicle about 50 feet below the road.

Engine 9 called Squad 1 and Squad 13 to help reach the person. Crews used a rope system and a Stokes basket, a stretcher made for steep ground. They secured the person in the basket, hauled them back up the embankment, and turned them over to EMS.

The department has not said how the person was doing or what sent the car off the road. The reported fact is the rescue itself, on Thanksgiving morning.
