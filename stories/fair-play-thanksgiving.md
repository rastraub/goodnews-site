Youth Empowerment Program students in Fair Play, Missouri, turned a $500 grant into Thanksgiving dinners for 10 families, KY3 reported. The Community Foundation of the Ozarks provided the grant. The students shopped full holiday meals for neighbors and hope to grow the effort.

Five hundred dollars is what they had to spend. Ten is the number of families who received a meal. The hope to grow it is what the students said they want next.
