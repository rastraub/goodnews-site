Kauai High School student Angela Querubin earned an honorable mention in NPR's Student Podcast Challenge, Hawai'i Public Radio reported. Her podcast is about harana, the Filipino practice of courtship through song.

The station spoke with Querubin and her teacher, Jonathon Medeiros, on The Conversation. The mention is a national nod for a student project made on Kauai, about a tradition carried in music.
