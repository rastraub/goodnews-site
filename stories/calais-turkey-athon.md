Washington County's 22nd annual Turkey-A-Thon raised $33,884.78 for local food pantries, the Bangor Daily News reported, with more donations still coming in. The total was short of a $40,000 goal. Organizers said the result still mattered in a tight economy.

On October 9, volunteers worked seven sites across the county. All of the money goes to community food groups. Classic Hits 92.7/95.3 FM broadcast live from the Dunkin' studio at V.L. Tammaro in Calais and turned the site totals into a friendly contest as the numbers came in.
