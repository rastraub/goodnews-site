Sarasota High football players handed out 125 Thanksgiving dinners to seniors, FOX 13 reported. The Sailors carried turkeys to cars and gave out complete meal kits at Senior Friendship Centers. The Salah Foundation funded the meals. Urban Edge and New College took part.

One hundred twenty-five is the number of dinners. The kits were complete meals, not only the turkey. The players did the carrying. The seniors were the ones who drove up to receive them.
