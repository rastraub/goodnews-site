Around 9 a.m. on Monday, November 2, Albuquerque Fire Rescue was sent to a house fire in the 9000 block of Zuni, near Central and Wyoming, the Albuquerque Journal reported. When firefighters arrived, they found three dogs in the home and brought all three out.

The department said nobody was hurt, including the dogs. Three people who live there were displaced, and the Red Cross is helping them. Fire investigators said the blaze was accidental and started because a candle was left burning.
