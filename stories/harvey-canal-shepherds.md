Around 4 p.m. Sunday, Harvey Volunteer Fire Company Two was called to a drainage canal where two German shepherds were stuck, FOX 8 reported. The canal was too deep for the dogs to climb out on their own. Two firefighters lowered ladders about 12 feet, calmed the dogs, and carried both of them back up.

The deputy fire chief told the station that both dogs were taken to the local shelter and that their owner has been located. Firefighters do not know how the dogs got into the canal. They said the lack of rain made the rescue easier.
