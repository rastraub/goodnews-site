Mike Castelli, 25, hand-delivered invitations to a Halloween display he calls the Pumpkin Spectacular, in the Hope village of Scituate. Nearly everyone on the street came. Neighbors who had not met before stood in the same yard, and people donated to his Special Olympics team.

The display was his. The gathering was the street deciding to walk over.
