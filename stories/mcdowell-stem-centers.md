Bradshaw Elementary, Iaeger Elementary, Coalfield Elementary, and Southside K-8 now have STEM centers, the first of their kind in West Virginia from the Cal Ripken, Sr. Foundation and TC Energy. WVVA reported on Oct. 12 that the rooms come with 3D printers, robots, and circuit kits.

Assistant superintendent Amanda Peyton told the station that students are already using the equipment, including small robots and circuit work, in a county where museums and science exhibits are scarce. The foundation is also looking at a robotics program for the county's secondary schools. The centers are open. They are a start, not a finished pipeline to a job.
