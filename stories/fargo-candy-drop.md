For the fifth year, students at Lewis and Clark Elementary in Fargo packed candy bags with handwritten notes, and South High honors students spent a day off delivering them, Valley News Live reported. The bags went to veterans, firefighters, police, and EMTs. The elementary students did the packing. The high school students made the deliveries on their day off. Five years is how long the candy drop has been a tradition.
