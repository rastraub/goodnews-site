Volunteers packed 500 Thanksgiving boxes in 90 minutes at Camp Courant in Farmington, WFSB reported. The KBE Foundation's 18th Gift of Gobble filled the boxes with turkeys, sides, and pies for families, with more than a dozen local groups helping.

Five hundred is the box count. Ninety minutes is the packing time. The 18th year is this edition of Gift of Gobble.
