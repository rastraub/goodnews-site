ARTE MUSEUM NAGOYA was due to open on November 18 in Chita, Aichi, Nikkei reported on November 17, 2026. It is the first of the Korean immersive-art museums in Japan, and the ninth site in a group that has also opened in cities such as New York. The paper said those museums have drawn 13 million visits in all. This one sits a three-minute walk from Shin-Maiko station on the Meitetsu Tokoname line, on the coast between Nagoya and Chubu Centrair airport. The operators, including Seoul-based d'strict, Meitetsu City Development, and the Chunichi Shimbun, said they hope the location will draw neighbors and travelers. Their target is 700,000 visitors a year. That figure is a goal, not an opening-day count.

The theme is nature that lasts. Eighteen works use video, sound, and scent. Six are new. Visitors can color dogs, foxes, and cats and watch those drawings move on screen. A work made only for Aichi, titled GARDEN, "Legacy of Light, Nagoya," brings in Nagoya Castle, Atsuta Shrine, the loom tied to the origins of the Toyota group, an orca from the port aquarium, and the Nanachan doll outside Nagoya station. Beckoning cats, a local specialty, are in the show as well. The news is this opening and this list of works. It does not say how many people came through the door on the first day.
