Longleat has a hippo calf for the first time in more than forty years. The Wiltshire safari park said on November 26, 2026, that the newborn, whose sex is not yet known, arrived as part of the European breeding programme and is the first hippo born in the UK since May 2024. The mother is Matylda. She and her sister Manon came from the Czech Republic in the summer of 2025. The father is Hodor. He arrived from Whipsnade Zoo with his mother, Lola, who is now a grandmother. The birth itself was about a week before the announcement.

Darren Beasley, head of animal operations, said keepers noticed Matylda putting distance between herself and the others, which hippos do when a birth is close, and separated her. The birth took a few hours. He said she is a new mother and is doing well, and that the first weeks are the critical ones, so the staff will watch the pair closely. Guests may see the calf following her outside the house. The other hippos are still using the paddocks and Half Mile Pond. The park is in the year it marks sixty years since it opened what it calls the first drive-through safari outside Africa. The news is a calf on the ground with its mother. It is not a count of wild hippos, and the sex is still unknown.
