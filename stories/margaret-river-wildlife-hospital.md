The Margaret River Wildlife Hospital began taking patients on Friday, with all nine places already booked. ABC News included the opening in its South West briefing on November 13, 2026. The first patients include a ringtail possum, kangaroo joeys, and several birds of prey. The public is being asked to call ahead before bringing an injured animal, so staff can explain how to handle it safely.

WA Wildlife chief executive Dean Huxley said the hospital's reach runs across the South West, with patients expected from Margaret River, Augusta, and possibly closer to Busselton, and from Busselton to Nannup and Augusta. The news in the briefing is the start of admissions and a full book of nine places on day one. It does not say how those animals are doing, or how many the hospital will be able to treat in a month.
