Fulton Hogan road workers pulled six to eight ducklings out of a mudtank in Alexandra on Wednesday morning. RNZ reported the rescue on October 21, 2026.

Cyclist Barbara Hall saw the birds from the rail trail that runs from Clyde. The mother duck was distressed. One worker held another's legs so he could reach farther down the drain. After the ducklings were out, the mother and about a dozen ducklings crossed the road while people watched for cars.

The account is this one brood and the people who stopped. It is not a survey of ducks on the trail.
