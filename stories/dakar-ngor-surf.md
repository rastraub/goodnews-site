On the beach at Ngor, a Lebou fishing village on Dakar's northern coast, local volunteers are teaching neighborhood children to stand up on surfboards. Africanews reported on November 9, 2026, that Anda Surf runs paid lessons and also keeps Sunday sessions free for children who live nearby. The report said boards are often shared, and that many of the young surfers still lack the clothing they would need to compete away from home. Equipment and travel costs remain the hard part, even as surfing spreads along Senegal's coast.

The free lessons sit beside a larger sporting moment. Senegal is hosting the Youth Olympic Games, described as the first Olympic sports event on African soil. Surfing is one of ten sports offered there for young people to take part in, without medals attached. The news is the Sunday sessions in Ngor and the fact that the village school is open to children who cannot pay. It does not say how many children have learned to stand, or that any of them are on a path to a Games team.
