Alex Anning, a 9-year-old from Tinley Park, turned his birthday into a food giveaway after seeing a man who did not have food, NBC Chicago reported. Working with the Montessori School of Englewood and other partners, the drive produced 500 bags.

The giveaway was in Englewood. Five hundred bags is the count for this birthday project, and the school and partners are what turned one student's idea into a distribution that size.
