Project Angel Heart's chefs are cooking Thanksgiving meals for people who are too sick to cook for themselves. The clients live with cancer, heart failure, kidney disease, and other illnesses, and the food is medically tailored to what they can eat. On Gobble Gobble Give day, those holiday meals go out across Southern Colorado with Care and Share Food Bank. A meal that matches a restricted diet, and that arrives at the door, is the whole point of the day.
