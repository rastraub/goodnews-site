Erquy's municipal council voted to make the Blé-en-Herbe library free for everyone, residents and visitors. Ouest-France reported the decision on October 20, 2026.

Culture deputy Maryvonne Chalvet proposed the change so access would be universal and the library simpler to run. Income from fees had been about 3 percent of the library budget. Printing and photocopying are also free, up to five pages a day for each user. Associations are not included in that printing rule. The council gave up a 9 percent discount on its book purchases so the money supports independent bookshops. Books withdrawn from the shelves can be resold cheaply rather than pulped, except copies that cannot be saved.
