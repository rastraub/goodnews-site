The Mill, a coffee shop in Bonduel, closed Tuesday so the staff could volunteer at Happily Ever After in Green Bay, WBAY reported. Workers learned how the sanctuary helps local animals and spent the day socializing cats and dogs waiting for homes. Many of the employees are high school students who were excused from class to go.

Owners Keith and Nicole Fischer said the shop is pet-friendly and that, without meaning to make it a rule, they keep hiring people who love animals. The Mill has worked with Happily Ever After for several years, including selling the group's shirts in the store.
