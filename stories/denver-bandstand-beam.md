Hundreds of Denver residents wrote their names and memories on a beam that will go into the City Park bandstand, 9NEWS reported. The bandstand is being rebuilt after a fire, and this beam is one of the pieces that will go into that rebuild. The effort has raised $250,000, including $90,000 from 9NEWS' Word of Thanks campaign. The signing is a way for neighbors to leave their names in the new structure. The money figure is what has been raised so far for the rebuilding, and the beam is still on its way into the building.
