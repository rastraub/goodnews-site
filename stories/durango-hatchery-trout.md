Colorado Parks and Wildlife staff at the Durango State Fish Hatchery sandbagged and pumped water to protect about 330,000 fish during historic flooding, The Denver Gazette reported. After the flood, the crew stocked the Animas River with 2,312 Gunnison River rainbow trout and 617 Colorado River cutthroat trout.

The stocking is meant to help rebuild the river's fishery. The hatchery is in Durango. The rainbow trout came from Gunnison River stock, and the other fish were Colorado River cutthroat. The sandbags and pumps were how the crew kept the hatchery fish protected while the water was high.
