Salisbury firefighters answered a crash on the afternoon of November 19 after a vehicle entered a pond along Tilghman Road, Delmarva Now reported. Downed power lines touched a nearby tree and started a small fire, which made the scene more hazardous for the crews.

Department divers went into the water. They reached the submerged vehicle and brought the driver and a small dog out through the windshield. Tilghman Road was closed while crews secured the scene and dealt with the lines and the tree.

The photo gallery does not name the driver or the dog, and it does not describe how either of them was afterward. What it does report is the rescue: firefighters in the water, and a person and a dog brought out of a vehicle that had gone under.
