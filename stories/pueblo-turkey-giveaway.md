Pueblo's turkey giveaway handed out 600 turkeys plus sides, and the cars waiting for them stretched about a mile. It began with one man offering a spare turkey on Facebook. Bikers, outreach groups, and volunteers now run it as a tradition outside Outpost Harley-Davidson. Last year the count was 500. The extra hundred birds are the growth. The line of cars is the proof that the Facebook offer did not stay a one-time favor.
