Seventy people made drums at a Cheyenne and Arapaho workshop in Oklahoma City. The gatherings have turned out about 500 drums in recent years. The songs travel with the drums, and the same workshops are also a place where people in recovery keep showing up for one another.

The count is drums finished and people in the room. The songs are what those drums are for.
