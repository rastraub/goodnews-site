Students at South Park Middle School rallied relatives to vote for science teacher Kimberly Loeper, WBAY reported. She won Bubbl'r National Teacher of the Year, an award that brings $10,000 to the school.

The school marked the win with an assembly. The campaign was the students' phone calls and asks, and the $10,000 is school money attached to the title, not a figure the class raised at a bake sale.
