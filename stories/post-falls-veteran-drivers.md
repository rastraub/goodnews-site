Nine veterans from nearly every branch of the military are driving school buses for the Post Falls School District, the Coeur d'Alene Press reported. They describe the work as community service and are inviting other veterans to take a route. The nine are the veterans now behind the wheel. The invitation is open to others who have served and want to do this next.
