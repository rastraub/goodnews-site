Louisville Mega Cavern gave Wednesday's Child free passes so 150 foster children could ride through its holiday lights, WLKY reported. The display has 7 million lights. This is the first year of that partnership.

The passes were free. One hundred fifty is the number of children on the ride. Wednesday's Child brought them. Lights Under Louisville is the name of the ride through the cavern's lights.
