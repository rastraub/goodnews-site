Zachary Ball Sr. led a fifth annual delivery to four Meridian nursing homes, WTOK reported. Donations collected since August, including warm items, went out with the visits. Volunteers also prayed and sang with residents.

Five years is the run of the drive, and four is the number of homes on this round. The collection started in August so the holiday visits would have something to carry in the door.
