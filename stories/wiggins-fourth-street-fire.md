Firefighters in Wiggins got two people out of a house fire on North 4th Street early Monday, WLOX reported. The call came in about 6:25 a.m. When crews arrived, heavy smoke was coming from the home.

They brought out one person who has a disability and helped a second person reach safety. They also carried several cats out of the house. WLOX said the home had working smoke detectors. The station reported that a cause had not been determined. It did not name the people who got out.
