Late Friday, Euclid police and firefighters were called to an apartment building on East 193rd Street, FOX 8 reported. Officers found heavy smoke, and some older residents could not get out on their own. One of them, a 91-year-old woman, could not get out of her bed.

Officers Joseph DiFranco and Jonathan Hankins wrapped her in a bed sheet and carried her down the stairs. Officers Joshua Gebler, Brant Bencina, and Phillip Victory worked with the firefighters to get the other residents outside. Police Chief Scott Meyer said the officers treated those residents as they would their own grandparents.

Fire officials said one person went to the hospital by ambulance. They said the cause of the fire was still under investigation. FOX 8 did not name the woman who was carried out, and it did not say whether she was the person taken to the hospital.
