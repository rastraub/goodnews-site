Sir Karl Jenkins has given his musical collection to the National Library of Wales. Nation.Cymru reported that he presented it at the library on November 10, 2026. The gift includes performance scores with his notes, photographs from his career, concert programmes, and promotional material. Staff will catalogue it, and the library expects the public to be able to see it early in 2027.

Chief executive Rhodri Llwyd Morgan said the collection keeps the record of a widely performed living composer with the national memory of Wales, alongside other Welsh composers already in the archives. Jenkins said his roots are in Wales and that the library was the right home. The paper noted Adiemus, from 1995, and The Armed Man: A Mass for Peace, from 2000, which it said has been performed more than 3,000 times since its premiere. It also said his recordings have earned 17 gold and platinum disc awards. Nia Mai Daniel, head of unique collections, said the archive will stay available to researchers. The news is the gift and the plan to open it after cataloguing. The scores are not on the public shelves yet.
