The first Mayors Taskforce for Jobs awards, given in Rotorua on October 14 and 15, honored young people who found work and the neighbors who helped them stay in it. Scoop published the Local Government New Zealand announcement on October 27, 2026.

The taskforce is a network of mayors, backed by the Ministry of Social Development, whose local teams match 16- to 24-year-olds with employers. Chair Ben Bell, mayor of Gore, said a placement sometimes starts with an employer opening a door, or with someone asking what a young person actually wants to do.

Whanganui won Most Unexpected Placement after a short orchard contract for Dillon White, Coby Sperling, Isaac Burns, and Jesse Harrington was extended four times. Jono Craig, of Central Hawke's Bay, was named Local Champion for a grassroots approach that employers described to the judges. Riko Ellison, of Grey District, won Rising Futures for security work and for the pride he took in it. Ruapehu District Council won Top Performer Relative to Population, the strongest employment result for the size of its youth population. The awards name four results. They do not say how many young people in those districts are still looking.
