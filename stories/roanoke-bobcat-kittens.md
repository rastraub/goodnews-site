Two bobcat kittens whose den was disturbed during land clearing in Chatham are in round-the-clock care at the Southwest Virginia Wildlife Center in Roanoke, WSLS reported. They arrived September 28 at about five days old, eyes still shut. The eyes opened about 10 days later. Founder Sabrina Garvin said a late-September arrival is unusual. Bobcats are typically born in the spring, and the center does not know why this litter came so late.

Garvin and her husband, Lucky, split the overnight bottles. Caregivers wear masks and keep quiet so the kittens do not habituate. Garvin said bobcat kittens can get used to people in a matter of days, which is why the goal is to keep them wild enough to leave.
