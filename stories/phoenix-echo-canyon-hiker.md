On Nov. 18, at about 2:30 p.m., the Phoenix Fire Department responded to a man with a leg injury near the top of the Echo Canyon trail on Camelback Mountain, 12 News reported. The Phoenix police helicopter flew to the mountain and landed on the slope. Firefighters helped the hiker over to that helicopter, which then carried him off Camelback.

The fire department said he turned down any further ride to a hospital and left in his own vehicle. No firefighters were hurt on the call. 12 News did not name the hiker.
