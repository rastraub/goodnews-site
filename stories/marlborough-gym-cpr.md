Marlborough police officer Alex Nevarez, the department's CPR instructor, was saved with CPR and an AED after he went into cardiac arrest at a Planet Fitness in Westborough on September 30, Boston 25 News reported. The city honored four people on Tuesday with citations: Planet Fitness employees Kauan Carvalho and Colin McGuiness, and gym members Anne Beckman and Robin Sommers, an off-duty nurse practitioner.

Marlborough police said their immediate actions, together with Westborough fire and police, helped save his life. Nevarez has returned to work on light duty and is still progressing toward full duty. The department called the rescue a reminder that CPR training, quick action, and a nearby AED can change what happens next.
