A 17-year-old hiking alone on Mount Tallac stopped sending updates to his family around 5 p.m. Sunday as he came down from the summit, The Sacramento Bee reported, citing the El Dorado County Sheriff's Office. The mountain had about six inches of new snow, with more snow, high wind, and poor visibility in the forecast. He had a cellphone and an emergency locator beacon. His family called, and the sheriff's office logged an overdue hiker just before 7 p.m.

Search-and-rescue volunteers used the beacon to narrow the search to an area called The Bowl, off the established trails. Raven, the Washoe County Sheriff's Office helicopter, started a search from the air and then had to leave because of the weather. The volunteers kept going on foot.

At The Bowl they found bootprints in the snow and followed them for several hours. They reached the teenager about 1:30 a.m. Monday in a remote spot northwest of Floating Island Lake. He was showing early signs of hypothermia and other cold-weather injuries. The volunteers gave him care and guided him back to the trailhead, where he met his family. The sheriff's office did not release his name.
