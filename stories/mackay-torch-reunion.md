ABC News reported on November 16, 2026, that Olympic torchbearers from 1956 reunited in Mackay on Saturday, 70 years after they earned the run. The men are now in their 80s and 90s. As teenagers they could run a mile in under six or seven minutes. Eric Ross said the training was the work itself. "You didn't really do any specific training other than you worked hard and you knew that you were fit," he said, and he described chasing cows in the paddock.

Ross ran his mile north of The Leap with a flaming torch that weighed 2 kilograms. It was a dirt road at about two in the morning. He waited until an army truck set up, took the flame from the runner ahead, and went. The briefing does not say how many of the old torchbearers were there. The news is the reunion and Ross's account of that mile. It is not a full roll of everyone who carried the flame through Queensland.
