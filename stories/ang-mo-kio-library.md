Ang Mo Kio Library opened again on November 20, 2026, on the fourth floor of AMK Hub, The Straits Times reported. The National Library Board moved it from a standalone building on Ang Mo Kio Avenue 6, closed since August 1, into the space a cinema used to occupy. In the main room, books run from the floor to shelves about six metres high. More than 5,500 of those higher books are pre-loved copies. The board said this is the first public library in Singapore with that double-height space through the main area.

The new room also has a six-metre immersive hub with the network's first 360-degree curved screen, an 18-metre mural of local landmarks, and a children's zone shaped by the town's old traffic garden. Readers can book 101 seats with power points, including seven study nooks. The study zone stays open from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, including public holidays, and from November 21 people can enter at 9 a.m. to browse and borrow even though staffed services start at 11. For the first time, books on the study-zone shelves can be borrowed on a public holiday through the library app. Regular staffed hours are 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Library manager Norlin Naim said patrons had asked for more time, and that the bookable seats fill up with students in exam season and with freelance workers. Resident Huang Pu Feng, 79, came with his grandson, who chose a stack of comics. Huang said the room looks more modern than the old site and hoped for book talks that would widen the boy's reading. The mall sits on the MRT station and the bus interchange, which is the practical change: a person running errands can walk in without a separate trip.
