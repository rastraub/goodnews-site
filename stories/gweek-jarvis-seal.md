Jarvis, a resident seal at the Cornish Seal Sanctuary in Gweek, is back with his pool mates after cataract surgery on his right eye. BBC News reported on November 17, 2026, that he was rescued in 2016 with cataracts in both eyes. A specialist eye vet operated on the right eye in October. Clare Addyman, an animal care specialist, said he moved back into his old pool this weekend with Bo and Buddy and settled easily. He is still on eye drops, and the eye is a little sore. Addyman said the cloudiness has mostly faded and that there are clear signs he can see better from that eye.

The sanctuary raised more than £15,000 toward surgery on both eyes. Staff have said they hope the left eye can be operated on in spring 2027, so this is not a finished restoration of his sight. The news is his return to the pool and the improvement in the eye that has already been treated.
