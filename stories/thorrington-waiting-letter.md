Rennie Chivers walked into the Red Lion in Thorrington and found a letter he had posted to himself in 1972, framed on the wall. BBC News reported the reunion on November 23, 2026. The envelope is a first-day cover: new stamps for the silver wedding of Queen Elizabeth II and the Duke of Edinburgh, postmarked on the day those stamps were issued. He had assumed it was lost. Under the frame, someone had written that it was still waiting for Rennie.

He had been out walking with friends and stopped for a drink. Nick Dunham, a friend of more than 50 years, spotted the frame. Chivers said he paid little attention until he saw his own name, then recognized the envelope at once. He told the BBC he had never been in the pub, knew no one connected with it, and had no explanation for how the letter arrived. A member of the bar staff called it something of a pub legend. The date made it sharper: he found it on November 20, fifty-four years after the postmark. He compared the odds to a lottery win and said his handwriting from that year was part of the shock. The report does not say who framed the envelope or where it spent the years in between.
