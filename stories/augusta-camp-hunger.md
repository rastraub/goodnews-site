Camp Out Hunger, an eighth annual radio-station campout at Sam's Club on Marketplace Drive in Augusta, is collecting food and money through Friday at 3 p.m., WABI reported. This year's donations are a record.

Everything collected stays in central Maine for the Capitol Region Salvation Army pantry. The record is the size of the giving during the campout, and Friday at 3 p.m. is when the collection stops.
