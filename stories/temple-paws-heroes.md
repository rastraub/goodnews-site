Temple's shelter is offering free adoptions in November to veterans and active-duty service members through Heart to Home's Paws for Heroes program, KCEN reported. Adopters show proof of service, and the usual fee is waived.

The offer covers November. It is a fee waiver for eligible adopters, not a count of animals that have already gone home, and the shelter still makes the match.
