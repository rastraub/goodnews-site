Hunters for the Hungry takes deer, bear, and moose that Maine hunters donate and turns that meat over to soup kitchens and food pantries. The program distributed nearly 17,000 pounds last year. By mid-November this year, hunters had already given 6,500 pounds. One deer can make about 200 meals. The meat does not sit in a freezer at camp. It becomes dinners for people who use the pantries.
