Proctor Academy in Andover kept a quarter-million cubic feet of snow under insulated covers all summer, the Concord Monitor reported. It is New Hampshire's first snow-farming effort. The saved snow gave the ski team runs in November.

A quarter-million cubic feet is the amount stored. The covers stayed on through the summer. The November sessions used that snow. The school is Proctor Academy, and the method is new for New Hampshire.
