Sandy Slater received the city's Alexander Griggs Award, the Grand Forks Herald reported. She is the longest-serving volunteer on the Historic Preservation Commission, with 38 years of service.

The award is the Alexander Griggs Award. The commission is Grand Forks' Historic Preservation Commission. Thirty-eight years is the length of her volunteer service, which is what makes her the longest-serving member. The honor came from the city.
