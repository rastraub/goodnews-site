The West Virginia Land Trust opened a Grounding Garden at the Marie Hall Jones Ancient Forest Preserve in Doddridge County. A local foundation and AARP grants paid for the work. The preserve is the old woods. The garden is a finished corner of it, built so visitors have a place to stand still inside a forest that was already being kept. The opening is the day that corner stopped being a plan.
