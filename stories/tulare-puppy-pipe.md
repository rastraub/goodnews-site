Tulare County firefighters pulled a puppy from an irrigation pipe on Thanksgiving morning and reunited him with his 3-year-old owner. Engine 25's crew found the dog five feet down a concrete pipe. That is deeper than a hand over the rim. They got him out, and he went home with the child who had been missing him. The holiday call was not a parade. It was a small dog, a pipe, and the ride back to the person who knew his name.
