Holden police officers, firefighters, and highway workers went to Holden Square Apartments with criminal justice students from Husson University, WABI reported. They brought boxes of food and $50 Hannaford gift cards to about 20 residents. The drop-off is part of the police department's 25 Days of Kindness.

Chief Eddie Benjamin said many of the residents are older adults living on fixed incomes, and he hopes the food and cards will help them through the winter. He said the students could see how that kind of work is done and might try something like it in their own careers. Husson senior Sophia Lamy said the officers were an example of caring for a community when people are doing well, not only in their hardest moments. Former Chief Chris Greeley started 25 Days of Kindness in 2017, and Benjamin said it has grown each year.
