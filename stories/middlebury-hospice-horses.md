Judith Sampers paints horses and collects pictures of them. Luminary Hospice took her to LoveWay's barn in Middlebury so she could be with the animals themselves. She groomed them, petted them, and hugged them. The visit was her wish. The pictures at home were the stand-in. For one morning the horses were in the barn, close enough to touch.
