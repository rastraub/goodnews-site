The crash was reported at 7:48 p.m. Sunday near Georgetown Pike and Bellview Road in McLean, WJLA reported. Fairfax County firefighters found a van that had gone into a roadside ditch. The van was still upright when they arrived, but it was leaning.

Crews stabilized the vehicle so it would not shift, then helped all nine occupants get out. The Fairfax County Fire and Rescue Department said no injuries were reported.
