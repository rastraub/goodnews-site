Sixty-four kids from the Pacific Northwest were chosen as sweepers at Skate America in Everett, OPB reported. After the world's top skaters performed, the kids cleared stuffed animals and flowers off the ice.

Sixty-four is the number selected. The job was to clear the ice. The competition was Skate America, and the rink is in Everett. The kids are from the Pacific Northwest. The toys and flowers are what the audience had thrown.
