Scouts in the Narragansett Council collected more than 87 tons of food in the Scouting for Food drive, the Warwick Beacon reported. The food went to the Rhode Island Community Food Bank and to more than 50 pantries. The 87 tons is the size of this drive. The pantries are where that food is headed.
