Liberty Dogs graduated its first dogs raised for the program, KTVN 2News reported. Four pairs crossed the finish: a veteran and a purpose-bred service dog in each one. Among them were retired Air Force Chief Master Sergeant Marc Allen and Daisy, a Labrador.

The ceremony in Reno marks a first class, not a claim that every veteran who wants a dog has one. The four pairs are the graduates this program has now sent home.
