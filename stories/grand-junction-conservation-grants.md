The Western Colorado Community Foundation, based in Grand Junction, granted $82,000 to 13 conservation projects, The Aspen Times reported. The money comes from its Conservation, Outdoor Recreation and Environment Giving Circle. The foundation received 34 applications asking for more than $266,000. Recipients include Aspen Valley Land Trust and EUREKA! McConnell Science Museum, along with groups working on wildfire recovery, habitat restoration, water monitoring, outdoor recreation, and hands-on environmental education. The $82,000 is this grant round. It does not fund every application that came in.
