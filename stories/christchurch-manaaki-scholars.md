The University of Canterbury reported on November 12, 2026, that 15 Manaaki New Zealand scholars have finished their studies and were celebrated this week. Family, friends, lecturers, and classmates joined in person and online, with representatives of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade and Education New Zealand. The scholarships are offered by the New Zealand government for students from developing countries.

This year's group at the university comes from 10 countries: Thailand, Cambodia, Papua New Guinea, Fiji, the Philippines, Malaysia, the Federated States of Micronesia, Indonesia, Kiribati, and Laos. Assistant Vice-Chancellor Engagement David Craig told them selection recognised academic ability, ambition, and the chance to contribute at home, and that finishing the degree was another milestone. The university's note does not list each scholar's subject or what they will do next. The news is 15 completed scholarships, marked with the people who got them through.
