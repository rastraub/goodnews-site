Allen Iverson joined Hampton, Newport News, and the Peninsula Foodbank to hand out 263 turkeys and 300 bags of food, WTVR reported. He credited his grandmother for teaching him to give.

The giveaway was in Hampton Roads, the region he came home to. Two hundred sixty-three turkeys and 300 bags are the totals. The cities and the food bank were the partners on the line with him.
