The YMCA/YWCA Hobby Craft Club in Marshalltown has sewn 822 fabric hearts for premature babies and their families, the Times-Republican reported. Mothers wear a heart so it carries their scent to babies in Des Moines NICUs.

The program is Holding Tiny Hands, run by a club member's grandson. The 822 count is the hearts the club has made for that effort, a way for a parent who cannot hold a baby all day to leave something familiar in the crib.
