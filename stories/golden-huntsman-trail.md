Jefferson County Open Space has opened the Huntsman Trailhead, a new way into Clear Creek Canyon west of Golden, the Golden Transcript reported. The segment is 1.5 miles long and 10 feet wide, and it meets ADA standards. It includes four bridges, five places to reach the creek, and new parking. The piece belongs to a longer trail the county is planning, about 65 miles from Denver to Loveland Pass. What is open now is this accessible stretch, not that full route.
