On a stormy afternoon in August, a 90-year-old driver lost control on the 1900 block of North Prospect Avenue in Champaign and went into a flooded retention pond. Good News Network reported on October 6, 2026, that the car sank up to its roof. Three community members went into the water. Four Champaign police officers helped pull her out. She was rescued.

On September 29 the three people who entered the water received Community Lifesaving Awards. The four officers received the chief's challenge coin for the risks they took. They did not receive the lifesaving awards. The driver came to the ceremony and thanked the people who got her to safety.
