Bystanders jumped into a St. Peters pond after an SUV rolled in, First Alert 4 reported. Austin Collins used a hammer someone handed him, broke a window, and pulled 3-year-old Melody out. Other people were in the water with him.

Melody is alive and home. Her mother thanked the crowd. The report is about that rescue, not a replay of how the vehicle entered the pond.
