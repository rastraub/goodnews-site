Students from Maple Avenue Elementary and Bartlett Elementary, along with their families, ran the Goffstown Rail Trail in a Color Fun Run, WMUR reported. The event raises money for field trips, school supplies, and playground equipment. Families took to the rail trail in clouds of color, and the money is meant for those school needs.
