A black kite that had been hanging from a live high-tension line since November 23 is off the wire. The Hindu reported that the Avian and Reptile Rehabilitation Centre said the bird was on a 400-kilovolt line at a height of more than 90 feet near Bannerghatta. The rescue started about 5:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 26. The line supplies nearly ten areas of Bengaluru, and the power company first said it could be switched off only at night.

The centre worried that a video already spreading online would send someone up a live line. Karnataka Power Transmission Corporation brought in an insulated aerial platform, imported from Brazil, that could reach the bird without a shutdown. The kite was lifted clear at 1 p.m., and the electricity stayed on. Forest department staff, the power company, the rescue group, and members of the public were in the coordination. The bird was injured and is in treatment. The news is a rescue completed without cutting power to those neighborhoods. It is not a report that the kite has recovered.
