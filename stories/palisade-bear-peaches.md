Peaches that could not be sold are feeding a much larger group of orphaned bear cubs than usual, the Grand Junction Daily Sentinel reported, in an account carried by the Delta County Independent. Talbott Farms in Palisade sent about 15 tons of that fruit to a wildlife rehab in Silt, along with apples at cost.

The center is caring for more than 30 orphaned cubs this year. A typical year is about five. The donation is this season's unsellable crop pointed at that larger group, not a standing supply for every rehab in Colorado.
