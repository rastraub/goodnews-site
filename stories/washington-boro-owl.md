A great horned owl hit near Carlisle rode headfirst in a truck's air dam for 70 miles and survived, CBS Pittsburgh reported. The truck stopped in Washington Boro. Raven Ridge Wildlife Center found no broken bones. Caregivers plan to drive the owl back and release it once its eye heals.

Seventy miles is the ride. No broken bones is the exam. The eye is what still has to heal before the trip back and the release.
