Powell County Search and Rescue found an overdue hunter safe beside a warm fire, NBC Montana reported. The team, including three new local volunteers, covered 4.5 miles at night west of Helmville and walked the 60-year-old out.

Safe beside the fire is how they found him. 4.5 miles is the night search. Three new local volunteers were part of the team that walked him out.
