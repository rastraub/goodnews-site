Loew's Jersey Theatre in Jersey City has reopened after a $130 million restoration, ABC7 New York reported. The 1929 building, known as a Wonder Theatre, had been kept from demolition by 20 years of volunteer work. It is open again as a place for performances and for community use. The dollar figure is the cost of the restoration. The two decades are the volunteer effort that stood between the theatre and the wrecking ball before that work was done.
