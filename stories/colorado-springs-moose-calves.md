Two orphaned moose calves, roughly 7 months old, are traveling from The Alaska Zoo's rescue program to Cheyenne Mountain Zoo in Colorado Springs, KRDO reported. Keepers have prepared a yard for them in Rocky Mountain Wild so the young moose have a place ready when they arrive. The calves were raised in the Alaska rescue program. The move is underway toward that prepared yard, which is the new home the zoo has set up for them.
