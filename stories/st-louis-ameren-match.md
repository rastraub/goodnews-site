Ameren is matching donations to the St. Louis Area Foodbank dollar for dollar through Nov. 30, with the match capped at $300,000, First Alert 4 reported. The drive is called Thanks4Giving.

On a separate shift, World Wide Technology employees packed more than 1,200 meals in under an hour. The $300,000 is the ceiling on what Ameren will add, and the meal count is what that employee crew packed in one short session.
