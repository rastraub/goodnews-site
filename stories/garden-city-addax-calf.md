Lee Richardson Zoo in Garden City welcomed a female addax calf born Oct. 3, KCLY Radio reported. The addax is a Saharan antelope with as few as 30 left in the wild. She was born through an Association of Zoos and Aquariums breeding program and has joined her family outdoors. The Oct. 3 birth is this calf. The figure of about 30 is the estimate for addax still in the wild, separate from the animals living in zoos.
