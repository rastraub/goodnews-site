An anonymous retiree in Illinois helped a stranger, and days later a Quick Pick ticket he bought in Sauget hit all six numbers for $1 million. He has said he plans to buy a minivan and take a trip to Las Vegas. The kindness and the ticket are days apart, not a bargain. TODAY reported the sequence: a good deed, then a winning ticket. He is not putting his name on either one. The million is the headline. The earlier help is why the story is not only a jackpot.
