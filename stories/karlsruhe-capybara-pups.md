Zoo Karlsruhe has capybara pups for the first time, and they are already in view. The local paper ka-news reported on November 19, 2026, from the zoo's own announcement, that three young were born to Nuara. The father is Sebastian. Zoo director Matthias Reinschmidt said he was delighted, and that watching the three explore is a particular pleasure because visitors already like the animals. The group also includes Necochea, Nuara's sister.

Sebastian was born on November 5, 2023, at Kolmården Zoo in Sweden and has lived in Karlsruhe since July 29, 2024. Nuara was born in March 2025 at Odense Zoo in Denmark and moved to Karlsruhe that November, as did Necochea. Adults can reach about 1.3 metres and 60 kilograms. They swim and dive, and the eyes, ears, and nostrils sit high on the head so an animal that is almost underwater can still watch the bank. Webbed toes help. The pregnancy is about five months, long for a rodent, and the young arrive with fur and open eyes, ready to follow the group. The zoo tied that life to South American wetlands, which are under pressure from farming and from changes in water. The news is these three pups, not a count of wild capybaras.
