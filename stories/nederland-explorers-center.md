TEENS, Inc. opened the Explorers Learning Center in Nederland, a mountain town where childcare spots have been scarce. The center cost about $7 million and has room for 60 children. It is already about 70 percent full. Families are not turned away because they cannot cover the full price.

Nederland has not had infant care before. That part of the center opens the Monday after the report. The new building is the town's way of making room for the youngest children and for the families who live around it.
