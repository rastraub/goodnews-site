A Czech community library opened on Rhodes on October 10 with more than 500 books. Rhodes Online reported the ribbon-cutting at the Czech school, with Ivo Šilhavý, the Czech ambassador to Greece, and Lucie Macháčková of the National Library of the Czech Republic.

The shelves hold books for children, teenagers, and adults. A Slovak collection is growing beside them. Donations have come from libraries, publishers, organizations, and private people, with an emphasis on children's books. The Czech association has worked with the national library for more than four years on community libraries abroad. On Rhodes, the association has been active since January 2025. Its school, in new rooms since September through a partnership with the Association of Multicultural Families of Rhodes, teaches Czech to children in Czech, Greek-Czech, and multilingual families, along with history and customs.

President Lenka Kanellia said the library should be a hub where people meet, not only a room of shelves. After the opening, Czech and Slovak residents talked about shared holidays and events for children. The report is an opening and a book count. It does not say how many people borrowed a book that week.
