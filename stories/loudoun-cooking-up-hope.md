More than 250 culinary students from three Loudoun County high schools, including Lightridge in the Aldie area, cooked full Thanksgiving dinners for families living in hotels. The project is Cooking Up Hope. They spent weeks taste-testing, then turned out 78 complete meals for households that do not have kitchens. A hotel room can hold a family. It often cannot hold a turkey in an oven. The school kitchens were the missing stove.
