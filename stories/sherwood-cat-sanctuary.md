Cat Alliance Team Sanctuary in Sherwood houses 140 cats, most of them considered unadoptable, InvestigateTV reported. Rooms hold 15 to 25 cats. One room is set aside for feral cats who will live there for good. Hobbit was born with no eyelids. Tommy has an underbite. Another cat came back after he kept attacking a cat in the home that adopted him.

Larson, who manages the cats, said the sanctuary was built for the animals other rescues pass over. Volunteer Lindemann helps with water, food, and litter boxes, and she calls herself another set of eyes. She told the station the work has made her calmer, given her a sense of accomplishment, and pulled her into a community outside herself. The sanctuary is recruiting more volunteers and says it is a good visit for parents and kids together.
