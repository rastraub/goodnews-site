Sveriges Radio reported on November 9, 2026, that Borås Animal Park has an unnamed southern white rhino calf, a week old that day. The calf is the fifth born to Mashiki. The station said the birth is part of the park's breeding work for the threatened species, and that the first pictures appeared on the park's social media.

Borås Tidning reported the same day that zoologist Johan Jönsson said the birth went according to plan and the calf nursed within an hour. The paper said Mashiki is 25, and that keepers had noticed the previous summer that she was not coming into season, so the birth was not a surprise. The birth fell between Sunday and Monday of the week before the articles. Neither report gives a sex or a wild census. The news is this fifth calf.
