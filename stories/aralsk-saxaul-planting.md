One hundred volunteers from Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Kyrgyzstan planted 500 saxaul shrubs on the dried bed of the Aral Sea. BAQ.KZ reported the work on October 22, 2026, during the GREEN ARAL: VOLUNTEERS FOR LIFE week. Ruslan Kayupov, head of the Kyzylorda regional public development department, attended.

Saxaul is planted on the old seabed because it can take hold in salty sand and help keep that sand from blowing. The dust that rises off the dry bed is one of the hardships left after the sea shrank. Five hundred shrubs are a single day's planting, not a forest, and they do not refill the Aral. They are roots in the ground where the water used to be.
