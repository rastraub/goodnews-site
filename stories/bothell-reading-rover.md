Reading with Rover, a volunteer program based in Bothell, brings trained dog teams to library events across Western Washington, KING 5 reported. The same teams also visit nursing homes and make crisis-comfort visits. A patient dog sits with a nervous young reader, and the visits also reach care homes and people who need a calm animal nearby.
