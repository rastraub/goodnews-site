The SPCA of Northern Nevada opened a Giving Tuesday match: donations are doubled up to $75,000 through Dec. 4, MyNews4 reported. The campaign is aimed at a $200,000 goal for rescue care, affordable veterinary services, and Todd's Medical Assistance Fund.

From January through September the organization spent a record $250,000. The $75,000 figure is the ceiling on the match, not cash already in hand, and the $200,000 is the amount the drive is trying to raise.
