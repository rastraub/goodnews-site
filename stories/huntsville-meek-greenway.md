Huntsville cut the ribbon on a 1.3-mile stretch of the Meek Greenway, the Huntsville Business Journal reported. The path runs from Sparkman Drive toward Alabama A&M and is named for Opal Meek, who advocated for years for the Treymore neighborhood.

The 1.3 miles is this newly opened section. The name is the city's way of tying the trail to that neighborhood work, and the ribbon-cutting is the opening, not a promise about later miles.
