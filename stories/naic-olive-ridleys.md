The Manila Times reported on November 12, 2026, that 53 olive ridley hatchlings from the Naic Sea Turtle Hatchery were released on the afternoon of November 8 in Barangay Labac. They hatched on November 7 and were sent out at once, because a longer hold would weaken them. Vice Mayor Jacinta Remulla, Roger Bilugan of the Labac Pawikan Patrollers, and residents watched. A short talk at the hatchery came first.

Bilugan said protection work in Naic began in 2011. The group is caring for 120 eggs across 19 nests. These 53 are the ones that have hatched. The eggs were found at Labac Bay on September 21, and 77 are still expected. Bilugan said the hatching came a bit early and suggested hotter sand linked to El Niño. That is his reading, not a measurement in the story. The released number is 53. The rest of the nest is still ahead.
