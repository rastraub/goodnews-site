Building Bridges Veterans Initiative served its free biweekly lunch to about 65 veterans in Greenfield, the Greenfield Recorder reported. A police honor guard took part. Guests also watched a mother-and-son video of Pioneer Valley residents thanking veterans.

The lunch is free and happens every two weeks. About 65 veterans were at this one. The video was the surprise played for the guests. The honor guard was part of the same gathering.
