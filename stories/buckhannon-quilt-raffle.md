Quilters at Heart and American Legion Post 7 raised $11,000 for the Buckhannon post with the guild's 2026 charity quilt raffle, My Buckhannon reported on November 5. Shelia Clark of Buckhannon won the quilt in the October 8 drawing. Her ticket was sold by Legion member Mike Brown. Post commander Mike Wallace drew the winning ticket and received the check at the guild's October meeting.

The guild makes a quilt each year and raffles it for a local charity. Members gave most of the credit to Legion members who sold tickets. The quilt for 2027 is already finished. The published figure is the $11,000 the raffle brought in. The release does not itemize how the post will use the money.
