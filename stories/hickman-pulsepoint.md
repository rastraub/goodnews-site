When Shawn Vernon's heart stopped in Hickman, her mother started CPR and her daughter called 911, KOLN reported. A PulsePoint alert reached an off-duty paramedic from Lincoln who was about three streets away and got there in about three minutes. A deputy's automated external defibrillator was part of the help. Vernon is recovering. The account is that chain: family CPR, the 911 call, the phone alert, and the defibrillator. It describes her as recovering. It does not give a later medical outcome beyond that.
