Huntington's Turkey Trot drew a record 2,200 runners for its 20th year, and the point of the race is an animal rescue. Proceeds and pet-food donations go to Little Victories Animal Rescue. Last year's race raised $64,000. The new record is a larger start line. The $64,000 is the proof that the same course, a year earlier, already turned registration into care for animals. This year's bags of pet food are the part that does not have to be converted from cash.
