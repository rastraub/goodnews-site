River Valley Kids Ski Too will give away free ski and snowboard gear on November 1 at Mountain Valley High School in Rumford, the Sun Journal reported. The hours are 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Gear goes first come, first served to children ages 3 through 18, and families can drop off items to donate or swap.

On October 27, founder Josh Hodsdon picked up more than 200 gently used pieces that Gary Savage of Mexico and his mother, Sandra Savage, had collected since January, largely from Bethel and Newry. Hodsdon said the helmets mattered most. This is the program's fourth year. He said it has probably reached about 400 kids, and a contact in New York sent 50 pairs of new high-end boots. Sandra Savage, 83, still picks up donations when she is out with friends.
