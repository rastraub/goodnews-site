Community Creatives, a Kenner music nonprofit that grew out of a song, has taught about 60 children since 2022. The lessons are free. At a recital, 14-year-old Emily Pineda played Mozart's Turkish March and the crowd stood. A standing ovation is a short thing. The lessons that get a student there are the years of work the nonprofit has been handing out at no charge.
