Commerce City Officer Jose Victoria got a one-and-a-half-year-old girl breathing again after her family pulled into a fire station that is no longer active, KDVR reported. They had noticed she was not breathing on the way to the hospital and pulled in looking for help. Victoria, a seven-year veteran, reached them on the 911 call.

He told KDVR there were very few signs of life. He picked her up and said, "Come on, please just breathe." He used a sternum rub and was getting ready to start CPR when she began breathing on her own. Firefighters took her to the hospital. He was later told she was in stable condition.

When she opened her eyes, he pointed at the patrol-car lights and said, "Hey, you're awake! Look at the lights!" He said he does not do the work for thanks. "I just do it. It's what I signed up for," he told KDVR. The police department posted a thank-you for the save.
