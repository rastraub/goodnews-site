A hiker spent seven days in the country near Sheeps Bridge, off Bloody Basin Road, after floodwater took his supplies while he crossed creeks, Arizona's Family reported. He got one message out. Then his phone died, and the search had to work from that last point.

A Yavapai County sheriff's helicopter found him early Saturday. He was exhausted and scratched and at the end of what his body could do, and he was in good health. The crew lifted him out of the flooded ground he had been walking since the creeks rose.
