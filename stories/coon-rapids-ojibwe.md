Anoka-Hennepin schools are teaching Ojibwe for the first time, MPR News reported. The class is in the district that includes Coon Rapids, and students described the language as worth learning.

The course is new to the district's catalog. It is one class, not a requirement for every student, and not a claim that the language is new. It is newly offered in these public schools.
