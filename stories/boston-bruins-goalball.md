Two Boston Bruins, including Tanner Jeannot, tried goalball with the Perkins School for the Blind's Towerhawks, WHDH reported. The players wore blackout shades and played the sport, which uses a ball with bells.

The shades take away sight, which is the point of the game. The Towerhawks are the Perkins students. The visit put the hockey players on the students' court.
