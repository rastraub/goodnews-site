The Cat's Familiar in Gardiner marked one year, WABI reported. The cafe celebrated 42 cats adopted into homes and the addition of a pet food pantry that does not ask questions.

Forty-two is the adoption count for the first year. The pantry is a separate door for people who already have a pet and need food. The anniversary is this cafe's first, not a total for every shelter in Maine.
