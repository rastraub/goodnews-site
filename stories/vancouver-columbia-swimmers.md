Just before 2:30 p.m. Tuesday, Vancouver police and firefighters went to the waterfront after callers reported a man calling for help about 100 feet from shore, KPTV reported. Two rescue swimmers from the Vancouver Fire Department went into the Columbia River and brought him in.

Crews on a Portland Fire & Rescue boat then took him to a nearby dock. From there he was taken to a hospital. Officials said he is expected to be OK. The report does not give his name, and it does not say how he ended up in the water.
