Orchard Elementary's student council collected more than 1,000 boxes of cereal, and with a match from Lee's Marketplace the gift topped 2,400 pounds, the Davis Journal reported. Center of Hope will use it to serve breakfast to the 500 families a month it already reaches.

The school is in North Salt Lake. More than 1,000 boxes is the student collection. The match doubled the effort into more than 2,400 pounds. The 500 families are the pantry's monthly reach.
