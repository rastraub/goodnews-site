The Low Income Housing Institute opened Cloverleaf Tiny House Village in Seattle's South Park neighborhood on October 27, KOMO reported. LIHI described it as the organization's newest and largest tiny-house village. It holds 90 small houses.

Each one is a separate front door in the neighborhood, a place a person can stay in rather than a cot in a shared room. The opening was about those houses and the people who will live in them.
