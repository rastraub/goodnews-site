Three Marines from MCAS Beaufort received the sheriff's Life Saving Award for pulling a driver from a crashed pickup, WJCL reported. Cpls. Isaac Ames, Nathan Armstrong, and Caleb Crawford got the driver out on Parris Island Gateway moments before the truck was engulfed in flames.

The award is the sheriff's Life Saving Award. The three are corporals stationed at MCAS Beaufort. The pickup had crashed, and the flames took the truck just after the driver was out.
