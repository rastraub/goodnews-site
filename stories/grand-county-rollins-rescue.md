Grand County Search and Rescue spent 11 hours bringing three snowbound hikers off the Continental Divide, The Denver Gazette reported. The team used ATVs on Rollins Pass Road, then hiked in at about 11,600 feet. They warmed and fed the hikers. Everyone was out safe by 4:18 a.m.

Eleven hours is the length of the mission. About 11,600 feet is where the hike in reached. 4:18 a.m. is the time the three hikers were clear. The ATVs covered the road; the last stretch was on foot.
