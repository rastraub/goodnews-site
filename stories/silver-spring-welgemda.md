The Food and Drug Administration approved Welgemda, an oral solution of ribitol, as the first treatment for any form of limb-girdle muscular dystrophy. The approval covers LGMD2I/R9. In a randomized trial of 112 patients, the medicine improved walking speed and lung function compared with a placebo. An oral solution is a liquid a person swallows. For a disease that weakens the muscles used to walk and to breathe, those two measured changes are what the trial was built to test.
