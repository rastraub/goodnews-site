Students filled the floor of the Fargodome for the 20th Fill the Dome food drive. Over two decades, students from North Dakota and Minnesota have raised more than 2.8 million pounds of food and nearly $1 million for the Great Plains Food Bank. The dome is built for games. On this day the floor is the collection point, and the running total is what twenty years of student drives have added up to.
