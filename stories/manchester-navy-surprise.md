Cameron Bishop surprised his niece at school the week he came home from nearly 11 months at sea, WMUR reported on November 5, 2026. Bishop, 22, serves on the USS Abraham Lincoln, a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier that had been operating across the Pacific and the Middle East. He asked the front office at Parker Varney Elementary in Manchester if he could make the visit, and the school kept it quiet. Meadow jumped into his arms when he walked in in uniform.

Bishop said it felt good to see family after so long. Principal Andrew Sims called it the kind of moment a person cannot help but be moved by. Meadow got more time with her uncle the next day. She told the station he scared her. Bishop is home for 21 days before he returns. The article is the surprise at school. It does not describe the deployment beyond those months at sea.
