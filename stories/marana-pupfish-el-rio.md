Marana and Arizona Game and Fish released 183 endangered desert pupfish into the 9-acre pond at El Rio Preserve, Tucson Local Media reported. The release is covered by a safe harbor agreement. Biologists hope the pond will become a source population, a group that can later supply fish for new sites. That is a hope for what the pond might do. The step that has happened is the release of these 183 fish.
