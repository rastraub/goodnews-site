John Smolinsky of Earthbound Forestry in Greensboro still logs with a horse. His Belgian, Ted, weighs about a ton and pulls logs off small woodlots that are steep or easily damaged. The work is a Vermont tradition about 200 years old.

A horse lifts its feet, so it packs the soil far less than a heavy skidder does. That difference is why Smolinsky still brings Ted into woods where a machine would leave a deeper mark.
